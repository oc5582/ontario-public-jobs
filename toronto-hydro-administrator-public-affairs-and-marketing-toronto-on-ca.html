The Administrator, Public Affairs and Marketing provides administrative and operational support to a department responsible for communications, marketing, public affairs, digital engagement, community relations, Indigenous Relations, and sustainability initiatives. The role coordinates schedules, meetings, events, employee engagement programs, and departmental processes while supporting the delivery of projects and activities. Acting as a key point of contact for internal and external partners, the role contributes to the efficient operation of the department through strong organization, collaboration, planning and customer service. T his posting is for an existing vacancy. KEY RESPONSIBILITIES: Provides comprehensive administrative support to leaders and team members within Public Affairs and Marketing, including calendar management, meeting coordination, scheduling, employee engagement planning, and preparation of presentations and materials Acts as a key point of contact for internal and external inquiries, coordinating responses and facilitating communication Supports communications, marketing, customer engagement, public affairs, and community relations initiatives through project coordination, document preparation, and administrative support Coordinates internal support teams Maintains departmental records, databases, invoices, purchase orders, digital assets, and document management systems in accordance with organizational standards Assists with
