Who runs this site

PublicJobs.ca is run independently by Osama Chaudhary. It is not a government website and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or acting for any government or any employer listed on the site.

What the site covers

The site lists current job openings from public employers in Toronto and the GTA, such as the City of Toronto and other GTA cities, towns and regions, the Toronto Police Service, Crown corporations, provincial and federal agencies, City of Toronto agencies, and some public-interest regulators.

We do not hire and we do not take applications. Every job page links to the employer's own posting, and you apply there. Job pages are free and need no account. The homepage can search job titles and employer names. All openings links to each current job. Lists show the newest 10, and the full filtered list is a membership. The employers page names each organization and links to its openings. The weekly email is free and optional.

Match your resume compares a resume with the current openings. The resume is read to find matches and is not stored. One match is free after you sign in, and it shows the top 5 jobs. A membership includes more matches, up to 20 a day. Common questions are answered on the FAQ.

How listings are collected

Listings are read from employers' public careers pages. The listings file records which system each posting came from. On this site those systems are {{SOURCES}}. The words on each job page are the employer's posting. A posting whose closing date has passed stays on its own page, marked no longer available, and is left off the homepage, the jobs list, and the employer pages. Always check the employer's posting for the latest information, including closing dates and pay.

How often listings are updated

Listings are refreshed every weekday and published automatically. Each listing stores the time it was fetched from the employer. {{FETCHED}}
