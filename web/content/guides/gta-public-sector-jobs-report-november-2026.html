PublicJobs.ca counted 777 open jobs at 80 public employers in Toronto and the GTA on November 6, 2026. This report breaks those openings down by employer, pay and city, using the counts on the site's job stats page. The numbers come from the last weekday refresh before publication: Friday, November 6, 2026, at 2:16 PM ET.

A public employer is a GTA municipality, a City of Toronto agency, the Ontario Public Service, an Ontario or federal Crown corporation or agency, a federal department, or a public-interest regulator. The 80 employers in this report are the ones that had at least one open job on PublicJobs.ca on November 6. The report is not a count of every public-sector job in the region.

Key takeaways

  • 777 jobs were open at 80 GTA public employers on November 6, 2026. Most were in the GTA, and some were in other cities.
  • Metrolinx had the most openings (55), followed by the Ontario Public Service (49), Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation (CMHC, 43) and the City of Toronto (43).
  • 515 of the 777 jobs (66%) posted a salary. The median posted minimum was $88,792.
  • 23 of the 80 employers posted no salary on any of their open jobs.
  • 213 jobs were set to close within 7 days of November 6, and 202 had no closing date.

How many jobs are open at GTA public employers?

On November 6, 2026, PublicJobs.ca listed 777 open jobs at 80 public employers in Toronto and the GTA. Most of those jobs were in the GTA. Some employers also list jobs in other cities, such as Ottawa (25) and Calgary (19).

When the stats page started on October 20, 2026, it counted 750 open jobs at 63 employers. The two numbers do not measure hiring growth. Since October 20, the site has started collecting from more sources, including the Ontario Public Service. So much of the change is wider coverage.

An open job is one that has not been removed and whose closing date is today or later in Toronto time, or that has no closing date.

How many jobs at GTA public employers show the salary?

Two out of three do. 515 of the 777 open jobs (66%) posted a yearly salary minimum or maximum. On October 20, 2026, the share was 52% (393 of 750). Part of that change comes from employers added since October 20, such as the Ontario Public Service, which posted pay on 88% of its jobs.

Pay disclosure varies a lot by employer. Seven of the 11 employers in the table above posted pay on at least 88% of their jobs, and Oakville, Burlington and Halton Region posted it on all of them. Across all 80 employers, 23 posted no salary on any open job. The largest were CBC / Radio-Canada (33 jobs), CPPIB (32) and Chartered Professional Accountants of Ontario (CPA Ontario, 18).

The median posted minimum is the middle starting salary among jobs that show pay. On November 6 it was $88,792, so about half of those jobs started below that figure and about half started above it.

How much do jobs at GTA public employers pay?

The bands add up to 515, the number of jobs that posted pay.

176 jobs had a posted minimum of $100,000 or more. A wider group of 312 jobs had a posted range that reaches $100,000 or more.

Among employers with at least 5 open jobs that all showed pay, the highest starting points were at Transport Canada (minimums from $88,792), Toronto Hydro (from $80,487) and Infrastructure Ontario (from $74,500). The lowest posted minimum overall was $14,491, at the Municipality of Clarington. The highest posted maximum was $244,642, at the Department of Justice Canada. A very low yearly figure can come from a part-time or seasonal role, so check how each posting states its pay.

Where are the jobs?

Toronto had the most open jobs by far: 450. Next were Oakville (50), Mississauga (33), Burlington (28) and Markham (25).

City names are shown as each employer posts them. That means North York (22), Etobicoke (10) and Scarborough (8) are counted apart from the 450 tagged "Toronto", even though they are part of the city.

Some employers have GTA offices but also hire elsewhere in Canada. That is why the city counts include Ottawa (25), Hamilton (21), Calgary (19) and Montreal (up to 30, listed under two spellings). A job listed in more than one city is counted in each city, so the city totals add up to more than 777.

How many jobs close soon?

213 jobs were set to close within 7 days of November 6. Metrolinx (29), the City of Toronto (23) and the Ontario Public Service (22) had the most of them. The TTC had none closing in that window. Another 202 jobs had no closing date. Those postings can stay open for a long time, so it is worth checking them again later.

What this report does not cover

This report counts only the 80 employers that had open jobs on PublicJobs.ca on November 6. It does not include:

  • Hospitals and school boards
  • Toronto Police Service jobs (that feed is paused)
  • Employers whose careers sites PublicJobs.ca does not collect from

It also counts openings, not hires. One posting can fill several positions, and a talent pool posting may stay open for months.

How we count

These rules come from the stats page:

  • Open job: not removed, and the closing date is today or later in Toronto time, or there is no closing date.
  • Posts a salary: the posting included a yearly minimum or maximum.
  • Salary bands: based on the posted yearly minimum.
  • Closing in 7 days: the closing date is today or within the next 7 days.
