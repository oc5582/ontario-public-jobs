Canada Mortgage and Housing Corporation (CMHC) hires in five steps. You apply online. If your application fits, a recruiter sets up a 30-minute phone or online call. Bilingual jobs include a language test. Next comes a panel interview, typically one hour, and then a hiring decision. If you get an offer, a security check follows.

This guide explains each step using CMHC's own careers pages and all 45 postings that were open on CMHC's careers site on November 25, 2026. Facts were checked that day, except the PublicJobs.ca job stats (November 24).

Key takeaways

  • CMHC is a federal Crown corporation. It runs its own hiring on its own careers site.
  • The steps are: apply online, a 30-minute call, a language test for bilingual jobs, a panel interview (typically one hour), and a hiring decision.
  • 29 of the 45 open postings listed Toronto as an office option. All 29 were hybrid.
  • 41 of the 45 postings showed pay. They used only four ranges, from $54,665.94 to $161,469.09 a year.
  • 26 of the 45 postings were bilingual (English and French). 44 of the 45 listed a security level: Reliability Status or Secret.
  • Permanent jobs list a defined benefit pension. Temporary and casual postings do not.
  • PublicJobs.ca is independent and not affiliated with CMHC. You apply on CMHC's own site.

What CMHC is and who it hires

CMHC's management and governance page says it is a Crown corporation governed by a Board and responsible to Parliament through a Minister. A Crown corporation is a government-owned company with its own board.

That matters for job seekers. CMHC posts jobs on its own careers site, uses its own pay ranges and runs its own hiring steps. If you have read our federal hiring guide, note that CMHC works differently. You apply to a specific posting on CMHC's careers site, with a resume.

CMHC's Working at CMHC page says it has 2,357 employees across Canada. The page says its work runs from funding transitional shelters to supporting first-time homebuyers. That includes mortgage loan insurance.

The 45 postings open on November 25, 2026 show what it hires for:

  • Technology: 32 of the 45, by our count. Software engineers, cloud and DevOps specialists, IT security, a scrum master, a solutions architect and managers.
  • Mortgage insurance and securitization: client relations specialists, an account manager and a senior administrator.
  • Corporate and other jobs: HR business partner, employee relations, financial risk, geographic information systems (GIS), and a data stewardship Community of Practice Lead.
  • Casual jobs outside the GTA: two enumerators (Alberta and New Brunswick), a busser and a grounds maintenance worker at Granville Island in Vancouver.

38 of the 45 were permanent full-time, 3 were temporary full-time and 4 were casual.

Is CMHC hiring in Toronto?

Yes, but none of the 45 was Toronto-only. Most postings list several offices. Ask the recruiter which ones the job can be based in.

On November 25, 2026, 29 of the 45 postings listed Toronto (ON) as an office option. All 29 also listed Ottawa and Montreal. Only one, a Bilingual Senior Specialist, Client Relations, named Toronto first.

All 29 were hybrid. 41 of the 45 postings say CMHC jobs need "some in-office presence." 39 say a work from home arrangement may be considered for First Nations candidates on reserve, or Indigenous candidates who live more than 125 km from a CMHC office.

Step 1: Apply online

The hiring section of the Working at CMHC page sets out the steps. For the application:

  • Pick a job and click Apply now.
  • Create an account, or sign in if you have one.
  • Upload your resume. A cover letter is optional.
  • Answer some short questions.
  • You get a confirmation email when your application is in.

All 45 postings said the competition "will remain active until filled." None showed a closing date. A posting can come down once the job is filled.

How to read a CMHC posting

Most CMHC postings start with the same block of facts. Here is what each line means, using postings open on November 25, 2026:

Line on the posting What it tells you Example
Position Status Permanent, temporary or casual Permanent Full Time
Position Type Hybrid, remote or on-site Hybrid
Office Location The offices you can work from Ottawa (ON); Montreal (QC); Toronto (ON)
Language Designation Bilingual or English Essential Bilingual
Language Skill Levels (Read/Write/Speak) The French or English level needed CBC
Security Requirement The security check you must pass Reliability Status
Salary The pay range $86,816.59 to $108,520.74

Then read What you should have line by line. Education and years of experience vary. 8 of the 45 postings say they accept "an equivalent combination of education and experience" in place of the stated education. The Senior Administrator job asked for one year in securitization or mortgage underwriting. The Toronto client relations job asked for seven years.

Make each requirement easy to find in your resume, using the same words as the posting.

Step 2: The 30-minute initial call

If your application fits, CMHC says a member of its talent acquisition (recruiting) team will reach out. The call is a 30-minute phone or video conversation.

CMHC says this is your chance to ask questions: "lots of questions." The recruiter also checks your interests. They may see if you fit the job you applied for, or another role.

Good questions to ask on this call:

  • Which office would I work from, and how many days a week in the office?
  • What language level does this job need, and when is the test?
  • What does the panel interview cover?
  • When does the team hope to make a decision?

Step 3: Language testing for bilingual jobs

If the job is bilingual, you take a language test in your second language, French or English. CMHC says its language team will email you to book it. English Essential jobs skip this step.

On November 25, 2026, 26 of the 45 postings were bilingual. Some technology jobs came in both versions. CMHC posted a Senior Specialist, Software Engineering (D365 ERP) once as bilingual and once as English Essential.

Bilingual postings list three letters for reading, writing and speaking. The Toronto client relations job lists CBC, and the Community of Practice Lead job lists BBB. All 19 English Essential postings show ZZZ. The postings and the Working at CMHC page do not explain the letters, so ask the recruiter what each letter means for your job.

If you only speak English, look for English Essential in the Language Designation line. 19 of the 45 were English Essential.

Step 4: The panel interview

CMHC says the panel interview is typically one hour. It is behavioural and/or technical. The panel is the hiring manager plus 1 to 2 other panellists.

A behavioural question asks how you handled something in the past. For example (our example, not CMHC's): "Tell us about a time you had to explain a complex rule to a client." A technical question tests the skills on the posting, such as Azure or Dynamics 365 for a software job.

To prepare:

  1. List each item under What you should have.
  2. Write a short story for each one: the situation, your task, what you did and the result. This is the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result). Our TTC hiring process guide explains STAR in more detail.
  3. For technical jobs, be ready to walk through real projects with the tools named on the posting.
  4. Read about the business line in the posting, such as mortgage loan insurance or securitization.

The postings ask you to tell CMHC if you need an accommodation for an interview or test.

Step 5: The decision and security check

CMHC says the hiring team "will take some time" to decide. If you are successful, you get a call with an offer and next steps, such as a security clearance. If you are not, CMHC says you will get an email.

44 of the 45 postings named one of two security levels:

  • Reliability Status: 32 of the 45 postings.
  • Secret: 12 of the 45 postings. 11 were technology jobs. The other was a financial risk job.

Both are federal screening levels. Our federal hiring guide explains what each one checks. Check the posting so you know which level your job needs.

What CMHC pays

On November 25, 2026, 41 of the 45 postings showed pay. The 4 casual postings did not. The 41 used only four pay ranges:

Pay range on the posting Postings Example job
$54,665.94 to $68,332.43 1 Senior Administrator
$86,816.59 to $108,520.74 10 Bilingual Human Resources Business Partner
$104,180.28 to $130,225.36 26 Senior Specialist, Software Engineering
$129,175.28 to $161,469.09 4 Advisor, Solutions Architect

Every posting that shows pay says it is "based on qualifications and experience." The postings do not say where in the range a new hire starts.

40 of the 41 postings with pay had a top of range over $100,000, but most were senior or technical jobs. For example, the Specialist, Software Engineering (MW-Azure) job asked for at least 5 years of Azure and C# .NET work.

Pension, bonus and benefits

CMHC's Working at CMHC page lists these benefits:

  • a defined benefit pension plan, plus group RRSP and TFSA programs,
  • health and dental coverage you choose, with leftover credits usable for a gym or a health spending account,
  • vacation, learning and development, and performance-based incentives.

A defined benefit pension pays a set income in retirement, based on a formula, not on how investments did.

The postings split this by job type. All 38 permanent postings list the defined benefit pension. None of the 3 temporary or 4 casual postings do. The two permanent postings we read also list paid vacation, an annual performance incentive and group insurance from day one. The two temporary postings we read list accrued vacation, an annual performance bonus and group insurance from day one.

How long does CMHC hiring take?

CMHC's careers pages do not give a timeline. Bilingual jobs have five steps. English Essential jobs skip the language test, so they have four.

CMHC's postings show no closing date. They stay open "until filled," so there is no fixed deadline, but a posting can close at any time. Apply soon after a job appears, and set up job alerts on CMHC's careers site or check the board often.

Frequently asked questions

How is CMHC different from a federal department?

CMHC is a federal Crown corporation, governed by its own Board and responsible to Parliament through a Minister. It posts jobs on its own careers site and runs its own hiring steps.

Do I need to speak French to work at CMHC?

Not for every job. On November 25, 2026, 19 of the 45 open postings were English Essential. The other 26 were bilingual and include a language test.

Can I work from home at CMHC?

Most jobs are hybrid. 42 of the 45 postings were hybrid, 1 was remote and 2 were on-site. 41 of the 45 say jobs need some in-office presence.

Summary

  • Apply online with a resume. The cover letter is optional.
  • Use the 30-minute call to ask about the office, language level and interview.
  • Prepare STAR stories and technical examples for the panel interview.
  • Expect Reliability Status or Secret screening after an offer.
