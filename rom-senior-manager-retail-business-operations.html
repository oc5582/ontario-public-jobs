Job description

WHO WE ARE Opened in 1914 and welcoming over 1.3 million visitors annually, the Royal Ontario Museum (ROM) is Canada's most-visited museum, ranking among the top 10 cultural institutions in North America. At ROM, we are dedicated to building and sharing global collections, sharing knowledge, inspiring learning, and fostering community engagement. As a globally recognized field research institute, ROM houses an impressive collection of over 18 million artworks, cultural objects, and natural history specimens, showcased in over 40 galleries and exhibition spaces. We are committed to creating a space where art, culture, and nature intersect, connecting people and communities to the past, present and shared future. As we continue to work toward our goals to transform and evolve as one of the world’s foremost 21st-century museums, we seek passionate and dynamic team members who embody our mission and values. WHO WE NEED We are seeking a strategic and results-oriented Senior Manager, Retail Business Operations to lead the Royal Ontario Museum's retail business operations across both in-store and e-commerce channels. Reporting to the SVP, Commercial Enterprises, this role oversees retail sales, customer service, merchandise buying, name-drop product development, Shopify and e-commerce operations. As a key leader within Commercial Enterprises, you will drive revenue growth, optimize operational performance, and deliver exceptional retail experiences that connect visitors and non-visitor shoppers with ROM's exhibitions, collections, and brand. Located within the Bloor-Yorkville shopping corridor. You will lead a team responsible for front-of-house and back-of-house retail operations while fostering strong relationships with artists, suppliers, manufacturers, and internal stakeholders. This is an exciting opportunity for an experienced retail leader who combines strong commercial acumen with a passion for customer experience, product innovation, team leadership, and operational excellence. Working in a dynamic cultural environment, you will help shape retail offerings that support ROM's mission while contributing to the Museum's long-term financial sustainability and visitor engagement goals. HOW YOU WILL MAKE AN IMPACT Take a hands-on approach to lead and inspire a high-performing retail team responsible for in-store sales, customer service, merchandise buying, ecommerce operations, and retail business administration, fostering a culture of accountability, collaboration, and exceptional service. Drive ROM Retail's overall financial performance by monitoring sales trends, product margins, inventory performance, and key performance indicators, ensuring revenue targets are met while delivering an outstanding visitor experience. Develop and execute retail merchandising strategies that support ROM exhibitions, galleries, public programs, and institutional priorities through thoughtfully curated product assortments and innovative retail experiences. Lead the growth and optimization of ROM's Shopify and e-commerce operations, identifying opportunities to expand online sales, improve operational efficiency, and strengthen customer engagement across digital channels. Build and maintain strong partnerships with artists, suppliers, manufacturers, distributors, and community-based organizations to source distinctive merchandise that reflects ROM's collections, exhibitions, and diverse communities. Oversee the development and production of branded and custom merchandise that enhances visitor engagement, supports curatorial initiatives, strengthens ROM's brand presence, and contributes to revenue growth. Collaborate with colleagues across the Museum to ensure retail programs align with institutional priorities, exhibition storytelling, digital initiatives, community engagement efforts, and brand standards. Champion operational excellence by implementing best practices in retail operations, inventory management, procurement, financial reporting, and customer service while ensuring compliance with ROM policies and procedures. WHAT YOU BRING University or college degree/diploma in Retail Management, or a related field, or an equivalent combination of education and experience. Supply Chain, Procurement, or Purchasing certifications are considered an asset. A minimum of 5–7 years of progressive retail leadership experience, including responsibility for business operations, merchandising, inventory management, sales performance, and customer experience. Demonstrated success leading and developing high-performing teams in a fast-paced retail, e-commerce, attraction, hospitality, cultural, or customer-facing environment. Strong financial and business acumen, with experience managing budgets, analyzing sales performance, monitoring KPIs, and driving revenue growth through data-informed decision-making. Experience sourcing, negotiating, and building relationships with suppliers, artists, manufacturers, distributors, or other external partners, with a demonstrated ability to balance quality, cost, and business objectives. Knowledge of merchandise planning, buying, name-drop product development, inventory management, and retail operations best practices. Experience supporting or managing ecommerce operations, including Shopify or similar ecommerce platforms, digital merchandising, and online sales performance. Excellent communication, relationship-building, negotiation, and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively across diverse teams and audiences. Strong organizational and project management skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities, meet deadlines, and adapt to changing business needs. Proficiency with Microsoft Office applications and experience working with retail, inventory, purchasing, or e-commerce systems. A customer-first mindset, a passion for delivering exceptional visitor experiences, and a commitment to fostering an inclusive and welcoming environment for all guests. Flexibility to work evenings, weekends, and special events based on operational requirements, seasonal business demands, inventory activities, and Museum programming.
