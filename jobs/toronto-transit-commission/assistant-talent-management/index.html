Career Opportunity

Our Talent Management Department is seeking an Assistant, Talent Management to join their team!

What You Will Do

The Talent Management Assistant is a key member of the Talent Management team, with a strong focus on supporting the Talent Acquisition function and helping deliver an exceptional experience for candidates and hiring managers. This role is instrumental in ensuring a seamless and efficient candidate journey through a commitment to service excellence, operational efficiency, and strong stakeholder engagement. The incumbent will also:

Coordinate recruitment activities throughout the hiring lifecycle, including interview scheduling, pre-employment process, onboarding, candidate communications, and recruitment status updates

Respond to candidate inquiries in a professional and timely manner.

Coordinate candidate pre-employment requirements and manage to completion

Ensure candidates receive accurate and consistent information regarding their progress through the pre-employment process

Track completion of pre-employment requirements and escalate issues as required

Manage the candidate onboarding process from pre-employment to Day 1, including ensuring compliance in documentation and processes.

Maintain accurate information within the system of record (Success Factors) and related HR systems.

Generate recruitment reports and monitor metrics to support operational and business requirements.

Monitor personal performance using data, dashboards, and reports to meet service and productivity targets

Support data integrity, record retention, and compliance requirements.

Support special Talent Acquisition and Talent Management projects as assigned.

What Skills Do You Bring?

Strong organizational and time management skills with the ability to manage multiple competing priorities.

A strong focus on accuracy, compliance, and process excellence

Excellent customer service and internal client management skills

Strong written and verbal communication skills.

Experience working with Applicant Tracking Systems and HRIS platforms

Ability to adapt to changing priorities, while maintaining quality and service standards

Ability to work independently, apply critical thinking and problems solve, while collaborating effectively within a team environment

Ability to work in a metrics-driven environment, using dashboards and reports to monitor performance and drive results

Ability to maintain confidentiality and handle sensitive information with discretion

Proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite, including Excel, Outlook, Word, PowerPoint, Bookings, Forms, Sharepoint and Teams

Understanding of hiring practices and relevant employment legislation to support compliant and equitable recruitment processes.

What Qualifications Do You Bring?

Diploma/Degree in HR or related field or a combination of education, training and experience deemed equivalent

1-3 years experience supporting a large-scale recruitment function, coordinating recruitment activities

Experience managing candidates in an Applicant Tracking System preferred

General Prohibition in Hiring Process

The TTC is also committed to a fair hiring process based on job related qualifications, merit, and abilities. You are expected to write your own materials and provide your own qualifications and experiences during the hiring process. The TTC will not tolerate the use of artificial intelligence or external resources in any of your submissions or responses, or the disclosure of interview questions to others. By submitting your application, you acknowledge and agree to the following:

The use of any artificial intelligence (AI) tool including, but not limited to, ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot, to generate or produce any information or materials to be submitted or any response to any assessment question or any interview question, is prohibited.

All information and materials you submit, including resume, cover letter, and assessment responses, are your own original work without the use of any AI tool

During any interview, your response to any interview question is your own without the use or assistance of any AI tool or any person.

You will not disseminate or disclose to any person any interview question.

Should you breach the prohibition of AI tool use, or information dissemination or disclosure, you will be in violation of TTC’s hiring process and TTC may withdraw your application and may prohibit you from participation in the TTC hiring process.

Relatives of TTC Employees

The TTC’s policy prohibits relatives of current TTC employees from being hired, assigned, transferred or promoted into positions, where there is a conflict of interest due to a relationship. Should you be selected for an interview, you will be required to disclose the name, relationship and position of any relative who is a current TTC employee.
