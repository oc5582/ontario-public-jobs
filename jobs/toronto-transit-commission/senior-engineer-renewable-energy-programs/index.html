Career Opportunity

Looking for a new and exciting job opportunity? We are looking for a Senior Engineer, Renewable Energy Programs to join our team! As part of the Renewable Energy Programs team, you will lead the corporate transition for renewable energy infrastructure. Come join our team during this exciting time!

What You Will Do

As a member of the Renewable Energy Programs Department (REP), you will report to the Manager, Renewable Energy Engineering. Your role will involve providing technical oversight for REP, which manages approximately $1 billion in programming. This includes the design, construction, installation, operation, maintenance, and renewal of TTC’s energy infrastructure aimed at supporting a zero-carbon future. Key components of this infrastructure include eBus and other fleet charging systems, battery energy storage systems (BESS), solar PV systems, and associated systems that support TTC’s Green Fleet Plan, Green Facilities, and other renewable energy and decarbonization initiatives.

Key Responsibilities:

Technical Oversight:

Ensure the technical integrity of both capital projects and operational activities.

Risk Management:

Identify technical risks and propose effective risk mitigation strategies.

Project Coordination:

Identify project interdependencies (e.g., fleet plans, proposed construction projects) and develop comprehensive technical coordination plans.

Quality Assurance:

Oversee the quality and timeliness of construction, commissioning and operational submittals and witness major equipment/system testing to ensure compliance with TTC requirements.

Safety Support:

Assist the project delivery team with construction safety plans as needed.

Documentation:

Review: Review and approve technical reports, testing results, and other documentation.

Failure Investigation:

Collaborate with REP staff and other TTC stakeholders/experts to investigate complex failures.

Issue Resolution:

Address operational issues with service providers and/or original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) and ensure successful resolution.

Procedure Review:

Evaluate existing operating procedures, test plans, and maintenance plans, recommending changes to maintain safety and quality standards in light of new REP systems.

Procedure Development:

Oversee and review the creation of new operating procedures, test plans, and maintenance plans for novel REP systems.

What Qualifications Do You Bring?

Your educational background will consist of a university degree in electrical engineering, or a related discipline with extensive experience in the design and systems engineering of critical industrial infrastructure, or any combination of education, training and experience deemed to be equivalent.

Mandatory registration as a licensed Professional Engineer in the Province of Ontario.

Registration as a Project Management Professional is an asset.

Comprehensive knowledge of formal systems engineering techniques applied to critical industrial electrical distribution systems.

Working knowledge of modern supervisory control software practices.

Proficiency in design, system assurances (e.g. reliability, availability, maintainability, safety), formal requirements capture and traceability, system integration and interface management.

Working knowledge of heavy vehicle charging systems.

Expertise in the integration of renewable energy such as solar, BESS, etc. and dispatchable loads into existing electrical systems.

Familiarity with government and safety regulations, legislation, procedures and standards applicable to complex electrical distribution systems and power generation systems in Ontario.

Working knowledge of project management principles, methods and practices with the ability to carry out and/or coordinate small projects and studies.

Sound judgement, supported by strong analytical and problem-solving skills;

Excellent organizational, research, design and technical writing skills.

Proficiency with computers and typical business software, including MS Office applications.

Successful completion of designated safety training courses, including re-certification as appropriate, by the Training & Development Department
