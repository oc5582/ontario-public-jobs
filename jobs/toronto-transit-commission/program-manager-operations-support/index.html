Career Opportunity

Looking for a new and exciting opportunity where you can make an impact? We are looking for a Program Manager, Operations Support, to join the team!

What You Will Do

In this role you will provide strategic advice and support on a range of projects and initiatives with the goal of improving Transportation Operations. The Program Manager works closely with senior management and project team members on delegated specific tasks, activities and deliverables. This position will ensure program/project management is coordinated and aligned to achieve goals, deliverables and objectives that are consistent with the standards set by the Transportation Group.

Other responsibilities of your role will include:

Leads the centralization, standardization, development, and implementation of Transportation Group programs, projects, policies, and initiatives aligned with departmental, divisional, and corporate objectives.

Manages and oversees the coordination, delivery, and alignment of complex, multi-stakeholder projects and programs, ensuring consistency across project teams and related initiatives.

Builds and maintains effective relationships with internal and external stakeholders, including departments, contractors, industry partners, transit authorities, and legal counsel, to support project and program objectives.

Conducts research, observes transit operations, gathers and analyzes data, and provides recommendations to support decision-making, strategic planning, and continuous improvement initiatives.

Reviews and provides recommendations on procurement activities, including Requests for Proposals (RFPs), Requests for Quotations (RFQs), tenders, and related project documentation.

Develops, communicates, and monitors program and project objectives, deliverables, schedules, budgets, risks, issues, and performance measures, including consolidated program-level reporting.

Facilitates program governance through meetings, project audits, milestone tracking, documentation, status reporting, and coordination with project managers and team leads.

Identifies, assesses, and manages project and program risks, contingencies, and inter-project conflicts to support successful outcomes.

Ensures compliance with organizational policies, standards, procedures, and regulatory requirements across all programs and projects.

Leads the development, monitoring, and reporting of departmental key performance indicators (KPIs), service standards, and improvement strategies to enhance performance, service delivery, and customer experience.

Participation in the TTC Customer Service Ambassador Program.

Demonstrates behaviours that support diversity, inclusion, and a respectful work and service environment that is free from discrimination and harassment. Helps to remove barriers and accommodate employees and customers (within their area of responsibility) in accordance with TTC’s commitments and obligations under the Ontario Human Rights Code (OHRC) and Related Orders, the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA), and TTC’s policies.

Relatives of TTC Employees

The TTC’s policy prohibits relatives of current TTC employees from being hired, assigned, transferred or promoted into positions, where there is a conflict of interest due to a relationship. Should you be selected for an interview, you will be required to disclose the name, relationship and position of any relative who is a current TTC employee.
