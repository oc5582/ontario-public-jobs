Career Opportunity

Are you passionate about infrastructure, problem-solving, and field-based engineering work? Join our Noise and Vibration team to assess track conditions, identify maintenance requirements, and contribute to the safe and reliable operation of one of the region's most essential transit networks.

What You Will Do

The successful candidate will provide technical support for the inspection, maintenance, testing, and improvement of the subway track network, with a specialized focus on noise and vibration mitigation. Responsibilities include conducting field inspections and condition assessments, investigating track-related defects and noise complaints, analyzing asset performance, and supporting maintenance and capital projects. The role also involves monitoring and evaluating rail grinding, milling, and lubrication programs, assessing their effectiveness in reducing noise, vibration, and rail wear, and recommending corrective actions where required. Working closely with engineers, contractors, and operations staff, the incumbent will prepare technical reports, maintain records and databases, and help develop solutions that support the safe, reliable, and efficient operation of TTC track infrastructure.

As a TTC employee you are responsible for treating passengers and/or employees with respect and dignity and ensuring the needs of passengers or employees with disabilities are accommodated and/or addressed (if applicable and within their area of responsibility) in accordance with the Ontario Human Rights Code and Related Orders so that they can fully benefit from the TTC as a service provider and an employer. You will also be participating in the TTC Customer Service Ambassador Program.

You have completed a Community College Diploma in Civil, Mechanical, or Electrical Technology, or a combination of education, training and experience deemed to be equivalent;

Sound knowledge of principles associated with design, analysis and maintenance of railways and/or roadways would be an asset.;
