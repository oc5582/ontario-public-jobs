Career Opportunity

Join our Infrastructure & Engineering team and help drive strategic projects, operational excellence, financial stewardship, safety, and continuous improvement initiatives that support the delivery of critical transit services across the organization.

What You Will Do

You will play a key role in driving operational excellence and continuous improvement initiatives across the Infrastructure Group. This position is responsible for developing, implementing, and managing project plans that support business continuity, operational efficiency, and departmental objectives while ensuring projects are delivered on time and within budget.

The position will lead the development and maintenance of key performance indicators (KPIs) for the Infrastructure Group and support the coordination of departmental work plans to ensure alignment with organizational priorities. The incumbent will oversee updates, process enhancements, and new developments to promote consistency and effectiveness across the department and group.

You will also lead the development, implementation, and evaluation of processes, procedures, and continuous improvement programs, providing strategic direction and support to project teams. They will monitor project goals, timelines, and budgets, evaluate the effectiveness of initiatives and facility improvement programs, and provide independent analysis, recommendations, and guidance to support informed decision-making and corrective actions.

This role is accountable for developing and managing departmental budgets, monitoring expenditures, analyzing financial variances, and ensuring adherence to budget constraints. The successful candidate will assess workforce planning requirements, identify financial impacts and resource constraints, and collaborate with management and external stakeholders to address issues affecting organizational performance. Responsibilities also include preparing and overseeing integrated cost and schedule forecasts, managing contingency drawdowns, and providing expert guidance to senior management on financial and budgetary matters.

The role also supports audit and compliance activities, including audit planning, analysis, corrective action monitoring, implementation, and effectiveness reviews. The incumbent will prepare reports, written analyses, and audit dashboards, communicate findings to stakeholders, and maintain all aspects of program data collection, retention, and documentation to support regulatory and organizational requirements.

As a TTC employee you are responsible for treating passengers and/or employees with respect and dignity and ensuring the needs of passengers or employees with disabilities are accommodated and/or addressed (if applicable and within their area of responsibility) in accordance with the Ontario Human Rights Code and Related Orders so that they can fully benefit from the TTC as a service provider and an employer. You will also be participating in the TTC Customer Service Ambassador Program.

All information and materials you submit, including resume, cover letter, and assessment responses, are your own original work without the use of any AI tool

Relatives of TTC Employees

The TTC’s policy prohibits relatives of current TTC employees from being hired, assigned, transferred or promoted into positions, where there is a conflict of interest due to a relationship. Should you be selected for an interview, you will be required to disclose the name, relationship and position of any relative who is a current TTC employee.
