Career Opportunity

Looking for a new and exciting job opportunity? We are looking for a Shift Supervisor, Operations in our Wheel-Trans Transportation Department!

What You Will Do

In this role, you will provide direct supervision of the Reservations, Dispatch and Service Support areas during Wheel-Trans operating hours. You will manage daily service issues and operational requirements to support efficient and reliable service delivery. The role also includes researching and compiling data for the annual operating and capital budget process, including reviewing service and technological innovations to improve operational efficiency and performance. You will assist the Supervisor in establishing, managing and reporting on goals, objectives and performance standards for the Operations area, ensure training programs and procedural manuals are kept current, and act as a substitute for the Supervisor as required.

Other responsibilities of your role will include:

Supervising and managing the Dispatch, Service Support and Reservations areas of the Section including evaluating employee performance, managing attendance, administering discipline, and investigating and responding to union and customer complaints/grievances

Providing a safe, efficient and reliable service including improving and maintaining vehicle productivity, on-time performance, and telephone call response; and achieving a high level of service quality with cost efficiency

Determining priorities, and initiating instruction, direction and assistance to Dispatchers, Service Support, Reservationists and other staff regarding service requirements/operations, operating policies/procedures, emergency instruction, etc.

Maintaining and compiling data, and preparing reports related to budgeting, vehicle productivity, service adjustments, key performance targets, goals and objectives, etc.

Recommending, developing and updating policies and procedures related to the work of the section

Participating in the TTC customer service Ambassador Program

Responsible for treating passengers and/or employees with respect and dignity and ensuring the needs of passengers and/or employees with disabilities are accommodated and/or addressed (within your area of responsibility) in accordance with the Ontario Human Rights Code and Related Orders so that they can fully benefit from the TTC as a service-provider and an employer.

What Skills Do You Bring?

What Qualifications Do You Bring?

Completion of a post-secondary college diploma or university degree in a related discipline i.e. business administration, human resources) or a combination of education, training and experience deemed to be equivalent.

Must have or rapidly acquire thorough knowledge of Wheel-Trans and TTC Bus Transportation, emergency operating rules and service adjustments

Must have or rapidly acquire strong knowledge of the Collective Agreement, TTC and Wheel-Trans policies and procedures as well as government regulations relative to transit operations

Sound judgement plus well-developed interpersonal, organizational and problem-solving skills necessary to co-ordinate and delegate multiple tasks simultaneously, and perform within a control centre environment involving a unionized workforce

Effective verbal communication skills to concisely convey information; and effective written communication skills for the preparation of various correspondence, reports, etc.

Must have or rapidly become conversant with the operations of the HoD Hastus On Demand computer system, Interactive Voice Response (IVR), two-way TETRA radio system, (ICS) Integrated Communication Software, GOCO Telephony System and other Dispatch/Reservations equipment

Strong knowledge of the street network as well as major points of interest relative to the GTA

Experience working in a supervisory position within a unionized environment; and will be required to attend and successfully complete courses related to effective supervision as well as technical courses related to the work

Must have or rapidly acquire a comprehensive knowledge of the Ontario Human Rights Code and Related Orders including disability accommodation and accessibility requirements pertaining to passengers and employees

The TTC’s policy prohibits relatives of current TTC employees from being hired, assigned, transferred or promoted into positions, where there is a conflict of interest due to a relationship.
