Career Opportunity

Work hands-on with the TTC's critical fare collection and transaction systems, maintaining and troubleshooting the technology that powers millions of customer journeys every day.

What You Will Do

You will perform a variety of duties supporting the operation, maintenance, and reliability of fare dispensing, collection, and transaction equipment throughout the TTC, including but not limited to PRESTO devices, Proof of Payment (POP) equipment, Fare Gates/LDCs, and security devices such as vaults, receivers, and pedestals.

Responsibilities include performing complex inspection, maintenance, overhaul, commissioning, modification, repair, testing, calibration, and troubleshooting work on the electrical, electronic, and mechanical components of the equipment listed above, as well as other Revenue Service Equipment Maintenance (RSEM) equipment as required.

Work assignments will range from routine repairs to comprehensive equipment failure analysis and major component replacements. Duties may include bench work involving the setup and operation of specialized and standard test equipment to diagnose, test, and evaluate the performance of electrical and electronic components and assemblies; removing, testing, troubleshooting, repairing, and reinstalling equipment components; and conducting or verifying the installation, commissioning, and operation of fare collection and related equipment.

You will also be demonstrating behaviours that support diversity, inclusion, and a respectful work and service environment that is free from discrimination and harassment and helps to remove barriers and accommodate employees and customers (within their area of responsibility) in accordance with TTC’s commitments and obligations under the Ontario Human Rights Code (OHRC) and Related Orders, the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA), and TTC’s policies.

What Qualifications Do You Bring?

You have completed Grade 12, (technical program) or its equivalent as determined by the Training Department, including trade subjects in machine shop practice

Must have successfully completed Community College level courses in electrical theory, programmable logic controllers (PLC), and MIG and TIG Welding; or provide proof of equivalence from an accredited academic institution

Must have practical working experience with solid state equipment, performing complex troubleshooting, repairs and testing operations

Must have a valid, non-probationary, class “G” or better Ontario Driver’s License or better

Must have or rapidly acquire a comprehensive knowledge of the Ontario Human Rights Code and Related Orders including disability accommodation and accessibility requirements pertaining to passengers and employees.

Other Requirements:

Must pass tests set by the Revenue Operations Department which correspond in content to the knowledge, theory, and/or practical requirements described above

Must pass the Canadian Police Information Centre (CPIC) Check

Must successfully pass a physical assessment (including the ability to lift and carry bags of supplies and tools while walking and climbing/descending stairs)

Must attend and successfully complete the Training Department’s instruction and testing for the operation of a TTC vehicle

Must successfully complete Defensive Driving, Surface and Subway Rulebook training and recertification as required

Must attend and successfully complete the Loss Prevention Section’s training for robbery prevention and other departmental training related to the work (e.g. procedures, safety & security practices, etc.)

You will need to provide your own set of hand tools as per an established tool list
