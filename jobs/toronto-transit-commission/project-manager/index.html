Career Opportunity

Looking for a new and exciting job opportunity? We are looking for a Project Manager to join our team!

What You Will Do

Lead the delivery of complex, multi-year capital projects from initiation through design, procurement, construction, commissioning, and project close-out.

Act as the TTC's representative, exercising project management authority and ensuring project objectives are achieved.

Manage multiple concurrent capital projects and contracts, with annual project portfolios valued at approximately $10M-$15M.

Define project scope, objectives, budgets, schedules, and deliverables, ensuring alignment with organizational priorities.

Engage and collaborate with internal stakeholders, external agencies, consultants, contractors, and government partners throughout the project lifecycle.

Coordinate and oversee engineering design, tender preparation, contract procurement, and construction management activities.

Develop, monitor, and maintain project budgets, schedules, forecasts, and cost-loaded work plans.

Identify project risks and implement strategies to mitigate impacts to scope, schedule, budget, quality, and safety.

Lead project reviews and provide recommendations on scope, schedule, budget adjustments, and value engineering opportunities.

Prepare and present project approvals, business cases, reports, status updates, and recommendations to senior management and external stakeholders.

Manage consultant and contractor performance, including contract administration, change management, progress payments, and claims resolution.

Review and provide input on legal agreements, contract documents, and commercial terms associated with project delivery.

Coordinate annual updates to the department's multi-year capital program and project forecasts.

Oversee on-site construction activities, ensuring compliance with project requirements, quality standards, safety regulations, and TTC procedures.

Direct project commissioning, turnover, and deficiency resolution to ensure successful project completion and operational readiness.

Foster effective collaboration among multidisciplinary teams to ensure projects are delivered on time, within budget, and to established quality standards.

What Qualifications Do You Bring?

University degree in Engineering, Construction Management, Project Management, or a related discipline, or an equivalent combination of education and experience.

Full membership in Association of Professional Engineers of Ontario (PEO) is an asset.

Project Management Professional (PMP) designation is an asset.

Extensive experience managing heavy construction, infrastructure, transit, or other large multi-disciplinary capital projects.

Familiarity with signaling systems and components.

Experience overseeing project scope, budgets, schedules, contract administration, construction execution, and commissioning activities.

Experience preparing and managing tender documents, consultant assignments, and construction contracts.

Demonstrated ability to lead complex, multi-disciplinary engineering and construction projects from concept through completion.

Proven experience managing large-scale capital projects, multiple stakeholders, consultants, contractors, and competing priorities.

Strong analytical, problem-solving, and decision-making skills with the ability to evaluate options and implement effective solutions.

Excellent leadership, organizational, interpersonal, negotiation, and conflict resolution skills.

Strong verbal and written communication skills, including the ability to prepare and present complex reports, business cases, and project updates to senior leadership and external stakeholders.

Experience negotiating contract changes, resolving claims, and managing project risks and issues.

Proficiency with project management software, scheduling tools, and Microsoft Office applications.

Ability to work independently, exercise sound judgment, and make timely decisions in a fast-paced environment.

Commitment to fostering safe, inclusive, and accessible workplaces and services.

Valid Ontario Class G driver's licence.

The TTC is also committed to a fair hiring process based on job related qualifications, merit, and abilities. You are expected to write your own materials and provide your own qualifications and experiences during the hiring process. The TTC will not tolerate the use of artificial intelligence or external resources in any of your submissions or responses, or the disclosure of interview questions to others. By submitting your application, you acknowledge and agree to the following:

The use of any artificial intelligence (AI) tool including, but not limited to, ChatGPT and Microsoft Copilot, to generate or produce any information or materials to be submitted or any response to any assessment question or any interview question, is prohibited.

All information and materials you submit, including resume, cover letter, and assessment responses, are your own original work without the use of any AI tool

During any interview, your response to any interview question is your own without the use or assistance of any AI tool or any person.

You will not disseminate or disclose to any person any interview question.

Should you breach the prohibition of AI tool use, or information dissemination or disclosure, you will be in violation of TTC’s hiring process and TTC may withdraw your application and may prohibit you from participation in the TTC hiring process.

Relatives of TTC Employees

The TTC’s policy prohibits relatives of current TTC employees from being hired, assigned, transferred or promoted into positions, where there is a conflict of interest due to a relationship. Should you be selected for an interview, you will be required to disclose the name, relationship and position of any relative who is a current TTC employee.
