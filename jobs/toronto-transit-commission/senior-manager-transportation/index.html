POSITION OVERVIEW

The Senior Manager, Transportation (Rail) provides strategic and operational leadership for the safe, reliable, and customer-focused delivery of Subway and Light Rail Transit services across the TTC. Reporting to the Head of Transportation, this role oversees day-to-day divisional operations and drives performance across safety, service reliability, workforce effectiveness, financial stewardship, and continuous improvement.

Working within a complex, unionized environment, the Senior Manager leads service delivery, emergency response, and operational readiness while fostering a culture of accountability and excellence. The incumbent mitigates risk, optimizes resources, and ensures the achievement of key operational and business objectives.

SCOPE & RESPONSIBILITIES

The duties of this role will include but are not limited to the below:

Operational Leadership & Service Delivery

Lead the safe, reliable, and efficient delivery of Subway and LRT services, ensuring operational performance aligns with TTC objectives.

Oversee operational readiness and service delivery across assigned divisions, providing strategic oversight of emergency response, incident management, service recovery, and after-hours operations in support of a 24/7 transit environment.

Drive continuous improvement initiatives through performance analysis, operational planning, and the implementation of best practices to enhance safety, customer experience, and service reliability.

Serve as a key advisor and delegate to the Head of Transportation, providing leadership continuity and operational expertise on complex transportation matters.

People Leadership & Labour Relations

Foster a culture of safety, accountability, employee engagement, and operational excellence.

Build organizational capability through workforce planning, performance management, succession planning, leadership development, coaching, and talent initiatives that support long-term operational excellence.

Develop and maintain productive labour relations, ensuring the consistent application of collective agreements, policies, and people practices while effectively addressing employee relations matters.

Strategic & Financial Management

Oversee operational and financial performance, including resource planning, budget management, and the identification of efficiency and productivity improvements.

Collaborate with internal and external stakeholders to support strategic priorities, special projects, and team objectives.

Champion organizational change and modernization initiatives, fostering a culture of continuous improvement, innovation, and operational excellence.

Analyze operational data, risks, and trends to inform decision-making, support business planning, and drive sustainable service improvements.

KEY SKILLS & EXPERTISE

Extensive leadership experience in transportation, rail transit, or a similarly complex operational environment, with expertise in service delivery, safety, workforce management, and regulatory compliance.

Proven ability to lead large, unionized teams, drive operational excellence, implement strategic initiatives, and manage financial and organizational performance.

Strong change leadership, labour relations, stakeholder management, and talent development capabilities.

Exceptional analytical, communication, and decision-making skills, with a demonstrated ability to influence outcomes and deliver results in a fast-paced environment.

Valid non-probationary Ontario Class G driver's license and access to a personal vehicle.

EDUCATION & PROFESSIONAL CREDENTIALS

Completion of a post-secondary diploma or university degree in a related discipline (i.e. Business Administration, Transportation), combined with extensive progressively responsible management experience in a unionized environment, or a combination of education, training, and experience deemed to be equivalent.
