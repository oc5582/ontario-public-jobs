We are seeking a creative

Bilingual Senior Specialist, Strategic Talent Acquisition Brand

to elevate our employer brand and attract top talent. This role sits at the intersection of recruitment, marketing, and storytelling—focusing on shaping how candidates perceive CBC/Radio Canada and ensuring a compelling and consistent candidate experience. You will be responsible for developing and executing our employer brand strategy. You will be crucial in defining our employee value proposition and connecting with Indigenous, diverse, and emerging talent.

In this role, you will have an opportunity to shape how talent connects with a mission-driven organization and work at the intersection of people, culture and brand. This role is ideal for someone who possesses a deep understanding of what attracts and motivates different talent segments and is committed to inclusive, accessible and authentic representation. If you’re curious, creative and like experimenting, this role is for you!

This is a 12-month temporary position with the possibility of extension and could be based in Ottawa, Toronto or Montreal.

Key Responsibilities:

Employer brand strategy

Develop and execute employer branding strategies to attract high-quality candidates across key talent segments

Define and refine the Employee Value Proposition using data, research and employee insights

Manage and evolve CBC/Radio Canada’s brand guidelines to ensure diversity and reconciliation commitments; consistency in voice, tone, and visual identity across all Employer Branding platforms and materials.

Content and storytelling

Collaborate with internal partners (hiring managers, communications, subject matter experts and recruiting teams) to identify needs and competitive advantages

Partner with Marketing to align messaging with overall brand identity

Develop compelling content such as social media posts, videos, blogs, and employee stories to cultivate community, increase influence, and effectively promote the company's culture and values.

Campaigns and talent attraction

Manage and optimize’s presence on employer review sites (e.g., Glassdoor, LinkedIn).

Design recruitment marketing campaigns, including job ads, email campaigns, and careers page content.

Develop, implement, and manage our TA social media strategy across platforms such as Instagram, LinkedIn, X, Facebook and TikTok

Insights and measurement

Establish Key Performance Indicators and analyze campaign performance and candidate engagement metrics; provide insights and recommendations

Ensure a consistent and positive candidate experience across all touchpoints

Monitor industry trends and competitor employer branding strategies.

What Success Looks Like

Success in this role means building a strong, credible, and differentiated employer brand that strengthens the organization’s ability to attract and engage top talent. This includes:

increasing the qualified candidate pipeline

improving employer brand awareness and perception

driving higher engagement across recruitment marketing channels, and

enhancing candidate experience metrics.

The Senior Specialist, Talent Acquisition Brand leverages data, storytelling, and strategic partnerships to deliver measurable impact and ensure alignment with organizational values, workforce priorities, and talent acquisition objectives.
