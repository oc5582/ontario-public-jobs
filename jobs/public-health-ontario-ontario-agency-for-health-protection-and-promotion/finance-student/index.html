Job description

Finance Student

Location: Toronto-661 University

Department: Accounts Payable

This posting is to fill a current vacancy.

The Role:

To support the department with routine financial and administrative tasks for day‑to‑day Finance operations.

Key Responsibilities -

Assist with routine financial and administrative tasks to support day‑to‑day Finance operations.

Support data entry, validation, and basic analysis in financial systems and spreadsheets to ensure accuracy and completeness of information.

Help prepare and organize financial documentation and supporting schedules for internal review and record‑keeping purposes.

Assist with reconciliations and transaction reviews under supervision, including identifying discrepancies and documenting findings.

Support purchasing card (P‑card) transaction reviews and reconciliations, ensuring appropriate documentation and coding.

Assist with employee travel and expense claim reviews and preparation for posting in the financial system.

Help document processes and procedures, including updating or drafting Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) as needed.

Participate in team meetings and learning opportunities to gain exposure to financial processes, controls, and internal workflows.

Provide general administrative and analytical support to the Finance team, including report formatting, data clean‑up, and tracking activities.

Perform other duties as assigned to support Finance and Planning functions, which may include assisting teams such as Procurement, Facilities, and/or Payroll, depending on capacity and priorities.

Learn to prepare journal entries, complete account reconciliations, investigate balance sheet variances, prepare supporting schedules.

Work on projects e.g. inventory management.

Help on ERM and internal control framework related tasks.

Duration: Student (Fixed Term), 5 month(s)

Hours of Work: Full time, 36.25 hours per week

Compensation Group:Ontario Public Service Employees Union

Compensation Range :$17.60 - $17.75
