Job description

Laboratory Attendant 2

Location: PHO Corporate Office & Toronto Laboratory, 661 University Avenue, Toronto, ON

Department: Microbiology and Laboratory Science, Third Party Funding

This posting is to fill a current vacancy.

The Role:

To provide technical laboratory services to the laboratory science and research team to support research project related activities.

Key Responsibilities-

Logs, sorts, labels all specimens submitted for laboratory testing/examination.

Transports samples to testing laboratory as required.

Handles, prepares and processes medical samples (e.g. culture media and reagents) for identification of micro-organisms, reagents and solutions and testing up to the point of result interpretation (e.g. set up of cultures, staining, extraction, loading analyzers).

Handles, prepares, processes and enters data.

Uses automated equipment or manual methods to prepare and aliquot human samples for testing.

Prepares, loads and unloads laboratory analyzers.

Performs inventory management functions including ordering, unpacking and storing of laboratory goods.

Files, stores, discards laboratory samples.

Cleans and maintains laboratory equipment, cleans and disinfects laboratory work areas.

Maintains laboratory stock supplies.

Assists with set-up of quality control and maintenance of stock cultures.

Answers telephone and resolves problems related to samples (e.g., incomplete information, mismatched tests and information, leaking samples).

Performs other duties or projects as assigned.

Knowledge and Skills-

Knowledge of medical terminology and laboratory safety procedures to provide a range of laboratory services in support of operations.

Knowledge of computer systems, familiarity with laboratory information systems, MS office products such as Word and Excel.

Proficient computer skills necessary to work with automated, computer driven equipment.

Education and Experience-

Certification as a Medical Laboratory Technician from a recognized community college program or relevant training and experience.

Attributes and Competencies-

Oral communication skills to exchange information and clarify test requests or discrepancies with other project team members.

Written communication skills to record information into logs/records.
