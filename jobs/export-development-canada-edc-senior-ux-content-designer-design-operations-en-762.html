Team Overview:

The Experience Design team is part of EDC's Customer Experience Centre of Excellence. Our mission is to help create simpler, more connected, accessible, and effective experiences for Canadian exporters across EDC's digital products, services, and channels.

We bring together expertise in user experience design, content design, accessibility, user research, service design, and design operations to better understand customer needs and translate those insights into meaningful experiences. We work closely with product teams, technology partners, business stakeholders, and customer-facing teams to ensure digital experiences are aligned, evidence-based, and designed around customer outcomes.

As a team, we establish and evolve the standards, patterns, research practices, and design capabilities that enable consistent and scalable experiences across EDC. This includes advancing accessibility and inclusive design, growing our enterprise design system, supporting customer research, and helping teams adopt best practices that improve experience quality and delivery effectiveness.

We believe great experiences are created through collaboration, continuous learning, and a shared commitment to understanding and solving real customer problems. Our goal is not only to improve individual interactions, but to help shape a more connected end-to-end experience across the customer journey.

What you will be doing:

Content design standards, governance & enablement

Support the development, maintenance, and governance of enterprise content design standards, guidelines, patterns, and best practices.

Help establish and evolve content standards that promote consistency, accessibility, clarity, and usability across digital experiences.

Conduct content reviews and provide recommendations to align deliverables with established standards and guidelines.

Partner with UX designers, accessibility specialists, product teams, and stakeholders to support adoption of content design practices.

Support the integration of content standards within design systems, templates, components, and delivery workflows.

Develop and maintain supporting resources, examples, and documentation that enable teams to consistently apply content standards.

Contribute to accessibility and inclusive design practices by incorporating WCAG requirements and plain language principles into content standards.

Monitor emerging content design, accessibility, and digital experience practices and recommend improvements.

User research operations & knowledge management

Coordinate user research activities in collaboration with internal stakeholders and external research partners.

Support user participant recruitment, screening, scheduling, and communications.

Manage research logistics, documentation, consent processes, and operational tracking.

Assist with research synthesis activities, including documenting findings, identifying themes, and preparing insights and recommendations.

Support validation of research findings with project teams and subject matter experts.

Maintain research repositories, knowledge management practices, templates, and operational processes that improve the accessibility and reuse of research insights.

Contribute to the ongoing evolution of research operations practices and standards.

Vendor & stakeholder coordination

Act as a liaison between internal teams and external research vendors and partners.

Coordinate vendor activities, timelines, deliverables, and communications.

Monitor progress and escalate risks, issues, or dependencies as required.

Support procurement, onboarding, invoicing, and administrative processes related to vendor engagements.

Design operations & practice enablement

Support the continuous improvement of design operations processes, governance, and ways of working.

Help maintain team standards, templates, repositories, design documentation, and shared resources.

Support planning, coordination, and tracking of design initiatives and operational activities.

Contribute to the management and evolution of design system documentation, content resources, and enablement materials.

Assist in developing metrics and reporting that measure adoption, effectiveness, and maturity of design practices.

Support onboarding, knowledge-sharing, training, and team enablement initiatives.

Success in this role will be demonstrated through:

Increased adoption and consistent application of content design standards across teams.

Improved quality, accessibility, usability, and consistency of digital content experiences.

Effective coordination and execution of user research activities and vendor engagements.

High-quality synthesis, documentation, and communication of research insights.
