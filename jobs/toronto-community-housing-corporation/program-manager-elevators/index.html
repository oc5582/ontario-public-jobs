About Toronto Community Housing Corporation

Toronto Community Housing Corporation (“TCHC”) is the largest social housing provider in Canada and the second largest in North America. We are wholly owned by the City of Toronto and operate in a non-profit manner. TCHC provides homes to over 41,000 low and moderate-income households in 88 of Toronto's neighbourhoods, with 93,000 tenants from many different backgrounds with a diversity in age, education, language, mental and physical ability, religion, ethnicity and race. We are an essential part of the City of Toronto's fabric and social system.

Our over 2,000 employees work to achieve our vision of quality homes in vibrant communities where people are proud to live and work.

Make a difference

Are you passionate about Elevators and looking for an opportunity to work with an organization that is dedicated to improving the lives of residents from diverse backgrounds? Do you want to use your experiences to influence construction programs in a multi discipline environment? If this sounds like your ideal career, then Toronto Community Housing is the place for you! We are searching for a collaborative and strategic team player who enjoys working in a dynamic and energetic environment. In this role, you will get to work with internal and external partners to manage the effective delivery of 24/7/365 demand services requests, planned maintenance, and preventative maintenance and construction programs in a multi discipline environment in relationship to the Building Compliance Program.

What you’ll do

Establish relationships and partnerships with staff, service providers, stakeholders and clients to ensure effective communication

Forecast resource requirements and recommends a flexible model including in house and market resources to manage the cyclical needs of TCH

Lead and resolve complex contract management and service issues to successful outcomes

Review and approve invoices on a regular basis

Liaise closely with the Vendor Management Team to recommend performance metrics and implement within the assigned programs

Recommend to Manager to plan and manage the program budgets including the recommendations for planning of asset extension/preservation using various analysis and forecasting tools

Work closely with the Facilities Team (Planning/Engineering) to develop and recommend corporate standards through market research and industry standards

Develop and recommend tender/scope strategies and specifications to meet TCH standards and needs

Lead complex maintenance and construction tenders through sound estimating, quoting and bidding practices, including calls for Tenders, RFQ’s & RFP’s and prequalification tenders

Manage service partners by developing service-level, operating-level and partnership agreements

Recommend, develop and implement the Key Performance Indicators as required to effectively monitor and improve operational delivery.

Assist Manager to develop a mentorship program in the designated discipline incorporating TCH’s succession plan. Identify the individual(s) for training and development in future leadership roles

Recommend, develop and implement training programs and performance management plans

Lead, manage and mentor a diverse group of unit supervisors, support and service staff

Contribute to the corporate development of metrics and measurement tools for performance

Provide technical support and information to TCH, external clients, sales account managers, client care centre, consulting firms, engineers and other business units

Collaboratively plan and execute maintenance and construction services for our residents

Manage the internal audit program of all elevator components for all properties

Planning, coordination, implementation, inspection of planned, preventative & emergency maintenance & construction programs

Assess operating systems, provide recommendations to replace, extend life cycle, reduce/mitigate potential damages or reduction in life cycle of building elevator components

Prepare/evaluate/execute service proposals (eg. RFP, quote, contract review etc.) for construction, preventative, and demand service requests for clients on an on-call 24/7 basis

Prepare, review, confirm, recommend on assigned projects; scope of work, contract reviews (including service level agreements).

Establish client communication protocols, financial reporting, progress updates, and scheduling of work activities to meet/exceed contract obligations.

Mitigate risks to client’s assets through establishment of proven service standards. Assist their direct reports and clients in providing business operations performance reports

Participate in JHSC meetings and/or committees as required

Supervise, mentor, train and direct unionized service and administrative staff

Coordinate all aspects of service delivery and staff deployment

Manage staff’s attendance: i.e. vacation requests, payroll input and monitor staffing capacity re: shifts, on call, call-out schedules

Coordinate with Human Resources and respective Unions to deal with grievances, conflict resolution

Health and Safety compliance, workplace accident and injury investigations & reporting

Supervision of multi-disciplined vendors’ pool, including performance evaluations, site inspection, service procurement and delivery strategies from concept to completion

Preparation/review/development of resources

Responsible for the project communication to all parties

Prepare/Review/Conduct progress meetings/minutes and presentations to stakeholders

Prepare and/or review project budgets including projections, tracking, adjusting and reporting costs anomalies, and reconciling costs quarter/year end

Recommend and develop capital replacement programs, purchase of new equipment and maintain inventory documentation

Participate in interactions with developers, architects, engineers and building owners to provide sound knowledge to ensure compliance with the provisions of applicable acts and regulations in order to meet adequate operation of elevator systems in new and existing buildings
