About Toronto Community Housing Corporation

Toronto Community Housing Corporation (“TCHC”) is the largest social housing provider in Canada and the second largest in North America. We are wholly owned by the City of Toronto and operate in a non-profit manner. TCHC provides homes to over 41,000 low and moderate-income households in 88 of Toronto's neighbourhoods, with 93,000 tenants from many different backgrounds with a diversity in age, education, language, mental and physical ability, religion, ethnicity and race. We are an essential part of the City of Toronto's fabric and social system.

Our over 2,000 employees work to achieve our vision of quality homes in vibrant communities where people are proud to live and work.

Make a difference

The Employee and Labour Relations Consultant provides a range of coaching, strategic advice and administrative support functions and participates in the provision of a wide range of services in the areas of labour and employee relations, interpretation of various collective agreements, employment and labour legislation, Toronto Community Housing policies and procedures to an assigned client portfolio(s).

What you’ll do

Provides advice/assistance/guidance to managers in an assigned client portfolio(s):

Provides a range of coaching, consulting and strategic advice to clients in the areas of human rights, labour and employee relations, interpretation of collective agreements, employment and labour legislation, policies and procedures

Provides advice/assistance/guidance to managers and consultants in all phases of re-organization

Works closely with people and culture personnel to communicate pertinent information and documents

Responsible for the timely and effective delivery of people and culture services including but not limited to consultation and advice in all disciplinary and non-disciplinary matters, investigations, complaints, collective agreement interpretation and workplace accommodations, etc. to clients to ensure business objectives are met

Provides guidance to people leaders and assist with formal accommodation plans for workplace accommodations

Assists in developing, organizing and leading various labour relations, collective agreement interpretation, and workforce planning workshops, guidelines, policies and procedures

Works closely with the various bargaining unions to sustain positive employee and labour relations:

Provides assistance in the grievance process including but not limited to grievance tracking, scheduling, investigating, attend and lead  Step 2 and 3 grievance hearings, preparation of written responses and settlement agreements

Responsible for gathering documentation in preparation for and or attending mediations and arbitration hearings

Participates as a team member on negotiation teams when negotiating collective agreements with unions

Interprets and administers multiple collective agreements, assists in the preparation and representation for grievance hearings and conflict resolution and the promotion of a positive labour relations environment

Participates in labour management meetings and other meetings with union representative on a variety of labour relations matters

Provides work direction and leadership within the labour relations unit

Mentors, coaches and provides work direction to Employee and Labour Relations Associates and HR Assistants and provides leadership and guidance to resolve complex People and Culture issues and ensures quality in service delivery.

Assists with recruitment related activities for an assigned client portfolio:

Assists with recruitment activities for an assigned client portfolio including but not limited to ensuring seniority calculations are performed in accordance with the collective agreement, participating and providing input at various stages in the recruitment process to ensure compliance with the collective agreement.

Develops employment equity hiring strategies for client groups.

Participates, implements and supports key initiatives as outlined in the labour relations strategy for heavily unionized portfolios

What you’ll need

Post-secondary education in Human Resources, labour relations, public administration or other relevant field

5 (five) years of progressive experience in Human Resources and Labour Relations in a unionized setting, preferably in the broader public sector or social service sector

Demonstrated knowledge of human resource and labour relations practices, contractual obligations, collective agreements and labour and employment legislation, i.e. Employment Standards Act, Labour Relations Act, Human Rights Code, etc.

Proficient in the use of Microsoft Word/Excel

Experience in a unionized setting is required; preferably in the broader public sector or social service sector.

Experience providing advice, assistance, and guidance to managers and consultants in all phases of re-organization.

Knowledge of human resource practices, contractual obligations, collective agreements and employment legislation, i.e. Employment Standards Act, Labour Relations Act, Human Rights Code, etc.

Experience developing and preparing correspondence and reports.

Experience using a variety of software products including Microsoft Word and Excel.

Strong customer service, interpersonal, negotiation and communication skills.

Well-developed analytical, problem solving and organizational skills.

Ability to work under pressure, multi-task and meet deadlines.

Demonstrated ability to work independently, take initiative and follow through on work assignments.

Ability to work in a respectful manner with a diverse community of clients, co-workers and external consultants.

Ability to work effectively within a team environment with strong relationship building skills to maintain productive working relationships with managers, employees, union officials and the general public.
