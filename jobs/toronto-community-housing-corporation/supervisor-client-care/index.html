About Toronto Community Housing Corporation

Toronto Community Housing Corporation (“TCHC”) is the largest social housing provider in Canada and the second largest in North America. We are wholly owned by the City of Toronto and operate in a non-profit manner. TCHC provides homes to over 41,000 low and moderate-income households in 88 of Toronto's neighbourhoods, with 93,000 tenants from many different backgrounds with a diversity in age, education, language, mental and physical ability, religion, ethnicity and race. We are an essential part of the City of Toronto's fabric and social system.

Our over 2,000 employees work to achieve our vision of quality homes in vibrant communities where people are proud to live and work.

Make a difference

Are you passionate about customer service and looking for an opportunity to work with an organization that is dedicated to improving the lives of residents from diverse backgrounds? Do you want to use your leadership experiences to influence community growth? If this sounds like your ideal career, then Toronto Community Housing is the place for you!

We are searching for a collaborative and strategic leader who enjoys working in a dynamic and energetic environment. In this role, you will get to actively engage frontline Maintenance Dispatch & Alarm Monitoring staff within the Client Care Centre’s 24/7/365 operation. You will be responsible for monitoring, managing, building reports and developing the performance of the team to identified objectives and performance standards in a manner that supports TCHC's tenant-focused culture.

What you’ll do

Provide day to day Supervision and direction to a team of staff, ensuring the delivery of effective and quality service and achievement of goals and targets

Provide staff training, lead performance reviews, create work schedules, monitor workload and ensure staff adherence to policies, procedures and standards

Motivate staff to surpass performance expectations

Approve bi-weekly payroll

Facilitate team communication that demonstrates correspondence that is consistent, clear and customer focused

Build a highly effective team of staff by supervising, leading, motivating, coaching, identifying needs and facilitating participation of staff in various training sessions

Ensure service level standards are maintained through appropriate staff scheduling, taking appropriate measures to ensure overtime occurrences/situations and cost/budget are accounted for during unpredictable high call volume periods

Collect, develop and prepare information and data analysis reports to meet the information and data analysis needs of the division, balancing ongoing reporting requirements with special projects.

Ensure the integrity, quality and accuracy of reports produced

Identify data gaps and emerging information needs to support business performance and progress on strategies

Work with departmental stakeholders, vendors and partners to resolve issues and identify on-going process improvement opportunities

Liaise with the Manager to recommend on-going improvement to the escalations process

Respond to site and tenant complaints, questions and inquiries and to emergency situations

Lend “real time” support to escalations, questions and troubleshooting

Monitor and maintain call quality through monthly sampling and assessment

Coach and train staff on how to handle different sensitive situations

Monitor and report performance indicators and identify those that are below the pre-determined standard and recommend solutions and approaches for improvement

Manage labour relations matters including hiring, performance management, the handling of grievances and the imposition of discipline, as necessary

Contribute to the planning and development of new initiatives within the Client Care and Maintenance Dispatch department

What you’ll need

Minimum 2-5 years of management experience in a Call Centre environment, managing and mentoring staff

Post-secondary diploma/degree in business administration, social services or related areas, or equivalent work experience

Experience as a team leader or project team lead with a proven ability to supervise and motivate a multi-disciplinary team

Flexibility to work occasional weekends

Experience managing in a unionized setting preferred

Highly proficient with MS Office, MS Outlook, databases and Call Centre software (Such as CIM Agent 10, Yardi Systems including HoMES and CRM)

Strong organizational and time management skills

Ability to effectively communicate both orally and in writing to all levels both within and external to the organization

Commitment to providing high-quality customer service to our residents

Ability to communicate effectively with a diverse tenant population in difficult situations and resolve sensitive issues

A background in building maintenance is an asset
