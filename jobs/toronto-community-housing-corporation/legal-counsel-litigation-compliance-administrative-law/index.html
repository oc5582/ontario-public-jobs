About Toronto Community Housing Corporation

Toronto Community Housing Corporation (“TCHC”) is the largest social housing provider in Canada and the second largest in North America. We are wholly owned by the City of Toronto and operate in a non-profit manner. TCHC provides homes to over 41,000 low and moderate-income households in 88 of Toronto's neighbourhoods, with 93,000 tenants from many different backgrounds with a diversity in age, education, language, mental and physical ability, religion, ethnicity and race. We are an essential part of the City of Toronto's fabric and social system.

Our over 2,000 employees work to achieve our vision of quality homes in vibrant communities where people are proud to live and work.

Make a difference

Are you a litigator who wants sophisticated, challenging work without leaving public purpose at the door? Join our in-house legal team where your advice can make a real difference in the lives of Torontonians.

Reporting to Senior Legal Counsel - Litigation you will deliver high quality, representation in quasi-litigation matters and litigation proceedings before all levels of courts, and administrative tribunals. You will Provide proactive legal advice in various areas of law to mitigate legal risks for the corporation and ensure legislative and regulatory compliance. You will be part of a team that manages complaints received through Toronto Community Housing’s (TCHC’s) Tenant Human Rights Complaint process. You will support compliance activities of the organization.

What you will do

Represent TCHC and its subsidiaries in litigation proceedings before all levels of tribunals and courts:

Conduct all steps in a litigation proceeding including drafting originating processes and defenses/responses, conducting examinations for discovery and other disclosure processes, negotiating settlements with opposing counsel, attending mediation and settlement conferences, representing TCHC at motions and application hearings, pre-trial and trial; conducting hearings, trials and appeals;

Interview and prepare witnesses as part of litigation/hearing proceedings;

Draft correspondence and legal documents including internal and external correspondence and memoranda, examination for discovery reports, pleadings, affidavits, motion/application materials, mediation, pre-trial and trial briefs and appellate pleadings.

Working as part of a team that manages TCHC’s Tenant Human Rights Complaint process:

Receive and review complaints submitted by TCHC tenants alleging a breach of TCHC’s human rights obligations and identify complaints that do and do not raise issues of

Human Rights Code

compliance;

Investigate complaints identified as raising issues of

Human Rights Code

compliance and direct complaints that do not raise such issues to others as appropriate;

Make determinations as to whether complaints alleging failure to comply with the

Human Rights Code

are supported and provide legal advice regarding TCHC’s obligations in relation to those matters;

Communicate with TCHC staff and tenants in relation to investigations;

Provide legal advice generally related to TCHC’s obligations to its tenants pursuant to the

Human Rights Code

.

Legal research and proactive legal advice and opinions to business units and executives:

Provide strategic legal and business advice, draft legal opinions and memoranda on a wide variety of issues affecting the corporation such as: human rights, TCHC’s legislative and regulatory compliance obligations, administrative law, TCHC’s corporate governance compliance;

Conduct legal research;

Attend meetings and provide proactive legal advice and recommendations to TCHC business division heads and other internal clients to mitigate litigation and business risks;

Draft corporate policies and processes;

Participate on policy review committees to provide strategic legal advice with respect to and/or drafts various corporate policies (in the past, this has included: Tenant Human Rights Complaint Policy, Human Rights Policy, Tenant Transfer Policy, E-Bike Policy, etc.).

Provide work direction to administrative staff and mentor Paralegals, Business Planner – Human Rights:

Provide work direction to Administrative Assistant – Litigation and Litigation Law Clerk;

Provide mentorship on various files and legal questions to Paralegals and Business Planner- Human Rights.

License to Practice law in Ontario (L1 license and membership in good standing with the Law Society of Ontario);
