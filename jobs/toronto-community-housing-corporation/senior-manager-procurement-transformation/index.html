About Toronto Community Housing Corporation

Toronto Community Housing Corporation (“TCHC”) is the largest social housing provider in Canada and the second largest in North America. We are wholly owned by the City of Toronto and operate in a non-profit manner. TCHC provides homes to over 41,000 low and moderate-income households in 88 of Toronto's neighbourhoods, with 93,000 tenants from many different backgrounds with a diversity in age, education, language, mental and physical ability, religion, ethnicity and race. We are an essential part of the City of Toronto's fabric and social system.

Our over 2,000 employees work to achieve our vision of quality homes in vibrant communities where people are proud to live and work.

Make a difference

Canada's largest social housing provider is looking for a procurement professional and dynamic leader to join the team with the impactful goal to help bring the department through a transformational journey and continuous improvements.

The Senior Manager, Procurement Transformation at TCH will report directly to the Senior Director, Procurement, with dotted line to the CFO’s office. This role will lead the transformation of procurement processes and enhance vendor risk management across the organization. This role aims to drive procurement efficiency, ensure regulatory compliance, and optimize strategic sourcing. You will oversee the deployment of advanced procurement tools and processes, ensuring they align with TCHC’s financial and operational goals.

What you’ll do

Procurement Transformation Leadership:

Lead and manage cross-functional projects related to vendor risk management and procurement transformation.

Develop and implement procurement processes and tools to enhance contract management and procurement efficiency.

Create and manage the annual roadmap for procurement initiatives, prioritizing key features for development.

Lead the deployment of procurement processes and tools across the organization.

Manage the procurement transformation budget and ensure alignment with TCHC's financial objectives.

Stakeholder Management and Advisory:

Collaborate with various stakeholders to gather requirements and provide tailored procurement solutions.

Manage communication with TCHC stakeholders, ensuring alignment with procurement strategies.

Provide strategic advice to senior management on procurement transformation and vendor risk management.

Address ad hoc inquiries from stakeholders, offering insights and recommending strategic actions.

Process and Tool Development:

Formalize procurement guidelines in collaboration with internal teams and external stakeholders.

Ensure the effective implementation of procurement processes and tools across TCHC.

Support the structuring and improvement of procurement data models and data quality initiatives.

Data Management and Analysis:

Improve procurement data models and participate in initiatives to enhance data quality.

Conduct thorough analysis of procurement data to provide insights and support strategic planning.

Develop performance indicators and metrics to monitor and evaluate procurement effectiveness.

Compliance and Risk Management:

Ensure all procurement activities comply with regulatory requirements and TCHC policies.

Develop and implement frameworks for vendor risk management and procurement compliance.

Drive adherence to procurement and outsourcing policies, emphasizing financial sustainability and alignment with TCHC’s strategic objectives.

What you’ll need

Postgraduate degree in Finance, Business Administration, or a related field.

Project Management Professional (PMP) certification required.

Six Sigma Belt Certification preferred.

Minimum 10-15 years of experience in procurement transformation, strategic sourcing, or related roles.

Experience in public sector or community housing procurement is preferred.

Proven track record of leading large-scale procurement projects.

Expertise in procurement practices and tools (e.g., Coupa, Salesforce, SAP).

Strong analytical skills and understanding of procurement data models.

Proficient in project management methodologies (Agile, Waterfall).

Proactive, autonomous, and highly organized.
