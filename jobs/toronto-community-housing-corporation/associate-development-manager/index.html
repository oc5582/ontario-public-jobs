About Toronto Community Housing Corporation

Toronto Community Housing Corporation (“TCHC”) is the largest social housing provider in Canada and the second largest in North America. We are wholly owned by the City of Toronto and operate in a non-profit manner. TCHC provides homes to over 41,000 low and moderate-income households in 88 of Toronto's neighbourhoods, with 93,000 tenants from many different backgrounds with a diversity in age, education, language, mental and physical ability, religion, ethnicity and race. We are an essential part of the City of Toronto's fabric and social system.

Our over 2,000 employees work to achieve our vision of quality homes in vibrant communities where people are proud to live and work.

Make a Difference

Are you a development professional and looking for an opportunity to work with an organization that plays an integral part in transforming Toronto? Do you have experience in land use planning and/or experience related to affordable rental and market housing development? If so, we have an opportunity for you!

Under the direction of a Senior Development Manager, the Associate Development Manager is responsible for assisting with planning and organizing various initiatives undertaken by the Development Division in a manner that supports TCHC’s tenant focused culture.

TCHC’s award-winning development initiatives include full-scale urban revitalizations, the development of mixed-use, mixed-income communities and the creation of infill high-rise, mid-rise and low-rise multi-family residential buildings. Community revitalizations are developed in partnership with private-market developers and involve working closely with our tenants and other community partners and stakeholders. Be part of building a better Toronto for all!

What You'll Do

Assist in tracking, monitoring and analyzing Development Division project budgets

Assist in preparing project cost proposals and project business plans (cash flow requirements, equity investments requirements, program funding requirements, operating pro-forma, etc.)

Assist in the creation and monitoring of project schedules

Assist in the contract administration of development partner agreements

Develop and managing proposal calls

Coordinate consultant teams

Prepare documentation and monitoring requirements to secure funding for affordable housing construction and ownership

Assist in the development of partnerships with local businesses, developers, senior levels of government, business associations and related agencies

Coordinate with other TCHC Divisions to support revitalization initiatives

Assist with identifying new revitalization sites, acquisition and property disposals to maximize value to the corporation and achieve corporate objectives

Support the relocation of TCHC residents during the revitalization process

Support local community agencies in the revitalization process

Review consultant reports prior to submittal to senior management/City staff

Report on feasibility studies in support of revitalization activities

Plan and execute public and community-based meetings, workshops, focus groups, etc.

Collaborate with revitalization stakeholder groups and participate in one-on-one meetings with stakeholders on TCHC’s behalf

Design and lead project tours for stakeholders
