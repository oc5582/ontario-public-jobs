The Administrator, Public Affairs and Marketing provides administrative and operational support to a department responsible for communications, marketing, public affairs, digital engagement, community relations, Indigenous Relations, and sustainability initiatives. The role coordinates schedules, meetings, events, employee engagement programs, and departmental processes while supporting the delivery of projects and activities. Acting as a key point of contact for internal and external partners, the role contributes to the efficient operation of the department through strong organization, collaboration, planning and customer service.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:

Provides comprehensive administrative support to leaders and team members within Public Affairs and Marketing, including calendar management, meeting coordination, scheduling, employee engagement planning, and preparation of presentations and materials

Acts as a key point of contact for internal and external inquiries, coordinating responses and facilitating communication across stakeholders

Coordinates team meetings, workshops, stakeholder sessions, community events, and departmental activities, including logistics, materials, registrations, and follow-up actions

Supports communications, marketing, customer engagement, public affairs, and community relations initiatives through project coordination, document preparation, and administrative support

Coordinates onboarding and offboarding activities, including access requests, workstation setup, technology provisioning, and related administrative requirements in partnership with IT, Facilities, and other internal support teams

Maintains departmental records, databases, invoices, purchase orders, digital assets, and document management systems in accordance with organizational standards

Assists with the preparation, formatting, distribution, and tracking of reports, presentations, communications materials, newsletters, and other departmental documents

REQUIREMENTS:

Post-secondary education in Business Administration, Office Administration, Communications or a related field

Two (2) to three (3) years of administrative experience providing support to multiple leaders within a corporate environment

Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Office Suite (Excel, Word, PowerPoint, Outlook)

Experience coordinating meetings, calendars, events, onboarding activities, and stakeholder communications

Demonstrated experience using social media platforms in a corporate setting

Experience coordinating projects and familiarity with project management tools such as Asana or Microsoft Planner

Experience creating engaging content for social media channels, such as photography or simple videos

Strong organizational and time management skills with the ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines

Ability to assess requests, prioritize tasks, and organize work effectively to ensure timely completion

Excellent verbal and written communication skills

Strong political acumen, with the ability to exercise discretion and maintain confidentiality when handling sensitive information

Highly responsible and proactive, with a customer-focused mindset and a proven ability to build strong, collaborative relationships with stakeholders

Demonstrated self-motivation, creativity, resourcefulness, and the ability to solve problems independently
