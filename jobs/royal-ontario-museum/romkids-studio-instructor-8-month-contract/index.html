Job description

WHO WE ARE

Opened in 1914 and welcoming over 1.3 million visitors annually, the Royal Ontario Museum (ROM) is Canada's most-visited museum, ranking among the top 10 cultural institutions in North America. At ROM, we are dedicated to building and sharing global collections, sharing knowledge, inspiring learning, and fostering community engagement. As a globally recognized field research institute, ROM houses an impressive collection of over 18 million artworks, cultural objects, and natural history specimens, showcased in over 40 galleries and exhibition spaces. We are committed to creating a space where art, culture, and nature intersect, connecting people and communities to the past, present and shared future.

As we continue to work toward our goals to transform and evolve as one of the world’s foremost 21st-century museums, we seek passionate and dynamic team members who embody our mission and values.

WHO WE NEED

ROM is seeking engaging, responsible, and creative educators to join our ROMKids team as Studio Instructors. In this role, you will deliver high-quality, hands-on learning experiences for children through our camp and club programming, bringing art, culture, and nature to life in dynamic and meaningful ways.

As a Studio Instructor, you will play a key role in facilitating inclusive, inquiry-based programming that reflects ROM’s commitment to experiential learning and accessibility. You will lead groups of participants in structured activities, support positive group dynamics, and ensure a safe, welcoming, and engaging environment for all learners.

This is an ideal opportunity for individuals with a passion for education, child development, and creative programming who thrive in interactive, fast-paced environments and are committed to delivering exceptional learning experiences.

HOW YOU WILL MAKE AN IMPACT

Deliver engaging, hands-on programming for children that brings ROM’s collections, research, and themes to life through creative, inquiry-based learning experiences

Plan and facilitate structured lessons and activities that align with program objectives, incorporating inclusive and accessible teaching practices

Foster a positive, safe, and supportive environment by effectively supervising participants, managing group dynamics, and addressing behavioural needs with sound judgment

Adapt programming to meet diverse learning styles and needs, ensuring all participants feel included, supported, and engaged

Integrate experiential learning approaches, including the use of museum objects, specimens, and creative materials, to enhance participant engagement and learning outcomes

Ensure adherence to program schedules, safety protocols, and supervision standards, maintaining high-quality program delivery at all times

Collaborate with team members, assistants, and volunteers to support smooth program execution and a consistent participant experience

Communicate effectively with caregivers and escalate any concerns as appropriate to support participant well-being

WHAT YOU BRING

Post-secondary education or equivalent experience in education, museum studies, art, culture, history, science, or a related field

Minimum of three (3) years’ experience working with children in educational, camp, or program-based environments

Demonstrated ability to deliver engaging, hands-on programming and facilitate learning in group settings

Strong classroom and behaviour management skills, with the ability to create a safe, inclusive, and supportive environment

Excellent communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to build positive relationships with children, caregivers, and team members

Sound judgment and conflict resolution skills, particularly in dynamic or high-energy environments

Experience adapting programming to meet diverse learning styles and accessibility needs

First Aid and CPR certification (or willingness to obtain) is considered an asset

Formal teaching qualifications (e.g., OCT, ECE, B.Ed.) and/or experience in creative disciplines such as visual arts, storytelling, environmental education, or media arts are considered assets
