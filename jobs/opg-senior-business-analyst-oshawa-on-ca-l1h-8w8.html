Ontario Power Generation (OPG) is looking for a dynamic, strategic and results-driven professional to join our team in the role of Senior Business Analyst . Job Overview Reporting to the Senior Manager, IT Services, this position is responsible for independently identifies and documents business requirements, develops workflow models, and contributes to the implementation and testing of business solutions. It bridges the gap between stakeholders and delivery teams by translating operational needs into functional outcomes that support business transformation. Key Accountabilities Requirements Elicitation & Analysis: Collaborate with stakeholders to gather, analyze, document, and validate business requirements, user stories, and acceptance criteria. Business Process Modeling: Develop and maintain business process maps, workflow models, and process documentation using methodologies such as BPMN and UML. Stakeholder Engagement & Facilitation: Lead workshops, interviews, and working sessions to define requirements, validate business rules, and achieve stakeholder alignment. Cross-Functional Collaboration, Reporting & Risk Management: Partner with IT, Operations, PMO, and business teams to support delivery, monitor solution performance, develop reporting metrics, and assess business impacts and risks. Qualifications Completion of a four-year university degree in a STEM discipline (Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics) or a related field. Minimum of 6 years of progressive experience performing business analysis in complex organizational environments. Demonstrated experience gathering, documenting, validating, and managing business and functional requirements. Proven ability to analyze, redesign, and optimize business processes to improve efficiency and effectiveness. The successful candidate will exhibit uncompromising integrity and commitment to upholding corporate values, and the OPG Code of Business Conduct. OPG is committed to fostering an inclusive, equitable, and accessible environment. If you require accommodation during the selection process, please contact AODA@opg.com . What makes a career at OPG different? With operations across Ontario, OPG is one of the most diverse power producers in North America. As the largest generator in Ontario, we meet approximately 50% of the province’s electricity needs, largely from low-carbon sources like nuclear and hydro. As we work to achieve our vision of Electrifying life in one generation, OPG and our family of companies are also helping advance the development of new low-carbon technologies such as Small Modular Reactors (SMRs), refurbishment projects, and electrification initiatives to help power the growing demands of a growing economy. Join OPG and make history. The Darlington New Nuclear Project is leading the way in the advancement of Small Modular Reactor (SMR) technology in Canada – the future of nuclear power generation! Using new and innovative technologies such as small modular reactors, hydrogen and battery storage, while expanding our hydroelectric and nuclear portfolios, we are changing the electricity landscape in Ontario.
