Help shape the future of new home construction

Are you passionate about Ontario's new home building industry and eager to make a meaningful impact? Join the Home Construction Regulatory Authority's (HCRA)

Industry Advisory Council (IAC)

.

Your Role and Impact

The IAC provides independent, non-partisan advice to the HCRA on emerging issues in the new home construction sector. As a member, you'll help support continuous improvement in the homebuilding industry and strengthen confidence in Ontario's new home marketplace.

Why Join?

Make a difference:

Help identify and provide insight on emerging issues and challenges affecting Ontario's new home construction sector.

Strengthen the homebuilding industry:

Offer advice to enhance industry awareness and engagement, and contribute ideas to improve service delivery, policies and procedures.

Connect and collaborate:

Share industry perspectives while collaborating with peers and professionals from across Ontario.

What We Ask of Members

A two-year term, with the option of reappointment.

Attendance at up to six meetings per year, held virtually on Microsoft Teams.

$250 for each meeting attended.

Keeping council discussions and materials confidential.

Who We're Looking For

We're seeking 3 candidates with experience in any of the following areas:

Small, medium, large, or high-rise building operations

Custom home building

Supervising day-to-day construction

Overseeing customer service for licensed builders

Members are expected to demonstrate integrity and professionalism, and to have a strong interest in supporting the HCRA's consumer protection mandate.

We welcome applicants who reflect the diversity of Ontario's communities, regions, backgrounds, and professional experiences.

This posting is for existing vacancies on the Industry Advisory Council.

To learn more, please see the

IAC Terms of Reference

.

How to Apply

Please submit a cover letter and resume through our application portal by Friday, October 30, 2026. In your cover letter, please highlight the criteria from the Terms of Reference that match your background, skills, and experience.

For questions, please contact

stakeholder.relations@hcraontario.ca

. Applications sent by email will not be considered.

Accommodation, if required, will be provided throughout the hiring process in accordance with the Ontario Human Rights Code.

What's Next

Shortlisted applicants will be invited to a virtual interview.

Your application will always be reviewed by a person. AI is not used to screen, source, or select applicants at any stage of our recruitment process, but may be used in a limited supporting way.
