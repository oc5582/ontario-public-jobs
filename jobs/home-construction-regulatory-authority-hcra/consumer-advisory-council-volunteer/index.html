Be a voice for consumer protection in the new home building industry

Are you passionate about consumer protection and interested in making a difference in the new home building industry? Join the Home Construction Regulatory Authority's (HCRA)

Consumer Advisory Council (CAC)

.

Your Role and Impact

The CAC provides independent, non-partisan advice to the HCRA on issues that matter to new home buyers. As a member, you'll help shape consumer-focused approaches to awareness, engagement and service delivery, contributing to a more informed and confident marketplace where buyers are protected and empowered.

Why Join?

Make a difference:

Help identify and provide insight on emerging issues and challenges affecting new home buyers in Ontario.

Strengthen consumer protection:

Offer advice to enhance consumer awareness and engagement, and contribute ideas to improve service delivery, policies and procedures.

Share your perspective:

Bring your experience and collaborate with peers and advocates dedicated to strengthening consumer protection.

What We Ask of Members

A two-year term, with the option of reappointment.

Attendance at up to six meetings per year, held virtually on Microsoft Teams.

$250 for each meeting attended.

Keeping council discussions and materials confidential.

Who We're Looking For

We're seeking 5 candidates with experience in any of the following areas:

New home buyer (freehold or condominium)

Member of a condominium board

Real estate agent

Chief building official or home inspector (retired or active)

Consumer advocate

Experience working in a regulatory environment

Members are expected to demonstrate integrity and professionalism, and to have a strong interest in supporting the HCRA's consumer protection mandate.

We welcome applicants who reflect the diversity of Ontario's communities, regions, backgrounds, and professional experiences.

This posting is for existing vacancies on the Consumer Advisory Council.

CAC Terms of Reference

How to Apply

Please submit a cover letter and resume through our application portal by Friday, October 30, 2026. In your cover letter, please highlight the criteria from the Terms of Reference that match your background, skills, and experience.

stakeholder.relations@hcraontario.ca
