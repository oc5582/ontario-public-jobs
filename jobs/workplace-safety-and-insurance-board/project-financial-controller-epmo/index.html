About the Workplace Safety and Insurance Board (WSIB)

We’re here to help. When an injury or illness happens on the job, we move quickly to provide wage-loss benefits, medical coverage and support to help people get back to work. Funded by businesses, we also provide no-fault collective liability insurance and access to industry-specific health and safety information. We are one of the largest insurance organizations in North America covering over five million people in more than 300,000 workplaces across Ontario. For more information, visit

wsib.ca

.

You’ll also receive a competitive salary and may be eligible to participate in our health and dental plan.

Help shape the financial success of enterprise initiatives that make a difference.

At WSIB, our work goes beyond numbers. Every day, we help people recover, return to life, and thrive after a workplace injury or illness. Behind that mission is a team of professionals who provide the financial insight, governance, and accountability needed to deliver meaningful change across the organization.

We're looking for a Project Financial Controller to join our Corporate Project Finance team. This is an exciting opportunity for a collaborative and analytically minded finance professional who thrives at the intersection of financial management, project delivery, and strategic decision-making.

In this role, you'll partner with project teams, portfolio leaders, and senior executives to provide financial support across WSIB's enterprise project portfolio. You'll help ensure projects are financially sound, risks are understood, and strategic investments deliver value.

Why This Opportunity?

This is more than a financial reporting role. You'll have the opportunity to:

Influence financial decisions on enterprise-wide projects and strategic initiatives.

Partner with leaders across the organization to drive responsible investment decisions.

Strengthen financial governance and controls that support successful project delivery.

Transform complex financial data into insights that inform executive decision-making.

Contribute to projects and programs that shape the future of WSIB.

If you're energized by combining financial expertise, strategic thinking, and business partnership, this role offers an opportunity to make a meaningful impact.

About the Role

Reporting to the Manager, Corporate Project Finance, the Project Financial Controller is responsible for portfolio financial reporting, budgeting, forecasting, analysis, and financial governance across enterprise projects and programs.

You will provide trusted financial advice to project teams and leadership, helping ensure sound financial management, effective controls, compliance with accounting standards, and informed decision-making across the project portfolio.

What You'll Do

Financial Reporting & Analysis

Lead portfolio budgeting, forecasting, expenditure tracking, and financial reporting activities.

Prepare monthly, quarterly, and annual financial reports for leadership and executive stakeholders.

Analyze project, program, and portfolio financial performance and provide actionable recommendations.

Develop dashboards, reporting tools, and financial models that support strategic decision-making.

Research and assess complex accounting and financial issues, identifying risks and opportunities.

Monitor the financial health of the project portfolio and communicate key insights to stakeholders.

Ensure project financial objectives align with WSIB's strategic priorities and enterprise goals.

Financial Governance & Controllership

Develop and maintain financial controls, processes, and reporting frameworks that support effective project delivery.

Monitor project expenditures, forecasts, resource utilization, and budget performance.

Validate financial forecasts through analysis of actual costs, progress achieved, and future projections.

Support the management of capital and operating expenditures across the portfolio.

Ensure compliance with accounting standards, financial policies, and governance requirements.

Contribute to the ongoing enhancement of portfolio financial management practices, tools, and reporting methodologies.

Strategic Partnership & Advisory

Act as a trusted financial advisor to project managers, portfolio leaders, and business partners.

Translate complex financial information into meaningful business insights.

Build strong relationships across the organization to support successful project outcomes.

Influence decision-making through evidence-based financial analysis and recommendations.

What You'll Bring

Required Qualifications

Post-secondary education in Accounting, Finance, Business Administration, or a related discipline.

3-5 years of experience in financial analysis, budgeting, forecasting, and financial reporting within a large, complex organization.

2-3 years of experience supporting projects, programs, or portfolio financial management.

Preferred Qualifications

Undergraduate degree in Accounting, Finance, Business Administration, or a related field.

CPA designation or progress toward designation.

PMP certification or project management training.

What Will Help You Stand Out

We're especially interested in candidates who bring experience with:

Oracle Fusion ERP

Financial reporting, data extraction, and transformation using Oracle Transactional Business Intelligence (OTBI)

Advanced Excel capabilities, including Power Query

SQL for data extraction, analysis, and reporting

Building and maintaining Power BI dashboards and reports

Applying Earned Value Management (EVM) methodologies to monitor project performance, measure progress, and support financial decision-making.

Who You Are

You're a curious and proactive finance professional who enjoys turning data into meaningful insights. You bring a strong foundation in financial analysis and controllership, paired with the ability to build trusted relationships and influence decisions.

You ask thoughtful questions, challenge assumptions respectfully, and provide practical solutions that balance financial discipline with business needs. Most importantly, you're energized by the opportunity to contribute to projects that create real organizational impact.

Disclosing conflicts of interest

As public servants, employees at the WSIB have a responsibility to act in an ethical way at all times to create a respectful workplace and maintain public trust. Job applicants are required to disclose any circumstance that could result in a real, potential or perceived conflict of interest. A conflict of interest is any situation where your private interests may impair or be perceived to impair the decisions you make in your official capacity. This may include: political activity, directorship, other outside employment and certain personal relationships (e.g. with current WSIB employees, customers and/or stakeholders). If you have any questions about conflict of interest obligations and/or how to make a disclosure, please contact the Talent Acquisition Centre at

talentacquisitioncentre@wsib.on.ca

.
