Job description

About the WSIB:

At the WSIB, we’re here to help. When a workplace injury or illness happens, we move quickly to provide wage-loss benefits, medical coverage, and support for recovery and return to work.

We are one of the largest insurance organizations in North America, supporting more than five million people in over 300,000 workplaces across Ontario.

Job summary:

As part of the Actuarial Services team, you will support the delivery of reliable, accurate and timely actuarial analysis for internal and external clients. You will contribute to a range of projects that support rate setting, liability valuation, financial forecasting and experience rating, helping provide actuarial advice to the WSIB and the Ontario MOL.

What you will do:

- Support actuarial projects through data analysis, model development, validation and reporting

- Assist with pricing, liability valuation, financial projections and experience rating work

- Help maintain and improve actuarial models, assumptions and systems

- Prepare analyses, reports and presentations for management and stakeholders

- Respond to information requests and support audits, studies and special projects

- Work with internal partners and external stakeholders to gather data and provide actuarial insight

What you bring:

- Post-secondary degree in mathematics, science, business or a related field

- Basic knowledge of actuarial principles

- At least 0-2 years of experience in insurance or financial services

- Proficiency in Microsoft Office

- Completion of 2 or more SOA/CAS exams, or equivalent experience

Assets:

- Degree in actuarial science

- 3 or more years of actuarial experience

- Experience with VBA, SAS, SQL or GGY AXIS

Why join the WSIB:

This is an opportunity to apply your actuarial skills in a purpose-driven organization, supporting key financial and policy decisions that help maintain a strong and sustainable workplace safety and insurance system in Ontario.

- Competitive salary and comprehensive benefits

- Flexible work options to support work-life balance

- Ongoing professional development and opportunities for growth

- A diverse, equitable, and inclusive workplace where your voice matters

- The chance to truly make a difference in the lives of Ontarians

Disclosing conflicts of interest

As public servants, employees at the WSIB have a responsibility to act in an ethical way at all times to create a respectful workplace and maintain public trust. Job applicants are required to disclose any circumstance that could result in a real, potential or perceived conflict of interest. A conflict of interest is any situation where your private interests may impair or be perceived to impair the decisions you make in your official capacity. This may include: political activity, directorship, other outside employment and certain personal relationships (e.g. with current WSIB employees, customers and/or stakeholders). If you have any questions about conflict of interest obligations and/or how to make a disclosure, please contact the Talent Acquisition Centre at

talentacquisitioncentre@wsib.on.ca

.
