About the Workplace Safety and Insurance Board (WSIB)

We’re here to help. When an injury or illness happens on the job, we move quickly to provide wage-loss benefits, medical coverage and support to help people get back to work. Funded by businesses, we also provide no-fault collective liability insurance and access to industry-specific health and safety information. We are one of the largest insurance organizations in North America covering over five million people in more than 300,000 workplaces across Ontario. For more information, visit

wsib.ca

.

participate in programs that support your health and wellbeing

You’ll also receive a competitive salary and may be eligible to participate in our health and dental plan.

Help Improve Outcomes for Workers Across Ontario Through Data and Insights

At WSIB, we're committed to helping people recover from workplace injuries and illnesses and return to meaningful work. As a purpose-driven public sector organization, the work we do has a direct impact on workers, employers, and communities across Ontario.

As a

Program Analyst, Operations Quality & Evaluation

, you will serve as a trusted analytics partner to Operations leadership. You will use data, reporting, and business intelligence tools to identify trends, measure performance, evaluate initiatives, and provide actionable recommendations that improve operational outcomes and support informed decision-making.

This role is ideal for someone who enjoys combining

technical analytics skills with business problem-solving

. You'll work with operational data, build dashboards and reports, automate processes, and help leaders understand what is happening in their business, why it's happening, and what actions they should consider next.

What You'll Do

In this role, you will:

Analyze operational data to identify trends, risks, opportunities, and emerging issues.

Develop dashboards, reports, and visualizations that support decision-making.

Provide insights and recommendations to management and senior leaders.

Collaborate with stakeholders across the organization to understand business needs and deliver data-driven solutions.

Support performance measurement, reporting, and continuous improvement initiatives.

Research leading practices and benchmarks to identify opportunities for improvement.

Prepare executive briefings, presentations, and reports to communicate key findings and recommendations.

Help automate reporting processes and improve data accessibility across Operations.

Disclosing conflicts of interest

As public servants, employees at the WSIB have a responsibility to act in an ethical way at all times to create a respectful workplace and maintain public trust. Job applicants are required to disclose any circumstance that could result in a real, potential or perceived conflict of interest. A conflict of interest is any situation where your private interests may impair or be perceived to impair the decisions you make in your official capacity. This may include: political activity, directorship, other outside employment and certain personal relationships (e.g. with current WSIB employees, customers and/or stakeholders). If you have any questions about conflict of interest obligations and/or how to make a disclosure, please contact the Talent Acquisition Centre at

talentacquisitioncentre@wsib.on.ca

.
