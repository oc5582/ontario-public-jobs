Job summary:

This position is responsible for delivering enterprise-grade solutions using Microsoft Power Platform and SharePoint Online within the Application Development and Services Branch. The role requires in-depth, hands-on experience in solution development across Power Apps (Model-driven and Canvas applications), Dataverse, Power Automate, Power BI, and AI Builder, with the expectation that the candidate will be productive from day one.

The successful candidate will apply advanced skills in application design, technical systems analysis, solution architecture, development, implementation, transition to operations, production support, and ongoing maintenance of enterprise business applications and platform-based solutions. Strong knowledge of platform governance, ALM, security architecture, and best practices for scalable enterprise delivery is essential.

This position also requires proven experience with SharePoint Online architecture, solution development, and business-facing application support, as well as foundational knowledge of Copilot Studio and emerging AI capabilities and how they integrate with the Power Platform ecosystem. The role may involve working independently or as part of a team on large or complex assignments and may also require acting as a subject matter expert while guiding other professionals and vendor partners. A strong focus is placed on Agile delivery, client engagement, operational excellence, and timely resolution of issues across new and existing services. This position demands a high degree of independence, accountability, ownership, and commitment to customer success.

Disclosing conflicts of interest

As public servants, employees at the WSIB have a responsibility to act in an ethical way at all times to create a respectful workplace and maintain public trust. Job applicants are required to disclose any circumstance that could result in a real, potential or perceived conflict of interest. A conflict of interest is any situation where your private interests may impair or be perceived to impair the decisions you make in your official capacity. This may include: political activity, directorship, other outside employment and certain personal relationships (e.g. with current WSIB employees, customers and/or stakeholders). If you have any questions about conflict of interest obligations and/or how to make a disclosure, please contact the Talent Acquisition Centre at

talentacquisitioncentre@wsib.on.ca

.
