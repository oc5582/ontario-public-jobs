Job Summary

Reporting to the Community Recreation Coordinator, the Program Instructor is accountable for planning and implementation of a safe and enjoyable beginner to intermediate program, teaching the fundamentals of the specified sport, art, dance, general interest, Leadership, etc.

Dance Instructor - Hip Hop (Other genres welcomed)

Job Duties Include

Develops and implements a safe and enjoyable 8-12 week Hip Hop Dance Program for children ages 6 to 13, under the supervision of the Community Recreation Coordinator. Current program requirements are for Hip Hop Dance (Other genres welcomed)

Leads groups of program participants and ensures the safety and well-being of your assigned class.

Provides an individualized approach that assists with the mastery of skills and development within the program.

Ensures that the necessary equipment and supplies are available, and that such equipment and supplies are maintained in a safe condition.

Takes appropriate action to deal with incidents, problems and emergencies, as outlined in the departmental policies and procedures.

Ensures that the attendance sheets are completed accurately every week and submitted to the appropriate supervisor at the end of each session.

Assists in the development and implementation of special needs programming in conjunction with supervisor.

Creates an environment that fosters a positive and inclusive program experience.

Creates an environment where participants strive to participate fully, perform the best of their ability, and learn new skills.

Works cooperatively and maintains ongoing and effective communication with participants, and the Community Recreation Coordinator.

Ensures the class is prepared before the participants arrive and that it is clean after the students leave.

Ensures the safety and well-being of your assigned class.

Abides by the Town of Ajax policies and acts in a professional manner at all times.

Ensures effective public relations, and problem solves as necessary.

Performs administrative tasks necessary to support the program.

Qualifications/Skills

Must be a minimum of 16 years of age or older.

Minimum 1 year of teaching/coaching Hip Hop Dance and/or leadership skills and a combination of training, education, and experience deemed equivalent.

Minimum 2-3 years of advanced skills/experience relevant to the activity being taught, acquired through multiple years of participation and lessons.

Knowledge and experience related to the delivery of recreation programs and services.

Experience working with various age groups

in a recreational setting.

Current Standard First Aid certification with CPR C certification.

HIGH FIVE ® - Principles of Healthy Child Development.

Able to provide a clear Criminal Reference Check (including Vulnerable Sector Search) that is current (completed within the last six months), upon being hired.

Must be available to work a variety of shifts including days, afternoons, evenings and weekends.

What is Offered to Staff

Compensation: We offer competitive pay that reflects the responsibilities of the position.

Hours of Work: This is a non-union position that works up to 24 hours per week. The hours of work are Sunday to Saturday, various shifts between 9:00 a.m. – 10:30 p.m. (Current program needs are for Wednesdays at the McLean Community Centre from 6:15 p.m. - 8:30 p.m.)

Town Perks: The Town offers part-time staff access to an Employee & Family Service Program, Health & Wellness Program, Town of Ajax Fitness Centre Combo Membership and a defined pension plan (OMERS pension -
