Job Summary

Reporting to the Program Supervisor – Camps/Playgrounds, Program Director – Camps/Playgrounds, and Community Recreation Coordinator, Camp Counsellors are accountable for planning and implementing a safe, enjoyable and diverse program for camp participants aged 2.5-16 years.

Job Duties Include

Develops and implements a safe, enjoyable and diverse program (including special events and theme days).

Ensures the safety and well-being of all participants and other staff during program hours.

Maintains program schedules and ensures deadlines are met.

Works cooperatively and maintains ongoing and effective communication with participants, parents, colleagues, and supervisors.

Performs daily site checks and ensures that the necessary equipment and supplies are available in a safe condition.

Takes appropriate action to deal with incidents/accidents and emergencies, as outlined in the departmental policies and procedures.

Completes and submits administrative tasks (program plans, attendance forms, etc.) within the specified deadlines.

Contribute to the evaluations of volunteers by assessing their ongoing performance.

Assists in the development and implementation of adaptive programming for participants with special needs.

Is actively involved in leading activities during program hours.

Ability to work outdoors and participate in swim trips (comfortable in the water).

Responsible for daily opening and closing procedures at Camp/Playground location.

Creates an environment that fosters a positive and inclusive program experience.

Acts as a positive role model for program participants and other team members.

Qualifications/Skills

Must be a minimum of 16 years of age or older by March 2, 2027.

Excellent organization, time management and planning skills.

Self-motivated, positive, confident and approachable.

Ability to work with minimal supervision.

Strong oral and written communication skills.

Current Standard First Aid with CPR C certification (If hired, must be obtained by March 2, 2027).

HIGH FIVE - Principles of Healthy Child Development certification (If hired, must be obtained by March 2, 2027).

Able to provide a clear Criminal Reference /Vulnerable Sector check that is current (completed in the last 6 months), upon being hired.

Must be available to work the full week of March Break (March 15 - 19, 2027) including mandatory training dates (Orientation, BMST, March Break Training).

Nice to Have

Knowledge and experience related to the delivery of recreation/camp programs would be an asset.

Preferred 1 year of experience working with children (i.e. babysitting).
