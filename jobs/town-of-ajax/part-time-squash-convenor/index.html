Job Summary

Reporting to the Active Living Supervisor, the Squash Convenor is accountable for overseeing the Squash program providing a safe and welcoming environment consisting of exceptional customer service, squash promotion and instruction to members and drop-in customers.

The Squash Convenor also has the opportunity to provide squash lessons in addition to scheduled hours.

Job Duties Include

Court supervision, on-court demonstration and technique assistance/feedback, general interaction with members.

General administrative tasks, emails, bulletin boards, member incentive support and preparations.

Create and monitor seasonal house league, round robins, and social events.

Provide tours of the facilities for potential members.

Equipment cleaning – racquets, goggles, operational checks.

Monitoring of membership cards and pay-go customers – i.e., ensuring payment was received / membership cards are current.

Educates the public on issues of squash and active lifestyles.

Listens to feedback from customers and responds in a professional and knowledgeable manner.

Prevents accidents through enforcement of facilities Code of Conduct rules and regulations.

Monitors safety in the squash courts and address/reports safety concerns.

Responds to emergency situations.

Administers First Aid in accordance with highest level of certification.

Qualifications/Skills

College diploma in health and fitness related studies or experience, training and education deemed equivalent.

Minimum of one year squash training experience.

Extensive knowledge of squash play.

Organization, time management and planning skills.

Self-motivated, positive, confident, and approachable.

Ability to work with minimal supervision.

Respectful of diverse opinions and perspectives.

Committed to quality customer service and innovation to improve efficiency and reduce costs.

An energetic personality and a passion to motivate others to live an active lifestyle.

Strong oral and written communication skills.

Current Standard First Aid with CPR-C Certification.

Able to provide a clear Criminal Reference Check, including Vulnerable Sector Search that is current (completed within the last 6 months), upon hire.

What is Offered to Staff

Compensation: We offer competitive pay that reflects the responsibilities of the position.

Hours of Work: This is a non-union position that works up to 10 hours per week with an option for up to 14 hours of lessons per week for a total up to 24 hours per week. The hours of work may vary Monday to Sunday from 6:00 a.m. to 10:30 p.m.

Town Perks: The Town offers part-time staff access to an Employee & Family Service Program, Health & Wellness Program, Town of Ajax Fitness Centre Combo Membership and a defined pension plan (OMERS pension - https://www.omers.com/).
