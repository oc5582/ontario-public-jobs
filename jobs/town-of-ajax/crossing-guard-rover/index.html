Job Summary

The Crossing Guard Rover supports the safe and effective operation of designated school crossings throughout the Town of Ajax. The position mentors assigned Crossing Guards by modelling proper crossing techniques, coordinating coverage for absences, and providing crossing coverage as required. Rovers ensure pedestrians of all ages cross safely at designated locations while providing friendly customer service and remaining alert to activity at the crossing.

Job Duties Include

The following responsibilities and duties of this position include but is not limited to:

Direct and supervise the movement of pedestrians across the roadway

Monitor vehicle and pedestrian traffic management at the crossing

Coordination of coverage for Crossing Guard absence(s)

Mentors assigned Crossing Guards by modelling proper crossing techniques

Educate pedestrians on safe crossing practices

Respond to emergency issues involving vehicles and pedestrian traffic

Report safety issues to supervisor

Provide customer service to the pedestrians and the public

Champion of safety in the community on behalf of the Town of Ajax

Skills/Abilities

Previous experience as a Crossing Guard is considered an asset

Ability to use email, cellphone technology is required

Able to stand at a crossing for up to five hours per day in all types of weather

Able to work in adverse weather conditions including extremes of heat, humidity, cold, rain, snow, sleet, and wind and be capable of working under all types of road conditions, exposure to traffic noise and exhaust from vehicles

Able to establish and maintain effective working relationships with the public

Able to remain calm and use good judgment in emergency situations

Able to work independently without supervision

Valid Class "G" Ontario Driver's License

Must provide a satisfactory Vulnerable Sector Screening Check

What we offer to staff

Compensation

: We offer competitive pay that reflects the responsibilities of the position

, including a daily Rover premium and a bi-weekly gas mileage premium.

Hours of Work

: This is a non-union position that works approximately 12 hours per week and depends on crossing location. School Crossing Guards work during the school year (September - June, excludes holidays and PA days) and must be available to work shifts between the hours of 7:30 am - 4:00 pm Monday - Friday, exact times are based on crossing guard location, but range from 2 to 4.5 hours per day. School Crossing Guards are required to be at their assigned crossing site in the morning, during lunchtime and after school.

Town Perks

: The Town offers part-time staff access to an Employee & Family Assistance Program, Health & Wellness Program, Town of Ajax Fitness Centre membership and a defined pension plan (OMERS pension - https://www.omers.com/).
