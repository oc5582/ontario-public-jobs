Job Summary

Reporting to the Supervisor of Accounting, the Senior Financial Analyst (Accounting) plays a key role in supporting the Town’s accounting and financial reporting functions. The position provides in-depth analysis of balance sheet accounts, oversees complex reconciliations and journal entries, supports year-end and audit requirements, monitors cash flow, and identifies opportunities to enhance accounting processes and efficiencies. The role also provides guidance to Accounting and Payroll staff and back-up support for key financial functions.

Job Duties Include

The following responsibilities and duties of this position includes but is not limited to:

Provides functional advice, financial analysis and direction to staff in other dep

artments to ensure compliance of financial/accounting requirements.

Supports and directs staff on all aspects of Accounts Payable including troubleshooting of issues.

Performs monthly accounting duties, oversees monthly financial reporting process.

Maintains internal controls related to the Town’s bank account, accounts payable, bank reconciliations and petty cash.

Prepares complex account reconciliations and reviews and approves balance sheet account reconciliations prepared by other accounting staff.

Approves vendor changes for accuracy, authorization and coding.

Prepares weekly cash flow projection.

Supports Financial Data Specialist in the review and approval of monthly HST returns.

Assists Supervisor of Accounting in monitoring annual work plans.

Development and implementation of an Input Tax Credit (ITC) framework for operating expenses, as well as the application of ITCs to eligible capital projects.

Works collaboratively with internal staff to monitor Town grants, ensure funding compliance, track eligible expenditures, and support reporting and reimbursement requirements.

Reviews, approves, processes, and posts employee expense reimbursements through Workday

Maintains and monitors monthly Municipal Accommodation Tax (MAT) records.

Prepares quarterly reconciliations and receivable vouchers for reimbursements from the Region of Durham.

Coordinates and prepares the FIR, including the collection, validation, reconciliation and submission of financial data to ensure compliance with legislative and reporting requirements.

Qualifications/Skills

University degree in Business Administration or similar field of study or a combination of training, education and experience deemed equivalent.

Holds the CPA (CA, CMA, CGA) professional accounting designation.

Minimum six (6) years’ experience in accounting, financial analysis, budgets, etc. in progressively senior level positions

Expert knowledge and technical skills working with Microsoft Excel

Advanced skills in other Microsoft Office applications (Word, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams)

Excellent research, analytical and problem-solving skills.

High level of accuracy and attention to detail required for analysis.

Ability to work in a fast-paced work environment, with multiple projects/tasks concurrently, with priorities often shifting.

Advanced communication skills both written and verbal.

Maintains professional and technical knowledge by attending educational workshops; reviewing professional publications; establishing personal networks; participating in professional societies.

Ability to work with minimal supervision.

Upon hire, ability to provide a Criminal Record Check that is satisfactory to the Town of Ajax.

Nice to Have

Completion of municipal specific multi module courses such as Municipal Finance & Accounting, Municipal Administration Program.
