Job Summary

The Project Manager II is a project management professional, working within the Digital Transformation-Project Management Office (PMO), responsible for leading the planning, execution, and delivery of small to medium technology and digital transformation initiatives that support Halton Region organizational priorities and strategic objectives.

The role manages project scope, schedule, cost, risks, stakeholder engagement, and ensures visibility into project and portfolio performance through effective reporting and communication.

Duties & Responsibilities

Apply relationship-building capability and project management expertise to support the organization in the achievement and delivery of transformational technology and digital solutions

Establish and maintain effective working relationships between Digital & Information Services (D&IS) technology department and business stakeholders by providing proactive and consultative project management expertise

Support the PMO governance, and the digital delivery lifecycle processes, through stakeholder management and, digital portfolio reporting dashboard

Lead projects from initiation to closing phases to deliver the technology solutions aligned with both business, functional and technical requirements

Plan, document and execute projects and ensure visibility for key stakeholders by balancing risk and overall performance

Prepare, manage and report project cost budgets, timeframe estimates and milestones in conjunction with detailed project plans

Collaborate with stakeholders to understand and document project needs and project deliverables

Identify trends, dependencies and risks within the digital projects portfolio and prepare mitigation strategies

Develop and deliver project status update presentations to senior management and steering committees

Collaborate with procurement, technical and business partners in the development of Request for Proposals (RFP) and Negotiable Request for Proposals (NRFP) for the purpose of achieving project objectives

Manage relationship, performance and deliverables across SAP/Application implementation partners, system integrators and third-party vendors

Perform other duties as assigned

Skills & Qualifications

Essential

Degree in Business Administration, Information Technology or a related discipline

Minimum 4 years of project management experience

Project Management Professional (PMP) designation or Certified Associate in Project Management (CAPM)

IAASC Lean Six Sigma Certification

Experience in Project Portfolio Management within a PMO

Excellent interpersonal, teamwork and communication skills

Excellent collaboration and relationship management skills

Experience with project documentation and project work plan preparation for technology projects

Experience with browser-based collaboration and document management platforms such as Microsoft Office Suite (Office 365), including SharePoint online

Good understanding of integrations between SAP SuccessFactors, SAP S/4HANA, Payroll, Finance and other enterprise applications.

Knowledge of Finance, Procurement, HR, Payroll and Workforce Management business processes within an SAP environment

Preferred

Agile or Scrum certifications

Knowledge of the latest developments and leading practices in the project management industry and profession (e.g. PMBOK®)

Working knowledge of ERP and CRM solutions and familiarity with the SDLC process including planning for all phases from define requirements, design, build/develop, test management, Release/Production.
