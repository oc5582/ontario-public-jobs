Job description

About Us The Ontario Cannabis Store provides safe, responsible access to recreational cannabis for adults 19 and older. We operate the sole legal online store for recreational cannabis in Ontario and are the provincial wholesaler of cannabis for private retail stores. Working at the OCS is a unique opportunity to be part of an agile start-up in a ground-breaking new industry. We’re a diverse team passionate about delivering a great customer experience, working together with mutual respect and building value out of our differences. We’re an inclusive organization that understands that delivering great results comes out of ensuring every voice is heard. About the Role We're looking for a Senior Advisor, Social Responsibility to join our team. In this role, you'll support the execution of the OCS Social Responsibility (SR) strategy by turning strategic priorities into coordinated, actionable programs and initiatives. Reporting to the Manager, Social Responsibility, you'll help ensure key initiatives are effectively planned, organized, and advanced across internal teams, partners, and systems.

Working closely with the Manager, you'll play a key role in moving projects from concept to implementation by coordinating stakeholders, navigating internal processes, and driving operational excellence across initiatives. This position is focused on execution and delivery, ensuring programs remain on track and aligned with organizational objectives.

You'll also contribute to the development of new ideas and approaches, bringing an understanding of cannabis policy, harm reduction, and public education within a regulated environment. This is an excellent opportunity for someone who enjoys connecting strategy to action and making a meaningful impact through collaborative program delivery.

About Your Day

​​Support the planning and execution of Social Responsibility initiatives aligned with OCS's corporate strategy. Translate strategic priorities into coordinated workplans, timelines, and implementation activities. Track progress, dependencies, and follow-ups to ensure initiatives advance and commitments are met. Coordinate with internal teams and external partners to drive deliverables, resolve issues, and maintain momentum. Support stakeholder accountability by monitoring progress and escalating risks or challenges as needed. Contribute to the delivery of cross-functional initiatives through effective coordination and execution. Support the development and tracking of KPIs, program metrics, and internal reporting. Maintain organized documentation to support program continuity, transparency, and compliance. Assist with budget tracking, procurement, contracts, and vendor management in accordance with OCS policies. Ensure required processes, approvals, and documentation standards are followed across initiatives. Identify delivery risks, delays, and process gaps, and recommend solutions. Coordinate partnership activities, stakeholder engagement, deliverables, and follow-up actions to support program implementation. Support research, data requests, and the preparation of briefings, summaries, and updates. Assist Communications with sponsorship and partnership opportunities and execution activities as required. Conduct research and environmental scans on cannabis policy, harm reduction, and social responsibility trends. Support the development and continuous improvement of Social Responsibility programs, engagement approaches, and operational processes.
