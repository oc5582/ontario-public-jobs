If you’re looking to use your expertise to drive strategic outcomes, we’d love to hear from you.

‎

We are looking for a motivated and detail-oriented student to join our tax team as a Tax Intern. This eight-month internship offers hands-on exposure to Canadian, U.S., and international tax matters in a collaborative professional environment. It will run from January 2027-August 2027.

The Tax Intern will report to the Senior Tax Manager and support the team across a range of tax compliance, reporting, research, and administrative functions, including:

Prepare T2 Canadian federal and provincial corporate income tax returns for tax-exempt and taxable entities

Prepare T3 Trust Income Tax and Information Returns for Canadian trusts

Assist with T5013 Partnership Information Returns and Statements of Partnership Income

Assist with the preparation of monthly and quarterly GST/HST reconciliations and returns

Assist with preparing responses to inquiries from tax authorities

Assist with reviewing and analyzing U.S. and foreign tax documents, including Schedules K-1 and K-3 and Forms 1042-S, 8804, and 8805

Assist with tax know-your-client requests, including FATCA withholding tax reporting and self-certification documentation

Assist with updating and preparing tax memoranda

Assist with preparing foreign withholding tax reclaim applications

Assist with pre-tax-season activities, including gathering and reviewing financial information, preparing tax compliance lists, and coordinating with internal and external stakeholders

Assist with post-tax-season projects, including reviewing and filing notices of assessment and updating tax trackers

Collect, organize, and validate tax information provided by internal and external clients

Conduct research on a variety of tax issues

Support other tax-related ad hoc projects

Requirements

The following are your keys to success in this role:

Ability to prioritize and manage multiple tasks effectively

Ability to work under pressure and meet tight deadlines

Strong teamwork, verbal communication, and written communication skills

Adaptability, curiosity, and a willingness to learn new tasks and technologies

A collaborative mindset and positive attitude

Proficiency with Microsoft Office Suite

Experience preparing tax returns, including T2, T3, and T5013 returns, is an asset

Experience with CCH iFirm and/or Taxprep is an asset

What experience will you gain?

During the internship, you will have the opportunity to build the following skills and experience:

Gain an understanding of how a corporate tax group operates and exposure to different areas of tax

Continue to develop tax compliance and research skills

Develop practical experience with Canadian, U.S., and international tax compliance and documentation
