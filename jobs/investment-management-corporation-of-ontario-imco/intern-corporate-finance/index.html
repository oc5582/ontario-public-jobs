Job description

At IMCO, our talent is among the best! IMCO offers a uniquely stimulating and rewarding environment where you can help build and drive organizational transformation, all while seeking to challenge yourself, learn, and grow your career.

Our culture is built on collaboration and passion, with a shared commitment to delivering lasting value to the clients we serve. Located in downtown Toronto, our vision is to be the partner of choice for Ontario’s public sector funds and to build a high-performing, value-driven asset management firm.

This job posting is for an existing vacancy. If you are ready to deliver best-in-class service and join a collaborative, motivated and fun team of professionals, IMCO offers the opportunity to do impactful work and broaden your expertise.

If you’re looking to use your expertise to drive strategic outcomes, we’d love to hear from you.

‎

This internship runs from January - April 2027 (4 Months)

We are looking for a highly motivated student to join our Financial Reporting team. The successful candidate will support IMCO's corporate accounting and financial reporting functions and gain hands-on experience in a dynamic asset management environment. In this role, you will contribute to the preparation of financial statements for IMCO Pool funds and IMCO Corporate, support the production of IMCO's Annual Report, assist with the year-end external audit, and participate in the month-end financial close process. The successful candidate will play an important role in maintaining efficient, effective, and scalable financial reporting processes and controls. This position provides a unique opportunity to contribute to IMCO's commitment to best-in-class financial reporting through the delivery of high-quality, transparent, and reliable financial information to management, the Board of Directors, clients, and other stakeholders.

Responsibilities Include:

Support the preparation of financial statements for IMCO Pool funds and IMCO Corporate, as well as IMCO's Annual Report.

Assist with the annual external audit process, including coordinating and responding to audit requests.

Liaise with custodians and Investment Operations teams to obtain supporting information required for the financial reporting of investment vehicles.

Assist in the preparation of annual financial statements for IMCO subsidiaries and other legal entities.

Support the preparation of financial information for regulatory and compliance reporting.

Partner with the Tax team to provide information required for annual tax filings.

Liaise with Legal and legal entity management teams to maintain corporate records and entity documentation.

Participate in the month-end financial close process, including preparing journal entries, account reconciliations, and analyses related to cash, accruals, capital assets, and prepaid expenses.

Support accounts payable activities, including expense report reviews and other accounts payable processes.

Contribute to initiatives focused on process improvement, automation, and operational efficiency.

Perform other projects and responsibilities as assigned.

What do you need to succeed?

Strong ability to deliver high-quality work accurately and within established deadlines.

Demonstrated initiative and a desire to identify opportunities for process improvements and operational efficiencies.

Excellent interpersonal and communication skills, with the ability to collaborate effectively with colleagues at all levels of the organization.

Self-motivated, adaptable, and capable of working in a fast-paced environment with competing priorities.

Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with exceptional attention to detail.

Interest in financial reporting, accounting, and process improvement within a dynamic asset management environment.

Proficiency with Microsoft Excel, Word, and PowerPoint; experience with Power Automate, Power BI, VBA, and AI tools is considered an asset.
