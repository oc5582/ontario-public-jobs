Job Posting Description:

This is an onsite role #LI-Onsite

Contract Duration: Up to 12 Months

Reporting to the Director, Talent Acquisition, you will help accomplish our talent attraction and selection projects. By providing outstanding administrative support to our high-volume Talent Acquisition team and ensuring data integrity and completeness. As the main contact for internal and external candidates, you will ensure positive candidate experience as you facilitate the stages of the recruitment process.

If you're a life-long learner, known for your attention to detail, then this role is for you!

About the Role

Respond to applicant inquiries regarding roles and recruitment and selection process

Update HRIS (Workday) as searches progress, ensuring data integrity

Schedule candidate interviews and coordinate interview logistics, initiate and monitor candidate background check processes, raising issues as they arise

Assemble employment offer packages, process new hires, ensure recruitment documentation is complete, and organize and update recruitment files

Provide reliable and timely resolution to inquiries from our People Operations team

Process invoices for our team, following up on issues and participating in annual budgeting conversations

Maintain and update reports with detailed and accurate information, providing data as needed

Act as a positive recruitment ambassador, offering support with recruitment policies and procedures and approaching changes with optimism

Offer input on process improvement to ensure a positive employee and candidate experience

Provide administrative support to the Talent Acquisition team and work collaboratively with People Operations, HRBP and other COE’s

Required Qualifications

University or College Degree in Human Resources or related field.

1+ years of experience in Talent Acquisition or Human Resources.

Previous experience providing administrative support to Talent Acquisition HR leader or team with a high volume of work.

Preferred Qualifications

Highly organized and able to prioritize your time when receiving multiple requests.

Impeccable follow up and attention to detail as well as excellent written and verbal communication skills, editing and proofreading.

Professional approach with the ability to handle highly sensitive information in a confidential manner.

Able to anticipate needs, recognize problems and offer creative solutions, with a mindset of continuous improvement.

Technically savvy, with proficiency in HRIS, Preferably Workday, Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint.

Collaborative teammate with superior influencing skills, developing relationships easily across HR to move recruitment forward.

Experience and enthusiasm for recruitment technologies.
