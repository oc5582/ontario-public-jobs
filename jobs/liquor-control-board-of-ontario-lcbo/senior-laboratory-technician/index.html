Job Posting Description:

This is an onsite role #LI-Onsite

Are you passionate about applying and growing your analytical expertise?

Reporting to the Senior Manager, Quality Assurance Laboratory, you will perform various analyses of beverage alcohol products to validate compositional requirements and compliance with applicable regulations. You will apply your analytical expertise and provide support to Quality Assurance functions of the laboratory to ensure compliance with ISO 17025 and ISO 9001 standard, and LCBO quality policies. You will have an opportunity to support the continuous improvement, research and innovative projects led by Quality Assurance.

If you excel in the analytical testing field, have a passion for quality and thrive in a dynamic, fast-paced environment, then this is the role for you!

About the Role

Analytical testing

Perform various analytical testing using specialized instrumentation such as GC-MS, GC-FID, HPLC, LC-MS, ICP-MS, CFA following established SOPs and procedures

Operate, maintain, troubleshoot, repair, validate and calibrate analytical instrumentation ensuring optimum performance. Support servicing and upgrades of analytical instrumentation

Prepare reagents, standards and samples for various chemical analysis and sensory testing

Assess instrument and analytical methods performance to ensure data validity, integrity, reliability and accuracy

Process, review, validate, interpret and report results in accordance with the laboratory Quality Management System (QMS) policies and procedures

Support various laboratory projects, as assigned

Laboratory Quality Management System

Adhere to the requirements of the laboratory quality management system and drive its continuous improvement

Support laboratory compliance and accreditation to ISO 17025 and ISO 9001 standards

Ensure analytical practices, documentation and technical records are aligned with QA policies, QMS and regulatory requirements

Support various laboratory technical and quality monitoring programs and processes

Ensure compliance with safety standards and policies

Provide and support training of laboratory personnel

Laboratory Operations Support

Assist laboratory management as required by providing technical input to analytical testing methodologies, instrumentation and QMS programs and processes

Maintain laboratory supplies and chemical inventories

Provide support for general laboratory and administrative tasks as assigned by laboratory management

Perform other duties and accept additional responsibilities as assigned to support the evolving needs of the department

About you

Required Qualifications

B.Sc. in Chemistry or related scientific fields

Relevant experience and knowledge in analytical chemistry and applications applied in a regulated laboratory environment using advanced analytical instrumentation. Applied relevant expertise with a variety of analytical techniques such as GC-MS, GC-FID, HPLC, LC-MS, ICP-MS, CFA, including sample preparation, extraction, purification/isolation and analysis.

Experience in method development and validation, research activities and statistical analysis

Advanced expertise in equipment troubleshooting, maintenance and repair, set up, validation, calibration and related software

Understanding of Quality Management Systems in accordance with relevant quality standards and/or regulatory/compliance requirements and their application in laboratory settings

Proficiency in statistical analysis and statistical process control and computer literacy i.e. knowledge in relevant MS Office software and other data processing applications including tools/software

Good technical writing and documentation skills.

Solid analytical, problem-solving and critical thinking skills with ability to exercise sound judgement and work effectively with minimum direction and supervision

Demonstrated initiative in self‑development to enhance skills and effectively enhance laboratory operations

Strong attention to detail, complemented by effective organizational and time management skills, ability to prioritize competing demands and multi-task effectively in a dynamic, fast-paced environment, ensuring timely delivery of results with high quality and accuracy
