Job Posting Description:

This is an onsite role #LI-Onsite

Reporting to the Senior Manager, Workplace Investigations (WI), you will lead complex workplace investigations, conflict resolution, culture assessments and restoration processes. The role ensures compliance with applicable legislation, including the Ontario Human Rights Code, Occupational Health and Safety Act, and Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act, while supporting a respectful, inclusive, and compliant workplace environment.

You will play a key role within the Workplace Investigations Centre of Excellence (WI CoE), providing independent, impartial support across the organization and contributing to continuous improvement of complaints management and resolution practices.

If you enjoy contributing to a safe workplace, then this is the role for you!

About the Role

Conduct interviews, gather and assess evidence, and document findings in accordance with best practices

Prepare clear, comprehensive investigation reports outlining findings, conclusions, and recommendations

Provide expert guidance to stakeholders on investigation processes and complaint resolution

Support early resolution, mediation, and workplace restoration efforts, where appropriate

Advise internal stakeholders on managing workplace complaints and investigation processes

Provide subject matter expertise to HR partners on early resolution and mediation strategies

Develop tools, resources, and frameworks to strengthen investigation practices and consistency

Lead the engagement of external investigators through the Vendor of Record (VOR) process

Act as an escalation point within the HR model, providing expert guidance on complex matters

Deliver training and awareness initiatives to support leaders and HR partners

Identify trends in workplace complaints and develop strategies to address recurring issues

Contribute to and champion WI CoE priorities, including complaints management, accountability frameworks, and training initiatives

Analyze metrics and reporting to inform business decisions and continuous improvement

Recommend enhancements to investigation processes, policies, and practices

Build effective partnerships with internal stakeholders to strengthen credibility and trust

Design and deliver tools, templates, and communications to support business leaders and HR

Develop training materials and job aids to improve consistency and accountability

Monitor and track investigation outcomes, recommendations, and organizational trends

Ensure compliance with applicable legislation, collective agreements, and LCBO policies

Maintain accurate and confidential investigation records and documentation

Prepare reports and presentations on trends, findings, and outcomes

About You

Post-secondary degree in Human Resources, Business Administration, Law, or a related discipline

Specialized training in workplace investigations, human rights, or alternative dispute resolution (ADR)

Minimum 5 years of experience in workplace investigations, human resources, or a related field

Proven experience managing complex human rights and workplace complaints

Experience working in a unionized or broader public sector environment is preferred

Advanced knowledge of human rights legislation, including the Ontario Human Rights Code, OHSA, and AODA

Understanding of administrative law, corporate governance, and compliance requirements

Familiarity with collective agreements and investigation best practices

Demonstrated professionalism, tact, and sound judgment in sensitive situations

High ethical standards and discretion in handling confidential information

Ability to work independently and collaboratively in a complex environment
