Job Posting Description:

Do you have experience with customers, their occasions and beverage alcohol? In this LCBO store leadership role reporting to the District Manager, you'll build a robust store team. Every day you'll achieve store results and promote responsible retailing.

The Role

You will deliver remarkable experiences as the trusted destination for the world's wines, beers and spirits.

You will create the customer experience in store by developing a customer-first team mentality in all aspects of the store's operation and program execution.

You will lead a team in all aspects of store retail operations including customer service, inventory management, financial management, merchandising, and retail sales.

You will oversee several areas for the store including social responsibility issues, facilities management, resource protection, health & safety, WSIB, workplace accommodation, environmental issues and workplace diversity.

About You!

High school graduate or bring the equivalent experience.

You have 5 years or more of retail experience in progressive roles such as Store Manager, Assistant Manager, Trainer, Departmental Manager, or District Manager.

You bring a customer-centric attitude. You enjoy spending time on the store floor and encourage giving back in support of the community for the greater good.

You have experience embracing new initiatives focused on continuous improvement

You enjoy providing feedback, empowering people, and mentoring in the moment.

You know your store numbers. You forecast and analyze sales and expenses. You build a plan to engage the team and grow the business.

Your skills span customer service, retail sales, inventory management, financial management, merchandising, facilities management, resource protection, health & safety, WSIB, workplace accommodation, and sustainability practices

Successful completion of Product Knowledge Essential, Foundations and Discovery e-learning programs

You are proficient in with technology tools including Blue Yonder Workforce Management, Workday, web-based ordering, and MS Office applications (Outlook, Word, Excel)

Available to work evenings, weekends, and holidays.
