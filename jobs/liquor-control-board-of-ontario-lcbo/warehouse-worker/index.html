About the Role:

Manually unload cases of product from containers and trailers with the

assistance

of powered belt conveyors and step stools

Physically able to load and unload cases of beer, wine, spirits, and cider that weigh between 40lbs (18 kg if you prefer the metric system) and 60lbs (27 kg). Working at heights up to eight (8) stories and working in isolation

Required to stand for lengthy periods of time with movement such as reaching, bending, twisting, and walking

Select, assemble, and prepare pallet orders for customers

Clean up accidental spills; we all pitch in to be safe

Fill out safety-related checklists, dock sheets and other required forms, checking purchase orders and inputting small data points into a computer or peripheral devices.
