Your Role As Senior Advisor, Portfolio Intelligence and Value Realization, you will be a trusted partner responsible for optimizing value flow and portfolio performance within Technology & Infrastructure (T&I). In this role, you will bridge strategy and execution by translating enterprise value-optimization principles into actionable, measurable practices. Working closely with your manager, you will foster a data-driven culture that makes the value generated by T&I (ROI, productivity gains, risk reduction, etc.) tangible and visible to leadership and decision-makers. Key Responsibilities Portfolio and Data Intelligence Design, build and maintain the value dashboard (initiatives, value, capacity, risks and decisions) in collaboration with the team to provide real-time visibility into investments. Assess and highlight how the portfolio aligns with strategic investment priorities while adapting to emerging business needs. Connect key operational tools (Jira, Confluence, Power BI, etc.) with consolidated delivery data to establish and maintain reliable single sources of truth. Lead the end-to-end design of delivery performance metrics (e.g., cycle time, throughput, predictability) for projects and programs managed by the Delivery team, as well as value metrics for executive stakeholders and governance bodies. Develop portfolio reports to guide resource allocation and prioritization during leadership meetings, presenting findings to governance committees as needed. Delivery, Value Monitoring and Coaching Establish and facilitate operational and tactical governance forums across value streams, guiding departments toward optimal delivery practices. Advise teams, managers and delivery leads on adaptive planning, value-stream management optimization and results-focused delivery. Identify potential roadblocks to portfolio optimization, working collaboratively with teams to strategize and execute effective solutions. Maintain thorough tracking and documentation of strategic directions, decisions and changes affecting the portfolio. Cultivate a culture where teams iterate quickly, measure impact and adapt using real-time delivery data and feedback. Required Skills and Qualifications Bachelor’s degree in project management, business administration, technology or a related field. Key personal attributes: Continuous learner and mentor, comfortable with ambiguity, empathetic, authentic, collaborative, transparent and solution-focused.
