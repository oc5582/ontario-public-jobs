Explore the BDC Way in our Culture Book Join a team shaping Canada’s venture capital ecosystem At BDC, the Government Funds team manages capital on behalf of the Government of Canada to strengthen and grow Canada’s venture capital market. This includes deploying $350M under the Venture Capital Action Plan (VCAP) , $450M under the Venture Capital Catalyst Initiative (VCCI) , and $450M under the Renewed Venture Capital Catalyst Initiative (Renewed VCCI) . These programs totalling $1.2B AUM are designed to crowd in private capital and support fund managers across the Canadian venture capital asset class. Building on this foundation, BDC is now deploying the $1B Growth Venture Capital Catalyst Initiative (Growth VCCI) , a landmark platform focused on scaling Canada’s most promising funds and innovative companies. This is a unique opportunity to work at the intersection of investment, policy, and ecosystem development , with direct exposure to the most prominent Canadian fund managers while contributing to a national priority initiative shaping Canada’s innovation economy . POSITION OVERVIEW As Director, you will combine leadership, investment expertise, and complex project management to support the deployment and oversight of government-backed venture capital programs, including VCAP, VCCI, Renewed VCCI, and upcoming Growth VCCI. You will play a lead role in investment execution, stakeholder management, and portfolio governance, while engaging closely with fund managers, institutional partners, and internal stakeholders including Government.
