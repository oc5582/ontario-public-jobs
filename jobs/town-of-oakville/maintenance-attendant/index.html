Make a Difference in Your Community – Join Our Team!

Are you looking for a job that keeps you active, helps you learn new skills, and lets you give back to your community? We’re hiring Facility Maintenance Attendants to help keep our community centres and ice rinks clean, safe, and welcoming for everyone.

As a Facility Maintenance Attendant, you’ll be a vital part of the team that keeps everything running smoothly — from setting up events to maintaining the ice and outdoor spaces.

Why Work with Us?

Flexible hours that fit around school, family, or other commitments

Supportive, community-focused team that values your contributions

Play a key role in supporting local programs and events — help ensure facilities are clean, safe, and ready for activities like skating, sports leagues, and public gatherings

Gain hands-on experience in facility operations, event support, and customer service

Build valuable workplace skills like teamwork, communication, and problem-solving

Meet new people and make a real impact in your neighborhood

What You'll Do:

Event & Activity Support

Set up and take down tables, chairs, and equipment for sports, meetings, and special events

Ensure all areas are clean, organized, and ready for the next scheduled activity

Facility & Grounds Maintenance Responsibilities

Cleaning and sanitizing restrooms, changerooms, hallways, and activity areas

Restocking essential supplies such as soap, paper towels, and toilet paper

Regularly removing garbage and recycling to maintain a tidy environment

Maintaining outdoor areas through sweeping, litter pickup, and snow removal as needed

Assisting facility operators and lead hands with general maintenance tasks and special projects

Customer Service

Respond to customer inquiries and concerns, including providing directions and information about the facility

Address service requests from staff and customers in a timely and helpful manner

Greet and interact with customers in a friendly, respectful, and approachable manner

Maintain a calm and courteous attitude, even in busy or challenging situations

Ice Rink Support

Assist with cleaning and maintaining a smooth, safe ice surface

Set up nets, temporary boards, and other rink equipment as needed

Help keep rink areas clean, organized, and safe for all users

Health & Safety

Proactively identify and report health and safety issues

Ensure safety procedures and protocols are followed

Communicate delays or complications clearly and promptly

Assist in emergencies (e.g. power outages)

What We’re Looking For

No previous experience required — we provide full training

A positive attitude and a willingness to learn new skills

Reliable, organized, and able to work independently or as part of a team

Comfortable working in active and occasionally cold environments (like ice rinks)

Physically able to lift/move equipment and stay on your feet during shifts

Employees are required to wear CSA approved Green Patch safety footwear while working for the Corporation and will be required to supply footwear at their own expense.

Please note that this position requires a satisfactory

criminal record and judicial matters check (CRJMC)

dated within the last 30 days as a condition of employment.
