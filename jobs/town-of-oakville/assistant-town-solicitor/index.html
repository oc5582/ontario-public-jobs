Reporting to the Town Solicitor, as a member of the in-house Legal Department, the Assistant Town Solicitor serves to safeguard and advance the public interest of the municipal corporation by providing legal services and advice to staff and Council, including representing the Town in litigation and hearings, providing oral and written legal opinions, drafting and reviewing legal documents, including agreements and by-laws, preparing and reviewing reports, and attending Council meetings.

What can I expect to do in this role?

Responsible for providing timely and strategic legal services and advice relating to all facets of municipal and planning law to Town staff and Council.

Represent the Town in arbitrations, mediations, and litigation proceedings before the courts and administrative tribunals, including the Ontario Land Tribunal.

Negotiate and draft legal documents that reflect applicable policies.

Develop legal strategies, gather and prepare evidence, conduct legal research, and prepare and present legal arguments and opinions.

Make and explain decisions with respect to interpretations of legislation and by-laws.

Act as instructing counsel to external solicitors.

Work with multi-disciplinary groups to determine the best way to regulate matters and deliver municipal services.

How do I qualify?

A Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.) or Juris Doctor (J.D.) and qualified to practice law in Ontario.

A member in good standing with the Law Society of Upper Canada with a minimum of 8 years’ related experience in municipal and planning and development law.

Experience in advocacy before the Ontario Land Tribunal is considered an asset.

Experience providing legal services and advice to municipal and/or broader public sector clients.

Experience negotiating, structuring, drafting, interpreting, and providing legal counsel and services on various types of legal documents, including agreements and by-laws.

Your strengths include demonstrated leadership, interpersonal, communication, and advocacy skills, as well as the ability to manage multiple tasks effectively amidst conflicting demands and tight timelines.

Ability to work both independently and as a collaborative member of a hard-working and collegial team of professionals with a commitment to excellence and client satisfaction.

Strong negotiation, facilitation, mediation, and presentation skills, adaptable for a diverse audience.

Advanced analytical, problem-solving, research, and decision-making abilities.

A well-developed capacity to interpret, anticipate, and convey complex, technical, and abstract information is crucial.

Ability to exercise authority and influence while handling sensitive information and highly confidential matters.

The successful candidate must hold a valid and unrestricted Ontario Driver’s License Class G minimum and will be required to supply their own personal vehicle for performing job duties/corporate business.
