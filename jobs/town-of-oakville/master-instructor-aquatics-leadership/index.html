We are looking for staff who are accommodating, reliable and enthusiastic! If you are a leadership instructor with a passion for delivering high quality programs, then this is an opportunity for you!

What we offer:

Great pay

Flexible schedules

Free recertifications

Inclusive work environment

In person & Virtual Trainings

Leadership instructors must hold instructor certification(s) for indicated leadership courses and must have a current NL and current Intermediate First Aid with CPR-C & AED. Candidates must hold an examiner status in their certified teaching stream by the start of the contract.

A Leadership Instructor may teach advanced leadership courses including but not limited to:

National Lifeguard

Intermediate First Aid with CPR-C & AED

LSS Swim and Lifesaving Instructors

Aquatic Supervisor
