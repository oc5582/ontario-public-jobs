Job Responsibilities:

As a Town of Oakville Master Instructor Fitness, you will:

Provide facility members and patrons with safe and effective fitness classes tailored to a variety of individual fitness levels and needs.

Professionally represent the facility and Town of Oakville as a whole, including consistent implementation of regulations and protocols, assisting participants and providing technique correction, responding to incidents/emergencies, providing excellent customer service.

Make recommendations to the Recreation Coordinator based on observations and user feedback regarding types of fitness classes, operations, and policy.

Professionally correspond with the Recreation Staff, Facility Operators and Front Desk Staff to ensure the smooth operation of the facility and its program spaces, including maintenance and customer service concerns.

Evaluate and monitor fitness class participants to ensure program content and level of difficulty are appropriate and adjusted as needed.

Review program description in detail for classes you will be teaching to ensure planned content aligns with program expectation

Ensure safety of participants.

Provide excellent customer service including professional conduct and communication and creating a welcoming environment for participants.

Support and promote the HIGH FIVE Principles of Healthy Development.

Other duties as assigned.

Qualifications:

Fitness certifications must be obtained from a Nationally recognized organization.

Experience in a specialized area is an asset: Yoga, Pilates, Group Fitness, Strength Classes, Stability Ball, Kettle Bell, TRX, Bosu, Spin, Kickboxing, Zumba, Cross-Fit, Child/Youth Fitness, Older Adult Fitness and exercise rehabilitation.

HIGH FIVE Principles of Healthy Child Development (PHCD) certification is a requirement for leading Children’s Fitness Classes

Current Intermediate First Aid with CPR C (formerly Standard First Aid with CPR C) from a WSIB-approved provider, issued within the last three (3) years and valid throughout the term of employment.

Please note that this position requires a satisfactory

criminal record and vulnerable sector screening check

dated within the last 30 days as a condition of employment. Candidates who are under the age of 18 at the time of hire will only be required to provide a criminal record check.
