Job Responsibilities:

The Oakville Centre for the Performing Arts is a unique operation within the Town of Oakville, requiring an experienced person to provide technical support. The position reports to the Supervisor, Technical Services.

Operation and maintenance of backstage equipment including work as a stagehand in a safe and efficient manner for all events at the Oakville Centre. The backstage area of the theatre is a very sophisticated environment full of potential hazards and the requirement for this position to maintain a safe environment cannot be over emphasized.

This work includes primary duties as follows:

Audio Technician - Set up / Operation of DiGiCo SD consoles and other mixing consoles, install microphones, effects and dynamics processing, playback devices, amplifiers, and speaker systems for events at the Oakville Centre and other venues as necessary. Troubleshoot, operate and mix sound for various events including theatre, musical theatre, spoken word, amplified music concerts and dance; Set up, troubleshoot and operate video projection systems. Program and operate audio playback systems such as QLab for theatrical use.

Lighting Technician – Programming and operation of Grand MA lighting control systems, create effective lighting for dance, theatre, musical theatre and all types of concerts. Hang, circuit, colour and focus conventional and moving lights. Program and operate lighting consoles. Operate a follow spotlight. Install and program DMX based control systems. Trouble shooting and maintaining conventional, automated and LED stage fixtures.

Stage Technician - Set up scenery and staging for all types of event. Repair scenic elements, rig flying scenery, operate single purchase fly system and perform scene changes for all types of events.

Theatre equipment and building maintenance.

Limited supervision of events in the Oakville Centre Studio Theatre - Supervise clients and crew in this venue and oversee all technical aspects of those events.

Qualifications:

Two-year college diploma or university degree in Technical Theatre coupled with 3 years related experience in a road house environment. An equivalent combination of education and experience may be considered.

A passion for the performing arts is essential in this fast-paced environment, with background in technical theatre.

Familiarity with a live performance environment is critical.

Skills required are: Lighting design, light board operation (proficiency programming Grand MA consoles is required), audio engineer (proficiency with DiGiCo SD consoles is required), set construction, carpentry, rigging, stage management, training, working with community volunteer crews.

Requires: Scissor lift training, Working at Heights certification and first aid / CPR

Successful completion of the Natural Resources Canada – Pyrotechnic Card – Theatrical required within 12 months of hire

Proficiency in Microsoft Office including Excel, Word and PowerPoint is required.

Requires advanced problem solving skills, job estimation skills, resourcefulness and possession of the initiative to perform at a high standard with little supervision.

Proven communication, interpersonal and customer skills are critical. The ability to work as part of a team is critical.
