Why join us? The IESO is committed to ensuring Ontario’s electricity system meets the province’s needs today and into the future. We are charged with purpose , delivering reliable, affordable, and sustainable electricity for homes, businesses, and communities across the province. We are energized to grow , embracing opportunities to shape the future of a dynamic and evolving energy sector while advancing our careers. We are powered by teamwork , supported by diverse and passionate colleagues who foster respect, celebrate successes, and thrive on shared achievements. At the IESO, it’s not just what we do— it’s who we are! Who we are Our central role includes managing the provincial power grid in real-time, overseeing and evolving Ontario’s electricity market, engaging with government, municipalities and Indigenous communities, and planning for Ontario’s future electricity needs. Our employees play a key role in driving grid innovation, protecting the system from threats, advancing the province’s energy conservation programs, and forecasting and procuring the electricity resources we’ll need in the decades to come. This is a unionized position that falls within the Contracts and Compliance Division in the Legal Resources and Corporate Governance Business Unit. What makes this role exciting The energy sector is rapidly evolving, offering exciting new opportunities for those who want to make a difference. The Analyst, Contracts is accountable to the Supervisor, Contract Management in the Legal Resources and Corporate Governance (LRCG) division of the Independent Electricity System Operator (IESO). The Analyst, Contracts will primarily focus on contracts associated with renewable energy facilities (e.g., solar PV, wind, waterpower, etc.) and will provide administrative support for the management of large, complex contracts. The Analyst, Contracts will provide support to ensure that contract management requirements are fulfilled and that documents and processes are followed to provide the best possible value to the IESO, Ontario’s power system, and Ontario’s ratepayers and taxpayers. The role involves the administration of contract obligations, including compliance monitoring, reporting, contract management database testing, reporting, as well as customer service. What you’ll do In this role, you’ll make a meaningful impact by contributing to the IESO team and supporting the electricity needs of the province through: Support and administer contract processes, ensuring that all obligations are fulfilled in accordance with the contract requirements, culminating in successful commercial operation of facilities and post-commercial operation compliance. Regularly use and maintain online contract database to manage ongoing contractual matters and to ensure compliance. Support Senior Analysts with contract issues by meeting with IESO staff, respective contract counterparties, and when applicable, external agencies/organizations (e.g., Local Distribution Companies, transmitters, etc.) Assist in the development of standard principles, strategies and processes for existing contracts. Advise on systems and procedures and recommend changes to support the successful management of contracts. Assist with the development of standard documents/templates to be used by contract counterparties and make recommendations to improve internal processes and where absent develop the necessary tools. Support the development and testing of information systems associated with contracts and work flow processes. Reach out to contract counterparties to provide support in online contract database related tasks. Interface with others – internally with the Legal, Planning, Conservation, Resource Adequacy, Corporate Services, and other IESO Departments; externally with Government and its agencies, such as the Ministry of Energy, Northern Development and Mines, Ministry of the Environment, Conservation and Parks, Local Distribution Companies, as appropriate, and with respective contract counterparties. In a typical day, you will Conduct research on topics and issues related to electricity supply resources and renewable energy generation. Assist with projects within LRCG or in other IESO departments. This includes facilitating meetings with other IESO departments. Other duties or responsibilities may be assigned. Role Requirements Our team consists of experts from diverse backgrounds, each bringing their unique perspectives and skills. To succeed in this role, you’ll need: Education: Requires knowledge of business principles and electrical generation technologies. This knowledge is considered to be normally acquired through the successful completion of 4 years of University education + 2 years of work experience. Experience: Requires experience and knowledge of: business principles and processes successful execution of strategies translating business requirements planning, organizational and financial skills analysis and problem solving contracts, financial and information systems Ontario’s electricity sector including the renewable energy sector and regulatory environment technical aspects of electricity generation, including renewable forms of electricity generation (e.g. solar PV, wind, waterpower, etc.) working with contract counterparties in a challenging environment various generation contracts used by the IESO, including the rationale and importance for each, as well as the various provisions in contracts. public presentation experience is an asset working within a group and effectively coordinating with IESO staff and external stakeholders demonstrating excellent oral, written and presentation skills demonstrating a detail orientation and the ability to handle multiple priorities in a fast-paced, challenging environment The minimum level of experience for the incumbent to perform at a satisfactory level of proficiency is 2 years and above, including the period of adjustment on the job itself.
