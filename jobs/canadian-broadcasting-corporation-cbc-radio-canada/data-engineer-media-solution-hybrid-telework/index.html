Responsibilities:

Design, develop, and maintain robust data pipelines and ETL processes to ingest, transform, and store data in Microsoft Fabric.

Collaborate closely with stakeholders to understand business needs and translate them into scalable data solutions.

Ensure data quality, accuracy, and consistency by implementing rigorous validation and governance standards.

Optimize the performance and efficiency of the cloud data warehouse for analytics and reporting purposes.

Assist in the migration of existing data assets to Microsoft Fabric, ensuring a smooth transition with minimal disruption.

Troubleshoot and resolve data pipeline and infrastructure issues as they arise.

Stay informed about industry best practices, emerging technologies, and updates related to Data Warehousing.

What are we looking for?

Proven experience as a Data Engineer or similar role, with a strong back‐ground in designing and implementing cloud-based data solutions.

Proficiency in Microsoft Fabric (or experience with similar cloud data ware‐house platforms such as Azure Synapse or Snowflake).

Advanced SQL skills and hands-on experience with ETL frameworks and tools.

Solid understanding of data modeling, architecture, and integration techniques.

Strong problem-solving skills and the ability to work effectively in a collaborative team environment.

Excellent communication skills to articulate technical concepts to non-technical stakeholders.

A degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or a related field (or equivalent experience).

French is a strong asset
