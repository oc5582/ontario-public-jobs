CBC GEM is looking for an Administrative Specialist (one-year contract) to join our CBC GEM Merchandising team.

We work to connect audiences with the streaming series, movies and specials they love, and this role will contribute by building the CBC GEM schedule and in-service merchandising, including all metadata, for our content slate.

Reporting to the Manager, CBC Gem Merchandising, the Administrative Specialist will collaborate with Product teams, Communication teams, Business & Rights, Programming, Media Solutions and Media Presentation.

The successful candidate must be able to perform in an ever-changing media environment and contribute to timely solutions. The successful candidate will be a results-driven, detail-oriented problem solver, who can work independently in a content management system and thrives in a positive team environment.

While work must conform to CBC standard operating and administrative procedures, there is both opportunity and a requirement for independent judgment, analysis and decision-making within sets of complex parameters. Work is subject only to general review and there may be a requirement to coordinate the work of others.

Key Tasks:

Responsible for maintaining and inputting the schedules (daily/seasonally) for CBC GEM;

Perform daily service updates within the CBC GEM content management system;

Assist in daily deliverables for CBC GEM;

Coordinate with multiple departments to source relevant metadata;

Communicate and facilitate last-minute changes or updates with stakeholders;

Ensure video playback meets accessibility guidelines;

Coordinate the implementation of special programming initiatives;

Perform quality checks to ensure accurate scheduling information; ensure that technical issues are reported, documented, and corrected as needed

Responsible for troubleshooting issues that arise on CBC GEM;

Uses knowledge in scheduling streaming properties to provide expertise and make recommendations within the framework of CBC’s mandate, budget and regulatory requirements;

Become familiarized with CBC GEM’s programming slate;

Collaborate with peers in Media Presentation, Network Programming and Production to ensure technical issues are effectively resolved;

Collaborate on projects with the CBC GEM Product team, contributing to new system implementation and testing.

We are looking for a candidate with the following:

Post-secondary education in a related field or equivalent experience;

Minimum of three years’ experience in an environment with exposure to broadcasting, scheduling, and digital platforms;

Excellent communication skills including writing, verbal and listening;

Ability to handle multiple priorities and quick decision-making skills under pressure;

Ability to exercise judgment and work independently, taking initiative when required;

Ability to solve technical problems;

Experience with digital workflows and broadcast operations;

Experienced relationship builder and self-starter;

Excellent organizational skills and attention to detail, particularly related to numbers and technical terminology;

Strong planning, analytical, problem solving, and conflict resolution skills;

Ability to multitask when required; establish priorities amongst conflicting priorities and work effectively under pressure to meet tight deadlines;

Experience with online publishing systems and multi-platform distribution;

Familiarity with closed captioning and described video.

Candidates may be subject to skills and knowledge testing.
