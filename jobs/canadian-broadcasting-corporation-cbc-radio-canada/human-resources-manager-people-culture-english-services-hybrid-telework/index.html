This role is a hybrid work arrangement. Work schedule to be discussed with the Hiring Manager according to the guidelines defined by the department.

Your Role:

Reporting to the Senior Director, Human Resources, you will serve as a key member of the leadership team for English Services client groups such as Marketing, Communications & Brand, Public Affairs, Sports, Digital, Transformation, Media Solutions, and Operations. Your deep expertise and broad knowledge across all HR functional areas will be vital assets as you partner with client leadership to drive successful workforce strategies, leverage internal HR centers of expertise, and offer proactive insights, tools, and analytics to address key business and people challenges.

You will lead, manage, and coach a team of Senior HR Generalists responsible for providing HR guidance and advisory services across English Services. You will foster a solutions-oriented mindset within your team, encouraging creativity, adaptability, open-mindedness, and rapid, effective resolution of issues.

This is a permanent position based in Toronto.

Key Responsibilities:

Build strong stakeholder relationships and strategic partnerships within English Services

Lead the implementation of the people management strategy for English Services teams;

Provide expert HR guidance to senior leaders and play an integral role in operational planning;

Conduct HR and industrial relations (IR) investigations as needed;

Deploy and execute medium- and long-term HR strategies to address complex operational and corporate priorities;

Foster positive, productive union-management relations by maintaining effective working relationships with local and national union representatives (e.g., CMG, APS);

Work closely with your team members, overseeing their activities and developing their professional potential;

Participate in regional and national HR initiatives and strategic projects.

Qualifications:

Degree in Human Resources Management, Industrial Relations, or equivalent field (CHRP/CHRL professional designation preferred);

Minimum of 5 years of progressive HR experience in a complex matrix, unionized environment (preferably within media, broadcasting, or a federally regulated sector) In-depth understanding of federally regulated workplaces, industrial relations, and collective bargaining agreements;

Demonstrated commitment to advancing equity, diversity, accessibility, and inclusion across English Services;

Superior interpersonal and communication skills, with proven ability to build strong partnerships and influence stakeholders at all organizational levels;

Strong leadership and coaching abilities with a track record of motivating and developing teams;

Bilingualism in English and French is required, or a strong commitment to develop French language skills.

Candidates may be subject to skills and knowledge testing.
