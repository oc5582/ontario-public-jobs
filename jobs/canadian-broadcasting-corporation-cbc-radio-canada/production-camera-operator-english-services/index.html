​About the Role

​We are seeking an experienced per occasion Production Camera Operator to join our crew for upcoming multi-cam, live-to-tape, and live broadcast productions. This isn't a role for beginners, we need a true broadcast professional who speaks the language of a control room, thrives under the pressure of a live tally light, and can deliver flawless, dynamic shots on demand. If you have a proven track record delivering the gold-standard quality expected by major networks we want you on our roster.

​Key Responsibilities

● ​Live & Live-to-Tape Execution: Operate high-end broadcast cameras (F5500) in a fast-paced, multi-cam studio or remote environment.

● ​Direction on the Fly: Seamlessly execute complex framing, tracking, and composition changes based on real-time cues from the Director and Technical Director.

● ​Technical Readiness: Perform pre-show camera checks, align zoom/focus controls, manage framing, and troubleshoot basic camera configurations.

● Setup and Strike: Comfortable with running fiber, building different camera bodies, etc

​Required Skills & Qualifications

● ​Experience: Minimum 3-5+ years of dedicated experience in multi-camera broadcast environments (Studio or Mobile).

● ​Network Standards: Comprehensive understanding of the technical and aesthetic standards required by major national/international broadcasters.

● ​Communication: Exceptional intercom/headset discipline; ability to listen to director commands while executing smooth camera movements.

● ​Adaptability: Ability to work irregular hours, including early mornings, late nights, weekends, and holidays based on production schedules.

​

Preferred Specialties

​While we are seeking an exceptional all-around studio operator, heavy preference will be given to candidates with verified expertise in one or more of the following: Pedestal, Jimmy Jib & Steadicam.

​What We Offer

​​An experienced, inclusive, and collaborative crew culture.

​A high-end, professional technical environment with top-tier gear.

The opportunity to work on large-scale multi-camera productions for some of Canada’s most popular and widely viewed television shows.
