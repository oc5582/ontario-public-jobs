Who we are

Our central role includes managing the provincial power grid in real-time, overseeing and evolving Ontario’s electricity market, engaging with government, municipalities and Indigenous communities, and planning for Ontario’s future electricity needs. Our employees play a key role in driving grid innovation, protecting the system from threats, advancing the province’s energy conservation programs, and forecasting and procuring the electricity resources we’ll need in the decades to come.

This is a non-unionized position within our Power System Development (PSD) business unit, based at our Toronto office.

What makes this role exciting

The energy sector is rapidly evolving, offering exciting new opportunities for those who want to make a difference.

As a trusted partner to senior leadership, the Executive Assistant provides high-quality administrative and operational support that enables leaders to focus on strategic priorities and business objectives. This role supports Senior Directors by managing a broad range of executive support activities, including scheduling and logistics, correspondence, document preparation, meeting coordination, and project execution.

The Executive Assistant builds strong relationships across the organization, exercises sound judgment, and handles confidential information with a high degree of discretion. The ideal candidate is highly organized, adaptable, and resourceful, with the ability to anticipate needs, manage competing priorities, and contribute to the smooth and efficient operation of the leadership team.
