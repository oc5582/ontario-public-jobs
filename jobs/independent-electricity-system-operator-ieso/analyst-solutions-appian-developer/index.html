Who we are

Our central role includes managing the provincial power grid in real-time, overseeing and evolving Ontario’s electricity market, engaging with government, municipalities and Indigenous communities, and planning for Ontario’s future electricity needs. Our employees play a key role in driving grid innovation, protecting the system from threats, advancing the province’s energy conservation programs, and forecasting and procuring the electricity resources we’ll need in the decades to come.

This is a unionized position that falls within the Business Solutions Division in the Information and Technology Business Unit.

What makes this role exciting

The energy sector is rapidly evolving, offering exciting new opportunities for those who want to make a difference.

The Solutions Development team is responsible for developing and supporting software applications used by market participants and IESO staff. The Analyst Solutions position will perform several key roles such as application support & development, project management, and solution analysis.

The level of this role (Analyst OR Specialist) will depend on the candidate's experience, qualifications and capabilities.

What you’ll do

In this role, you’ll make a meaningful impact by contributing to the IESO team and supporting the electricity needs of the province through:

Assist/contribute to developing integrated computer-based information solutions for business and operations processes for the reliable power system operation and other administrative needs of the IESO-Administered Markets

Duties include performing analysis of customer requirements, recommending functional specifications, and preparing recommended information solutions (e.g., impacting areas of software, hardware, communications and business processes)

Contribute to the management and evolution of existing information systems and tools related to the market operation, supply, security, and settlement services related to the management of the power system, to allow for additional functionality, improved reliability/maintainability and problem correction

Assist/contribute to identifying opportunities for information solutions that will allow effective and economic benefits for customers

Address the overall solution regarding communications, data sources, software tools, integration with existing functions, user interfaces, algorithms, etc.

Assist in monitoring and assessing assigned projects to develop and expand information systems and tools

Assist in providing project management support to projects or teams involved in expanding and improving information systems and tools

Contribute to and carry out close customer relationships and be alert to identifying potential improvements to existing systems and propose new information system requirements

Contribute as directed to the development of strategic plans and standards

In a typical day, you will

Assist in developing peers and associates to advance their learning and facilitate problem-solving skills within their respective areas of expertise

Contribute to the accepted life cycle management methodology for Information Technology solutions

Contribute to the security, archive and audit requirements in designing and implementing information technology solutions while considering Information Technology architecture standards and guidelines

Perform other duties as required

Role Requirements

Our team consists of experts from diverse backgrounds, each bringing their unique perspectives and skills.

To succeed in this role, you’ll need:

Education:

Requires academic solid knowledge of computer science and associated subjects, including strong practical ability and demonstrated all aspects of the software development life cycle

This knowledge is usually acquired through the successful completion of four-year university education in the appropriate field/discipline or by having an equivalent education

Experience:

Requires demonstrated expertise in web application development using Appian, HTML and JavaScript.

Requires demonstrated expertise in data queries (i.e., Oracle, MS SQL Server and MySQL)

Experience in one or more of the following areas would be an asset:

Experience with design, code, test, debug and document enterprise software applications

Knowledge of operating systems, application servers, web servers, and web application architectural concepts

Strong background in logical data modelling

Experience with workflow and user interface design

Familiarity with software delivery methodologies such as Agile/Scrum

Experience with Appian BPM, low-code, and Web Services solutions

A period of over four years, up to and including six years, is considered necessary to gain this experience. The selected individual may be required to be on-call to support applications 24x7 periodically.
