Job description

Bring Your CPA and Forensic Accounting Expertise To Our Investigations Team

CPA Ontario is not like other organizations

We operate at a rare intersection, both regulator and membership body, responsible for upholding the integrity of the CPA designation while supporting more than 105,000 members and 19,000 students across the province. Our work sits between business and the public interest, between the evolving demands of industry and the standards that make trust possible.

Careers built here tend to look different because the work itself is different. You are not simply contributing to an organization. You are helping shape a profession that touches nearly every sector of the economy at a moment when expectations, technology, and the nature of work are all being rewritten.

About This Opportunity:

CPA Ontario is currently in search of a

Senior Investigator (Forensic Accountant)

who will be responsible for carrying out investigations required for the Professional Conduct Committee (“PCC”) and as appointed by Counsel to the PCC. this role, you will be appointed to lead investigations or assist in investigations with other Investigators. As a leader within the Enforcement group you will participate in and lead projects and initiatives.

To Be Successful You Need:

CPA designation in good standing, with successful completion of the Master of Forensic Accounting (MFAcc), Diploma in Investigative & Forensic Accounting (DIFA), or an equivalent forensic accounting credential.

10+ years of progressive forensic accounting and investigative experience, with a proven ability to independently lead complex investigations from planning through to final report completion.

Demonstrated experience conducting investigative interviews, gathering and analyzing evidence, preparing investigation reports, and providing expert testimony in legal or regulatory proceedings.

Strong understanding of public accounting practices, with the ability to apply professional accounting expertise, investigative techniques, and sound judgment to complex matters.

Exceptional critical thinking, analytical, and problem-solving skills, with the ability to assess risk, identify issues, and draw well-supported conclusions.

Proven ability to exercise significant professional judgment and carry out investigative responsibilities with professionalism, integrity, and due care.

Excellent written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to prepare clear reports, deliver presentations, build relationships, and influence stakeholders.

Strong organizational and project management skills, with the ability to lead multiple investigations simultaneously while managing competing priorities and deadlines.

Proficiency with Microsoft Office applications, including Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Teams, and SharePoint, as well as experience using e-discovery and investigation management tools.

Demonstrated ability to work collaboratively across teams, contribute to departmental initiatives, and support broader organizational objectives.

What You’ll Be Doing:

Lead complex investigations into complaints against members, including evidence gathering, witness interviews, investigative planning, and report preparation.

Develop investigation strategies, manage case budgets, and collaborate with subject matter experts on specialized and high-profile matters.

Prepare evidentiary briefs and investigation reports, presenting findings and recommendations to decision-making committees.

Provide litigation support by assisting with allegation development, hearing preparation, and serving as a witness in disciplinary proceedings.

Mentor and guide investigators and support staff, fostering investigative excellence in planning, interviewing, issue resolution, and report writing.

Supervise, coach, and delegate work to Investigation Specialists and Coordinators to support effective case management and team development.

Contribute to departmental projects, strategic initiatives, training programs, and continuous improvement efforts.

Develop and deliver training, presentations, orientation sessions, and resource materials for internal and external stakeholders.

Participate in departmental planning, process enhancements, and cross-functional initiatives to strengthen investigative practices and outcomes.

The starting salary for this position is $162,100 per annum. The salary offered will be based on your qualifications, experience, and internal parity. Exceptions may be considered with further review.
