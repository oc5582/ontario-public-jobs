Make a difference

The Manager, Energy Management is responsible for developing and maintaining the enterprise energy management systems and programs to support continuous energy efficient operations. This role manages multi-year energy retrofit programs and energy efficiency functions related to engineered building systems.

What you’ll do

Leadership

Develops business and work plans for the designated programs to support TCHC corporate plans, direction and vision. Leads, manages and mentors a diverse group of vendors, support and service staff. Assists the Director to develop a mentorship program in the designated discipline incorporating TCHC’s succession plan. Identifies the individual(s) for training and development in future leadership roles. Recommends, develops and implements performance management plans. Establishes relationships and partnerships with staff, service providers, stakeholders and clients to ensure effective communication. Provides vision and direction towards making the TCHC portfolio smarter and greener by incorporating the “triple bottom line” considerations of economic, environmental and social sustainability. Recommends and develops plans to help TCHC meet the corporate Green Plan target to reduce 40% of GHG emissions.

Financial Accountability

Recommends to Director SBEM strategies to plan and manage the program budgets including the recommendations for planning energy programs using various analysis and forecasting tools. Provides regular monthly and annual program budget forecasts for services and expenditures to the Senior Management Team.

Planning and Delivery (Energy Programs)

Forecasts resource requirements and recommends a flexible model including in house and market resources to manage the cyclical needs of TCHC. Develops streamlined business processes to manage budgets, schedules and mitigate risk. Provides technical advice, updated knowledge of new regulations, best practices and innovative and industry leading solutions to support effective and efficient program delivery. Leads complex energy and capital tenders through sound estimating, quoting and bidding practices, including calls for Tenders, RFQ’s & RFP’s and prequalification tenders. Develops and recommends tender/scope strategies, specifications, to meet TCHC. standards and needs. Evaluates tenders through procurement process on cost, capacity, performance and deliverables. Manages energy partners by developing service-level, operating-level and partnership agreements. Leads and resolves complex contract management and service issues to successful outcomes. Works closely with the Facilities Team (Planning/Engineering) to develop and recommend corporate standards through market research and industry standards. Participates in capital planning and budgeting priorities with stakeholders.

Reporting and Performance Metrics

Contributes to the corporate development of metrics and measurement tools for energy performance. Recommends, develops and implements the Key Performance Indicators as required to effectively monitor and improve energy delivery. Contributes to senior management reports in regards to areas of responsibility. Assists in the compilation and updating of organizational energy management plan and sustainability.

What you’ll need

Professional certification or post-graduate degree in Engineering, Data Analysis, Building Science or equivalent. Certified Energy Manager, Certified Energy Auditor, Certified Business Energy Professional or Certified Measurement & Verification Personnel designation would be an asset. Minimum of 8 years of experience in energy systems management. Minimum of 6 years’ experience in building energy management, building automation, financial analysis or equivalent.

Knowledge of enterprise energy information management, building automation, energy auditing, continuous commissioning, measurement & verification, new and emerging energy and green building practices and technologies. Experience with energy analysis software (Faser, Gridlogix, Metrix, EnergyCAP) Full G Driver’s License and access to a vehicle
