How you’ll make an impact Be part of a team that delivers data-driven insights to support business strategy . As a Specialist, Financial Strategy and Performance, you’ll use your management accounting expertise to transform complex financial and business data into clear, actionable insights that support business decisions and improve performance. Working closely with cross-functional stakeholders – including business units, leadership and data teams – y ou’ll help stakeholders understand trends, identify opportunities and make informed, data-driven decisions. We’re looking for a collaborative analytical thinker who can translate complex financial concepts into executive insights and actionable recommendations. Someone who’s a natural relationship-builder comfortable engaging with diverse stakeholders – from technical discussions with data teams to strategic conversations with business leaders. If you thrive in a fast-paced environment, enjoy solving complex problems and are passionate about turning data into insights that influence decisions, this could be the role for you.
