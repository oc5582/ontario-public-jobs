Job description

Lifeguards ensure the safety of swimmers, guests, and facility users by actively monitoring aquatic activities and responding to emergencies as required. Lifeguards maintain a safe, clean, and welcoming aquatic environment while following Municipal procedures, rules and regulations, Lifesaving Society standards, and providing excellent customer service. Lifeguard positions are hired seasonally for the Winter, Spring, Summer, and Fall sessions, and employees are expected to commit to working the full duration of the season for which they are hired.

Minimum qualifications for Lifeguard positions:

Must be at least 15 years of age.

Must hold a current

Lifesaving Society National Lifeguard – Pool

certification.

Must hold a current

Standard First Aid with CPR-C

certification.

Must maintain all required certifications in good standing throughout employment.

Strong communication, teamwork, and customer service skills.

Ability to respond quickly and effectively in emergency situations.
