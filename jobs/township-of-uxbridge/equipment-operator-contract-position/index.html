Job description

DEPARTMENT:

Public Works, Parks and Trails Department

JOB TITLE:

Equipment Operator – Contract Position

AFFILIATION:

Union Position

TERM:

Full-time, 5-month contract

HOURS OF WORK:

8 hours/day, 40 hours/week, evenings, weekends and holidays are required

HOURLY WAGE:

Probation Rate $34.06

3 Month Rate $35.06

15 Month Rate $36.06

The Township of Uxbridge is seeking an energetic person to fulfill a contract position for winter maintenance in the Public Works Department for the period of November 2, 2026, to March 31, 2027.

Qualified and interested candidates should submit an electronic resume and cover letter marked “Contract Position” to Dave Magee, Roads Operation Manager at dmagee@uxbridge.ca no later than

October 26th, 2026, at 2:00pm.

We thank all those who apply, however only those candidates selected for an interview will be contacted.

The Township will consider cases requiring accommodation as stipulated by relevant employment standards legislation or regulation and/or the Ontario Human Rights Code.

The Township is an Equal Opportunity Employer in accordance with the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act, 2005 and the Ontario Human Rights Code (OHRC). The Township of Uxbridge will provide accommodations throughout the recruitment and selection and/or assessment process to applicants with disabilities and/or needs related to the OHRC. Personal information provided is collected under the authority of The Municipal Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act.

WINTER OPERATIONS:

Operate tandem dump trucks with plows and sanders for snow clearing and road treatment.

Perform snow plowing, sanding, and salting in accordance with municipal winter maintenance standards.

Respond to emergency weather events during evenings, weekends, and holidays as required.

Maintain clear records of operations and materials used.

Assist in the construction and maintenance of roads including:

o Asphalt patching

o Roadside brushing

o Haul and deliver construction materials such as gravel, asphalt, and equipment to job sites

o Other duties as assigned

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS:

Valid DZ Driver's License.

Driver’s Abstract dated within three (3) months of the application date must be submitted with resume.

Grade 12 Ontario Secondary School Diploma (OSSD) or equivalent.

The successful candidate will be required to provide a Criminal Record Check as a condition of employment. This document is only required following a conditional offer of employment.

EXPERIENCE AND ASSETS:

Experience operating tandem trucks for snow plowing and sanding operations.

Experience operating heavy equipment is considered an asset.

A valid AZ Driver’s Licence is considered an asset.
