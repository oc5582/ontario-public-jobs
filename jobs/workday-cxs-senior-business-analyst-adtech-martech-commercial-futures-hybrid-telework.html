ABOUT THE ROLE

Are you deeply curious about how the media and advertising ecosystem really works under the hood? We are seeking a high-caliber early-career analyst (fresh graduate in Business Management, Technology, or Futuresight-related disciplines) who possesses relentless intellectual curiosity and an appetite to explore the complex intersections that others overlook.

In this role, you will analyze the media ecosystem end-to-end—spanning commercial advertising models, digital distribution mechanics, subscription economics, and emerging tech stacks. You will be expected to thrive in uncertainty, challenge assumptions, and produce rigorous, evidence-backed insights to guide executive decision-making.

KEY ACCOUNTABILITIES & RESPONSIBILITIES

Ecosystem & Vendor Intelligence:

Deliver comprehensive vendor dossiers and category analyses within tight timelines, evaluating category fit, integration feasibility, exit costs, vendor viability, and clear decision confidence levels.

End-to-End Media Economics Research:

Map and analyze the overlapping dependencies between commercial ad mechanics, audience measurement, first-party data strategies, social tech platforms, and subscription revenue streams.

Rigorous Pilot & Hypothesis Testing:

Pre-register every pilot with clear hypotheses, success metrics, stop conditions, and market-as-control measurement designs before execution. Ensure all test results produce actionable, decision-grade findings rather than anecdotal summaries.

Governance & Constraint Screening:

Rigorously screen all technical and strategic recommendations against procurement, legal, privacy, and regulatory boundaries before presenting to leadership forums.

Competitive & Supply Chain Tracking:

Monitor industry shifts including programmatic supply chain developments, unified sales platforms, broadcaster consolidation, and platform-side API/policy changes.

Quantified Impact:

Track, validate, and own the quantification of vendor spend avoided and value unlocked through strategic analysis.

SKILLS & QUALIFICATIONS SOUGHT

Education:

Bachelor's degree in Business Management, Business Analytics, Technology Information Systems, Innovation Strategy, or a related field (Recent Graduates encouraged).

Structured Research & Synthesis:

Exceptional writing and analytical skills with a disciplined focus on writing for executive decision-making rather than word count.

Exceptional Technical Documentation:

Expertise in translating complex technical parameters and multi-dimensional tech transformations into clear, visual, and highly structured business summaries (such as matrices and process maps).

Skeptical & Evidence-Driven Mindset:

Skeptical by disposition; naturally treats vendor claims and market hype as unproven hypotheses requiring rigorous empirical proof.

Comfort with Uncertainty & Tech Stacks:

Eager to navigate complex, multi-layered environments involving programmatic infrastructure, log-level ad data, SSP/ad server reporting, and data clean rooms.

Financial & Statistical Literacy:

Capability to build financial models sufficient to size revenue opportunities and integration costs, and statistical literacy to distinguish actual signal from noise.

Uncompromising Integrity:

Willingness to state uncertainty explicitly and report negative or failing pilot results clearly without softening outcomes.

WHY JOIN US?

This position offers a rare launchpad for a forward-thinking graduate to work at the intersection of business strategy, commercial tech, and future market dynamics. You will have direct exposure to executive decision-makers (including C-level leadership) and the autonomy to investigate complex industry challenges end-to-end.
