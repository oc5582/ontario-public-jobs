Your mandate:

Media Solutions Finance (finance team supporting the sales department) is looking for an Analyst, Budgeting and Internal Reports, to support month-end processes and key operational needs, including monthly revenue analysis and royalty payments.

Reporting to the Senior manager, Budgeting and Internal Reporting, the analyst will be a member of a finance team closely involved in sales and content marketing strategies across CBC/Radio-Canada. This position is temporary and is based in Toronto.

Activities:

Obtain a good understanding of our existing day-to-day and month-end closing processes to ensure data accuracy and support special projects;

Support budget planning by coordinating administrative activities with key stakeholders and performing in-depth data analyses and reconciliation;

Participate, with the help of the Senior manager, to optimize and harmonize operational month-end processes;

Support the internal business client with their administrative needs;

Calculate and process producer royalties while acting as the main financial liaison for producer contacts;

Perform other related tasks or projects as needed.

Required profile:

University degree in accounting or equivalent; between three to five years of relevant experience

CPA accounting title or working towards the same is considered a strong asset;

Advanced skills in the Microsoft Office suite (Excel, PowerPoint, Word) and practical experience with Power BI;

Ability to manipulate, analyze and interpret a large volume of data for stakeholders not specialized in finance in the Media Solutions sector;

A keen eye for detail, autonomous and proactive;

Demonstrates ability to independently resolve problems and provide practical solutions to management;

Ability to build and maintain strong relationships with colleagues, partners, and members of management;

Ability to work with various systems, tools and software simultaneously and quickly adapt to new tools and technologies;

Desire to improve the efficiency of existing processes and contribute to maximizing revenues through the analysis and use of appropriate financial measurement instruments;

Fluency in French and English (spoken and written), a major asset.

Candidates may be subject to skills and knowledge testing.
