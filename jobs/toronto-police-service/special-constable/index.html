The Toronto Police Service is looking for a candidate that shares our core values:

Service at our Core Do the right thing Connect with Compassion Reflect and Grow

The Toronto Police Service is the fourth largest municipal police service in North America. With over 5,000 officers and approximately 2,500 civilian employees, we are dedicated to delivering best-in-class police services, in partnership with our communities, by being where the public needs the Service the most, by embracing partnerships to create safe communities, and by focusing on the needs of the City.

We are hiring Special Constables:

The Toronto Police Service is now accepting applications for anticipated 2027 Special Constable hiring requirements. We are seeking dedicated, community-focused individuals who are committed to promoting safety, professionalism, and public trust across the City of Toronto.

About the Role:

Special Constables play an essential role in supporting public safety and enhancing community well-being. This position requires strong communication skills, sound judgement, and the ability to remain calm, effective, and respectful in fast-paced and dynamic environments.

What We re Looking For

We invite applicants who demonstrate:

Excellent interpersonal and communication skills

The ability to multi-task and remain composed under pressure

Professionalism, maturity, and integrity in all interactions

Strong decision-making skills and sound judgement

A commitment to fairness, respect, and community service

Motivation, reliability, and the ability to work independently

Why Join Us?

This is an excellent opportunity to build a meaningful career in law enforcement while contributing to the safety and well-being of Toronto s diverse communities. As a Special Constable, you will gain valuable experience, training, and development within one of Canada s largest and most respected police services.

What can I expect to do in this role?

As a Special Constable, you may be required to rotate in any of the following positions throughout your career:

Assigned to Court Services

, you will be responsible for but not limited to:

Maintaining security and control of persons in custody who must attend court

Transporting prisoners to and from detention centres, immigration facilities, medical facilities, hospitals, police divisions, court houses, arrest locations or other detention locations

Controlling, monitoring and escorting persons lodged in holding cells and escorting them to court

Maintaining security and order in courtrooms

Monitoring courthouse entrances via electronic screening

Executing arrests when required

Obtaining DNA samples from persons in and out of custody, as ordered by the court

Attending court and testifying as required

Assigned to the booking area

of a division, you will be responsible for but not limited to:

Booking of prisoners, including searching, fingerprinting and photographing persons in custody

Maintain the security and wellbeing of prisoners in custody

Liaise and communicate with various stakeholders throughout the division and the Service regarding prisoner status and management

Manage persons in emotional crisis and physical distress, using available resources such as MCIT, Emergency Medical Services or arranging transport to a hospital, as appropriate

Attending court and testifying as required

Assigned to a division

, you will be responsible for but not limited to:

Report writing, evidence collecting, canvassing and assisting in search of missing persons.

Enforcement of some City of Toronto parking by-laws

Attending and assisting in protecting secured crime scenes under on-scene police constables or supervisors

Maintaining a notebook containing an accurate and thorough account of up-to-date- activities

Prisoner transportation, prisoner security and prisoner management

Attending court and testifying as required

How do I qualify?

Minimum Requirements:

Successful completion of Grade 12 (OSSD or equivalent)

Have a minimum of one year of related experience (i.e. working with the general public or working in a customer service environment)

Must be Canadian Citizen or Permanent Resident

License and Certification Requirements:

Possess valid "G" driver's license (upon submission of application) with no more than six demerit points, permitting you to drive an automobile in Ontario with full driving privileges

Current certification in Intermediate First Aid (formerly known as Standard First Aid) from an accredited agency by the time an offer of employment is given.

Possess a valid O.A.C.P. (Ontario Association of Chiefs of Police Certificate) certificate.

Have a minimum of 20/40 (uncorrected) vision, with normal colour acuity.

Successfully pass the hearing and vision standards as outlined by the O.A.C.P.

Successfully complete the PREP (Shuttle Run: Stage 6 & Pursuit Restraint Circuit within 3 minutes)

For further information on obtaining an OACP Certificate of Results and/or testing, visit:

https://oacpcertificate.ca

Please Note: If selected to move forward, applicants will be invited to attend the PREP test at the Toronto Police College.

Skills/Competencies:

Ability to deal effectively with persons in crisis and to work in a stressful environment that requires problem solving, judgement, conflict resolution, communication, teamwork and co-operation

Possess strong interpersonal and communication skills in order to deal with members of the Service, persons in custody and the public in a professional and tactful manner

Motivated self-starter, able to work with minimal supervision

Must be able to endure prolonged physical activity associated to rigorous defensive tactics training, on a daily basis

Conditions of Employment:

Must successfully pass the required Toronto Police Service driving test to operate a police vehicle (TPS Blue Card)

Deemed medically fit for the position through a medical assessment

Meet all requirements to attain the status of a Special Constable as outlined by the Ministry

Successfully complete the Special Constable Training Program that consists of:

15 week training program at TPC (70 Birmingham Street)

Monday to Friday 7am - 3pm (may also include evenings and weekends)

Probation Period:

6 months
