The Ontario Securities Commission (OSC) is the statutory body responsible for regulating Ontario’s capital markets in accordance with the mandate established in the provincial Securities Act and the Commodity Futures Act. The mandate of the OSC is to provide protection to investors from unfair, improper or fraudulent practices, to foster fair, efficient and competitive capital markets and confidence in the capital markets, to foster capital formation, and to contribute to the stability of the financial system and the reduction of systemic risk. This mandate is performed through policy, operational, and enforcement activities. The OSC also contributes to national and global securities regulation development.

We offer a diverse, fair, and flexible work environment and take pride in our challenging and rewarding work.

The

Investment Management Division

oversees the regulation of investment funds and structured products that offer securities to the public in Ontario. Our function in the OSC’s mandate of providing investors protection is a vital one. We have a leadership role in developing new rules and policies within the OSC and within the Canadian Securities Administrators (CSA) to adapt to the changing environment in the investment fund and structured products industry. Our scope of activity includes reviewing and considering investor protection issues related to the offerings of investment products (including mutual funds, exchange traded funds, structured products, scholarship plans and labour-sponsored funds); assessing product disclosure, including prospectus and continuous disclosure filings; and considering applications for discretionary relief from Ontario securities legislation and rules.

The

Business and Market Data Analyst

position will involve playing a key role in profiling the activities of the Investment Management Division by reporting on the success and impact of Division initiatives and core work processes. The Divisions core processes include rule and policy consultation, prospectus and exemptive relief application reviews and issue-oriented reviews of important regulatory development. The Business and Market Data Analyst’s primary function will be to support the Division’s core processes by conducting research, collecting and interpreting market data and activities and supporting special policy projects the Division undertakes (i.e. Risk Methodologies, and Regulatory burden-reduction initiatives).

Reporting to the Manager, Analytics, the

Business and Market Data Analyst

will be instrumental in developing, maintaining and analyzing key financial data to measure, and report on, internally and for publication (i.e. quarterly and annual reports).

Key Duties and Responsibilities:

Market Research for Division Core Processes

Provide research, including conducting background research on topics relating to investment activities in the funds industry, novel investment products, capital market development, and investor protection concerns, as requested.

With division staff identify, plan and conduct qualitative and quantitative market research to inform on mutual funds industry trends which impact on core Division processes and decisions, including prospectus, application and continuous disclosure reviews.

Conduct analysis of investment products’ attributes and risks using internally developed analytical datasets and frameworks.

Monitor, assess and interpret trends in the investment funds and structured products markets, both in Canada and globally, and prepare and design requirements on these trends.

Proactively identify additional market data and information to collect, maintain and analyze which is conducive to the efficient and effective operation of Investment Management Division.

Identify problems whereby efficiencies can be achieved, research and evaluate various alternatives and proceed with the most efficient solution.

Assess data quality, completeness, and limitations, and document analytical assumptions and methodologies. Present research findings to the Division management team, senior staff, policy project leads and/or committees (i.e. CSA) and address questions and/or defend analysis.

Data Collection, Preparation and Analysis

Work with other divisions to identify, evaluate, access, organize, clean, validate, and maintain internal and external data sources required to support Division research, analysis, and reporting.

Maintain analytical datasets and models to support market monitoring, operational oversight, and risk‑based analysis.

With division staff interpret information related to Division operations, analyze market data, and present and report issues, findings and trends that impact on the work of the Division.

Reporting, Dashboards, and Performance Measurement

Develop, maintain dashboards and report, using Division-wide operational performance measures, to better reflect how the Division is meeting its mandate (i.e. for use in quarterly reports).

Analyze trends, anomalies, and emerging risks in market and operational data and surface issues that may require supervisory, policy, or operational action.

Enterprise Systems and Data Access

Leverage enterprise systems and platforms to extract and use data required for Division analysis and reporting.

Coordinate with enterprise system and digital teams as required to ensure appropriate data access and alignment with organizational standards.

Skills & Qualifications:

Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills, with the ability to collect, organize, and translate data into clear insights.

Knowledge of financial markets and investment products, including mutual funds and ETFs, with the ability to analyze market trends and interpret data.

Excellent presentation, problem-solving and strategic thinking skills.

Clear and concise verbal and written communication skills.

Demonstrated tact and sensitivity when negotiating with diverse parties.

Ability to interact professionally with management and professionals at various levels.

Demonstrated ability to respond to ad hoc unstructured problems with planning, flexibility, communication and execution.

Demonstrated ability to conduct research and prepare dashboards.

Ability to work with minimal direction and prioritize and meet strict deadlines.

Knowledge or experience in the securities industry.

Understanding of investment funds regulation and funds’ operations.

Education & Training:

University degree in Business, Finance or a related discipline.

Experience working with data analysis and visualization skills with, data science tools such as Microsoft Power BI, Tableau, Python and SQL for data preparation, automation and analytical initiatives.

A minimum of 3-5 years of intermediate-level experience in the financial and/or regulatory sector, performing a similar function. Equivalent combinations of education, experience, and demonstrated competencies will be considered.

Understanding of the Canadian securities regulatory environment, with the capability of assessing the impact of various regulatory actions on stakeholders and the capital markets.

Basic knowledge of the Ontario Securities Act, regulations, policy statements, rules and internal operating procedures.

High proficiency in the MS Office suite of products.

Completion of the Canadian Securities Course, an asset.

This opportunity is considered to be a business-critical role supporting the Investment Management Division.
