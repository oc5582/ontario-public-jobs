POSITION DUTIES: Responsible for the management of information throughout its lifecycle including electronic applications, systems and physical documents. Reviews documents (hard copy and digital) sent for retention to ensure compliance with records planning and applicable by-laws and determines classification Updates the electronic records management system with incoming documents and information ensuring quality control of details, conventions, codes Creates, organizes, scans, indexes and classifies hard copy and digital records Performs imagining service for archival and access to information request purposes Assits in annual destruction undertaking Moves documents into physical storage and /or ensures digital copies are filed electronically and coordinates with vendors for transfer of information, records and supplies Files digital copies of documents Repackage and relabels damaged storage boxes Provides technical advice to City employees related to records management processes and supporting technology and applications and to coordinate file transfers and management Respond to external document requests from citizens and businesses Receives requests for documents and undertakes searches / research and provides appropriate materials Posts content to websites Assist with municipal elections Provides support to divisions with information management projects Drafts standard operating procedures and identifies process improvements opportunities Compiles statistics on inventories and day-to-day operations of the information holdings Selection Criteria Education: Minimum: completion of a certificate in records or information management Experience: Minimum of one year of previous experience in a records management role Other Skills and Assets: Records management practices and legislation and the specific policies, bylaw and procedures for the City Job-related office productivity software, applications and databases, and technology devices There is an expectation that the incumbent will remain current on relevant procedures, legislation and policy, both provincial and for the City of Brampton Provides customer service and technical advice to employees on records management and to coordinate services Receives requests from external and internal parties for documents and information Liaises with vendors to order products and schedule services Additional Information Interview: Our recruitment process may be completed with video conference technology. ** Various tests and/or exams may be administered as part of the selection criteria.
