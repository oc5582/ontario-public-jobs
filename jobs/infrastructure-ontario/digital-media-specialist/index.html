Job description

Infrastructure Ontario (IO), an Ontario crown corporation, provides a wide range of services to support the Ontario government’s initiative to modernize and maximize the value of public infrastructure and realty. IO has been recognized domestically and internationally for the way it does business and the success it has had with its major projects, real estate services, infrastructure lending, commercial projects, and development mandate. IO delivers some of the most complex, ambitious commercial and land development solutions for the Province and continues to evolve and expand its capabilities to maximize its public impact and deliver on its vision of creating a connected, modern and competitive Ontario.

Position Purpose:

The Digital Media Specialist is responsible for creating high-quality digital and creative products for IO’s internal and external audiences, including graphic design, multimedia, video, photography, web and social media content. Reporting to the Sr. Manager, the role works collaboratively with the Design Lead to apply IO’s brand and creative standards. The role manages the end-to-end delivery of assigned creative projects, from concept and planning through production and post-production. The role also creates and maintains website and intranet content using IO’s content management systems, working within established templates, components and design systems while applying UX/UI, accessibility, French language and visual identity requirements.

What You Will Do:

Design high-quality visual assets for IO’s digital channels including website and social media, digital campaigns, corporate reports presentations and print materials.

Develop video, photography, motion graphics and other multimedia content for IO’s digital channels and communications initiatives.

Collaborate with stakeholders to translate business objectives into compelling digital and visual communications.

Support the ongoing maintenance and development of content for IO’s external website and intranet, including publishing content, creating graphics and multimedia assets, and maintaining visual consistency.

Support digital campaigns by developing creative assets and monitoring engagement and performance metrics.

Manage, organize, and update photography, video, and other digital assets within established content management systems and processes.

Coordinate external vendors and creative service providers as required.

Identify opportunities to enhance digital communications, user experience, and content effectiveness through continuous improvement and emerging technologies.

Who You Are:

Diploma or degree in Digital Media, Graphic Design, Communications, Marketing, Web Development, or a related discipline is considered an asset.

Several years of experience producing and managing content across multiple digital communications disciplines, including graphic design, web, social media, video, photography, and multimedia communications.

Demonstrated experience developing and delivering integrated digital content and creative assets for a variety of platforms and audiences.

Proficiency with creative design and multimedia production software, content management systems (CMS), and digital publishing tools.

Strong understanding of digital communications, user experience (UX), web accessibility standards, and content development best practices.

Excellent communication, project management, and stakeholder relationship management skills.

What We Offer:

Commitment to creating a diverse, equitable and inclusive culture that promotes a sense of belonging and represents and reflects the needs of the communities we serve.

A Defined Contribution Pension Plan, with Infrastructure Ontario contributing the equivalent of 5% of a full-time employee’s annual salary and will also match any employee contribution up to an additional 5% of their annual salary. To provide further options for planning and saving for the future, IO offers a Group Retirement Savings Plan and Group Tax Free Savings Account to eligible employees.

A comprehensive package that covers health, dental, vision, out-of-country care, paramedical services, and more.

A pregnancy and parental leave program that offers expecting, eligible employees a top-up to 95% of their annual base salary for 31 weeks, and our parental leave program offers eligible employees a top-up to 95% of their annual base salary for 15 weeks.

Access to our $1000 Healthcare Spending Account to top up expenses not covered in the benefits program and a Lifestyle Account that expands the eligibility of health and wellness options and can include coverage for gym memberships and fitness equipment, nutrition counselling or financial planning.

Support for professional development opportunities for all colleagues through a broad range of learning programs that include in-person and online training, leadership development, and support for colleagues’ well-being. IO will also cover the costs associated with up to two membership and licence fees per year for eligible colleagues if directly related to the employee’s role.

Access to a suite of virtual healthcare options to support non-urgent medical needs

A convenient, central location at 1 Dundas St West, easily accessible by public transit.
