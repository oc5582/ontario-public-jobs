Join a team shaping Canada’s venture capital ecosystem

At BDC, the Government Funds team manages capital on behalf of the Government of Canada to strengthen and grow Canada’s venture capital market. This includes deploying

$350M under the Venture Capital Action Plan (VCAP)

,

$450M under the Venture Capital Catalyst Initiative (VCCI)

, and

$450M under the Renewed Venture Capital Catalyst Initiative (Renewed VCCI)

. These programs totalling $1.2B AUM are designed to

crowd in private capital

and support fund managers across the Canadian venture capital asset class.

Building on this foundation, BDC is now deploying the

$1B Growth Venture Capital Catalyst Initiative (Growth VCCI)

, a landmark platform focused on scaling Canada’s most promising funds and innovative companies.

This is a unique opportunity to work at the intersection of

investment, policy, and ecosystem development

, with direct exposure to the most prominent Canadian fund managers while contributing to a

national priority initiative shaping Canada’s innovation economy

.

POSITION OVERVIEW

As Director, you will combine leadership, investment expertise, and complex project management to support the deployment and oversight of government-backed venture capital programs, including VCAP, VCCI, Renewed VCCI, and upcoming Growth VCCI.

You will play a lead role in investment execution, stakeholder management, and portfolio governance, while engaging closely with fund managers, institutional partners, and internal stakeholders including Government.

CHALLENGES TO BE MET

Investment Expertise

Lead complex business and legal negotiations with fund managers and external stakeholders for execution of program delivery including fund investments.

Provide strategic oversight of investment activities across the portfolio, ensuring alignment between policy objectives, portfolio construction considerations, and market dynamics

Provide senior investment judgment and guidance on fund performance, risk management, and portfolio development matters

Fund Oversight & Governance

Serve as BDC’s representative on portfolio funds’ LPACs and other governance bodies

Monitor fund performance, governance, and compliance with fund agreements and program requirements

Provide strategic insight and constructive challenge to support strong governance practices and enhance fund outcomes

Guide fund managers on governance and operational best practices to strengthen the long-term sustainability of the venture capital ecosystem

Leadership and Team Management

Demonstrate leadership in team meetings and operations processes, ensuring effective resource allocation, quality control, operational excellence, and alignment with strategic priorities

Ensure the timely and accurate execution of operational activities, including capital calls, distributions, reporting, and stakeholder deliverables

Provide coaching, mentorship, and leadership to a team of Analysts, fostering a collaborative, high-performance culture focused on continuous improvement and professional development

Identify capability and capacity gaps, oversee recruitment and onboarding activities, and promote knowledge sharing and best practices across the team

Program Delivery & Stakeholder Management

Provide strategic input into the design, implementation, and continuous improvement of current and future government investment programs

Respond to complex and time-sensitive requests from internal and external stakeholders while maintaining a high standard of service and professionalism

Oversee the preparation and dissemination of quarterly and annual reporting, performance dashboards, and stakeholder communications

Ensure high standards of quality, consistency, accuracy, and timeliness across all reporting outputs and program deliverables

Portfolio & Ecosystem Leadership

Build and maintain strong relationships with fund managers, institutional investors, ecosystem stakeholders, and government partners

Represent BDC Capital and Government Funds at industry conferences, panels, events, and stakeholder forums

Stay closely connected to venture capital trends, market developments, and emerging opportunities to support strategic decision-making

Support and lead thought leadership initiatives, market studies, and research projects that generate insights from government investment programs and portfolio activities

WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

Experience and qualifications

Minimum of a Bachelor degree in Commerce or a related discipline (finance, accounting, law or business administration preferred), or an equivalent combination of academic training and relevant professional experience

10 years+ experience in venture capital, private equity, or growth investing, with direct exposure to deal-making at the fund level as well as investment underwriting, structuring, business and legal negotiations and execution

Demonstrated expertise in venture capital fund structures, LPAs, governance frameworks, and policy-driven investment programs

Demonstrated ability to lead complex negotiations and manage multiple high-priority initiatives simultaneously

Significant experience serving on LPACs, Boards of Directors, or equivalent governance bodies

Ability to manage complex stakeholder relationships involving fund managers, institutional investors, government representatives, and senior executives

Strong understanding of the Canadian venture capital ecosystem and a well-established network of industry relationships

Experience leading, coaching, and developing high-performing teams

Core competencies

Strong strategic and investment judgment

Demonstrated leadership and people management capabilities

Excellent communication, influencing, and stakeholder management skills

Ability to align operational execution with broader policy, program, and organizational objectives

High level of rigour, organization, and attention to detail

Ability to operate effectively in a complex, fast-paced, multi-stakeholder environment

Collaborative mindset with a commitment to team development, knowledge sharing, and excellence

The position is primarily based in Montreal or Ottawa, with a preference for these locations. Toronto is also acceptable.
