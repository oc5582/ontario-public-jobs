Job description

Restaurant Host

Regular Part-Time

(Up to 24 hours per week; must be available to work weekdays, evenings, weekends and holidays. Wednesday, Friday, and Sunday availability is prioritized.)

ART + AUDIENCE + LEARNING

Located in Toronto, the Art Gallery of Ontario is one of the largest art museums in North America, attracting approximately one million visitors annually. The AGO Collection of more than 120,000 works of art ranges from cutting-edge contemporary art to significant works by Indigenous and Canadian artists and European masterpieces.

AGO Values:

Respect:

We foster belonging and appreciate each other.

Openness:

We interact with an open mind and thoughtful engagement.

Collaboration:

We believe in the power of together.

Accountability:

We own our actions and outcomes.

We are currently seeking a Regular Part-Time,

Restaurant Host

to join our

Food & Beverage

team. Under the direction of the

Manager, Revenue Outlets Operations and Services, F&B

, the incumbent will greet guests and show them to the dining room promptly in order to provide efficient and attentive service.

Schedules dining reservations as required.

The rate of pay for this position is $19.14 - $24.03 per hour

What is this position responsible for?

Greets customers to the restaurant and seats accordingly.

Assists customers with their coats and bids farewell to guests as they are leaving.

Books reservations and responds to inquiries regarding menu and general restaurant operation.

Responds to customer comments and complaints; determines nature of complaint and refers to supervisor as required.

Prepares restaurant for opening and/or closing; make sure cleanliness and order; rearranges tables as required.

What are we looking for?

Minimum 2 years’ experience working as a host in a reputable, established restaurant

Directly related experience at taking, timing and callbacks of reservations to suit the table turnover

Demonstrated experience operating a POS System

Experience with Open Table is an asset

Ability to work independently and as a team player

High attention to detail

Demonstrated organizational skills

Demonstrated knowledge of restaurant/hospitality practices and procedures

Well-developed

customer relations and customer service skills

Strong interpersonal and verbal communication skills

What are the benefits of working at the AGO?

Discounts to GoodLife Fitness.

Free tickets to every major exhibit at AGO.

Discounts to major attractions as a part of the Provincial/City Reciprocal Programs.

Free admission to the AGO for friends and family, discounts at the gift shop, bistro, and Learning Centre.
