The AGO Collection of more than 120,000 works of art ranges from cutting-edge contemporary art to significant works by Indigenous and Canadian artists and European masterpieces.

We are currently seeking a Regular Part-Time

, Food & Beverage Outlet Server (AGO Bistro, Espresso Bar & Members’ Lounge)

to join our

Food & Beverage

team. Under the direction of the

Manager, Revenue Outlets Operations and Services, F&B,

the incumbent will execute and provide support for the service for the AGO Bistro, Espresso Bar, and Members’ Lounge with great attention to food & beverage presentation, sales, and guest experience.

The rate of pay for this position is $18.71 - $23.28 per hour.

What is this position responsible for?

Greets customers, provides a standard of high quality service at table and/or counter, takes and assists with all food & beverage orders, prepares and plates products as required, serves customers. Operates point of sale system and correctly manages payments. Acts as an ambassador of the gallery.

Cleans and busses tables, bars, chairs and stools.

Prepares the assigned areas for opening; ensures cleanliness, order and readiness of equipment, tables, chairs, stations and systems.

Cleans all work preparation and work surfaces, service equipment, display cabinets and storerooms/areas within assigned areas. Stocks work stations and server with relevant supplies. Returns food & beverage product to respective kitchens and storage areas as required.

What are we looking for?

Minimum of 2 years of experience within an innovative quality based restaurant and/or café with similar operations

Directly related experience with table service and high quality coffee/beverage preparation.

Well-developed communication skills

Demonstrated knowledge of food & beverage products, techniques and practices

Working knowledge of general food & beverage preparation and presentation

Demonstrated customer service and interpersonal skills

Demonstrated ability to operate a point of sale system including debit and credit transactions

Demonstrated understanding of food & beverage health and safety standards.

Smart Serve certification

What are the benefits of working at the AGO?

Discounts to GoodLife Fitness.

Free tickets to every major exhibit at AGO.

Discounts to major attractions as a part of the Provincial/City Reciprocal Programs.

Free admission to the AGO for friends and family, discounts at the gift shop, bistro, and Learning Centre.
