Cleaner (Part-Time)

Regular Part-Time

(Up to 24 hours per week; must be available to work evenings and weekends)

ART + AUDIENCE + LEARNING

Located in Toronto, the Art Gallery of Ontario is one of the largest art museums in North America, attracting approximately one million visitors annually. The AGO Collection of more than 120,000 works of art ranges from cutting-edge contemporary art to significant works by Indigenous and Canadian artists and European masterpieces.

We are currently seeking a Regular Part-Time,

Cleaner

to join our

Facilities Department

.

Under the direction of the

Assistant Manager, Facility Services,

the incumbent will provide housekeeping, event set-up, and general maintenance services.

Assists in the clean and orderly maintenance of AGO property.

The rate of pay for this position is $22.59 - $28.25 per hour.

What is this position responsible for?

Work safely and efficiently in a fast‑paced environment, responding to calls for service requests

Clean washrooms, including cubicles, mirrors, fixtures, waste receptacles, walls and floors; replenish paper products, personal hygiene items and soap.

Monitor all public areas and washrooms during open hours to maintain general cleanliness and restock supplies as needed.

Perform a variety of housekeeping tasks in offices and back-of-house areas, along with special projects and heavy‑duty cleaning to ensure the cleanliness of the gallery

Adhere to AGO service standards and policies, including Health & Safety.

Cleans and maintains equipment used for all aspects of the job; reports building and equipment maintenance issues to the supervisor.

Sets up furniture for events.

What are we looking for?

Ability to perform repetitive activities such as lifting, reaching, pushing, pulling, bending, stooping, walking, answering radio calls

Follow oral and written instructions, and communicate effectively with others

Ability to organize and prioritize tasks to meet timelines

Remain flexible and adjust to situations as they occur

Experience with general cleaning equipment an asset

Ability to work independently or within a group without direct supervision

Excellent attendance, punctuality and attention to detail required

Willingness to be an integral part of an organization possessing a positive, can‑do attitude

What are the benefits of working at the AGO?

Discounts to GoodLife Fitness.

Free tickets to every major exhibit at AGO.

Discounts to major attractions as part of the Provincial/City Reciprocal Programs.

Free admission to the AGO for friends and family, discounts at the gift shop, bistro, and Learning Centre.
