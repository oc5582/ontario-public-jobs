Position Summary

Reporting to the Coordinator, Recreation this position will assist with the safe and engaging delivery of aquafit programs that are appropriate for all levels and abilities of participants. Successful candidates must be able to work an average of 3-4 hours per week including flexible hours consisting of daytime, evening and weekend hours.

Major Job Responsibilities

Key Responsibilities Include:

Deliver and conduct safe instructional programs/classes following

industry standards through accurate body position and communication with a focus on quality customer experiences:

Prepare, plan and execute classes to ensure all participants are working to the best of their ability while providing excellent customer service

Modify classes to appeal to all fitness levels of program participants

Establish positive relationships and clear communication channels with all staff and participants

Maintain a safe and clean environment, including set-up and takedown, and proper usage and storage of equipment.

Proactively communicate program needs to facility maintenance staff and communicate any facility or equipment deficiencies with Coordinator, Recreation

Evaluate situations for potential risks and proactively take action to address such risks

Maintain communication with the Customer Service Desk to provide relevant program updates and to request support in the event of an emergency

Maintain a high level of professionalism and handle sensitive situations with mature judgement

Comply with all health and safety practices as it relates to the work, standard operating guidelines and the Occupational Health and Safety Act

Report any incidents, concerns or site damage to Coordinator, Recreation

Attend and participate in mandatory staff functions

Submit required forms (payroll and onboarding requirements)

Ensure applicable certifications or awards are current and provided to Coordinator, Recreation

Education & Experience

Minimum Qualifications

Secondary school diploma

One (1) year of instructing experience

Current Standard First Aid and CPR C certification

Current personal training or fitness instructor certification in a recognized organization

CALA or WaterArt or AQX aquafitness instructor certification

Excellent customer service, communication, and interpersonal skills

Strong problem-solving skills with the ability to work well independently and in a team environment

In accordance with the Corporate Criminal Record Check Policy, the position requires the incumbent to submit a Criminal Records Check with Vulnerable Sector Screening upon employment

Preferred Qualifications

Attending or graduate of post-secondary degree/diploma in Fitness & Health Promotion

Two (2) or more years instructing experience in multiple fitness disciplines

HIGH FIVE Principles of Healthy Aging

Fitness: Fitness Instructor Specialist or equivalent
