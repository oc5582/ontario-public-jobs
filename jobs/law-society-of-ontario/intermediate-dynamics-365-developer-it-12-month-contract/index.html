Job description

Overview

At Law Society of Ontario we believe in the importance of a collaborative and supportive work environment that provides high quality and professional service to the public, lawyers and paralegals, and other stakeholders.

We are always on the lookout for exceptional individuals who are ready to make a meaningful impact, innovate and grow with us.

We value each member of our team and invest in your professional learning and development.

We provide access to the resources needed to support professional growth , mental health and well-being is a priority.

Our competitive compensation package includes a defined contribution pension plan, health and dental benefits plan and a robust Employee and Family Assistance Plan (EFAP).

With our progressive employment policies, high levels of employee engagement and the impressive architecture and beautiful grounds of the historic Osgoode Hall, there are many reasons to work at the Law Society.

We hope you will consider joining the Law Society!

Job Purpose

Supports the development and maintenance of the Law Society’s Microsoft Dynamics 365 and Power Platform solutions by automating business processes and improving system integrations, while collaborating with cross

‑

functional teams to deliver reliable, scalable solutions and address issues using Power Platform tools such as Power Apps, Power Automate, and Dataverse.

Qualifications

Requires a minimum of a 2-year College program in Computer Science, Information Technology or a related discipline.

Microsoft certification related to Dynamics 365 platform is preferred but not required

• Requires a minimum of

3 years experience in software development specifically around the Microsoft Dynamics 365 platform

Microsoft certifications (Dynamics/Power Platform/Azure) is an asset

Experience in Azure Data Factory is an asset

Technical Skills:

Microsoft Office Suite / 365 (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, etc.).

Experience with Microsoft Dynamics 365 CE, including configuration, Power Automate flows, plugins, security roles, and overall Power Platform components (Power Apps and Dataverse)

Experience customizing Power Pages, including use of Liquid, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript

Experience supporting integrations using Azure services such as Logic Apps and Azure Functions

Experience with C# / .NET development in a Dynamics 365 or Power Platform context

Experience using Azure DevOps for work item tracking, source control, and deployment processes

Understanding of the Dynamics 365 and Power Platform development lifecycle, including configuration, customization, testing, deployment, and documentation practices

Competencies:

Analytical and problem-solving skills.

Interpersonal, oral, and written communication, and presentation skills.

Organizational and multitasking skills with attention to detail.

Key Accountabilities

Client / Customer Service Delivery

Assists in designing, developing, and customizing Dataverse entities/tables, forms, views, business rules, and security roles/teams within the Law Society’s Microsoft Dynamics 365 solutions

Supports and customizes integrations using Dataverse Web APIs, Azure Functions, and Logic Apps under established integration patterns

Configures and customizes Power Pages and portal components, including web pages, templates, forms, and content snippets

Troubleshoots and resolves assigned defects, enhancements, and support tasks in collaboration with senior team members

Collaborates with business analysts, project leads, and functional teams to understand requirements and contribute to technical solutions

Works closely with team members and client departments to assist in identifying, diagnosing, and resolving issues related to the Law Society’s Microsoft Dynamics 365 system

Creates and maintains appropriate technical and support documentation

Special Projects / Initiatives

Participates in assigned projects related to Law Society’s Dynamics 365 system

Research

Researches and analyzes technology options and configuration approaches related to Microsoft Dynamics 365 and Power Platform, assessing potential impacts to the Law Society’s systems and business requirements, and provides recommendations for review and approval.

Maintains awareness of new and evolving Dynamics 365, Power Platform, and related technologies through technical documentation, online resources, training, and knowledge sharing with other professionals.

Customer Relationships, Communication & Influencing Others

Shares knowledge and practical experience with the team regarding the Law Society’s Dynamics 365 applications, helping keep team members informed of system issues, updates, and ongoing initiatives

Contributes to client department teams by supporting the effective use of information technology solutions to help improve business efficiency
