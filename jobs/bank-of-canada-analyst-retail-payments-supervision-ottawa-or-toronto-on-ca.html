About the Supervision Department The payments landscape in Canada and globally is evolving rapidly, driven by technological advancements that are introducing innovative ways for consumers and businesses to make payments. Under the Retail Payment Activities Act (RPAA), the Bank of Canada is responsible for registering retail payment service providers (PSPs) that perform retail payment activities, maintaining a public registry of PSPs, and ensuring PSPs’ compliance with operational risk management, fund safeguarding and reporting requirements. The Bank assesses whether PSPs are managing risks effectively, identifies areas for improvement, and promotes compliance through a range of supervisory, compliance and enforcement tools. These positions will support the ongoing implementation and continuous improvement of the Retail Payments Supervisory Framework. What you will do As an Analyst within Retail Payments Supervision, you will contribute to fulfilling the Bank’s regulatory mandate by supporting work across one or more areas of Registration, Risk Supervision and Enforcement. You will conduct analysis and support the development of sound recommendations to help fulfil the Bank’s legislative and regulatory authorities under the RPAA. Depending on the area of focus, you may assess registration applications and scope questions, support the evaluation of PSPs’ operational risk management and fund safeguarding practices, review PSP reporting and incidents, investigate potential non-compliance, or support the use of appropriate supervisory and enforcement tools. As an analyst, you will: Analyze issues relating to the Bank’s supervision of PSPs Contribute to business documentation, operational policies, procedures, guidance, training materials, and continuous improvement initiatives Collaborate with colleagues and internal or external partners to support registration, supervision, compliance and enforcement activities Contribute to the development of recommendations to support decision making What you need to succeed You have a desire to build your knowledge of the broader financial system, the retail payments ecosystem, financial technology, PSPs, and the role of regulation and supervision in maintaining a well-functioning system.
