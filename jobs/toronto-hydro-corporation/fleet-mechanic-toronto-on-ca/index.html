The Fleet Mechanic inspects, repairs, and maintains automotive, mobile, hydraulic, portable, and stationary equipment.

This posting is to fill up to one (1) position, which is an existing vacancy. Toronto Hydro reserves the right, in alignment with the applicable collective agreement, to finalize the number of positions filled.

Days/Shifts: 40 hours per week, evening shift (Monday-Thursday evening shift (2:30pm to 12:30am).

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:

Demonstrates safety awareness and adheres to safety rules and regulations

Familiar with Occupational Health and Safety Act, Utility, and EUSA Rule Books

Conducts and/or directs work in a manner that is sensitive to customer and public needs

Performs inspections and tests on vehicles and equipment and completes documentation as required

Diagnose and perform maintenance and repairs to vehicles and equipment as required

Uses gas and electric welding equipment for repair work and fabrications

Processes work orders and inspection forms as required

Maintains work equipment and tools in a safe working condition

Maintain, update and prepare files, reports, records and documentation

Initiate, receive and satisfy inquiries or complaints by telephone, in person or by correspondence

Performs duties of equal or lower classification providing they are within their experience and training, Operates vehicle and associated equipment

REQUIREMENTS:

Must possess a Truck and Coach Technician Certificate (310T)

Valid Class “DZ” Driver's Licence in the Province of Ontario, or willing to obtain if selected

Thorough knowledge of hydraulics

Ontario Grade 12 or equivalent

Automotive Service Technician Certificate (310S) preferred

Knowledgeable in safety regulations, rules, and procedures

Demonstrated experience in a variety of business computer software packages

Proficiency using diagnostic tools and software (e.g., Cummins, INSITE, etc.)

Experience working with Drive On systems or comparable fleet management / maintenance platforms

Ability to diagnose, repair, and perform preventative maintenance on engine systems

Ability to interpret technical manuals, wiring diagrams, and service data
