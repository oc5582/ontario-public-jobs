As an Engineer, you provide a wide range of engineering support services that will ensure technical soundness, reliability, safety and cost effectiveness of the utility and its Electrical Distribution Power System. The Engineer performs technical studies and assessments; participates in short- and long-range strategic planning; drives initiatives and innovative projects; prepares engineering, technical or business reports and studies; and demonstrates effective project management and process efficiencies.

This posting is to fill one (1) position, which is an existing vacancy. Toronto Hydro reserves the right, in alignment with the applicable collective agreement, to finalize the number of positions filled.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:

Perform system studies to ensure the safe, reliable, efficient and economical operation of our distribution system (i.e., Protection and Coordination studies, Short Circuit studies, Arc Flash studies, Load Flow analysis, etc.)

Enhance and manage engineering tools and distribution system modelling software

Review and perform quality checks on study results and reports as produced by other team members

Lead and support key Intelligent Grid Initiatives by leading technical discussions, scoping projects, and managing overall project progress

Liaise with customers, planners, technical consultants, internal stakeholders and external vendors to support projects

Stay abreast of trends and technical innovations that translate into business development opportunities

Justify, defend and manage capital programs

Propose and evaluate grid solutions to enhance grid reliability and efficiency

Prepare engineering, technical or business reports and studies; performs analysis, testing and evaluations

Provide timely technical support and consultation through effective project management and the development of process efficiencies

Pursue a high level of internal and external client satisfaction, and foster a customer-centric focus

Reviews, develops and/or presents business proposals, plans, designs, processes, operational procedures and practices

Manage relationships with inter-organizational business units and external customers as required

Active participation in industry working groups and committees to keep apprised of industry best practices and influence policy development and decision making

Support the continued development and improvement of network analysis systems and tools

Provide technical support or leadership on strategic technical projects

REQUIREMENTS:

Registered Professional Engineer in the Province of Ontario

Bachelor’s degree in engineering

Four (4) + years of Engineering experience, preferably in the utilities industry

Excellent knowledge and understanding of general utility business, knowledge of electrical distribution system design and operation

Excellent knowledge and experience with use of power distribution network analysis software, such as CYME

Proficiency in programming and data management tools (e.g., Python, SQL, VBA, Alteryx, Tableau) with demonstrated application in utility engineering

Demonstrated knowledge and experience in the design and specification of protection, coordination and control systems used in power distribution and electrical substations

Knowledge and experience with distribution systems analyses such as short circuit, protection & coordination, load flow and arc flash, etc.

Knowledge of Distributed Energy Resources (DERs)

Knowledge of reliability projection modelling

Valid Class “G” Drivers License recommended

Experience with grid automation and SCADA/DMS systems

Fluent in the application of information technology, such as general office software, and engineering records

Excellent understanding of Toronto Hydro’s Conditions of Service and Standards

Excellent relationship management, customer service excellence and key account management skills

Superior interpersonal, verbal/written communications skills

Effective skills in project management operations, budgeting, business process and productivity improvements

Aptitude and experience in adapting to, managing and facilitating change demonstrated through self-motivation and stakeholder management

Ability to predict and understand technological trends and their effect on business or strategic work plans

Self-motivated, operates with a high degree of independence and can visualize possibilities and opportunities

Demonstrated versatile leadership; encourages organizational teamwork, exemplifies collaboration and educated risk taking to develop innovative solutions and support the corporate goals

Excellent analytical, organization and planning skills

Proven effective problem-solving and analytical ability
