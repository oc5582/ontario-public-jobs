The Executive Producer leads Toronto Hydro's in-house corporate production team, providing strategic, creative, and operational leadership for the organization's video, multimedia, graphics, and content production services. This role inspires and develops a team of creative professionals while actively contributing to content development and production execution, translating corporate priorities into compelling stories and engaging content that strengthen the organizational brand, enhance the employee experience, and support business objectives.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:

Lead Toronto Hydro's corporate production, graphics, and design functions, fostering a high-performing and creative team while ensuring content and production services align with organizational priorities, brand objectives, and business goals

Lead the development and delivery of end-to-end productions, including videos, livestreams, events, executive communications, and internal and external campaigns, ensuring projects are delivered on time, within budget, and to established quality, brand, and accessibility standards

Provide creative direction and storytelling leadership, translating complex business priorities into compelling content that engages audiences, strengthens the organization's brand, and enhances the employee experience

Build strong partnerships with senior leaders, stakeholders, and external vendors, serving as a trusted advisor on content strategy, production planning, and communications initiatives

Drive innovation, governance, and continuous improvement by optimizing production processes, leveraging emerging technologies, managing risk and compliance requirements, and measuring the effectiveness and impact of production services

REQUIREMENTS:

Bachelor’s degree in Communications, Marketing, Media Production, Business Administration or a related field

Ten (10) or more years of progressive experience in content creation, media production, creative development, and production leadership

Three (3) to five (5) years of progressive leadership experience managing production teams and delivering end-to-end productions, with accountability for people leadership, budget management, and vendor oversight

Extensive experience leading and executing end-to-end video productions, with demonstrated expertise in videography, production techniques, creative development, filming, directing, editing, and final delivery

Exceptional storytelling and creative direction skills, with the ability to translate corporate priorities and business objectives into compelling and engaging content

Demonstrated portfolio of successful in-house productions and content initiatives that delivered measurable business, brand, or audience engagement outcomes

Strong project management and organizational skills, with proven experience managing complex productions, budgets, vendors, contracts, timelines, and resources while balancing competing priorities in a fast-paced environment

Strong knowledge of video production, live events, digital content creation, and end-to-end production workflows, supported by proficiency with industry-standard production and editing tools, including Adobe Creative Cloud and Mac-based editing platforms

Experience leveraging emerging content technologies, digital platforms, and AI-assisted production tools to enhance creativity, efficiency, and production quality

Strong understanding of copyright, licensing, media rights, release requirements, and accessibility considerations

Excellent communication and relationship management skills, with the ability to build credibility, influence stakeholders, and foster strong partnerships across all levels of the organization

Strong political acumen, sound judgment, and ability to deliver high-quality work under tight timelines and competing priorities

Portfolio Preferred:

Candidates are encouraged to include a link to their portfolio in their resume or cover letter, highlighting in-house productions, content strategies, and creative initiatives that delivered measurable business, brand, or audience engagement results.
