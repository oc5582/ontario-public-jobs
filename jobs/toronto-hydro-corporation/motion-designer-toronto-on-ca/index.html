The Motion Designer brings Toronto Hydro's stories, brand, and messages to life through compelling motion graphics, animation, video, and digital content. Combining creativity with technical expertise, this role transforms complex ideas into engaging visual experiences that support marketing, communications, and business objectives across a variety of channels and platforms. Partnering closely with creative teams and internal stakeholders, the Motion Designer creates impactful content that strengthens brand consistency, enhances audience engagement, and delivers accessible and engaging user experiences.

REQUIREMENTS:

Post-secondary education in Motion Design, Graphic Design, Multimedia Design, Digital Media, Animation, Visual Communications, Film Production or a related field

Seven (7) or more years of experience in motion graphics, animation, or multimedia working within an in-house creative team, broadcast environment, agency, or corporate marketing and communications environments

Extensive experience creating motion graphics, animated content, and visual packages for video, multimedia, and digital communications

Strong knowledge of motion design, animation principles, visual storytelling, and video production techniques

Advanced proficiency in Adobe After Effects and Premiere Pro, with strong working knowledge of Photoshop, Illustrator, Audition, and Media Encoder

Demonstrated experience in animation, video editing, audio integration, visual effects, and post-production workflows

Demonstrated ability to create compelling visual concepts and apply design principles, including typography, composition, colour theory, layout, and visual hierarchy

Experience developing and maintaining visual identities, creative assets, and branded content across digital channel

Knowledge of brand standards, accessibility requirements, and digital content best practices

Portfolio demonstrating excellence in motion graphics, animation, video editing, and digital creative work

Strong communication and relationship management skills, with the ability to build effective relationships and partner with stakeholders across the organization

Strong problem-solving skills, sound judgment, and the ability to deliver high-quality work under tight timelines and competing priorities

Demonstrated curiosity and passion for exploring and applying emerging technologies, design trends, AI-assisted creative tools, and innovative storytelling techniques

Portfolio Preferred:

Candidates are encouraged to include a link to their portfolio in their resume or cover letter, highlighting samples of their motion graphics, animation, video editing, and digital content work.
