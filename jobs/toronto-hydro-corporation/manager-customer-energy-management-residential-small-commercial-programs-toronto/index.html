Reporting to the Senior Manager, Energy Products and Planning, the Manager, Customer Energy Management leads the delivery of electrification, climate action, and energy efficiency programs for residential and small commercial customers. In this role, you will develop customer engagement strategies, build strategic partnerships, and advance innovative solutions that support Toronto Hydro's climate and energy objectives. Working across the organization and with external stakeholders, you will help drive the energy transition by delivering exceptional customer experiences, removing barriers to electrification, and ensuring successful program outcomes.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:

Lead the delivery of customer experience and account management services for residential and small commercial customer portfolios, ensuring consistent, responsive, and high-quality service outcomes.

Oversee the design, implementation, and enhancement of climate action, electrification, and energy efficiency programs that support Toronto Hydro's strategic objectives and the energy transition.

Build and manage strategic partnerships with customers, industry stakeholders, and clean technology providers to expand program reach and impact.

Identify and address barriers to electrification through innovative solutions, funding opportunities, stakeholder collaboration, and customer engagement strategies.

Provide strategic advice on customer programs, climate initiatives, and emerging industry trends while managing complex customer and stakeholder issues.

Lead, coach, and develop a high-performing team while driving operational excellence, continuous improvement, and strong customer outcomes.

Oversee program performance, service delivery metrics, and continuous improvement initiatives to enhance customer outcomes and maximize program effectiveness.

Secure and leverage external funding opportunities while managing strategic partnerships that increase customer participation and support electrification goals.

REQUIREMENTS:

Undergraduate degree business, engineering, public policy, environmental studies, or a related discipline

Minimum seven (7) years of relevant experience in program management, service delivery (utility industry preferred).

Advanced degree in business, environmental studies, law, and/or public policy (Preferred)

Five (5) years of experience in process management, quality management, project management, business case development and change management

Three (3) to Five (5) or more years people leadership experience preferred

Experience supporting customers through hands on account management, including performance tracking, program eligibility and issue resolution

Professional certifications in energy systems, (preferred)

Project management professional (PMP) (preferred)

Strong knowledge of electricity markets, regulation, systems, and technology, as well as DERs, and DSM/CDM

Exceptional understanding of public policy at the federal, provincial, and municipal levels

Excellent understanding of NRCan, ECCC, MOE, IESO, OEB, City of Toronto roles and climate programs

Strong understanding of website platforms and data analysis tools\

Strong familiarity with cleantech/energy services providers and products, especially related to buildings initiatives (e.g. heat pumps, water heaters, DERs)

Excellent understanding of grants, loans, and other governmental programs and incentives, preferably in the energy and climate sectors

Strong knowledge of applicable safety and legislative requirements and associated regulations and rules, both in relation to utility distribution systems and customer-owned electric facilities (e.g. Electrical Safety Authority)

Strong stakeholder management and engagement skills; highly adept at managing politically charged situations/conversations

Reporting and analytics methodologies, including data interpretation, trend analysis, and performance benchmarking

Process improvement methodologies (e.g., Lean, continuous improvement, service optimization)
