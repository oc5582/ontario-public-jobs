WORK ILLUSTRATION:

The Certified Power Cable Apprentice is responsible for the construction, maintenance, operation, and troubleshooting of the underground electrical and distribution systems. Working with all voltage types, the incumbent is expected to maintain and repair underground powerlines and cables, and other associated equipment, such as insulators, conductors, lightening arrestors, switches, metering systems, transformers and lighting and control systems. They are also expected to splice and terminate conductors and related wiring to connect power distribution and transmission networks.

These duties will be performed to the level of applicable skills and knowledge of the incumbent, under the direction of a Certified Power Cable Person.

NOTE:

Successful applicants may be rotated through the various work centres and departments to ensure familiarization with all aspects of Toronto Hydro’s distribution system.

The incumbent is expected to behave ethically and follow the established code of business conduct, policies and internal control procedures, laws and regulations governing Toronto Hydro.

This posting is to fill up to 5 positions, at least one of which is an existing vacancy. Toronto Hydro reserves the right, in alignment with the applicable collective agreement, to finalize the number of positions filled.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:

Demonstrates safe work practices and adheres to safety rules and regulations

Communicates with customers and staff

Engages in job planning with the Crew Leader and/or Manager and follows the plan

Selects, operates and maintains tools and equipment

Installs and maintains conductors and control wiring

Troubleshoots, installs, and maintains underground distribution system

Assists in connecting, applying, analyzing and fault locating equipment including testing and locating grounds on 600V system

Installs, maintains and removes transformers on voltages up to 27.6kV

Troubleshoots, installs, operates and maintains system protection, control and instrumentation equipment

Handles energized lines using rubber protective equipment

Handles energized lines using live line tool methods

Provides adequate insulation while working on cables energized up to 750V

Perform switching operations on secondary (up to 750v) and primary (up to 13.8kV) equipment

Communicates with customers and staff

Follows regulatory procedures and employer’s procedures

Identifies defects, contraventions and dangers

Applies discretion to solve occupational health and safety problems, and reports are unresolved problems

REQUIREMENTS:

Minimum two (2) year college diploma or one (1) year Electrical Techniques Certificate with relevant demonstrated practical experience preferred or 309A Electrician (Red Seal) certification with a high school diploma and relevant experience

Valid Class G Ontario Driver’s License or higher in good standing with the ability to obtain a DZ or ARZ post-hire

Physically able to perform the essential duties in all weather conditions

Comfortable working at heights and in confined spaces

Ability to read and interpret distribution system construction drawings and supporting documents

Knowledge of the Electrical Safety Authority Regulation 22/04, Toronto Hydro and Utility Work Protection Code, WHMIS, CPR, First Aid and all other applicable legislation

Demonstrated proficiency in a PC Windows environment and associated desktop software applications
