Responsible for the day-to-day, end-to-end delivery of an outsourced portfolio of the organization’s planned capital, maintenance, and reactive programs and projects. This includes providing technical guidance and management of projects and programs using selected vendors through all phases of the project or program delivery to ensure on-time and on-budget completion of assigned work program in compliance with approved Toronto Hydro standards, procedures, and commitments. The incumbent is responsible for supervising a small team of professional and technical resources (internal and external) including providing coaching, feedback and mentoring on organizational processes and procedures.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:

Provides day-to-day guidance and support to assigned vendors to ensure the work package viability (cost, customer, assets function, reliability, safety, and environment) and timely, cost-effective work completion (schedule adherence, actual vs. estimate, change order management) of assigned work programs

Participates in both pre and post construction meetings (planned and actual), working closely with the assigned third-party auditor to ensure compliance with all required processes and procedures is maintained during project work

Reviews assigned projects and scope packages to ensure material is current and relevant and validates the assigned contractor has the right skills and experience for the required work (based on previous history and performance)

Reviews budgetary estimates and initiates, as required, the change request process through Program Management

Manages the relationships with assigned vendors to ensure project execution is completed according to work plans

Leads short-interval planning and review meetings with vendors to ensure safety management, adherence to standards and quality, schedule attainment and resourcing

Monitors contractor performance (including legal compliance, non-conformance reports, and compliance to Toronto Hydro policies), identifying issues and solutions to close gaps as required

Takes action to mitigate and resolve project delivery issues as they arise with internal and external stakeholders that could impact work program attainment

Reviews and oversees external services contractors to ensure the terms of the negotiated agreements are met, including invoicing, budget reconciliation for project, and unit price contract management

Participates as a subject matter expert on organizational projects and initiatives, to improve systems, processes and practices

REQUIREMENTS:

Bachelor Degree or College diploma in Engineering (Electrical discipline preferred)

Certified Engineering Technologist (CET) or Professional Engineer (P.Eng) in the province of Ontario or eligibility to obtain in future.

Project Management Professional Certification (PMP) (Preferred)

Five (5) years of professional experience in electrical distribution, system planning, design and construction

Three (3) - Five (5) years of supervisory or project management experience

Driver’s license (G Class) required

Strong knowledge of the electrical distribution system, utility design, civil infrastructure, construction standards and operations

Strong knowledge of applicable safety and legislative requirements and associated regulations and rules

Experience identifying, leading and implementing productivity enhancements (e.g., automation, AI, process change)

Vendor/contractor management skills

Knowledge of OHSA, Toronto Hydro/EUSA Rule Book and relevant safety, electrical and environmental legislation and regulations

Familiar with reading electrical drawings, work protection codes, feeder/operating prints, overhead, underground work practices and standards

Understanding of project management methodology, project oversight and control

Experience in developing and administering contracts to leverage external resources to support capital work program

Strong working knowledge of the Microsoft Suite (Excel, PowerPoint, Word)

Strong stakeholder engagement and influencing skills

Excellent written and verbal communication skills
