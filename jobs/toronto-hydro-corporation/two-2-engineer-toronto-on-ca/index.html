Reporting to the Manager of Stations & Distribution Automation, the Engineer will join a highly skilled engineering team, tasked with the upkeep and expansion of critical electrical infrastructure found in municipal transmission and distribution substations. Projects involve the installation, alteration, extension and/or repair of a variety of electrical equipment; controlled, monitored and/or automated by complex circuity, electronics, and advanced communication networks.

This posting is to fill up to 2 number of positions, at least one of which is an existing vacancy. Toronto Hydro reserves the right, in alignment with the applicable collective agreement, to finalize the number of positions filled.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:

Provide both conceptual and detailed design services for substation capital and reactive maintenance projects

Co-ordinate projects, prepare cost estimates, and collaborate with key stakeholders, including: equipment suppliers, contractors, and other utilities

Perform engineering studies and/or calculations, including: short circuit analysis, load flows, arc flash hazard assessments, protective device coordination, equipment selection & sizing, protection relay settings, and ground grid design

Develop equipment testing and commissioning plans, witness and approve equipment FAT, SAT testing

Configure and program SEL and GE station control systems, protection relays and distribution system network communications equipment

Prepare, provide guidance or assist engineering staff with the creation or modification of single line and three line diagrams, AC/DC electrical schematics, wiring diagrams, and electrical layout and elevation drawings

Research, evaluate, test and recommend station equipment

Provide technical field support for operations staff

Develop standards and specifications

Plan and design processes, operational procedures, and safe work practices

Participate in short term and long term strategic planning

Prepare engineering or business reports (both oral and written)

Review, develop or present business proposals

Participate on committees or project teams as required on a wide variety of business initiatives

Support senior management on ad-hoc assignments

Mentoring and training designers and team members

Provide engineering support for Metrolinx transit expansion projects across Toronto—including LRT, subway, and GO Corridor works—by coordinating design, construction, commissioning, and maintenance activities while ensuring Toronto Hydro standards and asset protection requirements are met.

REQUIREMENTS:

Bachelor’s degree in Engineering (Electrical preferred)

Registered Professional Engineer in the Province of Ontario

6+ years of Engineering experience

Must hold valid Class “G” Drivers License.

Proficiency in IEC 61850 GOOSE messaging protocol, including configuration, testing, and troubleshooting of peer‑to‑peer protection and control signals within utility communication networks is strongly preferred.

Knowledge of protection and control concepts preferred

Familiarity with various station equipment, including switchgear, breakers and power transformers; proficiency in programming GE and SEL protection relays preferred

Familiarity with SCADA systems, RTUs and data concentrators; station network topologies; relevant ethernet and serial communication protocols; copper, fiber optic and wireless communication systems preferred

Experience in the development of protection philosophies and the application of substation protection schemes

Proficiency in system modeling using engineering analysis software such CYME, ETAP or SKM an asset

Knowledge of, and ability to look-up and correctly interpret relevant industry standards, including: CSA, ANSI, IEC and IEEE; relevant code, including: OESC, NESC, DSC, TSC, OBC, EUSR and UWPC; and relevant legislation, including: OHSA and Reg. 22/04

Familiarity with distribution system automation technologies

Strong written and verbal communication skills demonstrated through report writing and delivering presentations

Demonstrated competence in computer programming

Project management skills with the ability to organize the project, work with key stakeholders, manage project risks, and deliver projects on time and within budget

General knowledge of overhead and underground distribution system design

Customer focused and demonstrates a pro-active service orientation; must be able to meet customer needs in a flexible but cost-effective manner

Self-motivated, operates with a high degree of independence and can visualize possibilities and opportunities for change in support of continuous process improvement

Accepts work assignments with clear understanding of related quality, quantity, cost and performance targets/expectations

Acts as a safety leader by incorporating safety and environmental considerations into business decisions and actions

Ability to work with internal and external stakeholders, e.g. designers, contractors, suppliers, HONI, various tradespersons, etc.

Adaptability to fast-paced environment and ability to pivot and react to changing priorities and demands
