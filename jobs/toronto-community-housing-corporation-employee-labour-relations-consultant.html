Make a difference The Employee and Labour Relations Consultant provides a range of coaching, strategic advice and administrative support functions and participates in the provision of a wide range of services in the areas of labour and employee relations, interpretation of various collective agreements, employment and labour legislation, Toronto Community Housing policies and procedures to an assigned client portfolio(s).     What you’ll do Provides advice/assistance/guidance to managers in an assigned client portfolio(s):

Provides a range of coaching, consulting and strategic advice to clients in the areas of human rights, labour and employee relations, interpretation of collective agreements, employment and labour legislation, policies and procedures Provides advice/assistance/guidance to managers and consultants in all phases of re-organization Works closely with people and culture personnel to communicate pertinent information and documents Responsible for the timely and effective delivery of people and culture services including but not limited to consultation and advice in all disciplinary and non-disciplinary matters, investigations, complaints, collective agreement interpretation and workplace accommodations, etc. to clients to ensure business objectives are met Provides guidance to people leaders and assist with formal accommodation plans for workplace accommodations  Assists in developing, organizing and leading various labour relations, collective agreement interpretation, and workforce planning workshops, guidelines, policies and procedures

Works closely with the various bargaining unions to sustain positive employee and labour relations:

Provides assistance in the grievance process including but not limited to grievance tracking, scheduling, investigating, attend and lead  Step 2 and 3 grievance hearings, preparation of written responses and settlement agreements Responsible for gathering documentation in preparation for and or attending mediations and arbitration hearings  Participates as a team member on negotiation teams when negotiating collective agreements with unions Interprets and administers multiple collective agreements, assists in the preparation and representation for grievance hearings and conflict resolution and the promotion of a positive labour relations environment Participates in labour management meetings and other meetings with union representative on a variety of labour relations matters

Provides work direction and leadership within the labour relations unit

Mentors, coaches and provides work direction to Employee and Labour Relations Associates and HR Assistants and provides leadership and guidance to resolve complex People and Culture issues and ensures quality in service delivery.

Assists with recruitment related activities for an assigned client portfolio:

Assists with recruitment activities for an assigned client portfolio including but not limited to ensuring seniority calculations are performed in accordance with the collective agreement, participating and providing input at various stages in the recruitment process to ensure compliance with the collective agreement.   Develops employment equity hiring strategies for client groups.  Participates, implements and supports key initiatives as outlined in the labour relations strategy for heavily unionized portfolios
