Job description

About Us

The Ontario Cannabis Store provides safe, responsible access to recreational cannabis for adults 19 and older. We operate the sole legal online store for recreational cannabis in Ontario and are the provincial wholesaler of cannabis for private retail stores.

Working at the OCS is a unique opportunity to be part of an agile start-up in a ground-breaking new industry. We’re a diverse team passionate about delivering a great customer experience, working together with mutual respect and building value out of our differences. We’re an inclusive organization that understands that delivering great results comes out of ensuring every voice is heard.

About the Role

We're looking for a Systems Specialist.

​As the Systems Specialist, you will serve as the primary business administrator and subject matter expert for all Customer Care systems, ensuring stable operations while supporting continuous improvement, automation, and AI-enabled innovation.

​This role serves as the primary liaison between Customer Care, Information Technology, vendors, and project teams, leading system enhancements, user acceptance testing (UAT), operational readiness, and strategic improvement initiatives. The position plays a critical role in optimizing the customer and employee experience across all customer-facing and support technologies.​

About Your Day

Maintain the business administration, operational performance, and continuous improvement of all systems utilized by OMNI Customer Care

​Act as the primary Customer Care Subject Matter Expert (SME) for customer service technologies, including CRM, telephony and IVR platforms, workforce management systems, portals, reporting tools, knowledge management systems, and order management applications

​Serve as the primary liaison between Customer Care, IT teams, vendors, and business stakeholders regarding system functionality, incidents, enhancements, and operational impacts

​Monitor system performance and coordinate issue resolution to minimize disruption to customers and frontline employees

​Identify operational risks and coordinate mitigation actions with the appropriate decision-makers

​Participate in governance, prioritization, and release planning discussions to ensure Customer Care requirements are represented

​Maintain system documentation, support procedures, escalation processes, and operational controls

​​

​​Represent Customer Care in all technology projects, implementations, upgrades, fixes, and system enhancements

​Gather, document, and validate business requirements and operational impacts for Customer Care initiatives

​Lead End-to-End User Acceptance Testing (UAT) for all Customer Care-related projects, enhancements, releases, and defect corrections

​Develop testing strategies, test cases, scenarios, acceptance criteria, and testing documentation.

​Coordinate test participants, test environments, defect management, retesting activities, and business sign-offs

​Assess operational readiness and provide recommendations to customer care leadership regarding implementation risks and deployment readiness

​Conduct post-implementation validation to ensure solutions meet business requirements and operational expectations

​Track project milestones, dependencies, risks, and deliverables impacting Customer Care operations​

​​​Partner with IT Business Relationship Managers (BRMs), technology teams, vendors, and Customer Care leadership to identify and prioritize improvement opportunities.

​Maintain a structured backlog of enhancements, process improvements, and innovation initiatives.

​Analyze customer feedback, operational data, system issues, and employee insights to identify opportunities for efficiency and experience improvements.

​Lead and support initiatives focused on automation, digital transformation, self-service capabilities, and operational optimization.

​Identify, evaluate, and champion Artificial Intelligence (AI) opportunities that improve customer experiences, employee productivity, service quality, and operational efficiency.

​Support proof-of-concept initiatives and pilot programs involving automation and AI technologies.

​Ensure all proposed solutions align with organizational privacy, security, accessibility, and governance requirements.

​Measure and communicate the effectiveness and business value of implemented improvements.​​

​​​Administer the Automated Reconciliation Tool (ART) and associated operational processes.

​Monitor order flow, payments, refunds, exceptions, and transaction failures across integrated systems.

​Investigate trends and recurring issues, coordinating corrective actions with appropriate stakeholders.​​
