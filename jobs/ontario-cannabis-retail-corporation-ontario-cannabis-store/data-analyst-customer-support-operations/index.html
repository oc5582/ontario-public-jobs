About Us

The Ontario Cannabis Store provides safe, responsible access to recreational cannabis for adults 19 and older. We operate the sole legal online store for recreational cannabis in Ontario and are the provincial wholesaler of cannabis for private retail stores.

Working at the OCS is a unique opportunity to be part of an agile start-up in a ground-breaking new industry. We’re a diverse team passionate about delivering a great customer experience, working together with mutual respect and building value out of our differences. We’re an inclusive organization that understands that delivering great results comes out of ensuring every voice is heard.

About the Role

We're looking for a

Data Analyst, Customer Support Operations

to join our team!

In this role, you will transform retailer support, customer experience, sales, and operational data into meaningful insights that help drive informed business decisions. You will be responsible for developing and maintaining dashboards, scorecards, recurring reports, and ad hoc analyses that enable internal teams to better understand service performance, retailer needs, operational trends, and opportunities for continuous improvement.

Working closely with Customer Support Operations, CRM, retailer-facing teams, Data Science, and technology partners, you will gather reporting requirements, validate data, support enhancements to the Retailer Data Program, and promote the consistent use of metrics across the organization. The role also involves translating complex data into clear, actionable insights for both technical and non-technical audiences, while helping to improve the accuracy, accessibility, and overall value of operational reporting.

​Partner closely with the Data Science team to design, test, validate, and implement new reporting features and analytical capabilities.
