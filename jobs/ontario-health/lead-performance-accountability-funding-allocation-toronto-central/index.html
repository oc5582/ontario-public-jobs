The Lead, Performance, Accountability & Funding Allocation is a key regional partner responsible for administering accountability agreements, monitoring Health Service Provider (HSP) performance, supporting funding decisions, and advancing system improvement. Working across Ontario Health, the Ministry, and provider organizations, this role brings together performance management, relationship leadership, risk insight, and data-informed planning to improve equitable outcomes for the Toronto Region.

This is an opportunity for a collaborative health system leader who can translate complex performance, funding, and accountability requirements into practical action with meaningful impact.

Here is what you will be doing:

Lead performance management and monitoring for assigned Health Service Providers across sectors, including hospitals, community services, long-term care, and primary care.

Administer and complete accountability agreements, including work-back planning, supporting documentation, contract monitoring, and issue escalation.

Provide subject matter expertise on performance measurement, funding allocation, health system data, operational risk, and priority health system issues.

Use data, financial analysis, and stakeholder insight to support funding processes, HSP proposals, volume-funded procedures, reallocations, and Ministry/Ontario Health directed programs.

Build trusted relationships with HSPs, Ontario Health teams, the Ministry, and regional partners to resolve sensitive issues and advance shared system goals.

Identify and implement opportunities to improve performance, strengthen processes, support health equity, and scale innovative practices across the region.

Support risk management, audits, auditor general inquiries, capital project review, enterprise risk activities, and regional consistency in performance monitoring and funding allocation.

Lead and participate in committees, HSP performance meetings, internal planning groups, working groups, and inter-Ontario Health discussions as required.

Develop clear tools, communications, reference materials, and reporting resources that improve team effectiveness and support informed decision-making.

Here is what you will need to be successful:

Knowledge of performance management and evaluation techniques in one or more health sectors related to administrative and/or clinical performance

Ability to translate health system questions into data analyses, and to clearly explain results and limitations of analyses. Ability to simplify complex information.

Demonstrated experience with financial analysis and resource allocation

Experience with evaluation methodologies an asset.

Excellent knowledge of community resources (e.g., services and programs), the roles of health care professionals and of direct care/care coordination models used in community health care organizations

Demonstrated leadership skills including the ability to influence others and facilitate meetings
