Want to make a difference in your career?  Consider this opportunity.

Join Ontario Health in a strategic, hands-on role that helps shape performance across a coordinated network of more than 50 public, hospital, and community laboratories. Reporting to the Manager, Performance Management & Analytics, you will turn complex health data into clear insights that strengthen laboratory services and support better decisions across Ontario.

As Lead, Performance Management, you will guide analysis and reporting for emerging provincial laboratory medicine issues, high-priority initiatives, and testing programs. You will develop meaningful visualizations and key performance indicators, connect provincial datasets, identify measurement gaps, and advise on new data collection.

You will also be the primary lead for the Ontario Laboratory Medicine Program’s Key Performance Indicator Framework—working across Ontario Health and with system partners to improve planning, monitoring, reporting, and evaluation. Your work will reveal quality gaps, explain regional and institutional variation, inform executive decisions, and translate complex data challenges into practical action.

Here is what you will be doing:

Design, develop, and optimize performance-management capabilities for Ontario’s laboratory network, including monitoring, analytics, key metrics, and reporting.

Lead KPI processes and translate provincial and regional trends into actionable insights for Laboratory Medicine testing.

Review and refine operational dashboards, scorecards, targets, tracking tools, audits, and feedback reports—including the performance-management system for Respiratory Virus testing.

Identify and resolve network operating issues, and deliver timely ad hoc analyses and recommendations to senior leaders.

Partner across Ontario Health, ministries, agencies, and laboratory-sector stakeholders to provide accurate, timely information for data-driven decisions.

Collaborate with Performance & Accountability on funding requirements, allocations, costing models, and accountability agreements.

Communicate complex findings through compelling data stories, executive reports, and practical recommendations for improvement.

Build trusted stakeholder relationships, gather feedback on program and service needs, and apply evaluation methods to guide the Ontario Laboratory Medicine Program’s evolution.

Here is what you will need to be successful:

Education and Experience

Master’s degree in Business Administration, Operations Management, Health Informatics, Data Analytics or a related health care field

Project Management (PMP) Certification is an asset

8+ years experience in the provision of expertise and services in the area of data analytics within the health care sector

Laboratory-related experience is an asset

Knowledge and Skills

Specialized knowledge of health data with an ability to understand and interpret qualitative and quantitative data to both identify the need for and assess the impact of changes and inform strategic decisions and goals

Knowledge in performance measurement, metrics and reporting

Demonstrated ability to influence cross-functional stakeholders, provide guidance and technical advice to solve issues as they arise, implement change and motivate team to drive strategic objectives

Exceptional judgment and extensive skillset in data analytics, evidence-based decision making, and storytelling through data visualization

Highly proficient in excel and proficiency/familiarity with other analytical tools (e.g. Python, R, PowerBI and Tableau)

Strong supervisory and project management experience that allow for managing multiple priorities at once across various stakeholder groups

An ability to distill complex and/or ambiguous problems into clear, clear, concise, compelling messages

Self-directed, with enthusiasm, initiative and demonstrated ability to work independently to deadlines under high pressure
