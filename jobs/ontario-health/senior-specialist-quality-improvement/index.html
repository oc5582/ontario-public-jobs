Ontario Health is seeking a passionate and experienced Quality Improvement professional to join the Acute and Hospital-Based Care portfolio. As a

Senior Specialist, Quality Improvement

, you will collaborate with provincial, regional, and local partners, along with clinical leaders, to advance impactful quality improvement initiatives across the health system.

In this role, you will help drive the implementation of programs, clinical and quality standards, accelerate the adoption and spread of leading practices, strengthen connections across the quality ecosystem, and build quality improvement capacity and knowledge. This is a unique opportunity to influence system-wide change by applying a provincial perspective to complex clinical quality challenges and helping remove barriers that enable meaningful improvements at the point of care.

This is an exciting opportunity to address complex healthcare challenges from a system-wide perspective, influence frontline change, and support the ongoing success of Ontario Health's Quality Improvement Plan (QIP) program.

Here is what you will be doing:

Supports Quality Improvement Programs

Working in partnership with internal and external stakeholders, develops, implements and evaluates strategies to facilitate the large scale spread of provincial programs designed to promote quality and to reduce clinical variation and inequities

Develops dissemination and implementation strategies to support the use of data (such as quality indicators, MyPractice reports, performance measures, etc.), the adoption and synthesis of evidence (such as Quality Standards and clinical guidelines), and the use of QI methodology to change clinical practice at the frontline

Supports provincial QI programs in various sectors of the healthcare system (e.g., acute, primary care, long-term care and the community) with a focus on patient engagement, equity and advancing integrated care

Responsible for developing reports, briefing notes, implementation plans, tools and resources presentations and other dissemination materials

Leads adoption of quality improvement best practices using a suite of supports and multiple media platforms

Ensures timely response to concerns arising from a large and diverse audience

Relationship and Stakeholder Management

Acts as an Ontario Health ambassador both formally (e.g., working with regional and local stakeholders, at provincial or national events) and informally, through day-to-day interactions

Establishes trust and builds strong internal and external relationships with partners and key stakeholders to remain current on local priorities, emerging issues, and trends

Supports committees and working groups comprised of both internal and external stakeholders

Works collaboratively across Ontario Health portfolios, including the regions, and with system partners

Builds QI Capacity and Knowledge

Demonstrates subject matter expertise on priority issues such as: integrated care delivery models, public and patient engagement, improving patient and caregiver experience, coordinated care management, transitions in care, access to care, health care equity, and other clinical priority areas

Drives an understanding of relevant indicators needed for areas of focus with colleagues and stakeholders

Recommends relevant indicators in consultation within OH and external partners

In consultation with senior analyst, designs mixed methods analysis, incorporating into reports

Leads learning collaboratives and communities of practice to drive improvement in priority areas

Understands how to navigate multiple BI platforms and health system performance reporting products (e.g., Quality Standards eReports, Quality Based Procedures, Power BI dashboards)

Able to synthesize reporting outputs with evidence reviews to identify improvement opportunities and support the design, prioritization, and evaluation of QI initiatives

Shares resources and tools to support adoption of QI methodologies in the field

Here is what you will need to be successful:

Education and Experience

An undergraduate degree in Health Policy, Health Administration, Systems Engineering, Quality Improvement, Health Sciences, Health Informatics, Applied Psychology or other relevant disciplines is required; A master’s degree is preferred

A minimum of 5 years’ experience in clinical leadership or health system policy, that includes responsibility for improving quality in a care setting

A minimum of 3 years’ experience as a QI leader with experience in front line facilitation and coaching of interdisciplinary teams, successful use of organizational change strategies, and use of a range of knowledge exchange methods such as communities of practice, workshops, on-line collaborative space

Training or certification in implementation science, quality improvement (e.g., Lean or Six Sigma), change management, or knowledge translation is required

Registration with a regulated health profession and/or experience in a clinical setting is desirable

Knowledge and Skills

Excellent knowledge and understanding of Ontario’s health system and health policy issues, priorities and enablers and barriers to improving quality of care

Experience engaging clinicians, front line staff, patients and caregivers in design and delivery of clinical care

Skilled in use of textual and qualitative analysis

Skilled in use of common surveys for outreach and outputs such as Survey Monkey, Survey Gizmo

Project management and/or strong organizational skills to manage competing priorities

Excellent oral and written communication skills

Ability to work independently and in a team

Experience with data analysis and data interpretation

Professionalism, reliability and detail orientation

Excellent computer skills, with proficiency in the use of Microsoft Office software (including Word, Excel, Power Point and Teams), including use of virtual communication platforms such as Zoom
