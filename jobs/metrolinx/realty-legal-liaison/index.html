Job description

Metrolinx

is connecting communities across the Greater Golden Horseshoe. Metrolinx operates GO Transit and UP Express, as well as the PRESTO fare payment system. We are also building new and improved rapid transit, including GO Expansion, Light Rail Transit routes, and major expansions to Toronto’s subway system, to get people where they need to go, better, faster and easier. Metrolinx is an agency of the Government of Ontario.

At Metrolinx, equity, diversity and inclusion are essential to living our values of serving with passion, thinking forward and playing as a team.

This role is an in‑office position based at our 10 Bay Street, Toronto location. Our team is highly collaborative, and much of our work benefits from in‑person co‑creation, relationship‑building, and hands‑on partnership with leaders across the organization.

Our Capital Projects Group workforce is made up of driven and dedicated professionals who care deeply about working together to deliver infrastructure and make a positive impact in our communities. Join us as we build a knowledgeable and collaborative team that courageously changes how transit projects will be delivered.

Our Property Acquisitions Division within the Capital Projects Group is seeking a Realty and Legal Liaison to manage our data on real estate and/or legal transactions, develop reporting for Senior Management, and assisting with the creation of internal documentation.

What will I be doing?

Oversees, reviews, and analyzes realty and legal files and ensure internal tracker is up to date;

Review, gather, interpret and summarize information from expert reports including appraisals, surveys, memos, and planning reports.

Interpret property data to assist with and to develop:

Property budgeting and forecasting reports.

Reporting for Senior Management (PowerPoint Presentations, Excel spreadsheets and briefing memos)

Develop, review and administer internal documents (approval memos, briefing notes, etc,) to ensure efficient completion, accuracy and documentation.

Manage internal documentation to ensure folders are up to date and file structure is organized.

Administer, review and complete internal forms to ensure accuracy.

Attend and provide support during meetings with internal and external stakeholders.

Acts as liaison between acquisitions/realty/legal business units to facilitate requests for additional information and/or documentation, and provide timely problem resolution.

Coordinates with Community Engagement, Real Estate and Legal departments to plan required resources, manage document/transaction processing workloads.

Develops, implements, and updates procedures and protocols to track and monitor outstanding real estate and/or legal issues that affect corporate infrastructure projects.

Coordinates project budget and tracks costs within approved budgetary limits.

What Skills and Qualifications Do I Need?

Completion of a diploma in Business Administration, Urban Planning, Construction, Legal Paraprofessional or related field – or a combination of education, training and experience deemed equivalent; additional training/coursework in project management, urban/regional planning, real property administration and/or real estate are assets.

Experience working with Microsoft Office.

Experience developing reports in PowerBI is an asset.

Demonstrated experience with construction projects, property development, real property administration or similar function, ideally within the public sector.

Experience with reviewing Legal, real estate, and property-related agreements, contracts and Memorandums of Understanding (MOUs), general legal matters pertaining to real property rights, property law/valuation, property assessment, survey plans/documents.

Experience coordinating projects and managing multiple tasks.

Attention to detail to ensure work output is accurate.

Theories, principles and practices of project management; project management software.

Interpersonal, oral/written/presentation skills to clearly, concisely, and logically structure information

Knowledge with or previous work experience with the Expropriations Act and Building Transit Faster Act would be an asset.
