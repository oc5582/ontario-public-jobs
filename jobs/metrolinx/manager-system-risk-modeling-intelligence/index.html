Job description

Metrolinx is connecting communities across the Greater Golden Horseshoe. Metrolinx operates GO Transit and UP Express, as well as the PRESTO fare payment system. We are also building new and improved rapid transit, including GO Expansion, Light Rail Transit routes, and major expansions to Toronto’s subway system, to get people where they need to go, better, faster and easier. Metrolinx is an agency of the Government of Ontario.

At Metrolinx, equity, diversity and inclusion are essential to living our values of serving with passion, thinking forward and playing as a team.

In Engineering and Safety division we play a key role in ensuring the safe, reliable, and cost-effective service of our transit system as the Metrolinx engineering technical authority and owner of more than $20 billion in assets and growing to $60 billion by 2030.

Our System Safety and Engineering Management Office is looking for a Manager, System Risk Modeling and Intelligence. The Manager will lead development and execution of analysis, modeling, reporting and insights for the safety, risk, and reliability of our GO Rail, GO Bus and UP Express business, as well as supporting other financial, operational and safety KPI that measure the health of Metrolinx and holding vendor and third party commercially and operationally accountable for performance. This role drives effective measurement and improvement to protect our reputation with customers, the government and future ridership revenue growth.

What will I be doing?

Lead analysis, modeling, reporting and insights for the primary source data on the safety, risk, and reliability of our GO Rail, GO Bus and UP Express business.

Provides single source data to senior leaders within the organization that is used in commercial discussions (Ex. liquidated damages, where to focus improvement) with partners

Provides teams such as operational planning, service design, engineering and safety with data analysis inputs into service planning simulation tools to assess the impacts to new/revised train service plans and future state infrastructure requirements. As well as provide MX with monthly and annual forecasting of safety, risk, and reliability performance with an effort to hold operations and asset teams across Metrolinx in CPG, RT and Operations accountable for their performance.

Leads a team of technical senior and junior performance advisors to perform timely deep dive reports for emerging issues or trends for senior executives. This work helps executive understand where issues are, so they can make informed decisions on service reductions, deployment of additional human and financial resources and where transformational change maybe required.

Drives lean adoption through execution of analysis to identify root causes and the Plan-Do-Check-Act cycle to improve outcomes, which drives customer satisfaction, revenue and efficiency of operations.

Leads the overall Reliability, Availability, Maintainability and Safety (RAMS) risk and performance regime for Metrolinx in accordance with EN50126 ensuring a successfully integrated RAMS program for all assets and capital works.

Communicates and provides support to both internal and external stakeholders to enhance and achieve organizational goals

Keeps up to date on emerging trends, behaviors and industry best practices by researching, developing, and maintaining awareness of industry best practices

Establishes processes, controls and measures to ensure appropriate expense allocation, cost management, and overall fiscal responsibility

Develop and maintain an environment of positive collaboration through strong working relationships and linkages between business units and departments; and between Metrolinx and others.

Manages and directs staff (e.g. defines roles, responsibilities, and expectations); delivers timely feedback to ensure a cohesive operational unit and the optimum use of team skills; manages staff and monitors work assignments to ensure adherence to timelines; monitors the achievement of performance objectives; develops and implements action plans to address performance gaps

What Skills and Qualifications Do I Need?

Completion of a degree in Engineering, Business Administration, Data Analytics, Statistics, or a related discipline – or a combination of education, training and experience deemed equivalent

Demonstrated years of leadership experience

Professional Engineering designation is an asset

Interpersonal and oral/written/presentation skills to liaise with all levels within the organization; to work effectively with specialists across multiple sectors

Project management skills, including work planning, monitoring and evaluation expertise, to manage multiple projects with competing priorities and tight deadlines

Knowledge of performance analytics to monitor project performance and quality and to collaborate on continuous improvement initiatives

Proven leadership, interpersonal and mentoring skills with the ability to clearly articulate creative ideas and direction to staff

Interpersonal and oral/written/presentation communication skills

Proficiency in MS Office (e.g. Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, etc.)
