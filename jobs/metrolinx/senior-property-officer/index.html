Metrolinx

is connecting communities across the Greater Golden Horseshoe. Metrolinx operates GO Transit and UP Express, as well as the PRESTO fare payment system. We are also building new and improved rapid transit, including GO Expansion, Light Rail Transit routes, and major expansions to Toronto’s subway system, to get people where they need to go, better, faster and easier. Metrolinx is an agency of the Government of Ontario.

At Metrolinx, equity, diversity and inclusion are essential to living our values of serving with passion, thinking forward and playing as a team.

This role is an in‑office position based at our 10 Bay Street, Toronto location. Our team is highly collaborative, and much of our work benefits from in‑person co‑creation, relationship‑building, and hands‑on partnership with leaders across the organization.

Our Capital Projects Group workforce is made up of driven and dedicated professionals who care deeply about working together to deliver infrastructure and make a positive impact in our communities. Join us as we build a knowledgeable and collaborative team that courageously changes how transit projects will be delivered.

Our Property Acquisitions Team, within the Pre-Construction Services Division of the Capital Projects Group (CPG), is growing and looking for a Contract Senior Property Officer. The successful candidate will fill key roles in the day-to-day activities related to Light Rail T

t

ransit (LRT) projects. They will ensure that property rights necessary for the project are acquired by Metrolinx in accordance with Ministry and Corporate guidelines. This is a great opportunity to make significant contributions on a project that is transforming transportation in the region!

What will I be doing?

Support project delivery teams in the identification and acquisition of property required to implement major infrastructure transit projects

Coordinate and collaborate with project delivery teams, internal and external stakeholders to acquire property and secure access to land requirements

Oversee internal staff activities and external service providers in the delivery of property appraisals, surveys, legal agreements, reports and other property related documents

Monitor consultant performance, budgets, deliverables, and schedules to ensure compliance with contractual requirements and organizational expectations.

Provide subject matter expertise and support to project delivery teams through the preparation of property cost estimates, identification of potential delivery risks and recommendation of mitigation measures to support project delivery objectives

Negotiate with property owners, provide oversight on contracts /fees with consultants,

Coordinate acquisition of land or interests by expropriation in accordance with Corporate Policy and Procedures, the Expropriations Act and the Building Transit Faster Act, including providing support to Legal Services and interface with the Ministry of Transportation, as may be required

Collaborate with the internal Real Estate Management team on property management and maintenance requirements for newly acquired project related property

Prepare, coordinate, and maintain property related reports, briefing documents and presentation material for various client groups and senior executives
