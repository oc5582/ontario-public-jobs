ABOUT THE MTCC

Located in the heart of downtown Toronto, the Metro Toronto Convention Centre (MTCC) is Canada’s most successful convention and trade show facility. The MTCC welcomes approximately 1 million visitors each year, hosting a variety of events from meetings and conventions to public shows and galas. The Centre is an economic driver, a proud supporter of our community, and a meeting place where meaningful connections are made. Beyond helping planners create successful events, we believe that “Our People are the Centre.” Our talented team is our greatest strength, and we offer a corporate culture that values work-life balance, great benefits, professional development, wellness, and fun.

JOB DESCRIPTION

The Kitchen Department is currently looking for a Second Cook. This position involves delegating tasks and ensuring high quality preparation of all food items in a timely manner. The successful candidate is a dedicated, passionate individual who is knowledgeable; customer service oriented and with al proven demonstrated abilities in a professional kitchen.

Reporting to the Sous Chefs, you are an enthusiastic, organized, service oriented individual. Duties will include setting up all food preparations for functions according to MTCC culinary and food sanitation standards.

JOB RESPONSIBILTIES

Consistently offer professional, friendly and proactive guest service while supporting fellow colleagues.

Review the daily production sheets and prepare necessary food items in accordance with standards in a timely and efficient fashion

Adhere to standardized recipes and specifications in order to maintain consistency and ensure all standards are met.

Understands cooking percentages

Functional knowledge of kitchen areas including classic working stations coupled with fine operational skills in preparation and execution of modern cuisine

Assists with social parties, banquets and special events

Customer service focused

Ensures kitchen is clean and sanitary and is in compliance with all provincial requirements

JOB SKILLS & QUALIFICATIONS

Graduation from Culinary college

2-3 years of professional culinary experience

Completion of apprenticeship or equivelant

Ability to work nights and weekends

Strong culinary skills

Strong time management and organizational skills

Outstanding customer service skills with a can-do attitude

Food Handler Certification & WHIMIS

Basic computer skills

Detail oriented, ability to handle multiple projects simultaneously

Work well under pressure, both independently, and as team members
