ABOUT THE MTCC

Located in the heart of downtown Toronto, the Metro Toronto Convention Centre (MTCC) is Canada’s most successful convention and trade show facility. The MTCC welcomes approximately 1 million visitors each year, hosting a variety of events from meetings and conventions to public shows and galas. The Centre is an economic driver, a proud supporter of our community, and a meeting place where meaningful connections are made. Beyond helping planners create successful events, we believe that “Our People are the Centre.” Our talented team is our greatest strength, and we offer a corporate culture that values work-life balance, great benefits, professional development, wellness, and fun.

JOB DESCRIPTION

We are currently recruiting for a Cleaning Services Supervisor in our Cleaning Services Department. Reporting to the Cleaning Services Manager, the successful candidate will ensure that the building is maintained to highest standard of cleaning that the MTCC has been recognized for throughout its history. If you have a passion for customer service and enjoy working in a high energy and creative work environment then this position is for you. The successful candidate will have strong work ethic and demonstrated ability to foster an engaging team environment.

Cleaning Services Supervisor plays an integral part to the success of the cleaning services team. Encouraging, coaching and teaching staff to complete the assigned tasks up to the MTCC’s high standard is of the upmost importance to this role’s success.

JOB RESPONSIBILTIES

Ensure that the building is maintained to highest standard of cleaning

Ensure that the building is completely clean after each shift including booth and equipment cleaning, bulk trash and tape removal

Administer and ensure that company policies and procedures are adhered to, including all health and safety and WHMIS legislation

Administrative duties which including scheduling, timesheets and accident reports

Supervise to guide and instruct for the purpose of performance and use of equipment and supplies.

Inspect buildings daily to ensure cleaning is maintained to high standard

Assisting with department weekly schedule to ensure alignment with the Collective Bargaining Agreement and operational demands

Training and overlooking job responsibilities of new and existing staff

Train staff on Health & Safety procedures

Check Sign In/Out sheets for equipment and ensure accuracy

Track and complete staff shift sign in sheets and ensure accuracy

Inspect and record bulk trash amounts for each event for the purpose of billing

Track supply inventory levels and request additional supplies when needed

Distribute reminders on show floor to increase sales and promote booth cleaning to clients

Provide and complete client order forms on the show floor

In absence of Manager, represent the department

Please note that this position includes rotated shifts, nights, weekends and holidays. On occasion you would be required to fill in for the Cleaning Services Manager.

JOB SKILLS & QUALIFICATIONS

Strong working knowledge of power sweepers and scrubbers, plus high-speed floor machines

3-5 years of supervisory experience in a cleaning industry

Previous housekeeping experience preferably within a large facility is required

Work shifts on a rotational basis

Solid computer skills are required

Knowledge of Microsoft Word and Excel

Fluency in English is essential, French is an asset

Excellent customer service skills

Good communication skills
