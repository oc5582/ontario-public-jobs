BDC’s

Thrive Venture Fund (TVF)

is the largest of its kind globally, investing in Canada’s leading female entrepreneurs to drive transformative impact. The $350M fund was launched in the Fall of 2022 with a focused objective of investing in women-led, high growth, innovative technology companies in Canada.

We invest in venture-profile, women-led technology companies at the pre-Seed through Series C stage that are:

Solving problems with technology in large markets with growth potential

Pairing big ambition with execution capabilities

Dependent on stage, demonstrating early product-market fit with the potential for long-term sustainable competitive advantages

This role is temporary from January to August 2026.

POSITION OVERVIEW

As a student, you will support the TVF with a variety of tasks, including:

Investment Support:

Assist in preparing and writing investment memos, synthesizing key insights from due diligence and financial analysis. Research and analyze emerging markets, sectors, and business models aligned with the different focus areas.

Portfolio Monitoring

:Track portfolio company activity and relevant trends across social media and digital platforms to inform internal updates and storytelling.

Event Support

: Assist in the planning and organization of events, workshops, and ecosystem gatherings, including logistics, materials, and follow-up.

General Team Support:

Participate in team meetings, brainstorming sessions, and ongoing projects that contribute to strategic priorities.

WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

Excellent organizational skills and ability to manage priorities.

Excellent written and oral communication.

Collaborative spirit and strong interpersonal skills.

Autonomy, initiative and attention to details.

Basic knowledge of financial products and concepts (interest rates, mortgages, loans, financial statements).
