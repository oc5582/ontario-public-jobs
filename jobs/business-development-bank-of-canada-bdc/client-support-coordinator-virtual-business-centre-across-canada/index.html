POSITION OVERVIEW

As a Client Support Coordinator in BDC’s Virtual Business Centre, you will provide high-quality administrative and client support to clients across Canada. BDC is the only bank dedicated exclusively to Canadian entrepreneurs, supporting the growth of small and medium-sized businesses across Canada. You will respond to client inquiries, complete administrative activities, maintain accurate documentation, and collaborate with colleagues in a virtual team environment. Your work contributes directly to the service provided to Canadian businesses.

CHALLENGES TO BE MET

Provide client service to Virtual Business Centre clients by responding to basic inquiries and escalating more complex requests to the appropriate team members.

Respond to clients promptly and professionally while following established policies, procedures, and processes.

Complete administrative tasks through a queue management system, including preparing correspondence according to established processes.

Maintain accurate client information and documentation and complete required follow-ups.

Prioritize multiple administrative activities and meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment to maintain established service standards.

Stay current on changes to policies, standardized procedures, and processes.

Collaborate effectively within a virtual team while completing assigned work independently.

At BDC, we value inclusion, equity, and diversity, and you can be yourself at work while contributing to the success of Canadian businesses.

WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

Education: College diploma in Office Administration or bachelor’s degree in Business Administration.

Experience: Minimum of 3 years of experience in an administrative role.

Bilingual communication: Excellent French and English, both written and spoken.

Microsoft Office: Proficiency with Microsoft Word, Microsoft Excel, and Microsoft Outlook.

Ability to organize and prioritize multiple administrative tasks, maintain accurate information, manage documentation, and complete follow-ups.

Demonstrated ability to provide client service through active listening and problem-solving.

Ability to work independently and collaboratively within a virtual team environment.

Ability to work effectively in a fast-paced environment and consistently meet deadlines.

Ability to adapt to changes in policies, procedures, processes, and work activities.

Ability to work in a hybrid environment, both in-office and virtually.

Preferred Qualifications

Teamwork and collaborative approach.

Flexibility and creativity in responding to changing work requirements.
