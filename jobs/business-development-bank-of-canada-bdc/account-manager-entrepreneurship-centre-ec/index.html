Do you have a passion for business development and a strong network within Durham Region? Join Canada's only bank dedicated exclusively to entrepreneurs and play a meaningful role in helping local businesses grow while contributing to Canada's economic success.

POSITION OVERVIEW

BDC is Canada's only bank devoted exclusively to entrepreneurs. As a Commercial Account Manager, you will combine business development and relationship management responsibilities, helping entrepreneurs achieve their growth ambitions through tailored financing and advisory solutions.

In this role, you will focus on building new client relationships while managing a portfolio of small and growing businesses. You will partner with entrepreneurs to understand their challenges, identify opportunities, and provide solutions that drive long-term success. Joining BDC means becoming part of a collaborative, inclusive, and purpose-driven team committed to supporting Canadian businesses and communities.

OPPORTUNITIES OF THE ROLE

Meet with entrepreneurs to understand their business objectives, challenges, and growth plans, recommending financing and advisory solutions tailored to their needs.

Execute a business development and portfolio management strategy aligned with Business Centre objectives to grow and retain client relationships.

Build and maintain a robust pipeline of new opportunities through prospecting, networking, referrals, and community engagement.

Structure, negotiate, and present financing proposals while exercising sound credit judgment and risk assessment.

Develop and maintain strong relationships with Centres of Influence (COIs), including accountants, lawyers, brokers, and other strategic partners to generate referrals and business opportunities.

Represent BDC within the Durham Region business community through networking events, industry associations, and local initiatives.

Leverage your analytical, financial, and relationship management skills to deliver value-added solutions that support entrepreneurs at every stage of their growth journey.
