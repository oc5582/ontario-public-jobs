POSITION OVERIVEW

The Manager / Senior Manager, Portfolio Management contributes to updating the Bank’s loan portfolio by processing service requests and annual reviews required during the life cycle of a loan. He or she analyzes and recommends complex and advanced service requests and supports the Business Development team in developing efficient solutions to meet the client’s requests while adhering to the Bank’s policies and directives.

CHALLENGES TO BE MET

Follow up on outstanding issues in the files in accordance with BDC’s audit policies, processes, and procedures by ensuring that the forms and information are accurate and complete, the conditions are met, and the signatures are obtained

When completing an annual review, validate the profitability of the portfolio by monitoring the client’s borrowing activities and identify additional opportunities (financing or consulting) and be proactive to retain existing clients

Review financial statements, complete the financial analysis, and evaluate the risk rating

Actively address situations where potential risks are identified, such as arrears, risk deterioration and declining financial results

Evaluate high and advanced risk requests and offer proactive solutions to restructure agreements, minimize risk and mitigate losses in collaboration with Credit, the business centres and other internal colleagues

Authorize, decline, or recommend services, within the established parameters, and ensure a high calibre of analysis and due diligence for all loan amendments and service requests

Maintain relationships with business centre personnel and colleagues to foster good teamwork and the pursuit of common objectives and the consistent application of policies

Coach, mentor and share experience with team members

Keep abreast of economic trends in his or her region and any potential impact for the Bank’s clients

Keep abreast of changes made to policies, procedures, and detailed processes and of trends in credit and regulatory issues that may have an impact on BDC

WHAT WE ARE LOOKING FOR

Bachelor’s degree in Commerce or Business Administration

Three to five years’ experience as an account manager in the banking sector

In-depth knowledge of banking products, services, policies, processes, and procedures

Ability to analyze complex financial structures

Ability to identify and solve problems pro-actively with good judgment

Demonstrated flexibility to adapt to a changing environment

Good time management skills and ability to set priorities to meet tight deadlines

Cooperative attitude

Bilingualism is an asset
