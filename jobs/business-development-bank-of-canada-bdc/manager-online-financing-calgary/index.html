POSITION OVERVIEW

As Manager, Online Financing, you support BDC’s online financing and digital strategies by delivering an efficient and positive client experience throughout the loan application and authorization process. You assess online loan applications, conduct due diligence and credit analysis, resolve client issues, and escalate exceptional or unusual situations when required.

BDC is the only bank dedicated exclusively to Canadian entrepreneurs. Your work contributes directly to supporting the growth and success of small and medium-sized businesses across Canada.

CHALLENGES TO BE MET

Conduct thorough

due diligence and investigations on online loan applications

, including Know Your Customer (

KYC

) requirements, credit and loan analysis, and preparation of required documentation for final loan approval.

Apply BDC’s

online financing policies, procedures, credit criteria and decision-making requirements

consistently.

Prepare high-quality

credit recommendations for loan authorization or decline

, based on credit scoring and analysis.

Identify potential

credit and fraud risks

and prepare fraud files when required.

Analyze and negotiate financial deals and develop solutions that balance client needs with BDC’s lending requirements.

Communicate clearly and promptly with clients regarding the status of loan applications, including communicating declined applications professionally and diplomatically.

Collaborate with business centre colleagues and internal partners to provide a consistent client experience and application of lending policies.

Maintain accurate and up-to-date client information in BDC systems.

Stay current on changes to

credit policies, procedures, processes, CRM practices and regulatory requirements

that may affect Online Financing.

Help resolve

compliance issues or material changes

arising as financing transactions are negotiated and structured.

Provide support to Online Financing Analysts and other teams as required.

Contribute to an inclusive workplace where people can be themselves while working together to support Canadian entrepreneurs.
