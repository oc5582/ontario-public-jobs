How you will contribute as part of the TRCA team:

The Senior Manager, Geoenvironmental Services provides expertise and leadership in the development and delivery of TRCA programs, policies and services relating to hydrogeology and geotechnical engineering. Primary responsibilities relate to requirements defined by the Source Water Protection Act, the Conservation Authorities Act (e.g. slope stability/erosion hazards), and the Planning Act. The team consists of three hydrogeologists and three geotechnical engineers, with 4 staff being direct reports. Work activities are split between team mentoring and management, advising on hydrogeology and geotechnical review of development and infrastructure permit applications, source water protection including project delivery, ground water monitoring and analysis, and reporting; providing analysis and advice to municipal and provincial partners, and acting as an expert witness/subject matter expert on behalf of TRCA.

Major Responsibilities:

Guides the review of complex permit applications, in collaboration with the planning team, to determine the scope of the hydrogeological review required and confirm or adjust review scheduling as needed

Leads the documentation, analysis, and updates to hydrogeology & source water management processes and procedures, workflows and business processes to maximize efficiency and long-term management of data collection files in support of TRCA’s strategic goals

Leads the preparation of technical reports and communications related to projects and programs to guide hydrogeology & source water management research and development activities

Collaborates with planners on high level or politically sensitivity planning applications to guide client meetings and to support TRCA’s business objectives

Participates in Ontario Land Tribunal (OLT) and other hearings, tribunals, investigations that may be required as part of a dispute or legal action regarding a permit application, as required

Leads and manages a team of hydrogeologists and geotechnical engineers to ensure the application of TRCA policy and industry best practice on development applications

Communicates with team members regularly to assess workload and status of activities, amends work and reassigns staff as needed to direct workflow and accommodate for any situational circumstances.

Manages employee and team performance.

Ensures business processes are streamlined, documented and cascaded to team members.

Builds and maintains external stakeholder relationships; working strategically with internal and external stakeholders, utilizing networks to establish and activate connections to achieve organizational goals and to explore new opportunities
