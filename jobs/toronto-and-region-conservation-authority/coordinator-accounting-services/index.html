About TRCA:

With more than 60 years of experience, Toronto and Region Conservation Authority (TRCA) is one of

36 Conservation Authorities

in Ontario, created to safeguard and enhance the health and well-being of watershed communities through the protection and restoration of the natural environment and the ecological services the environment provides.

TRCA is considered the provincial leader in conserving, restoring, and managing natural resources to advance safe and sustainable development.

How you will contribute as part of the TRCA team:

The Coordinator, Accounting Services supports the coordination of key day-to-day financial activities and services.

Major Responsibilities:

Performs quality assurance review of accounts receivable and other financial activities for accuracy and completeness; collaborates with other accounting team members to ensure consistency in approach

Responds escalated accounts receivable, invoicing, and general accounting inquires

Identifies and investigates any issues with provider bills and obtains mutual resolution of discrepancies

Administers and processes procurement card statement logs and monthly journal entry,

corporate vehicle and equipment usage and processing of 407 charges; reviews related monthly statements for accuracy and ensuring appropriate authorization in compliance with policy

Creates and maintains financial accounting reports; supports month end close process, forecasts, cost analysis, to support financial reporting and business/operational analysis.

Analyses data and information to summarize account information, identify trends and discrepancies that require attention

Prepares accounting reports and information as required for audit purposes

What will you need to succeed:
