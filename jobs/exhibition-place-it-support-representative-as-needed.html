Job description

About us: Exhibition Place is Canada’s largest entertainment venue, attracting over 5.5 million visitors a year. The 192-acre site is an integral component of Toronto and Ontario’s economy, particularly with respect to conventions, sport, festivals, recreation, culture and tourism. Located at Exhibition Place is the award-winning Enercare Centre, Canada’s largest exhibition and convention centre which is now LEED Platinum certified, and Beanfield Centre conference venue which is LEED Silver certified. Since 2004, Exhibition Place has undertaken an environmental stewardship initiative, entitled GREENSmart; which includes the promotion of sustainable development, environmental initiatives and leading edge green technologies and practices across the site. Our environmental initiatives have resulted in award winning recognition across North America, such as the Platinum Waste Minimization Award from the Recycling Council of Canada and have established Exhibition Place as a world leader in energy-efficient technologie What can I expect to do in this role? Reporting to the IT Manager, the IT Support Representative will provide support for data networks associated hardware and telecommunications systems at Exhibition Place. Install pre-terminated Ethernet network cabling for events and clients. Assist with IT show operations and general computer networking infrastructure. Provide support to computer users for various systems, including Windows, Apple iOS, and other types of wireless network clients Assist end-users with connectivity and diagnosing issues on client side devices Provide support to third party events and clients related to the planning, installation and troubleshooting for internet, network and telecommunication devices Install, test and network cabling for shows and events Perform other duties as assigned How do I qualify? Knowledge of TCP/IP networking protocols, telecom, and Ethernet and some understanding of ICT services. Good interpersonal skills and ability to communicate effectively. Experience in Information Technology, including end-user support an asset. Knowledge of the IT structured cabling environment would be considered an asset. Proven customer service skills. Ability to troubleshoot effectively. Ability to work independently and in a team environment. Ability to lift up to 20 kg and perform job functions in a safe manner that adheres to health and safety guidelines. Must be detail oriented with an ability to communicate information via e-mail etc. Ability to workdays, evenings and weekends including holidays, as required to support operational requirements.
