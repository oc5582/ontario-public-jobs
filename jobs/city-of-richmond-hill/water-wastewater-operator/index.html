Department Community Services

Section Water and Wastewater

Shift Monday to Friday 7:30am - 4:00pm

Job Type Permanent Full Time

Replacement/New Position Replacement for Existing Vacancy

Posting Type Internal and External

Posting Date 10/01/2026

Application Deadline 10/18/2026 Position Summary Reporting to the Supervisor, Water and Wastewater, you will be responsible for the maintenance and operations of Water and Wastewater infrastructure. Key Duties and Responsibilities Water Quality Sampling Wastewater and Storm Effluent Sampling Maintenance and repair to water, wastewater, and storm infrastructure Confined space entry Respond and investigate sewer backups and storm flooding Visual inspection of sanitary, storm manhole and valve chambers Restoration Activities; sodding, concrete, interlock, and asphalt placement Traffic control and signage set up Sanitary sewer flushing Supervise contractor during infrastructure repairs Interacting with EMS, YRPH, MOL, Council and the public Watermain flushing Fire Flow testing Perform utility (water, wastewater, storm) locates Hydrant meter service and installation Customer Service water off/on, water quality complaints Perform regulatory sampling for lead, THM’s, Nitrates and Chlorine residual UV system inspection Perform inspection of PRV, drain and air valves Camera inspections of wastewater and storm infrastructure Inspection and maintenance of lift stations Cross connection investigation and remediation Provide spills response and cleanup; interact with MOECC and SAC Operate heavy equipment (e.g., dump truck, loader, flusher, backhoe, compressor, etc.) All duties must be performed in accordance with the Occupational Health and Safety Act, Safe Drinking Water Act and associated Regulations, City policies, procedures and practices Available for standby and shift work Other duties as assigned This position requires you to obtain Water Distribution Subsystem Class 2 and Wastewater Collection Class 2 within 3 years of hire Education and Experience High school diploma or equivalent Level 1 Certified is required First Aid CPR and AED training is an asset Spills Response Training is an asset Riggers Licence is an asset Trench Training is an asset Propane Training is an asset Book 7 Training is an asset 2 years prior experience is required Required Skills/Knowledge Successful completion of the Walkerton Clean Water Centre (WCWC) Entry-Level Course for Drinking Water Operators Ontario Class "G" driver license in good standing and must provide a current satisfactory Ontario driver's abstract from the M.T.O. Ability to obtain an Ontario Class "DZ" driver license within 6 months of the date of hire at the expense of the successful applicant Demonstrated experience in water distribution, wastewater collection systems and sanitary pump station maintenance Demonstrates the City’s corporate values of care, collaboration, courage, and service Demonstrated ability to prepare and keep records and read and interpret engineering drawings General knowledge of the Occupational Health and Safety Act, Safe Drinking Water Act, and sewer use by-laws Physically able and competent to perform the physical demands of this position under adverse weather and site conditions, including confined space entry Ability to work overtime in accordance with the provisions of the C.U.P.E. Local 905 Collective Agreement The shifts and hours are subject to change at management’s discretion in accordance with the C.U.P.E. Collective Agreement Compensation will be determined based on the certification level of the successful applicant The successful candidate will also be required to provide a satisfactory Vulnerable Sector Check within one month of commencing the position. This position is a member of the Canadian Union of Public Employees (C.U.P.E.).
