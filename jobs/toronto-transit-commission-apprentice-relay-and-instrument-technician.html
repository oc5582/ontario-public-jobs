Career Opportunity

We are currently seeking an Apprentice, Relay and Instrument Technician. This position follows a 40- hour workweek with various shifts and off days. Come join our team!

This recruitment may be utilized for future opportunities that become available.

What You Will Do

Successfully completing a training program involving progressively more complex testing, maintenance, repair and calibration work on electrical, electronic and microprocessor equipped power control, safety, timing, recording and detection equipment

Performing work involving the use of electronic test equipment and diagnostic procedures to determine the reason for faulty operation

Performing major overhaul and modification work, as well as responding to emergency situations and capable of physical work some heavy as required

Keeping up-to-date with the latest electrical/electronic instrumentation systems, and with the various equipment repaired by the section

Must successfully complete the Training Department’s C.P.R., W.H.M.I.S., and Subway/SRT Rule Book Training program and pass all associated tests

Must successfully complete The Training Department’s instruction for the operation of a TTC vehicle and pass all of the associated tests

Must successfully complete the Subway Infrastructure Department’s training program for Substation Hazard Awareness

Responsible for treating passengers and/or employees with respect and dignity and ensuring the needs of passengers or employees with disabilities are accommodated and/or addressed (within the area of responsibility) in accordance with the Ontario Human Rights Code and Related Orders so that they can fully benefit from the TTC as a service provider and an employer

What Skills and Qualifications Do You Bring

You require an education normally obtained through the completion of a 2-year Community College Diploma in the field of Electronics and Electromechanical, or its equivalent

You must have a valid non-probationary Province of Ontario Class “G” Driver’s License and in good standings.

You must successfully pass the S/E/C Department’s electronic aptitude test as part of the selection process.

Must have or rapidly acquire a comprehensive knowledge of the Ontario Human Rights Code and Related Orders including disability accommodation and accessibility requirements pertaining to passengers and employees

Note: Upon completion of the apprenticeship and successfully passing the associated examinations, the graduate will be classified as a Relay & Instrument Technician (Grade 1) in Wage Group 11.
