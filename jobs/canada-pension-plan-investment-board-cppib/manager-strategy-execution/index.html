Job description

Purpose. Performance. People.

Joining CPP Investments means joining one of the world’s most admired and respected institutional investors to drive a single mandate: to deliver strong, sustainable returns for generations of Canadians.

With a long-term horizon and global reach, we deploy capital at scale across public and private markets. Our size, stability, and disciplined investment philosophy allow us to pursue complex opportunities and build enduring partnerships worldwide.

For our people, this means meaningful work with tangible impact, real opportunity, and collaboration with exceptional colleagues who value partnership and performance. Here, you’ll contribute

to outcomes that matter alongside team members committed to excellence and shared success.

The Role

As Manager, Strategy Execution, you will support complex strategic initiatives across CPP Investments, helping define business outcomes, manage execution risks, strengthen governance and enable effective decision-making by senior leaders.

The Team

CPP Investments is a global investment management organization established to help the Canada Pension Plan remain financially sustainable for the long term. The organization’s strategy is built on diversification, active management and disciplined capital allocation to help the Fund remain resilient through market cycles.

The Strategy Execution Function supports the successful execution of complex strategic initiatives and helps build strategy execution capabilities across CPP Investments. The team partners cross-functionally to deliver key business outcomes, enhance efficiency and support long-term organizational goals.

Accountabilities

Support strategic initiatives across the project lifecycle, including project setup, execution, governance, budgeting, close-out and impact measurement

Define business opportunities, outcomes and OKRs to help initiatives achieve intended value and impact

Apply structured problem-solving, analysis and content development to support senior leader decision-making

Identify project risks, roadblocks and mitigation strategies, including change management recommendations

Provide ongoing support to MDs and Directors, including documenting meeting outcomes, actions, risks and decisions

Develop clear presentations, reporting and visual materials for senior leaders and Steering Committees

Establish KPIs and metrics to monitor business performance and provide insights to stakeholders

What You Bring

5+ years of relevant experience in strategic transformation and execution, ideally managing enterprise-level change initiatives

Experience in consulting, investment management or financial services

Strong ability to analyze and synthesize complex information and identify key insights that move initiatives forward

Experience working on complex projects or initiatives, including change management, stakeholder management and strategic frameworks

Ability to develop senior management materials, reporting and decision-support content

Strong stakeholder management skills, with the ability to build engagement across cross-functional groups

Ability to use innovative tools, including AI, to identify risks, opportunities and emerging issues, with a curious mindset and confidence engaging in thoughtful, practical conversations about AI

You are motivated to contribute to something larger than yourself, approach complex challenges with rigor, and hold yourself to

high standards in a collaborative, performance-driven environment.

We provide colleagues with cutting-edge AI tools, dedicated learning time, and practical support to help them deliver with greater impact.
