Job description

Purpose. Performance. People.

Joining CPP Investments means joining one of the world’s most admired and respected institutional investors to drive a single mandate: to deliver strong, sustainable returns for generations of Canadians.

With a long-term horizon and global reach, we deploy capital at scale across public and private markets. Our size, stability, and disciplined investment philosophy allow us to pursue complex opportunities and build enduring partnerships worldwide.

For our people, this means meaningful work with tangible impact, real opportunity, and collaboration with exceptional colleagues who value partnership and performance. Here, you’ll contribute

to outcomes that matter alongside team members committed to excellence and shared success.

The Role

As an Intern Analyst in the Systematic Strategies Group (SSG), you will contribute to systematic investment research across global markets and multiple asset classes.

Starting in May 2027, you will support end-to-end research on systematic signals, develop forecasting and monitoring tools using macroeconomic, market and alternative datasets, engineer robust data workflows, and help translate academic research into enhancements to investment models.

The Team

The Capital Markets and Factor Investing (CMF) department invests globally across public equities, fixed income securities, currencies, commodities and derivatives, and manages relationships with investment managers and co-investments in public market securities.

Within CMF, the Systematic Strategies Group develops and manages systematic investment strategies across global equities, fixed income, commodities, currencies and volatility. The team invests in absolute return and long-only strategies designed to provide a diversifying return stream to the total Fund.

Accountabilities

Support end-to-end research on systematic signals across multiple asset classes, including rates, equities, currencies and commodities

Build forecasting and monitoring tools that synthesize macroeconomic, market and alternative datasets

Engineer robust data workflows to support systematic investment research

Translate relevant academic research into enhancements to investment models

Contribute to practical research related to financial markets, portfolio management, portfolio construction and asset pricing

Qualifications

Currently pursuing a PhD in Finance, Economics, Computer Science, Statistics, Applied Mathematics or a related quantitative discipline; candidates at the dissertation stage are preferred

Experience working with financial and/or economic data

Strong programming skills, preferably in Python and SQL

Strong empirical modelling, econometric, analytical and problem-solving skills, with attention to detail and intellectual curiosity

Interest in applying quantitative research to financial markets, portfolio management, portfolio construction and asset pricing

Strong written and verbal communication skills and demonstrated ability to collaborate effectively within a team

Commitment to CPP Investments’ Guiding Principles of Integrity, Partnership and High Performance

You are motivated to contribute to something larger than yourself, approach complex challenges with rigor, and hold yourself to

high standards in a collaborative, performance-driven environment.

We provide colleagues with cutting-edge AI tools, dedicated learning time, and practical support to help them deliver with greater impact.
