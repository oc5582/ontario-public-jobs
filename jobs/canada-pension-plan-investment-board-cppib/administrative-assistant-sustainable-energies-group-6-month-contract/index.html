Job description

Purpose. Performance. People.

Joining CPP Investments means joining one of the world’s most admired and respected institutional investors to drive a single mandate: to deliver strong, sustainable returns for generations of Canadians.

With a long-term horizon and global reach, we deploy capital at scale across public and private markets. Our size, stability, and disciplined investment philosophy allow us to pursue complex opportunities and build enduring partnerships worldwide.

For our people, this means meaningful work with tangible impact, real opportunity, and collaboration with exceptional colleagues who value partnership and performance. Here, you’ll contribute

to outcomes that matter alongside team members committed to excellence and shared success.

The Role

As an Administrative Assistant, Sustainable Energies Group, you will provide day-to-day support to investment professionals across Toronto and New York. You will manage complex calendars and international travel, coordinate meetings and events, process expenses, and help the team operate effectively across global offices and time zones.

The Team

CPP Investments’ Sustainable Energies Group includes investment professionals working across London, New York, Toronto, Mumbai and São Paulo. Based in Toronto, you will work closely with investment professionals, administrative colleagues, and internal and external partners to support the team’s day-to-day activities.

Accountabilities

Manage complex calendars, meetings and correspondence for multiple investment professionals across levels and time zones

Coordinate global travel arrangements, including detailed itineraries and related logistics

Organize internal and external meetings, presentations and events in collaboration with colleagues, partners, assistants and vendors

Reconcile corporate credit card expenses and submit accurate reports through the Global Expense Reporting System

Prepare team members for meetings, track follow-up activities and help ensure action items are carried forward

Handle sensitive and confidential information with professionalism, discretion and sound judgement

Collaborate with administrative colleagues and teams across CPP Investments to manage requests, timelines and deliverables

What You Bring

Experience working in a fast-paced professional environment and supporting multiple professionals with competing priorities

Demonstrated experience managing complex calendars, senior-level meetings, events, travel and expense reimbursement

Strong organizational and prioritization skills, with close attention to accuracy and detail

Clear written and verbal communication skills and a professional, approachable manner

Proficiency with Microsoft Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and calendar-management tools, with an interest in learning new applications

Adaptability and sound judgement, with the ability to remain composed while managing time-sensitive work

Flexibility to adjust working hours when business requirements require support across global time zones

You are motivated to contribute to something larger than yourself, approach complex challenges with rigor, and hold yourself to

high standards in a collaborative, performance-driven environment.

We provide colleagues with cutting-edge AI tools, dedicated learning time, and practical support to help them deliver with greater impact.
