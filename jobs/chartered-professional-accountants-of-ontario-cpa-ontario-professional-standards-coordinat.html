Job description

Professional Standards Coordinator

Support legal excellence and fair, effective professional regulation.

CPA Ontario is not like other organizations

We operate at a rare intersection, both regulator and membership body, responsible for upholding the integrity of the CPA designation while supporting more than 105,000 members and 19,000 students across the province. Our work sits between business and the public interest, between the evolving demands of industry and the standards that make trust possible.

Careers built here tend to look different because the work itself is different. You are not simply contributing to an organization. You are helping shape a profession that touches nearly every sector of the economy at a moment when expectations, technology, and the nature of work are all being rewritten.

About This Opportunity:

CPA Ontario is seeking a

Professional Standards Coordinator

to support the investigation and prosecution of professional misconduct complaints against members, students, and firms on behalf of the Professional Conduct Committee. In this role, you will assist the Senior Vice President, Enforcement, and our team of Professional Standards Counsel with investigation and disciplinary processes, provide administrative and operational support to investigators, and help ensure matters are managed in accordance with departmental procedures. As part of CPA Ontario's Regulatory and Standards Unit, this role supports the protection of the public interest and the integrity of the CPA designation.

What You’ll Be Doing:

Prosecutions Execution:

Draft, prepare, and coordinate legal documents, court filings, and prosecution materials for counsel review.

Manage prosecution, judicial review, discipline, and appeal files, ensuring accurate records, compliance, and adherence to deadlines.

Conduct file reviews, conflict checks, legal research, and case law searches to support legal proceedings.

Liaise with courts, tribunals, process servers, and external stakeholders regarding filings, service, records, and procedural matters.

Monitor and administer decisions, orders, compliance activities, and post-hearing file management processes.

Investigations Execution:

Coordinate and support investigation files, tracking activities, budgets, deadlines, and case progress.

Prepare, organize, and manage legal and committee materials for investigations and Professional Conduct Committee proceedings.

Liaise with investigators, counsel, members, and internal stakeholders to facilitate meetings, communications, and file administration.

Maintain investigator records and support evidence review, document management, reporting, and investigation-related research activities.

Provide advanced document production, records management, and cross-functional administrative support to the investigations team.

Budget, Finance and Statistical Analysis:

Track and administer investigation, discipline, and legal proceeding costs, invoices, accruals, and payment processes.

Prepare financial reports, budget forecasts, cost recovery documentation, and departmental reporting.

Analyze and report on key performance indicators, caseload metrics, and operational trends.

Develop executive reports, dashboards, and presentations using advanced Excel and data analysis skills.

Support financial planning, budgeting, and reporting activities to inform decision-making and resource management.
