WHO WE ARE Opened in 1914 and welcoming over 1.3 million visitors annually, the Royal Ontario Museum (ROM) is Canada's most-visited museum, ranking among the top 10 cultural institutions in North America. At ROM, we are dedicated to building and sharing global collections, sharing knowledge, inspiring learning, and fostering community engagement. As a globally recognized field research institute, ROM houses an impressive collection of over 18 million artworks, cultural objects, and natural history specimens, showcased in over 40 galleries and exhibition spaces. We are committed to creating a space where art, culture, and nature intersect, connecting people and communities to the past, present and shared future. As we continue to work toward our goals to transform and evolve as one of the world’s foremost 21st-century museums, we seek passionate and dynamic team members who embody our mission and values. WHO WE NEED We are seeking an Assistant Manager, Visitor Experience to provide leadership for ROM’s front-of-house operations while driving the behind-the-scenes initiatives that help deliver an exceptional, visitor-centred experience across the Museum. Reporting to the Senior Manager, Visitor Experience, you will lead and support a large team of Visitor Experience Concierges, Lead Concierges, and Contact Centre Representatives while overseeing daily operations across key visitor touchpoints. You will take ownership of operational initiatives and projects that strengthen how the department works, including workforce planning and scheduling, technology and systems implementation, process improvement, training, budgeting and procurement, and operational reporting. You will use data, sound judgment, and a continuous-improvement mindset to identify challenges, improve efficiencies, and turn departmental priorities into practical solutions. Working closely with teams across ROM and external partners, you will help translate Visitor Experience goals into effective day-to-day operations while supporting new initiatives and organizational change. You will be comfortable assessing issues, establishing priorities, coordinating people and resources, and moving work forward with a high degree of independence and accountability. This role is ideal for an experienced hospitality, attraction, or customer experience leader who combines strong people leadership with operational and project-management skills. You are proactive, highly organized, digitally confident, comfortable navigating change, and energized by solving problems. You thrive in a busy public-facing environment while recognizing that an exceptional visitor experience depends just as much on the systems, processes, planning, and teamwork happening behind the scenes.
