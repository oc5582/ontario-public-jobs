Job Description

Canada Post is an exciting place to work! And you can be part of it!

We deliver billions of items to Canadians each year – and you can be a big part of making this happen. As a regular part time parcel delivery agent, you’ll be the face of Canada Post in your community and make sure customers and businesses get the parcels they’re waiting for.

This position offers a

guaranteed minimum of 15 hours per week and requires availability to work weekends

.

Does this sound like you? The role of delivery agent would be a great fit if you:

enjoy being outside and active year-round

want to work for a great national company

would be proud to serve your fellow Canadians

Job Responsibilities

What will you do?

Sort, scan, sequence, load, and deliver parcels

Operate corporate delivery vehicles safely in all weather and traffic conditions

Collect signatures and payments for C.O.D. (Cash on Delivery) and other accountable items

Complete delivery records using a Portable Data Terminal (PDT)

Job Responsibilities (continued)

Load and unload parcels, containers, and postal equipment

Perform scheduled and ad hoc parcel pickups and clearances

Perform internal facility duties such as shuttling corporate vehicles between parking location and loading platforms as well as loading/offloading incoming vehicles

Operate dock levellers, conveyor belts and other manual/mechanical equipment.

Qualifications

What must you have?

A permanent (NOT GRADUATE) provincial valid Class 5 or Class G driver’s licence with a safe driving record for at least 3 years (driver's abstract required)

The physical ability to lift items weighing up to 22.7 kg (50 lb) and carry items weighing up to 15.9 kg (35 lb) regularly throughout the day

The ability to work in all types of weather

Availability to work weekends is required

The ability to read, write and speak English and/or French

These help, too!

Experience in customer service

Experience making deliveries or working as a driver

In return, we offer:

Competitive pay

Extensive classroom and on-the-job training about your duties and safety on the job

Opportunities for full-time employment
