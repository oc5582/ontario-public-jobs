Make a difference

The Senior Manager, Operations provides senior leadership and operational command for the Community Safety Unit's 24-hour service-delivery functions. The position ensures that Operations contributes to safe, equitable and healthy TCHC communities through consistent, tenant-centred service delivery. Reporting to the Senior Director, Community Safety, the Senior Manager, Operations is accountable for the safe and effective delivery of field operations, Dispatch and Investigations, as assigned to the Operations portfolio. The position is responsible for operational command, deployment and service continuity; critical-incident response; workforce leadership; operational performance; and compliance within the portfolio. The Senior Manager translates approved CSU strategies, policies and service standards into operational plans and works with Corporate Services and other functional leads on policy, analytics, training, quality assurance, procurement and program development, while functional accountability for those areas remains with the responsible lead.

What you’ll do

Operational Command and Service Delivery

Provides senior operational command and oversight for the 24-hour service-delivery functions assigned to Operations, including field operations, Dispatch and Investigations. Establishes daily operational priorities, deployment plans and resource allocations in accordance with approved CSU strategy, service standards, assessed risk and demand for service. Maintains service continuity across shifts, manages emerging workload pressures, approves temporary resource reallocations and escalates material capacity or service risks. Leads the management response to critical incidents, major events and operational emergencies, ensuring timely command, notifications, coordination, documentation and follow-up. Ensures effective coordination and handoffs among Dispatch, field operations, Investigations and relevant TCHC teams. Translates approved Community Safety and Well-Being Strategy commitments assigned to Operations into practical service-delivery plans that balance prevention, engagement, de-escalation, diversion and appropriate enforcement. Coordinates CSU's operational response to fire and life-safety incidents and other corporate emergencies; accountability for the corporate Fire and Life Safety program remains with the designated corporate function. Participates in the scheduled management on-call rotation and ensures appropriate operational management coverage outside regular working hours.

People and Workforce Leadership

Leads and holds accountable the managers and other direct reports assigned to Operations, setting clear expectations for service, conduct, performance and collaboration. Builds a respectful, equitable, inclusive and psychologically safe working environment that supports employee well-being and effective service to tenants. Oversees workforce planning, staffing levels, scheduling requirements, attendance management and succession planning for Operations in collaboration with People and Culture and the responsible support functions. Manages employee performance and conduct within delegated authority, including coaching, performance improvement, recognition and discipline, in accordance with collective agreements and TCHC policies. Represents Operations in labour-management discussions, grievance processes, recruitment and promotional processes, while working within the authorities of People and Culture, Legal and the Senior Director. Identifies operational training and competency requirements, ensures staff complete required training and evaluates the application of learning in the workplace without assuming responsibility for administration of the training function. Ensures appropriate support, debriefing and follow-up for employees affected by critical or traumatic incidents. Develops the leadership capability of managers and supervisors and promotes consistent management practices across all operational functions and shifts.

Operational Performance and Continuous Improvement

Establishes and monitors operational work plans, service standards and performance expectations aligned with approved CSU and TCHC priorities. Reviews operational information concerning calls for service, dispatch performance, response times, workload, deployment, investigations, report quality and recurring service pressures. Uses analysis provided by Research and Analytics and other approved data sources to make deployment decisions, identify service gaps and direct corrective action. Conducts supervisory operational reviews and after-action reviews to identify lessons, improve execution and reduce the recurrence of preventable operational issues. Implements corrective actions arising from independent quality-assurance reviews, audits, complaints, investigations and other oversight processes applicable to Operations. Documents operational processes and recommends improvements to procedures, technology, workflows and interdepartmental handoffs. Provides operational requirements for contracted security services and monitors frontline service performance, escalating contractual, procurement or invoicing matters to the accountable functional owner. Develops and manages the Operations operating budget, controls expenditures within approved allocations and exercises signing authority in accordance with TCHC policy. Provides timely operational briefings, critical-incident notifications and performance reports to the Senior Director and other authorized stakeholders.

Operational Compliance, Risk and Readiness

Ensures that operational staff comply with the Community Safety and Policing Act, 2019, applicable regulations, Special Constable appointment conditions, the applicable memorandum of understanding, legislation, collective agreements and approved TCHC and CSU policies and procedures. Identifies, assesses, manages and escalates risks arising from operational activity and implements approved controls within the Operations portfolio. Ensures that incident reports, investigative records, use-of-force documentation, arrest-related records and other operational records are complete, accurate, timely and handled in accordance with approved requirements. Ensures that managers and supervisors maintain appropriate oversight of officer conduct, operational decision-making, enforcement activity and adherence to approved authorities. Assesses operational readiness, including staffing, equipment, technology, communications and contingency requirements, and communicates requirements to the responsible support functions. Provides operational subject-matter input into the development or revision of policies and procedures and ensures consistent implementation of approved requirements within Operations. Maintains operational emergency preparedness and business-continuity arrangements for the functions within the portfolio.

Operational Partnerships and Coordination

Maintains effective operational relationships with the Toronto Police Service, Toronto Fire Services, paramedic services, emergency-management partners and other public-safety agencies. Coordinates joint operational activities under approved strategies, agreements and authorities, including incident response, planned deployments and information sharing. Works with TCHC regional teams, Toronto Seniors Housing Corporation and other internal partners to coordinate operational responses, escalation pathways, follow-up and service handoffs. Participates in tenant meetings, safety initiatives and community-engagement activities where operational leadership, information or follow-up is required. Provides operational advice and implementation input to CSU strategy, policy, planning and Community Safety and Well-Being initiatives while respecting the accountability of the responsible functional owner. Represents Operations on internal and external committees and working groups as assigned. Performs other operational duties consistent with the purpose and level of the position.

What you’ll need

Post-secondary degree or diploma in public administration, business administration, criminal justice, criminology, emergency management, sociology or a related field, or an equivalent combination of education and progressively responsible operational experience. A university undergraduate degree and relevant senior-leadership, emergency-management or public-safety credentials are preferred. Minimum eight years of progressively responsible experience in community safety, public safety, law enforcement, security, emergency management or a similarly complex service environment, including at least five years leading managers or supervisors in a unionized, 24-hour operation. Demonstrated experience directing multi-function operations, workforce deployment, service continuity and shift-based response in a complex organization. Demonstrated experience managing critical incidents, operational emergencies and time-sensitive decisions involving public and employee safety. Strong people-leadership capability, including performance management, coaching, succession planning, attendance management, employee relations and discipline. Working knowledge of collective agreements and experience participating in labour-management, grievance, recruitment and promotional processes. Comprehensive knowledge of community-safety and law-enforcement operations, including incident response, investigations, enforcement authorities, de-escalation and operational risk management. Knowledge of the legislative and governance framework applicable to Special Constables, including appointment conditions, oversight requirements and the applicable memorandum of understanding. Demonstrated ability to balance prevention, engagement, de-escalation, diversion and appropriate enforcement when serving diverse communities. Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, including the ability to interpret operational data, assess service performance and direct evidence-informed improvements. Demonstrated experience managing operational budgets and allocating staffing, equipment and other resources within delegated authority. Excellent oral and written communication skills, including the ability to prepare executive briefings and communicate effectively with tenants, employees, senior leaders, elected officials, unions and external agencies. Strong negotiation, facilitation, conflict-resolution, mediation and relationship-building skills. Ability to exercise sound judgement, discretion, tact and composure while working under pressure in a complex and highly visible environment. Possession of a valid, non-probationary Province of Ontario Class G driver's licence in good standing and ability to satisfy all screening and eligibility requirements associated with the position and Special Constable appointment.
