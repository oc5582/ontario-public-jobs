Job Overview

Ontario Power Generation (OPG) is looking for a dynamic, strategic and results-driven professional to join our team in the role of Assistant Project Leader.

Reporting to the Manager, Projects, this position is responsible to support the successful delivery of assigned projects within multidisciplinary portfolios by assisting with project organization, coordination, execution, and documentation. Working under the direction of the Supervisor or delegate, you will provide technical support to matrix project teams while helping ensure Project Management and Contract Management governance requirements are followed.

Key Accountabilities

• Support the coordination and control of assigned projects or project packages through defined stages of project management, in alignment with established Project Management governance. • Assist with the implementation of approved project management processes, including phase-gating, integration, scope, schedule, cost, change control, estimating, communications, reporting, and risk management. • Support the planning and delivery of engineering, procurement, and construction activities to help ensure project objectives are achieved within safety, quality, budget, and schedule targets. • Assist in preparing business case submissions, project approval materials, funding requests, planning inputs, and related documentation for senior management, the Executive Office, and the Board of Directors. • Monitor and report on project or work package performance, including cost, schedule, variances, risks, issues, changes, corrective actions, and progress against project commitments. • Maintain effective communication and coordination with internal teams, field construction forces, operations, contractors, suppliers, regulatory bodies, agencies, and other stakeholders to support project objectives. • Support project meetings, documentation, turnover activities, and deficiency tracking to ensure project information is complete, accurate, and aligned with process requirements. • Visit project sites, manufacturers, and suppliers as required, while maintaining awareness of applicable procedures, project management practices, construction methods, and industry advancements to support effective project execution.

Qualifications

• Completion of a four-year university degree, preferably in Engineering, finance, business or economics • Minimum of two years of related experience supporting project management, project controls, or project delivery activities. • Strong understanding of project controls principles, including scheduling, cost management, risk management, resource planning, and performance tracking. • Experience reviewing project requirements and plans, preparing cost estimates, supporting request for proposal processes, participating in contract bidding and award activities, and contributing to projects through execution and close-out. • Proficiency with Microsoft Office and Power BI, including experience developing, maintaining, and analyzing dashboards and project performance reports. • Ability to analyze project data, identify trends, assess performance against key metrics, and communicate insights to support decision-making. • Strong problem-solving, organizational, communication, and interpersonal skills, with the ability to manage priorities in a fast-paced project environment. • Demonstrated ability to work effectively in a team environment and collaborate with internal and external stakeholders to support successful project outcomes.

The successful candidate will exhibit uncompromising integrity and commitment to upholding corporate values, and the OPG Code of Business Conduct.

What makes a career at OPG different?

With operations across Ontario, OPG is one of the most diverse power producers in North America. As the largest generator in Ontario, we meet approximately 50% of the province’s electricity needs, largely from low-carbon sources like nuclear and hydro.

As we work to achieve our vision of Electrifying life in one generation, OPG and our family of companies are also helping advance the development of new low-carbon technologies such as Small Modular Reactors (SMRs), refurbishment projects, and electrification initiatives to help power the growing demands of a growing economy. Join OPG and make history.
