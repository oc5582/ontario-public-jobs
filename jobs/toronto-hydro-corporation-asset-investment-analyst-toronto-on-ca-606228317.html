Reporting to the Manager, Engineering, the Asset Investment Analyst will provide a wide range of engineering support services that will ensure technical soundness, reliability, safety and cost effectiveness of the utility and its Electrical Distribution Power System. The incumbent is responsible for leading the development, management, and continuous improvement of processes and systems that are cross-functional. The Asset Investment Analyst will lead and/or support a number of initiatives in relation to stations planning, asset management, customer connections, strategic initiatives, and analytics used to address various engineering initiatives and draw insights that drive decisions.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:

Lead and/or support the development and analysis of projects related to Stations planning

Participate in site visits to obtain information necessary for project planning and execution

Collaborates with stakeholders in ensuring asset management related concerns considering system requirements

Demonstrates exceptional communication skills and tact in resolving complex issues that arise up, down, and across the organization and builds strong relationships with cross-functional teams

Participates in development of workflows and processes and models (descriptive, predictive and prescriptive) to drive quality, efficiency and effectiveness of reliability related initiatives

Compares alternatives based on cost/benefit considerations, operational constraints, standards, safety, and engineering principles, and recommends the optimum solution.

Provides timely support and consultation, demonstrates effective project management and process efficiencies; reviews develop, and/or presents business proposals, plans, designs, processes, operational procedures, and practices.

Actively supports the Manager in preparation of the monthly status report by measuring, monitoring, and updating relevant KPIs

Participates in implementing corporate goals/objectives

REQUIREMENTS:

Undergraduate degree in Engineering (Electrical Preferred).

Three (3)+ years of relevant professional experience in utility (preferred), engineering, design, manufacturing, consulting or industry with a focus on data analysis and management.

G class license preferred

Fluent in the application of enterprise tools for planning and project estimation (i.e. GEAR, SAP, etc), general office software, CAD, AM/FM, engineering records, and specialized engineering application software.

Project Management experience with good knowledge of planning processes is an asset

Experience with Alteryx, SQL and Tableau (preferably in a utility environment) is considered an asset

Experience with data analysis and data mining techniques to address and resolve gaps in data

Fully proficient in use of Microsoft Office including MS Excel, Access, Visio, and other Project Management tools.

Effective skills in project management operations, budgeting, business process, and productivity improvements

Knowledge of ML/AI concepts such as Supervised and Unsupervised Machine Learning models with a demonstrated project or prior work experience considered an asset

Strong verbal and written communication skills

Knowledge/training in asset management practices is an asset

Specific knowledge in power systems, substation design, and system planning is an asset

Self-motivated, operates with a high degree of independence and can visualize opportunities for change in support of continuous process improvement

Aptitude and experience in adapting to, managing, and facilitating change, willing to challenge the status quo

Strong negotiating/relationship-building ability

Proven effective problem-solving and analytical ability

Customer focused

Demonstrated knowledge of relevant safety and environmental legislation and regulations
