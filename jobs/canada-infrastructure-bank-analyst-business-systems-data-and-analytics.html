Job description

Headquartered in Toronto, the Canada Infrastructure Bank (CIB) is responsible for investing in new infrastructure projects. The CIB’s mission is to work with provincial, territorial, municipal, federal, Indigenous and private sector investor partners to transform the way infrastructure is planned, financed and delivered in Canada.

The Analyst, Business Systems, is based in Toronto and reporting to the Manager, Information Technology, requires deep expertise in data, analytics and modern data architecture using Microsoft Fabric. This role sits at the intersection of business strategy, data engineering, analytics and platform architecture, ensuring business requirements are translated into scalable, governed and trusted Fabric-based data products.

The analyst will work across the full delivery lifecycle—from discovery and problem framing to data design collaboration, backlog refinement, testing and production support—ensuring data products meet quality, security, governance and performance expectations.

The ideal candidate will ideally have an undergraduate degree in information systems or a related field, over three years of experience in data analysis and reporting and be self-directed.

Your responsibilities include:

Business and requirements analysis (core BSA)

• Lead discovery and analysis sessions to understand business objectives, reporting needs, KPIs and operational processes

• Translate business needs into:

• Epics, features and user stories

• Functional and non-functional requirements

• Clear acceptance criteria aligned to data outcomes

• Develop and maintain key artifacts:

• Business and data requirements documentation

• Process flows and use cases

• Business glossaries and metric definitions

• Serve as the primary liaison between business stakeholders and Fabric delivery teams

Microsoft Fabric data architecture enablement

• Experience with data modelling:

• Dimensional modelling (facts, dimensions, measures)

• Transactional vs. analytical data structures

• Data grain and aggregation strategies

• Experience contributing to enterprise data warehouse design, including:

• Subject-area modelling

• Historical data handling

• Data retention and archival strategies

• Understanding of:

• Query performance considerations from a modelling perspective

• Partitioning and summarization concepts (at a requirement/design level)

• Ability to assess downstream reporting impact of modelling decisions

• Define and document:

• Source-to-target mappings

• Transformation logic and business rules

• Data lineage and traceability across Fabric workloads

• Support design and delivery across Fabric workloads, including:

• Data engineering (lakehouse, notebooks, pipelines)

• Data warehouse (T-SQL analytics)

• Real-time analytics (event-based use cases, where applicable)

Microsoft Fabric and Power BI–specific skills

• Experience with Power BI semantic models, including:

• Measures, hierarchies and relationships

• Dataset reuse across multiple reports

• Familiarity with self-service enablement patterns in Fabric:

• Certified and promoted datasets

• Workspace design for business domains

• Shared OneLake data products

• Strong understanding of self-service BI and analytics concepts

• Experience enabling business users to create their own reports and dashboards using governed datasets

• Ability to define semantic layer requirements that balance flexibility and control

• Experience documenting:

• KPI logic and calculation rules

• Business-friendly data definitions

• Reporting usage and limitations

• Strong data literacy and ability to explain data concepts to non-technical audiences

• Understanding of Direct Lake vs. Import trade-offs from a business perspective

• Ability to support Power BI report validation and UAT with business stakeholders

Governance and controls for self-service analytics

• Ensure self-service reporting adheres to:

• Enterprise data governance standards

• Security, privacy and access controls

• Metric consistency across domains

• Partner with governance teams to:

• Maintain business glossaries and metadata

• Ensure discoverability through Microsoft Purview

• Help prevent metric duplication and “multiple versions of the truth” by enforcing standardized definitions and datasets

Your ideal profile:

• Experience with Microsoft Fabric, including:

• Experience with Microsoft Purview, an asset

• Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills

• Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to interact with technical and non-technical stakeholders
