Job Requisition Id: 201945 Business Function: Delivery Agent or Letter Carrier Primary City: Etobicoke Other Location(s): Province: Ont. - GTA Employment Type: Part-Time Employment Status: Permanent Language Requirement: English Essential Number of Vacancies 38 Salary: $23.81 Job Closing Date (YYYY-MM-DD): 2026-09-24 Job Description Canada Post is an exciting place to work! And you can be part of it! We deliver billions of items to Canadians each year – and you can be a big part of making this happen. As a regular part time parcel delivery agent, you’ll be the face of Canada Post in your community and make sure customers and businesses get the parcels they’re waiting for. This position offers a guaranteed minimum of 15 hours per week and requires availability to work weekends . Does this sound like you? The role of delivery agent would be a great fit if you: enjoy being outside and active year-round want to work for a great national company would be proud to serve your fellow Canadians Job Responsibilities What will you do? Sort, scan, sequence, load, and deliver parcels Operate corporate delivery vehicles safely in all weather and traffic conditions Collect signatures and payments for C.O.D. (Cash on Delivery) and other accountable items Complete delivery records using a Portable Data Terminal (PDT) Job Responsibilities (continued) Load and unload parcels, containers, and postal equipment Perform scheduled and ad hoc parcel pickups and clearances Perform internal facility duties such as shuttling corporate vehicles between parking location and loading platforms as well as loading/offloading incoming vehicles Operate dock levellers, conveyor belts and other manual/mechanical equipment. Qualifications What must you have? A permanent (NOT GRADUATE) provincial valid Class 5 or Class G driver’s licence with a safe driving record for at least 3 years (driver's abstract required) The physical ability to lift items weighing up to 22.7 kg (50 lb) and carry items weighing up to 15.9 kg (35 lb) regularly throughout the day The ability to work in all types of weather Availability to work weekends is required The ability to read, write and speak English and/or French These help, too! Experience in customer service Experience making deliveries or working as a driver In return, we offer: Competitive pay Extensive classroom and on-the-job training about your duties and safety on the job Opportunities for full-time employment Other Information Today’s Canada Post - Canada Post is an e-commerce powerhouse, delivering billions of items every year. We help small businesses grow. We enrich lives in remote communities. We connect Canadians with each other and to the rest of the world. And we do all this by providing residential and business customers with the best possible delivery experience in the country. We like to promote from within and can offer you a career rather than a job. While you’ll start out as a regular part time employee, you’ll have the opportunity to grow with us and obtain a regular full-time position … even try out new jobs in different areas of the company. Canada Post’s values and behaviours Our Values - Trust , Respect and Deliver represent our fundamental promise to ourselves, our expectations of one and another and our shared duty to our country. Our behaviours – Make the call, Know the destination, Deliver for others, Ignite our pride; embody our values, bringing them to life and guiding our actions.
