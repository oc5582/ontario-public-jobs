Hydro One is proud to be the largest electricity transmission and distribution provider in Ontario, serving nearly 1.5 million customers. We have a long history in the industry with our roots dating back over 110 years to 1906. Since then, we have worked to grow and evolve to meet the changing needs of our customers and communities across Ontario. Today, we’re focused on providing exceptional customer service and ensuring we are building safe communities where we live, work and play.

It’s an exciting time to join the team at Hydro One!

Job Function:

Collect and analyze information related to service level agreements, contracts or processes. Assist with compiling and reviewing information and estimates on distribution and transmission work. Assist with business process development needs, and participate in the provision of liaison and project integration. Provide advice and direction on processes related to service level agreements and contracts

Job Description:

Collect and analyze large volumes of data related to service level agreements, key performance indicators and Distribution work programs

Work with various stakeholders to define and develop business reporting for Distribution work programs leveraging tools such as Business Intelligence (BI), Microsoft Excel, Access, SharePoint, and SAP

Prepare reports and provide information, data or advice in response to internal and external requests

Utilize computer systems, software and analytical techniques to carry out forecasting and analytical studies to interpret and assess the execution of work programs within the Distribution business

Develop and document and/or utilize systems for the collecting, analyzing, presenting and reporting of information and estimates related to service levels, contracts, spend and accomplishments

Perform monthly and quarterly reporting and analysis activities for the Distribution Analytics team. This includes development and maintenance of scorecards, dashboards, performance metrics to support monitoring, reporting and continuous improvement of Distribution work programs, reports to support business planning, and others.

Prepare analysis and initiate studies or investigations where opportunities for improvement are apparent. This includes collaborating with other lines of businesses and analyzing operational, financial and productivity data to measure efficiency, track savings initiatives.

Assist in discussions, problem solving and issue resolution regarding ongoing work program performance

Participate in the assessment of organizational performance as it relates to the execution of work programs

Assist in the development of communication requirements relating to a wide variety of business operation processes and related subjects making presentations to various audiences

Provide advice and direction on service level agreements, contracts, processes and related activities and assist with audits to ensure compliance

Perform other duties as required

Selection Criteria:

Requires knowledge of computer information systems and business administration to be able to develop and/or utilize systems and processes for analyzing, presenting and reporting large volumes of information

Strong and demonstrated skills in Microsoft Excel

Proficient in software programs including Microsoft PowerPoint, Word, PowerBI, Tableau, SAP enterprise software, BI/BW or equivalent systems

Requires a proficient knowledge of English including written and verbal communication skills to be able to effectively prepare written correspondence, assist in discussions, interact with customers and deliver presentations

Requires experience handling multiple tasks while adhering to stringent deadlines

Requires some experience monitoring organizational performance

Requires knowledge of service level agreements or contracts and analytical techniques to be able to analyze the associated information and provide advice and direction

Requires experience in working effectively with a wide range of people as well as the ability to work independently

This knowledge is considered to be normally acquired through the completion of 4 years of University training (Grade XII plus 5 years). A degree in Business Administration, Commerce, Finance, Statistics, or Economics is considered an asset

A period of over 4 years and up to and including 6 years is considered necessary to gain this experience
