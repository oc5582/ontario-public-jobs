Hydro One is proud to be the largest electricity transmission and distribution provider in Ontario, serving nearly 1.5 million customers. We have a long history in the industry with our roots dating back over 110 years to 1906. Since then, we have worked to grow and evolve to meet the changing needs of our customers and communities across Ontario. Today, we’re focused on providing exceptional customer service and ensuring we are building safe communities where we live, work and play.

It’s an exciting time to join the team at Hydro One!

You will be a member of the above group, which is responsible for:

Load forecasting, load research and analysis projects

Your job duties may include

:

Undertake load research and data analysis to support Conservation and Demand Management (CDM) projects and load forecasting.

Undertake data analysis related to customer electricity usage.

Gather information about major industrial developments in Ontario.

Tabulate results and prepare reports.

This job will provide you with the following professional development

:

Hands-on knowledge working with economists to undertake economic and load research projects to support all business units in the company

Job Requirements

:

- Excellent computer and data analysis skills to analyse large data sets

- Excellent oral and written communication skills

- Academic Level: Completion of 3rd year study minimum

Discipline: Computer science, mathematics, engineering, economics, business

Experience in large data analysis, statistical analysis, data management and computer programming skills (SAS software or equivalent) is considered an asset
