Hydro One is proud to be the largest electricity transmission and distribution provider in Ontario, serving nearly 1.5 million customers. We have a long history in the industry with our roots dating back over 110 years to 1906. Since then, we have worked to grow and evolve to meet the changing needs of our customers and communities across Ontario. Today, we’re focused on providing exceptional customer service and ensuring we are building safe communities where we live, work and play.

It’s an exciting time to join the team at Hydro One!

You will be a member of the above group, which is responsible for

:

Acquisition of new property rights (fee simple or easement) for transmission line, transformation and distribution line work, including

acquiring/releasing easements on private properties

dealing with encroachments on transmission and distribution properties

managing low voltage rights

Market investigation and valuation

Providing land rights management services for electrical transmission and distribution facilities across the province, including: negotiating licenses/leases for secondary land uses on corridor lands owned by the Provincial Government or the Company dealing with encroachments on transmission and distribution properties acquiring/releasing easements on private properties managing low voltage rights managing property tax payments.

Reviewing and responding to municipal planning circulations that may affect Hydro One transmission assets.

Managing payments for easement rights on lands over which the Company's transmission or distribution lines cross.

Assessing and analyzing various real estate issues and preparing applicable real estate reports, summaries, spreadsheets and other similar documents

Recording, documenting, and storing property documents related to the management of Hydro One property rights and real estate assets

Your job duties may include:

Assisting Hydro One Real Estate staff in the above activities, with an emphasis on acquisition of property related activities but there could be an opportunity to assist with property management related activities.

This job will provide you with the following professional development:

Experience with the market investigation, valuation, acquisition and management of real estate assets of a large, publicly held company in Ontario's energy industry

Interactions with government (e.g. Provincial Ministries), public and private sector land owners, as well as legal professionals and elected representatives

Job Requirements:

Discipline: Real Estate (e.g. Bachelor of Commerce, Real Estate and Land Use Planning)

Academic Level: Minimum 2nd year of program completed

Other requirements: Must valid licence to drive in the Province of Ontario and access to a vehicle for periodic travel to work locations

Hydro One requires that all students applying for student opportunities be enrolled in post-secondary level studies and be returning to full-time studies upon the completion of their work term. Exceptions will be made in cases where students require a work term in order to graduate.
