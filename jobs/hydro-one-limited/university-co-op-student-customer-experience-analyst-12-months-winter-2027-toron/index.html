Hydro One is proud to be the largest electricity transmission and distribution provider in Ontario, serving nearly 1.5 million customers. We have a long history in the industry with our roots dating back over 110 years to 1906. Since then, we have worked to grow and evolve to meet the changing needs of our customers and communities across Ontario. Today, we’re focused on providing exceptional customer service and ensuring we are building safe communities where we live, work and play.

It’s an exciting time to join the team at Hydro One!

Job Duties:

Extract, transform and analyze Distributed Energy Resource Management System datasets in Microsoft Excel from Virtual Power Plant operations

Analyze market penetration and equipment adoption trends to inform business cases.

Analyze customer interaction records to identify and address customer pain points.

Communicate insights in PowerPoint or other formats with clear data visualization to convey complex concepts

Write reports and prepare presentations for internal briefings and external knowledge sharing

Facilitate cross-functional collaboration with technical teams and vendors, e.g. preparing meeting materials and actioning next steps

May perform additional duties upon request

Selection Criteria:

3rd/4th year or in a university master’s program in Math, Computer Science, Economics, Business, Earth and Social Sciences, Environmental Studies or Public Policy.

Excellent data analysis (especially using Microsoft Excel), research and problem-solving skills

English fluency and comfort with report writing

Experience in/knowledge of the Electricity Industry (Utilities, Renewables, Conservation, Demand Response, etc.) is preferred

Strong communication, interpersonal and collaboration skills

Skills you will develop:

Critical thinking

Data analysis

Strategic communication and presentation skills
