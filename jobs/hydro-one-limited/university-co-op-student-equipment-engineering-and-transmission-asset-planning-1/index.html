Hydro One is proud to be the largest electricity transmission and distribution provider in Ontario, serving nearly 1.5 million customers. We have a long history in the industry with our roots dating back over 110 years to 1906. Since then, we have worked to grow and evolve to meet the changing needs of our customers and communities across Ontario. Today, we’re focused on providing exceptional customer service and ensuring we are building safe communities where we live, work and play.

It’s an exciting time to join the team at Hydro One!

You will be a member of Equipment Engineering and Transmission Asset planning which is responsible for managing transmission station’s civil and electrical assets. This includes but not limited to maintenance planning, budget forecast and control, establishing strategies, and support rate filing activities.

Your job duties may include assisting equipment engineers in:

Updating major electrical equipment data base is SAP

Keep meeting records and Coordinate meetings with vendors and multiple disciplines in Hydro One

Upgrade and maintain the team SharePoint site

Developing technical specifications for major electrical equipment in transmission stations

Assisting asset managers in:

Data gathering and analysis using SAP and Business Intelligence module

Data analysis

Assigning maintenance plans

Writing codes and macros to automate data update and maintenance planning

Writing job aides

Strategy writing

Site visit for data gathering

Tracking projects’ progress

Budget control

Administrate team mail box

This job will provide you with the following professional development:

Technical Skills:

Obtain basic understanding of how power networks operates

Obtain basic understanding of High voltage and high power equipment

Obtain basic knowledge of maintenance planning

Obtain basic knowledge of Data base

Hands on experience working with SAP

Getting exposure to financial aspect of engineering

Technical writing

Soft skills:

Working in a professional environment

Obtain basic understanding of how a regulated business operates

Getting exposure to engineering project flow
