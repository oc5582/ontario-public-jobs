Hydro One is proud to be the largest electricity transmission and distribution provider in Ontario, serving nearly 1.5 million customers. We have a long history in the industry with our roots dating back over 110 years to 1906. Since then, we have worked to grow and evolve to meet the changing needs of our customers and communities across Ontario. Today, we’re focused on providing exceptional customer service and ensuring we are building safe communities where we live, work and play.

It’s an exciting time to join the team at Hydro One!

As part of a collaborative team environment, the successful candidate will support a variety of business and technology initiatives while gaining exposure to enterprise systems, automation, data management, and utility operations.

Your job duties may include:

Reviewing and understanding business requirements, functional specifications, and technical documentation to support solution development.

Utilizing system utilities, networking tools, and diagnostic resources to identify issues and enhance system performance.

Assisting with troubleshooting operational, user, and data-related issues.

Participating in technical design discussions, demonstrations, and semi-weekly stand-up meetings.

Supporting AI automation initiatives, Microsoft Copilot, SharePoint, Microsoft 365 (including Teams), Power Platform solutions, and SAP, including both direct and indirect user support.

Assisting field staff, business users, and support teams by responding to inquiries and helping resolve issues.

Supporting the development, testing, and implementation of process improvements and automation opportunities.

Assisting with periodic data cleanup, validation, reporting, and review activities.

Contributing to the sustainment of Hydro One's business operations and field work execution processes.

Performing other duties as assigned.

This job will provide you with the following professional development:

This position will provide opportunities to:

Learn about Hydro One policies, procedures, and operational practices.

Gain exposure to industry standards, best practices, and utility operations.

Learn how SAP enables key business processes within Hydro One.

Gain hands-on experience with enterprise applications, AI tools, automation technologies, and Microsoft 365 solutions.

Learn how technology solutions support field operations, work management, scheduling and work execution.

Enhance communication and collaboration skills by working with multiple departments and stakeholders across the organization.

Job requirements:

Currently enrolled in a degree or diploma program in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Data Science, Information Technology, Business Technology Management, or a related field.

Proficiency with Microsoft Office applications, including Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and Outlook.

Experience with SharePoint, Microsoft 365 applications, Microsoft Copilot, Power Automate, Power BI, SQL, scripting, or other AI and automation tools would be considered an asset.

Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with a passion for continuous improvement.

Excellent communication, writing, research, and presentation skills.

Demonstrated interest in technology, innovation, automation, and continuous learning.

Ability to learn new systems and technologies quickly.

Self-motivated and capable of working independently with minimal supervision.

Ability to manage multiple priorities and adapt to changing business requirements.

Valid Ontario Class G driver's licence.

Ability to travel occasionally across Ontario, as required.

Administer and support your assessment;

AI-generated scores and insights will support Hydro One’s recruitment process but will not independently determine the outcome. Authorized Hydro One personnel will evaluate candidates and make final hiring decisions.
