Hydro One is proud to be the largest electricity transmission and distribution provider in Ontario, serving nearly 1.5 million customers. We have a long history in the industry with our roots dating back over 110 years to 1906. Since then, we have worked to grow and evolve to meet the changing needs of our customers and communities across Ontario. Today, we’re focused on providing exceptional customer service and ensuring we are building safe communities where we live, work and play.

It’s an exciting time to join the team at Hydro One!

Planning Co-op Student will have the opportunity to learn about modeling of power system, application of AI and using power system analytical tools such as PSS/E along with internal tools within Hydro One.

Your job duties may include:

Assist system planners in executing the Regional Infrastructure Planning process

Work with planners in preparing various departmental reports, spreadsheets, and presentations

Assist in developing alternatives based on load forecast and analyzing asset investments related to transmission system planning

Assist system planners in conducting Customer Impact Assessment

Learn, research standards and other matters related to System Planning and leverage AI

Learn and Perform power flow studies and contingency analysis using PSS/E

Research, review, and provide inputs to industry policies and standards, such as NERC, NPCC, ORTAC, CSA, IEEE, IEC, and OEB–TSC/DSC in the context of transmission system planning and electricity industry

Work with other line of business units to develop regional historical load database, including data gathering and processing

Create report templates and conduct data analysis using VBA and Excel macros

Assist with enhancement and maintenance of SharePoint, incorporating AI, and supporting the Region Planning website

Perform other duties as required

This job will provide you with the following professional development:

An in depth understanding of operational, regulatory and financial framework behind utilities in Ontario and North America

Understand concepts related to transmission system by working with planners, attending meetings, and participating in webinars related to regional planning

Understand the power system of Ontario and its interconnections to the neighboring systems

Exposure to investment planning processes and optimization strategies

Enhance communication skills by working with multiple departments and market participants

Explore academic field of interest by taking on different tasks

Job Requirements:

Discipline: Electrical Engineering or Computer Science student is preferred. Candidates studying Mechanical Engineering with strong AI-related skills and experience are also encouraged to apply.

Academic Level: 2nd year and higher

Understanding of basic electricity principles and power systems

Computer skills and experience with Microsoft Word, Excel, Access, Project, and PowerPoint

Excellent communication, writing, research, and presentation skills

Understanding of System Planning or Regional Planning process is an asset
