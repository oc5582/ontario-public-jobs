Hydro One is proud to be the largest electricity transmission and distribution provider in Ontario, serving nearly 1.5 million customers. Enrironmental Reporting & Data Management Co-op Student supports internal and external reporting requirements through data tracking, consolidation, analysis, and process improvement initiatives. Assists with the organization and maintenance of SharePoint sites and document libraries, supports Business Intelligence (BI) and project reporting dashboards, and helps develop standardized reporting processes to improve data quality, accessibility, and efficiency. Works with stakeholders to maintain reporting tools, document management systems, and performance metrics while contributing to continuous improvement projects across the Environmental Services team.

Key Duties:

Track, consolidate, and validate reporting data.

Support internal, regulatory, and stakeholder reporting requirements.

Maintain and enhance BI and project reporting dashboards.

Organize, clean up, and maintain SharePoint sites and document libraries.

Develop and document reporting processes and procedures.

Analyze data and identify opportunities for process improvements.

Support document management, records management, and reporting initiatives.

Selection Criteria:

Currently in 2nd or 3rd year of a 4-year Business or IT Management program

Proficient in scripting and debugging.

Strong working knowledge of Microsoft Office (Excel, Word, PowerPoint, Visio, SharePoint)

Power BI or analytics tools experience is an asset

Strong communication, planning, and organizational skills

Knowledge of IT and business administration

Ability to function as a team member and agent of change

Excellent analytical, written, and oral communication skills
