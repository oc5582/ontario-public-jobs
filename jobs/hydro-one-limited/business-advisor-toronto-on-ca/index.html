Job purpose:

This exciting rotational opportunity on the Corporate Ethics team reports directly to the Chief Ethics Officer and offers the chance to make a meaningful impact across the organization. You will support ethics enquiries and investigations while providing guidance to leaders on risks related to the Code of Business Conduct and Whistleblower Program, including conflicts of interest, harassment, fraud, and reputational matters.

Job Description:

The role provides analytical, planning, process, communications and supports leaders and lines Of business planning cycles and business activities. prepares reporting, and ensures effective coordination and collaboration across functions. This role is primarily office-based with a strong emphasis on analysis, customer support, and operational execution.

Duties and Responsibilities:

Serve as a key point of contact for assigned leaders and business units.

Synthesize corporate priorities, challenges, and performance drivers to provide informed recommendations.

Prepare leadership briefs, meeting materials, reports, strategies and business updates to support decision making.

Provide guidance on processes, timelines and expectations for planning and reporting cycles and lead implementation of new initiatives.

Prepare and execute communications materials and strategies to support business initiatives and strategies.

Create, maintain, and distribute recurring business performance reports and dashboards.

Analyze data to identify trends, risks, and opportunities. Provide clear, actionable insights and recommendations.

Support adherence to internal processes, controls, and reporting standards.

Track and monitor deliverables, approvals, and documentation required for audits or corporate governance.

Escalate risks, issues, and dependencies when needed/required

Implement standard templates, tools, and reporting formats to improve efficiency and consistency.

Collaborate on continuous improvement initiatives and support the adoption of new processes. systems or technologies.

Build, foster and manage relationships with partners.

Support and contribute, as required, to the operation and performance of the business and partnerships.

Other duties as assigned.

Qualifications

Undergraduate degree in a related field such as Business Administration or Communications.

3-5 years of recent experience with data insights, business planning cycles and business activities, reporting, and providing coordination across functions.

Effective communication and critical thinking skills.

Able to effectively exercise sound judgment, confidentiality, discretion and integrity.

Able to build and maintain relationships.

Effective time management skills.

Able to effectively apply flexibility and identify constantly shifting priorities and deadline.

Financial acumen considered an asset.

Project management/project coordination experience considered an asset.

Advanced proficiency in Microsoft Office applications and knowledge of SharePoint, as well as SAP considered an asset.
