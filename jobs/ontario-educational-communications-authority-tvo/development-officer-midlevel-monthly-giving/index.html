TVO Media Education Group (

TVO

) is devoted to inspiring learning that changes lives and enriches communities. We are an internationally recognized digital learning organization that strives to engage, inform, and educate Ontarians of all ages.

Who we are

We are an agency of the Ontario Ministry of Education and a registered charity that contributes to learning, civic engagement, and public service media across Ontario. We provide free, trusted, and accessible educational content that can support Ontarians of all ages, regardless of background, location, or circumstance. To fulfill our mandate, TVO delivers curriculum-connected resources through TVO Learn and TVOkids, offers Ontario’s largest online high school through TVO ILC, and produces journalism, documentaries, current affairs programming, and podcasts with TVO Today that help Ontarians understand the issues shaping their communities.

What we’re looking for

Are you a high-energy relationship builder with the ability to inspire donors? Do you enjoy creating meaningful connections and engaging donors with an organization’s mission? TVO is seeking a passionate, collaborative, and results-oriented fundraising professional to manage our monthly and midlevel donor portfolios. Does this describe you? If so, we want to hear from you.

The Development Officer, Monthly and Midlevel Giving will be responsible for growing revenue and strengthening TVO’s community of loyal supporters. The incumbent will manage a portfolio of monthly and midlevel donors and prospects, engaging them with TVO’s programs and impact to encourage renewed and increased support. Using a mix of personalized outreach, direct marketing, and digital engagement, the Development Officer will provide thoughtful stewardship and create meaningful opportunities for donors to connect with TVO’s mission.

How you will make an impact

Manage a portfolio of 500+ monthly and midlevel donors and prospects, with responsibility for achieving annual revenue, retention, renewal, and upgrade goals

Develop and implement annual strategies and workplans for monthly and midlevel donor engagement in collaboration with the Manager, Annual Giving

Engage donors with TVO’s programs and impact to deepen their connection to the organization and inspire continued support

Encourage donors to renew and increase their support through thoughtful, timely, and relevant communications

Develop and implement strategies to grow TVO’s monthly giving program, including donor acquisition, conversion, upgrading, reactivation, retention, and recognition

Review annual giving campaign responses and donor data to identify and qualify new monthly and Midlevel prospects

Identify monthly and midlevel donors with the interest and capacity to become major or legacy giving prospects

Plan and support donor stewardship activities and events

Coordinate with external vendors on donor premiums, gift processing, and other services that support the Philanthropy team

Collaborate with Marketing, Communications, and other internal teams to develop donor communications, engagement opportunities, and stewardship materials

Oversee and contribute to the creative development, messaging, and segmentation of monthly and midlevel donor mailings, emails and outreach campaigns

Monitor and report on program performance, donor activity, and campaign results, recommending opportunities to strengthen engagement and increase revenue

Maintain accurate and timely donor records, contact reports, and portfolio activity in Raiser’s Edge and related systems

Conduct ongoing evaluation of TVO’s monthly and midlevel giving programs and recommend improvements to program design, donor journeys, and engagement strategies

Ensure fundraising activities reflect donor stewardship standards, ethical fundraising practices, and TVO’s corporate and legal requirements

Ensure vendor and supplier agreements meet TVO’s corporate and legal requirements

What you will bring to TVO

Minimum of three years of fundraising, donor relations, or donor stewardship experience

Knowledge of fundamental fundraising practices, including donor cultivation, solicitation, and stewardship

Demonstrated ability to build strong relationships and engage donors

A genuine interest in learning about donors’ motivations, interests, and connection to TVO’s mission

Strong interpersonal and communication skills, with the confidence to initiate conversations and make personalized fundraising asks

Experience managing a donor portfolio and developing tailored engagement or stewardship strategies

Strong organizational and time-management skills, with the ability to manage multiple priorities simultaneously

Experience using donor databases and maintaining accurate records, with Raiser’s Edge experience considered an asset

Ability to review donor information and campaign results to identify trends, prospects, and opportunities for increased engagement

Resourceful, proactive, and comfortable working independently with minimal supervision

Collaborative and able to work effectively with colleagues across teams and departments

Flexible and solutions-oriented, with the ability to anticipate, identify, and resolve problems

Demonstrates TVO’s values of Excellence & Innovation, Ownership, Collaboration, Trust and Respect
