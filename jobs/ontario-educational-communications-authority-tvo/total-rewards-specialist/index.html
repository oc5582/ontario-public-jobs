TVO Media Education Group (

TVO

) is devoted to inspiring learning that changes lives and enriches communities. We are an internationally recognized digital learning organization that strives to engage, inform, and educate Ontarians of all ages.

Who we are

We are an agency of the Ontario Ministry of Education and a registered charity that contributes to learning, civic engagement, and public service media across Ontario. We provide free, trusted, and accessible educational content that can support Ontarians of all ages, regardless of background, location, or circumstance. To fulfill our mandate, TVO delivers curriculum-connected resources through TVO Learn and TVOkids, offers Ontario’s largest online high school through TVO ILC, and produces journalism, documentaries, current affairs programming, and podcasts with TVO Today that help Ontarians understand the issues shaping their communities.

What we’re looking for

TVO is seeking a Total Rewards Specialist to join our People & Culture team. This position will be responsible for supporting various processes and programs within Compensation, Benefits and Pension including market research, data analysis, benefits program support as well as assisting with the development and management of other Total Rewards Initiatives. If this sounds like you, read on!

How you will make an impact

These responsibilities are cyclical and are carried out at different times throughout the year.

Total Rewards Program Administration:

Support in the development, analysis, implementation, administration and communication of Total Rewards programs.

Manage the annual compensation cycle including market research, creating and administering salary increases, bonus/pay for performance data files, and ensure accuracy and compliance.

Respond to and investigate Total Rewards queries from staff and management. Work with service providers to resolve claims, coverage and service issues.

Perform audits on payroll, compensation and benefits to ensure compliance and accuracy.

Support the design, administration, participation and analysis of salary, benefits and related surveys to inform the ongoing development of our Total Rewards strategy.

Provide analytical and modelling support; present data-driven recommendations to HR leadership and business leaders to show overall market trends and competitiveness. Identify opportunities to improve employee retention and engagement.

Serve as a key consultant to Talent Acquisition and Management, providing data-backed compensation recommendations for new hires, promotions, and internal transfers.

Conduct analysis of internal and external salary data to assess the competitiveness of our compensation programs and ensure internal equity across all departments and levels.

Calculate Pension Adjustments (PA) and create PA files for reporting purposes.

Ensure compensation and benefit programs are evaluated regularly for effectiveness, utilization, and cost efficiency and identify anomalies and make recommendations to improve competitiveness and maintain internal equity to the Director Total Rewards & HRIS.

Job Evaluation:

Conduct job evaluations for new/revised roles. Participate in the Job Evaluation Committee and foster strong working relationships with the Unions.

Manage and audit TVO’s job description library, ensuring all content is current, accurately reflects role responsibilities and is graded appropriately.

Stakeholder Collaboration:

Responds to Employee and management inquiries, provides guidance on total rewards programs and policies.

Support the Labour Relations Team in preparation for collective bargaining negotiations, gather and analyze total rewards data, conduct cost modelling, compile reports and documentation.

Work with service providers and Labour Relations team to implement collective bargaining agreement changes related to total rewards; calculate wage rate increases per collective bargaining agreement and update pay scales.

Work with Finance for year-end related tasks (e.g. pay for performance) and providing cost estimates as required by Finance.

Act as a point of contact with Total Rewards Vendors (e.g. Ontario Pension Board, Manulife, Telus Heath) to support a strong partnership and ensure TVO’s needs are met.

Overarching Support:

Develop and maintain reports, dashboards and analytics related to compensation, benefits and total reward.

Ensure timely completion of government reports including Workforce Data Analysis, Span of Control Analysis.

Identify opportunities for process improvements to enhance efficiency, accuracy, and controls in total rewards processes and make recommendations.

Participate in HR programs, special projects and process improvement initiatives.

May serve as backup coverage during vacation, absence or peak periods to support business continuity for:

HRIS Analyst in HRIS administration including ensuring system security by managing end-user access, preparing dashboards on HR metrics, ad hoc reports generation, mass data uploads etc.

People & Culture Coordinator in responding to employees’ inquiries regarding paid time off, benefit entitlements etc.

Payroll Specialist in processing and running regular bi-weekly/monthly payroll and administering payroll deductions and remittances.

Other duties as assigned

What you will bring to TVO

A degree in Human Resources Management with 3 to 5 years of experience in Total Rewards or as an HR Analyst

Professional designation (CCP, CEBS or other equivalent designation in progress or completed) is an asset

Strong knowledge of compensation principles, salary structures, market benchmarking and benefits administration

Experience with job evaluation, salary benchmarking, benefits renewal and vendor management

Advanced MS Excel (i.e. pivot tables, VLOOKUP’s, advanced formulas) and modelling with large and complex HR/Financial data sets

Proficient in the use of Microsoft applications: Outlook, Word and PowerPoint

A high degree of accountability and pride in accuracy and data integrity

Track record for results, analytical acumen while managing multiple projects with competing deadlines

Strong attention to detail and a demonstrated aptitude for data analysis and problem solving

Solid knowledge of HRIS (e.g. Ceridian, ADP, UKG)

Motivated to pursue new and alternative strategies along with a focus on continuous improvement.

Experience working in an unionized environment and public sector is an asset

Handles confidential information with sound judgment, discretion, and professionalism.

With learning at the centre of everything we do, we invest in developing our people and promoting a culture of continuous learning. You might even get to meet Polkaroo!
