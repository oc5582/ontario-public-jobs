Job Function: Work closely with line of business stakeholders to influence and address their use of technology to improve their processes and work functions. Take an active role in the annual work program of complex technology projects over the full project life-cycle. Provide leadership, guidance and technical expertise in the management of projects, directing staff, contractors and vendor personnel with respect to execution of projects, monitoring of activities and achievements, maintenance of schedules and monitoring of costs. Be responsible of how the business will evolve through deployment of new technology. Coordinate stakeholders to create consensus on requirements which will be translated into business applications. Facilitate documentation of these requirements. Liaise with specialists within and outside the company for the exchange of data and consultation in areas of mutual interests and to integrate the recommendations into the overall strategies and program plans. Plan, schedule and assign the work, provide guidance and check the work to maintain quality, accuracy and productivity. Manage staffing needs, interview/selection process, staff development and adherence to HR/Corporate policies and administer department budget. Take an active role in highly complex multi-year projects aimed at implementing required new functions, system evolution and sustainment refresh of the technology supporting multiple lines of business within the Company. Manage several small to mid-size projects while estimating, planning and overseeing entire program of in-scope projects being implemented by other project managers, technical staff and service providers. Help create and support implementation of Project Management best practices within the Program Management Office. Create & maintain a project library and coordinate the collection and filing of project documentation for audit and continuous improvement purposes.
