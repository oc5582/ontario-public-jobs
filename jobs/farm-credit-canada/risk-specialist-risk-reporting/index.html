How you’ll make an impact

Leverage your expertise in enterprise risk management to strengthen how FCC reports on risk. As a Risk Specialist, you’ll collaborate with key risk management partners, including senior leaders, to develop clear risk reporting and provide insights and recommendations that influence business decisions.

As a strong communicator and analytical thinker, you’ll research and apply industry best practices to produce accurate, concise risk reporting for management committees and the Board of Directors.

If you’re a team player with strong attention to detail and can turn complex information into clear reporting insights for senior leadership, this might be the role for you.

What you’ll do

Work with stakeholders and partners to understand and translate business practices and information into meaningful risk reports

Support and advise on enterprise risk reporting activities, including our Top & Emerging risk program and quarterly risk reports

Guide business partners through reporting expectations, effective governance, and the roles of the three lines of defence

Use expertise, research and analysis to advance and enhance FCC’s enterprise risk reporting practices

Develop and maintain risk reporting processes, methodologies and tools for reporting, and coach others on how to apply them effectively

What you’ll bring to the team

Required qualifications:

A degree in economics, business administration or a related field

At least four years of experience in a risk management environment (or an equivalent combination of education and experience)

Extensive knowledge of enterprise risk management in areas such as risk reporting

Experience developing reporting tools

Experience with industry standards and frameworks

Preferred qualifications:

Certifications related to risk management, finance or accounting, such as CPA, CRM or CRMP

Experience in financial services or regulated industries

Knowledge of corporate insurance and artificial intelligence (internal, external and vendor use)
