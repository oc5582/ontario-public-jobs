How you’ll make an impact

Leverage your extensive legal expertise in ethics and values, regulatory compliance, privacy, and access to information to lead programs that support the highest level of integrity at FCC.

As Director, Ethics, Regulatory Compliance and Privacy, you’ll provide strategic direction and ensure the continuous improvement of these programs, integrating them into the organization’s operations and risk management practices. Your professional judgment, analytical thinking, and leadership will enable you to set clear standards, oversee reporting to senior management, the President and CEO, and the Board of Directors, and lead a team of professionals.

If you are an ethical, collaborative, and influential person who can clearly communicate complex information and address significant issues in a fast‑paced environment, this could be the role for you.

What you’ll do

Develop and implement a strategic plan defining employee integrity standards in accordance with legal requirements, public‑sector guidance, and standards applicable to Canadian Crown corporations

Lead integrity, regulatory compliance, privacy, and access to information programs, and ensure their continuous improvement

Align the regulatory compliance and privacy programs with the organization’s risk appetite, in collaboration with risk management leaders

Oversee reports for senior management, the President and CEO, and the Board of Directors, and make recommendations regarding tolerance, reporting approaches, and program outcomes

Develop and maintain strategic relationships with federal officials, legal counsel and counterparts at Canadian Crown corporations

Provide leadership, guidance, and support to the ethics, regulatory compliance, and privacy teams by establishing clear expectations, priorities, and objectives

What you’ll bring to the team

Required qualifications:

A university degree in law, business administration, risk management, governance, public policy, or in a related field

A minimum of ten years of progressive, relevant professional experience, ideally in a complex, regulated, or public-sector organization, or an equivalent combination of education and experience

Demonstrated ability to translate legislative, regulatory, or policy requirements into practical, risk‑proportionate frameworks, programs, and controls

Proven ability to lead a high‑performing team of specialists, along with strong coaching, communication, and relationship‑building skills

Significant experience in one or more of the following fields: regulatory compliance, risk management, ethics, governance, privacy, law, audit, or a related field

Proficiency in both official languages (English and French) and proven ability in oral and written communication

Preferred qualifications:

Experience integrating regulatory compliance or privacy programs into enterprise risk management

Experience building relationships with federal oversight bodies or counterparts at Canadian Crown corporations

Experience developing learning, coaching, or communication strategies to raise awareness and strengthen organizational standards

Excellent judgment, rigorous analytical thinking, and a high degree of professional ethics and integrity
