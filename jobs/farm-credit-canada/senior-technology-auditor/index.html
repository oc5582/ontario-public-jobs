How you’ll make an impact

Lead, plan and execute risk-based audits focused on information technology systems,

and information management. As a collaborative, risk-focused Senior Technology Auditor, you’ll be instrumental in strengthening FCC’s ability to manage technology risks by defining audit scope focused on high-risk areas, determining the key controls required to mitigate those risks, and assessing its design and operating effectiveness.

Using an Agile auditing methodology, you’ll translate complex technical issues into clear, actionable insights that support informed decision-making by leadership. You’ll also validate the implementation of outstanding audit recommendations and collaborate closely with IT and Internal Audit colleagues to strengthen control practices and support the organization’s strategic objectives.

What you’ll do:

Plan and conduct audit engagements focused on information technology risks

Document appropriate audit evidence, findings and conclusions

Prepare draft audit reports to include recommendations targeted at the root cause of the deficiency

Conduct follow-up testing to validate that management action plans are implemented and effective

Recommend improvements to processes and procedures to enhance audit quality

Adhere to professional standards and best practices

Provide coaching and mentorship to senior auditors

Required qualifications:

A bachelor’s degree in computer science, information systems, accounting/commerce or a related field

At least eight years of IT/technology audit experience

A Certified Internal Systems Auditor (CISA) or other applicable designation (for example, CIA, CPA)

Extensive knowledge of internal auditing practices and application of internal auditing standards

Demonstrated capability across end-to-end audit delivery, including risk-based planning, field work/testing, clear reporting and effective stakeholder engagement
