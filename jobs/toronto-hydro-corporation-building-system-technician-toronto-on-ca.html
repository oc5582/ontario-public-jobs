The Building System Technician is responsible for operation, maintenance and troubleshooting on System buildings and equipment. This role involves hands-on work across a variety of building systems, including electrical, mechanical, plumbing, and life safety systems. The incumbent will inspect, maintain, repair equipment, and ensure compliance with safety and operational standards.

This posting is to fill 1 position, which is an existing vacancy. Toronto Hydro reserves the right, in alignment with the applicable collective agreement, to finalize the number of positions filled.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:

Checks equipment, layout work and makes reports and recommendation, acting in a lead hand function

Performs general maintenance of buildings and building systems.

Participates in the installation, servicing, repair and operation of electrical and mechanical equipment and systems, including AV systems.

Maintains building plumbing and drain systems

Assists trade and service personnel to maintain various building systems

Distributes, follows up, checks work, answers inquiries and gives guidance as required

Maintains building and yard electrical equipment

Maintains and repairs office furniture, equipment and materials

Conducts and or directs work in a manner that is sensitive to customer and public needs

Supports Capital Project work

Assists with the maintenance of various Life Safety Systems throughout the facilities

Maintains stock of building supplies, monitors equipment and maintains records

Operates a vehicle requiring a class G licence and associated equipment related to the position

REQUIREMENTS:

A valid Red Seal certification with a high school diploma or a two (2) year college diploma with relevant demonstrated practical experience

Valid Class G Ontario Driver’s License or higher in good standing

Physically able to perform the essential duties in all weather conditions

Knowledge of the Ontario Building Code and any other applicable legislation

Demonstrated proficiency in a PC Windows environment and associated desktop software applications

Safety Performance – Complies with safety guidelines

Customer Service & Trust – Provides timely and effective services. Builds credibility and trust with customer and treats them respectfully and professionally

Leadership – Keeps people informed, promotes team morale, safety, and productivity
