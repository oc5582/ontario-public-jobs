Job Posting Description:

Senior Test Analyst

This position is onsite #LI-Onsite

Would you like to play an important role in the Testing Centre of Excellence? Reporting to the Manager, Testing Center of Excellence (TCoE), you will lead planning, designing, executing and reporting on software testing activities and results for assigned projects. You will craft and execute test strategies, test cases, coordinate the resolution of defects and bugs, and ensure effective use of available testing tools. You will work with other testers that may require assistance with their testing activities, create test plans and test executions.

If you have a customer-oriented focus, and enjoy a fast, exciting and process driven environment, then this role is for you!

About the Role

Provide regular and timely reporting on the status of test planning, testing metrics, defects, and execution activities, including providing regular status updates to the project team and executives

Lead and conduct project gate reviews to ensure they adhere to gating criteria

Participate in all SDLC lifecycle phases using the following methodologies (Hybrid/Waterfall/Agile) for small to mid-sized projects, complete test planning /strategy, and test estimation processes

Gather process requirements and analyze information to ensure appropriate test coverage. Recommend the different types of testing and approach that is required

Communicate and negotiate testing timelines, resources, scope, and critical targets with project managers and the TCoE manager from an efficiency and a risk management perspective

Conduct Risk based assessments to ensure we have the right amount of testing in the correct areas

Ensure timely notification of issues, risks, and problems, and make recommendations for resolution and/or escalation to the appropriate manager

Create automation-ready manual test cases or recommend automated regression suites for and end-to-end test cases; modify existing scripts when they fail to run

Conduct functional, E2E, System, SIT and UAT testing where required as well as conduct bug triage meetings

Guide, assist and provide mentorship to project testing resources on policies, procedures and testing activities associated with the TCoE processes

Other duties as required

Must Have:

Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science or equivalent work experience

5+ years of IT Quality Assurance and execution experience on projects in agile and waterfall environments

5+ years experience in testing mobile, web, and back-end services

Hands-on experience with JIRA, Zephyr, confluence

Strong manual and automated testing

proficiency using industry standard best practice quality assurance techniques and methodologies, preference will be given to individuals that have some test automation hands-on experience maintaining and executing scripts

Some Retail system testing would be considered an asset

Customer-focused, collaborative, with good communication skills; enjoy working with all levels of business

Adept at managing and meeting multiple priorities and conflicting deadlines in a highly matrixed organization

Nice to Have:

Experience with SAAS Based applications: SAP, BY OMS, AFW

, GK POS

Experience with TOSCA automation tool

In-depth experience in maintaining test automation scripts and a dedication to continuous improvement

QAI CSTE, ISTQB Certification or equivalent experience
