Job description

Purpose. Performance. People.

Joining CPP Investments means joining one of the world’s most admired and respected institutional investors to drive a single mandate: to deliver strong, sustainable returns for generations of Canadians.

With a long-term horizon and global reach, we deploy capital at scale across public and private markets. Our size, stability, and disciplined investment philosophy allow us to pursue complex opportunities and build enduring partnerships worldwide.

For our people, this means meaningful work with tangible impact, real opportunity, and collaboration with exceptional colleagues who value partnership and performance. Here, you’ll contribute

to outcomes that matter alongside team members committed to excellence and shared success.

The Role

As an Executive Assistant, you will support four department-aligned Managing Directors within Technology & Data, serving as a key coordination point across their priorities and leadership engagements. You will proactively manage complex calendars and competing demands, establish an effective operating rhythm, and anticipate needs to help maximize leadership effectiveness and focus. Success in this role requires strong judgment, discretion, and the ability to connect priorities and look beyond individual calendars.

The Team

Technology & Data provides the technology and data capabilities that support CPP Investments and its investment activities. The Executive Assistant will work across four department-aligned Managing Directors, helping coordinate priorities, activities and administrative requirements while partnering closely with Executive Assistants and teams across the organization.

Accountabilities

Provide proactive administrative and coordination support to four Managing Directors and their broader teams, helping manage competing priorities and maintain an effective operating rhythm

Manage complex calendars and coordinate internal and external meetings, calls, room bookings and logistics across global departments and offices

Coordinate complex global travel arrangements and itineraries, including related administrative and expense processes

Prepare and coordinate Executive- and department-level documents and submissions, ensuring accuracy, attention to detail and timely delivery

Anticipate meeting requirements, help ensure leaders are prepared and support the coordination and follow-through of action items and critical internal processes

Coordinate department and team events, employee onboarding and meetings with external partners, assistants, vendors and other stakeholders

Partner closely with other Executive Assistants and teams to improve coordination and efficiency, while handling sensitive and confidential matters with discretion

What You Bring

College diploma or bachelor’s degree

6+ years of administrative support experience in a fast-paced, professional environment

Experience supporting senior professionals, managing competing priorities and exercising sound judgment and discretion

Demonstrated experience managing complex calendars, coordinating high-profile meetings and events, arranging travel and processing expense reimbursements

Strong written and verbal communication skills in English

Advanced knowledge of Microsoft Office, including Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Outlook, with an interest in learning new applications

Experience in financial services or a related industry is an asset

You are motivated to contribute to something larger than yourself, approach complex challenges with rigor, and hold yourself to

high standards in a collaborative, performance-driven environment.

We provide colleagues with cutting-edge AI tools, dedicated learning time, and practical support to help them deliver with greater impact.
