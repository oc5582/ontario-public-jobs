Job description

As a leader in modernization, innovation, and creativity, East Gwillimbury is proud to be selected as one of Greater Toronto’s Top Employers for 2025. Located along Highway 404 in northern York Region, East Gwillimbury is a premier centre for growth within the GTA and is also Canada’s fastest-growing municipality (based on 2021 Census data). Those joining Team EG will enjoy several benefits including competitive wages, and access to numerous outdoor spaces which allow you to enjoy the beauty of EG while at work!

The Town of East Gwillimbury is an equal opportunity employer committed to an inclusive, barrier-free recruitment and selection process. As we grow, it is important for our workforce to reflect the diversity of the citizens we serve. We respect, encourage, and celebrate our diversity. Join our team and see the EG difference!

The Town of East Gwillimbury is currently searching for enthusiastic and dedicated Events Staff. Reporting to the Recreation and HALP Programs & Events Coordinator, the Events Staff are responsible for assisting with the preparation and overseeing various casual events within the Parks, Recreation and Culture department.

Key responsibilities include:

Setup and takedown of event materials and equipment

Managing and overseeing events

Communicating with participants, vendors and general public

Other duties as assigned

The individual will work at various municipal facilities including the Health and Active Living Plaza, Sports Complex and the Civic Centre. The preferred candidate will have previous experience planning and overseeing events and must have excellent customer service skills. The successful candidate will be required to work shifts including evenings and weekends.

Applicants must provide a current Vulnerable Sector Screening Police Reference Check prior to employment.

Hourly Rate: $18.07

Deadline for Applications is October 31, 2026

We thank all applicants for their interest, however, only those being considered for an interview will be contacted.
