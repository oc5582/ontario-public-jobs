Position Overview

Reporting to the Manager, Facilities Design & Construction, this position is responsible for the technical and coordinator aspects of the corporate facility asset management program including the corporate facility asset database management system, internal and external project management, and budgeting capital facility renewal needs.

Responsibilities

Completes facilities and buildings capital plan by prioritizing projects at buildings owned by the City.

Engages with all departments including local boards and joint ventures.

Develops and assists Manager with replacement and expansion plans for corporate assets including preparation of long-term capital renewal plans based on full life cycle cost.

Develop corporate standards for asset tracking, work-plan tracking, standard operating procedures and workflow charts.

Attend City-wide project meetings and engage with other departments to align goals with City management.

Identify cost effective opportunities to extend asset life in facilities.

Lead on projects such as asset management plan, climate action plan and enterprise resource program.

Provide asset management advisory services to internal customers.

Supervise the development and maintenance of the City's facility asset management system including field data collection, condition assessments and development of rehabilitation and financing strategies.

Create forecasting financial models based off current objectives and budgets.

Provides day-to-day management of the facilities asset team, including assigning work, training, and performance management.

Duties and responsibilities are inclusive, but not limited to the above.
