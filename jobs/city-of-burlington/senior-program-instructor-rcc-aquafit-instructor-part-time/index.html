Position Overview

The City of Burlington is looking for certified Aquafit Instructors that love fitness, motivating people and are committed to delivering high quality, safe and enjoyable classes for all levels of participants. This position is a part-time position with a flexible schedule based on programming needs. Hours of work may include morning, afternoon, evening and/or weekend shifts.

Responsibilities

Aquafit Instructors are responsible for:

Delivering deep and/or shallow fitness classes from the pool deck

Choreographing fitness routines to music

Modifying and adapting programs to suit various fitness levels

Requirements

The successful candidates will have:

One of the following certifications: Current CALA, WaterArt, YMCA, AQX or equivalent from an accredited organization

Excellent communication and interpersonal skills

A passion for fitness

The ability to inspire and motivate others

First Aid and CPR C

Preference will be given to applicants with previous fitness instructing experience. This posting is for current and future opportunities and will be filled on an as needed basis. Applications will be considered for any openings that become available between now and June 22, 2026.

Police Check Requirement

This position requires the successful candidate to provide a clear criminal record check with a vulnerable sector screening at their own expense.
