Location

Work location is subject to change at the discretion of the City due to operational demands. Effective September 2027, this role will be required to be on site 50% of the time each month.

Position Overview

An exciting opportunity to bring your social media expertise to one of Canada’s best cities to further enhance our social media success and ensure Burlington continues to be an engaging city. This position is part of the Communications and Engagement section of the Chief Administrative Officer’s Office and reports to the Manager, Corporate Communications.

Responsibilities

The Specialist, Social Media and Digital will be responsible to:

Manage the City’s reputation at all times while communicating through social media

Capture photo and video content on location at events, highlighting City departments, and other initiatives

Create engaging video content for social media, including filming and basic editing.

Develop, coordinate, and implement social media strategies and targeted social media postings

Monitor internal and external social media channels and engage in effective communication with the community, as required

Measure and report on the success of social media for the City

Work with Managers, Communications Advisors and other departments to develop social media messaging and creative assets

Support corporate marketing and advertising campaigns, promotional activities and events

Support emergencies by monitoring online conversations for sensitive or inaccurate information

Serve as subject matter expert on digital and social media trends tools and platforms

Provide training and guidance to internal stakeholders as needed and identify trends and emerging issues for digital and social media opportunities

Support digital media including web, video, photography, and basic design

Manage paid social media campaigns

Requirements

You will have a related post-secondary degree or diploma in an area such as Design, Journalism, or Communications, and 2-4 years of related experience municipal preferred. You will also have proficiency with social media tools and an advanced understanding of the needs/requirements of target audiences, and the impact and responsibility of information disseminated through social media. You will have a working knowledge of design, video and web editing tools/software. You will be a team player who engages others to build relationships and create the best communication products. You will be required to work some evenings and weekends and work may occur outside of regular work hours. This role requires some in-person work, attending events and activities across Burlington to support the City’s communications and marketing efforts.
