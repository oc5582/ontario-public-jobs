Current Opportunity Cleaner Department Cleaning Services Job Classification Casual Shifts Days, Evenings, Nights, as Required Bargaining Unit LIUNA 506 ABOUT THE MTCC Located in the heart of downtown Toronto, the Metro Toronto Convention Centre (MTCC) is Canada’s most successful convention and trade show facility. The MTCC welcomes approximately 1 million visitors each year, hosting a variety of events from meetings and conventions to public shows and galas. The Centre is an economic driver, a proud supporter of our community, and a meeting place where meaningful connections are made. Beyond helping planners create successful events, we believe that “Our People are the Centre.” Our talented team is our greatest strength, and we offer a corporate culture that values work-life balance, great benefits, professional development, wellness, and fun. Want to take a look behind the scenes? Visit our employee Instagram page: @lifeatmtcc JOB DESCRIPTION As a vital part of our facility operations team, our Cleaning Services team plays an essential behind-the-scenes role in creating a welcoming, world-class environment for every guest who walks through our doors. Reporting to the Cleaning Services Supervisors, this casual on-call position is responsible for maintaining the cleanliness and appearance of our award-winning venue, from meeting rooms and tradeshow floors to washrooms, offices, escalators, elevators, and parking areas across both our North and South buildings. JOB RESPONSIBILTIES Perform floor cleaning duties, including sweeping, mopping, operating walk-behind scrubbers, hand mops, and ride-on floor vacuums. Clean and sanitize washrooms, including floors, common surfaces, toilets, and replenishing supplies. Collect and remove garbage from designated areas. Maintain cleanliness in common areas such as the cafeteria, security office, administrative offices, stairways, parking and dock areas, washrooms, and theatre spaces. Complete special cleaning tasks as required, including vent cleaning, floor scrubbing, wall washing, high dusting, and operating power or pressure washers. Safely operate cleaning equipment and tools, including pneumatic wet vacuums, walk-behind vacuums, carts, bins, brooms, and various cleaning supplies. JOB SKILLS & QUALIFICATIONS Minimum 1-year previous cleaning experience. Ability to perform repetitive lifting and carrying of heavy loads (up to 50lbs). Handle large volume garbage removal and extensive cleaning tasks such as vacuuming and mopping for long periods. Comfortable standing and walking for extended periods and cleaning public washrooms. Proficiency in reading, writing, and speaking English fluently A pleasant and courteous demeanor, with the ability to provide exceptional customer service Strong team-building skills to effectively work in a collaborative work environment Why Join us?
