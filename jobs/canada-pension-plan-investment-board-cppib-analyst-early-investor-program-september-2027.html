Job description

Purpose. Performance. People.

Joining CPP Investments means joining one of the world’s most admired and respected institutional investors to drive a single mandate: to deliver strong, sustainable returns for generations of Canadians.

With a long-term horizon and global reach, we deploy capital at scale across public and private markets. Our size, stability, and disciplined investment philosophy allow us to pursue complex opportunities and build enduring partnerships worldwide.

For our people, this means meaningful work with tangible impact, real opportunity, and collaboration with exceptional colleagues who value partnership and performance. Here, you’ll contribute

to outcomes that matter alongside team members committed to excellence and shared success.

The Role

Join CPP Investments as an Analyst in our Early Investor Program, a full-time opportunity designed for exceptional new graduates to launch their career at a leading global investment organization.

You will be placed directly into one team within Credit Investments or Real Assets, where you will contribute to meaningful work from the start. From day one, you will combine the depth of being embedded in an investment team with a structured development experience designed specifically for early-career investors.

Your development will extend beyond your day-to-day role – and it doesn’t stop after initial training. You’ll begin with CPP Investments’ LAUNCH onboarding and Wall Street Prep training, building a solid foundation in investment concepts, technical skills, and analytical frameworks. Throughout the multi-week program, you’ll continue to develop through curated learning experiences, mentorship and coaching from experienced investors, exposure to senior leaders and opportunities to learn alongside your peers.

While anchored in one team, your experience will extend across CPP Investments’ interconnected investment platform. Program-wide learning, networking and cross functional exposure will introduce you to diverse investment perspectives and help you understand how capital is deployed across global markets. You will have the opportunity to develop deep expertise in your asset class while building the broader perspective and network of a global investor.

You’ll also join a community that encourages curiosity, inclusion, and continuous learning. Through employee resource groups, early-career mentorship, online learning tools, and connections across the organization, you will have the support and resources to continue shaping your development and building a career at CPP Investments.

Credit Investments (CI) Department

Americas Structured Credit;

Invests across asset-backed and specialty finance opportunities, delivering tailored capital solutions across debt, hybrid and equity investments. Analysts gain exposure to a diverse range of asset classes while evaluating new opportunities and supporting underwriting, due diligence and portfolio management.

Credit Strategy:

Manages investments across global public and private credit markets, with a focus on single-name fundamental credit, relative value analysis and portfolio construction for the global Credit Investments platform. The team analyzes individual companies and securities, monitors existing investments, and compares risk and return opportunities across markets to identify where capital can be most effectively deployed. These insights inform portfolio construction, asset allocation, and risk management across Credit Investments. Analysts gain broad exposure to company and industry analysis, credit markets, investment monitoring and relative value, while developing an understanding of how individual investment decisions fit within a broader portfolio.

Public

Credit;

Invests globally across public credit markets, with a strong focus on fundamental company and industry research, single-name credit analysis, and relative value. The team invests across liquid credit products and capital structures, combining investment judgement with data and technology to generate insights, monitor credits, and identify opportunities across global markets.

Real Assets (RA) Department

Sustainable Energies;

Invests globally across the full energy value chain, from power and renewables to midstream, upstream, and distributed energy opportunities. Analysts support transactions, underwriting and portfolio management across a diverse set of assets and investment structures, gaining hands-on exposure to how capital is deployed across the evolving energy sector.

Private Equity (PE) Department

Invests in private markets by backing top-performing private equity funds and investing directly alongside leading managers, spanning buyouts and growth equity across sectors and geographies. The team seeks out managers with strong, proven track records, and partners with them in their highest-conviction opportunities. Analysts gain experience of how private opportunities and sponsor relationships are originated, evaluated and monitored, developing skills in company, fund and industry analysis, financial modelling and partner diligence, while building an understanding of how investments fit together within a broader private equity portfolio.

Accountabilities

Analyzing and evaluating a wide range of industries, companies, funds, and securities, including the performance and capabilities of management teams.

Conducting valuation analysis of companies, assets, and securities to assess potential risk and return.

Performing due diligence or asset management of funds, assets, and companies, evaluating financial performance, market dynamics, competitive positioning, management quality, and expected merits and risks.

Building financial models to evaluate and compare investment positions or opportunities.

Supporting the investment process by providing insights, analysis, and recommendations that inform how we deploy capital around the world.

Collaborating with your team to prepare investment reviews, industry and market overviews, and written investment rationales.

Exploring opportunities across global markets to identify and assess new areas for investment.

Applying statistical and qualitative analysis to strengthen investment decisions and portfolio performance.

Researching and reporting on macroeconomic trends that influence markets and investment strategies.

Engaging directly with external management teams, industry experts, and other investors to gain insights and inform your analysis.
