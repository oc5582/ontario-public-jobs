Job description

Purpose. Performance. People. Joining CPP Investments means joining one of the world’s most admired and respected institutional investors to drive a single mandate: to deliver strong, sustainable returns for generations of Canadians. With a long-term horizon and global reach, we deploy capital at scale across public and private markets. Our size, stability, and disciplined investment philosophy allow us to pursue complex opportunities and build enduring partnerships worldwide. For our people, this means meaningful work with tangible impact, real opportunity, and collaboration with exceptional colleagues who value partnership and performance. Here, you’ll contribute to outcomes that matter alongside team members committed to excellence and shared success. The Role As a Senior Software Engineer, you will design, build, test and support technology solutions and products that enable business capabilities across CPP Investments. You will partner closely with business and technology teams, applying your understanding of the technical landscape and business needs to assess requests, develop well-reasoned solutions and continuously improve engineering practices. Alternative Investments Technology partners with investment teams to deliver and support technology solutions that enable CPP Investments’ investment activities. Within this group, the Real Assets technology team supports Real Assets, which oversees CPP Investments’ global investments in real estate, the energy sector and infrastructure focusing on long-term growth and sustainable value creation. The Team Alternative Investments Technology partners with investment teams to deliver and support technology solutions that enable CPP Investments’ investment activities. Within this group, the Real Assets Technology team supports Real Assets, which oversees CPP Investments’ global investments in real estate, energy and infrastructure, with a focus on long-term growth and sustainable value creation. Accountabilities Design, build, test and support technology solutions and enhancements independently within an Agile environment Apply engineering best practices and drive continuous improvement through coaching and influence Develop a strong understanding of the technical landscape and supported business capabilities to apply critical thinking to business requests Foster agility and innovation through experimentation, early feedback and responsiveness to evolving business needs Frame complex problems clearly, collaborate with colleagues and build support for well-reasoned recommendations Research and develop proficiency in new and complex technologies while adhering to Agile software development lifecycle practices Foster collaboration, mentorship and continuous learning while building strong relationships with business, technology and vendor partners What You Bring Undergraduate degree or college diploma in a related field, such as Engineering or Computer Science 5+ years of relevant experience Extensive experience with programming languages such as Python and Java Experience with software development practices, including version control, testing methodologies and Agile development Ability to write clean, readable and well-documented code while adhering to coding standards Ability to independently research and develop proficiency in complex technologies Strong collaboration and communication skills, with the ability to engage colleagues and develop well-reasoned recommendations You are motivated to contribute to something larger than yourself, approach complex challenges with rigor, and hold yourself to high standards in a collaborative, performance-driven environment. We provide colleagues with cutting-edge AI tools, dedicated learning time, and practical support to help them deliver with greater impact.
