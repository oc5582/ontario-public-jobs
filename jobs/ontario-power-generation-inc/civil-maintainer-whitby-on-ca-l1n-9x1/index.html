Job Overview

This position supports the relocation of OPG’s Supply Chain facility to a new work location. The successful candidate will assist with the safe and efficient movement of tools, equipment, materials, furniture, supplies, and inventory between the current and new facility. Responsibilities include loading, unloading, staging, organizing, and setting up items to support a smooth transition of Supply Chain operations.

This hands-on role requires physical capability, strong teamwork and organization skills, and a commitment to following established health, safety, and operating procedures.

Key Accountabilities

Support OPG site moves by safely relocating tools, equipment, materials, furniture, and supplies between assigned work locations.

Load, unload, carry, stage, and organize heavy or bulky items using safe material-handling practices.

Operate forklifts, trucks, mobile equipment, hand tools, and power tools required to complete relocation assignments.

Pack, label, inventory, store, deliver, and distribute materials, tools, and equipment to support moves and site setup.

Assemble, dismantle, install, and relocate workstations, shelving, storage units, temporary structures, and other facility equipment.

Perform general maintenance, minor repairs, and basic construction work related to preparing, restoring, or setting up work areas.

Conduct housekeeping, waste handling, cleanup, and site-restoration activities before, during, and after relocations.

Inspect and maintain assigned tools, equipment, lifting devices, and safety equipment; report defects or hazards promptly.

Follow all OPG health, safety, radiation protection, contamination-control, technical procedure, and operating requirements while protecting the safety and well-being of self and others.

Qualifications

High school diploma or equivalent.

Valid Ontario Class G driver’s licence; AZ or DZ licence is considered an asset.

Experience safely operating forklifts, trucks, cranes, power tools, hand tools, and other material-handling equipment.

Experience with moving, loading, unloading, staging, and transporting heavy equipment, materials, tools, or furniture between work locations.

Ability to perform physically demanding work, including lifting, carrying, pushing, pulling, and working in a fast-paced, safety-focused environment.

Demonstrated knowledge of workplace health and safety practices, including safe material handling, use of personal protective equipment, hazard identification, and following established procedures.

Ability to work from heights (climbing ladders, scaffolding, stairs).

Strong organizational, planning, communication, and teamwork skills, with close attention to detail.

Must be able to work day shifts for the duration of the training period and transition to afternoon shifts thereafter. Overtime may also be required.

The successful candidate will exhibit uncompromising integrity and commitment to upholding corporate values, and the OPG Code of Business Conduct.
