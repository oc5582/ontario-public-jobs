Job Overview

Reporting to the Senior Manager, Investment Operations and Compliance, the Manager, Investment Operations supports the day-to-day investment operations for OPG’s investment portfolios of over $53 billion AUM across the pension fund and nuclear segregated funds (together, “the Funds”) This role supports the Senior Manager in ensuring the accurate and timely processing of transactions, reconciliation, reporting, and compliance with overall fund level policies and legislation.

The ideal candidate will possess deep expertise in investment operations, financial markets, and the ability to manage multiple responsibilities in a fast-paced environment. The successful candidate will demonstrate excellent communication and problem-solving skills, along with keen attention to detail, as the role involves close collaboration with portfolio managers, custodians, and other key stakeholders.

This is an exciting opportunity to work in an environment where you will contribute to both Ontario Power Generation’s strong, stable pension fund and a healthy environment for all.

Key Accountabilities

Oversee the day-to-day investment operations activities supporting public and private market portfolios, ensuring the accurate and timely processing of investment transactions, settlements, cash movements, and corporate actions.

Oversee investment performance reporting, benchmark comparisons, and variance analysis across all asset classes.

Monitor the accuracy and completeness of investment data across custodians and internal systems, and resolve discrepancies in a timely manner.

Provide oversight of financial reporting activities, including the review of the Funds' quarterly and annual financial statements, coordination of the annual financial statement audit, and liaison with the Corporate Reporting team to support reporting and disclosure requirements.

Support the Senior Manager in the preparation and submission of regulatory filings and compliance reports, ensuring compliance with applicable regulatory requirements and internal policies and procedures.

Identify opportunities to streamline, standardize, and automate investment operations processes to improve efficiency, scalability, and data quality.

Build and maintain relationships with custodians, investment managers, and service providers to support the efficient execution of operational processes, reporting requirements, and issue resolution.

Develop, maintain, and enhance operational controls, policies, procedures, and documentation to support effective governance, regulatory compliance, and audit readiness.

Qualifications

Undergraduate degree required; Masters degree or other advanced degree or equivalent work experience an asset

Professional Designations (CPA, CFA) an asset

At least 8 years of experience in investment management with strong understanding of investment operations and comprehensive knowledge of investment products

Communication and collaboration: strong verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to interact with internal and external stakeholders effectively

Advanced knowledge of financial and management reporting, including external financial reporting and disclosure requirements, taxes (corporate, personal and commodity), audit, financial analysis

Understanding of investment management and associated risks, including asset allocation, performance measurement, and investment strategies across multiple asset classes (equities, fixed income, alternative investments, etc.)

Data analysis: ability to perform detailed financial analysis, reconcile accounts, and prepare accurate reports

Advanced Excel and technical skills: proficiency in Excel (e.g., pivot tables, macros), and familiarity with data analysis or database management tools (SQL, Python, or similar)

Attention to detail: strong organizational skills with a keen eye for accuracy, particularly when working with large data sets and managing competing priorities
