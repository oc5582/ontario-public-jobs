Job Overview

Ontario Power Generation (OPG) is seeking a dynamic, strategic, and results-oriented Senior Investment Analyst to join our Public Markets team.

Reporting to the Managing Director, Public Markets, the Senior Investment Analyst will support the oversight and management of OPG’s Pension and Nuclear Funds. The successful candidate will bring investment management expertise—ideally gained in a pension fund, asset manager, mutual fund, endowment, or similar institutional investment environment—to monitor investments, funding requirements, portfolio risks, and manager performance.

Working closely with internal partners, external investment managers, custodians, and advisors, the Senior Investment Analyst will provide sound analysis, insightful reporting, and well-supported recommendations that help advance the long-term objectives of OPG’s funds.

Key Accountabilities

Monitor Ontario Power Generation’s Pension and Nuclear Funds’ investments and funding requirements and take appropriate actions, as required.

Support portfolio strategy and investment monitoring activities including manager research and due diligence activities in support of investment initiatives.

Conduct research on capital markets, asset classes, investment strategies and managers, fund governance best practices and/or other areas relevant to the funds.

Effectively liaise with internal and external stakeholders. Undertake independent comprehensive assessments, analyses, prepare reports, briefings/presentations, and recommendations on OPG Pension and Nuclear Funds' investment initiatives. Responsibilities to include:

Analysis of asset mix and assist in rebalancing accounts.

Preparation of presentation material for various internal committees and other ad hoc requests.

Monitoring the controls for the funds' investments, ensuring that the custodian has properly recorded and reported all transactions

Preparation of regular reporting, all compliance reports and reporting required by legislation and other regulatory agencies, and other reports as required.

Qualifications

4 years of University plus 1 year of further concentrated study or a professional designation (e.g. CGA, CFA, CAIA, MBA)

A minimum of four years of relevant investment management experience.

Experience in pension fund, mutual fund, nuclear decommissioning fund, endowment fund, or other institutional investment management environments, including direct experience working with third-party investment managers.

Knowledge of organizational plans, policies, procedures, information sources, terminology, and governance practices, with experience supporting the development, implementation, and administration of investment-related policies and practices.

Advanced knowledge of modern portfolio theory, capital markets, and financial markets.

Strong understanding of quantitative methods, statistics, forecasting, and risk analysis, with the ability to translate external market drivers into meaningful analysis, forecasts, and recommendations for OPG investments.

Experience with at least one programming language or analytical tool, such as Python, VBA, R, or SQL.

Demonstrated passion for capital markets, strong analytical judgment, and excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to build effective relationships and communicate clearly with internal and external stakeholders

The successful candidate will exhibit uncompromising integrity and commitment to upholding corporate values, and the OPG Code of Business Conduct.
