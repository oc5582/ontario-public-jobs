Our Rail Fleet & Maintenance Team is seeking a Director, Rail Fleet Maintenance Management to provide strategic leadership, direction and oversight to the delivery of heavy rail fleet (coaches, locomotives, and DMUs) in a way that ensures optimal performance for our customers (availability, reliability, quality, cleanliness). This leader will build, sustain and continuously improve an integrated team of staff and maintenance service providers to ensure that all critical performance deliverables are achieved.

This position will work collaboratively with divisions throughout Metrolinx including Commercial Management, Service Design, Rail Fleet Engineering and Asset Management and Safety as well as external maintenance service providers to flawlessly execute service and deliver optimal, value for money, on-time performance and customer satisfaction

What will I be doing?

Lead the successful day-to-day delivery of the Maintenance Plans developed to align with the Asset Management Plans provided by Rail Fleet Engineering and Asset Management.

Champion continuous improvement of maintenance delivery, within a matrix structure and through maintenance service providers to drive optimal reliability and availability and value for money through LEAN manufacturing techniques

Provide regular feedback to Rail Fleet Engineering and Asset Management to improve standards, specifications, and maintenance plans.

Develop and implement Standard Operating Procedures for Rail Fleet Maintenance to ensure the safe and efficient delivery of maintenance services

Accountable for overseeing the optimal day-to-day delivery of rail fleet through rigorous management and oversight of service providers, holding them accountable to high standards of fleet reliability and quality, while ensuring compliance with corporate and applicable legislative requirements.

Provide shop floor Contractor management oversight to ensure compliance with agreement terms and conditions governing the delivery of rail fleet and facility maintenance services

Establish and manage quality assurance programs for contracts, monitor key fleet availability, reliability and quality performance indicators, and drive the development and implementation of countermeasures by maintenance service providers

Ensure that Rail Facilities are optimized and that service providers are maintaining all rail facilities in a way that ensures uninterrupted operations.

Act in the capacity of Rail Fleet and Facilities Assets” informed owner”.

Establish and deliver on objectives which are coordinated and aligned amongst all Rail Services and Operational functions

Lead the implementation and management of internal and external management control systems to continuously monitor and maximize safety, customer satisfaction, and quality of services

Actively participate in contractual negotiations for maintenance contracts with third parties

Represent GO Transit contractual and transportation development issues, with suppliers, industry associations, consultants, commuter rail agencies, and local transit agencies

Represent GO Transit, with regulatory agencies such as Transport Canada to ensure compliance with prevailing safety regulatory policy, best practices, and emergent issue

Liaise with regulators to address and manage regulatory changes and inspection service agreements

Develop and manage capital and operating budgets for Rail Service maintenance delivery including accountability for variances, reporting and identification of risk mitigation strategies

Meet service delivery objectives and manage department activities within budgetary limits

Monitor progress to ensure that planning objectives are delivered on time and within budget, and that anticipated business results are achieved including the ability to explain off-plan overages and exceptions

Transform, as part of an integrated team, the delivery of rail maintenance services from time based, reactive maintenance services to data-driven condition based collaborative maintenance delivery focused on optimal asset use and performance.

What Skills and Qualifications Do I Need?

Completion of a degree and / or professional qualifications in Engineering, or a related field – or a combination of education, training and experience deemed equivalent.

Demonstrated years of progressively responsible experience ideally in the transportation or integrated logistics sector; prior management experience within a unionized environment would be beneficial

Contract management and oversight experience would be an asset

In-depth knowledge of applicable legal and regulatory requirements governing delivery of rail services, including rules and regulations established by Transport Canada (TC), Federal Railway Administration (FRA), American Association of Railways (AAR), American Public Transportation Association (APTA), Transportation Association of Canada (TAC), Ontario provincial and municipal standards and best practices, building codes, contracts, and environmental legislation is required

Knowledge of Metrolinx and the provincial and national transit industry is a key asset

Service-focused leadership and influencing skills

Senior leadership skills to manage, mentor, and develop unit teams

Ability to plan, set priorities, schedule and manage personal and team deliverables

Experience with vendor management and contract management

Knowledge of budget development and management processes

Oral, written, and presentation skills from both a technical and a business audience perspective

Interpersonal and oral/written/presentation skills to develop and implement operating procedures and schedules and emergency and major event plans

Project management principles and practices to lead and direct cross functional teams and consultants engaged in simultaneous projects (i.e., development, project progress, related staff and project resources) and monitor project activities for adherence to timelines and project performance objectives

Knowledge of asset management strategies and concepts is an asset

Familiarity with relevant legislation (e.g., Metrolinx Act, Places to Grow Act, Planning Act, Revised Statutes of Ontario (RSO), Municipal Act, and other applicable acts and policies) is an asset

Knowledge of Human Resources legislation (e.g., Employment Standards Act, Occupational Health & Safety Act, etc.) to monitor and manage employees and contractors, including experience in the application of collective agreement provisions.
