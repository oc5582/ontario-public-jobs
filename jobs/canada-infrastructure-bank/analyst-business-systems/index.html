Job description

Headquartered in Toronto, the Canada Infrastructure Bank (CIB) is responsible for investing in new infrastructure projects. The CIB’s mission is to work with provincial, territorial, municipal, federal, Indigenous and private sector investor partners to transform the way infrastructure is planned, financed and delivered in Canada.

The Analyst, Business Systems, will be based in Toronto and report to the Manager, Information Technology. The Analyst, Business Systems, is pivotal in documenting business requirements, designing, developing and supporting business applications. The Analyst, Business Systems, will collaborate closely with business stakeholders, IT leadership and cross-functional teams to ensure that solutions are robust, scalable and aligned with organizational goals.

The ideal candidate will have an undergraduate degree in Information Systems or a related field, over three years of experience as a Business Systems Analyst, and be self-directed.

Your responsibilities include:

Collaborate with stakeholders, project managers, developers and technical teams to identify, document and manage business requirements and specifications. Ensure business requirements are complete, correct, consistent, concise and achievable, securing sign-off from business owners

Establish and maintain strong partnerships with business users to determine optimal solutions for business and system needs. Set stakeholder expectations appropriately and effectively communicate complex concepts to non-technical audiences

Design, configure, and deploy custom applications and automated workflows. Ensure solutions are efficient, compliant and meet business objectives

Act as a subject matter expert for Dynamics CRM and Charles River Private Markets (CRPM) applications and their integrations, including data synchronization, error resolution and process optimization. Collaborate with CRPM teams to ensure accurate data mapping, timely refreshes and alignment with investment management workflows

Provide ongoing support, troubleshooting and guidance on business applications

Design, develop and deploy comprehensive reports using Power BI, ensuring data accuracy, insightful visualisation and actionable analytics. Utilise advanced reporting techniques to translate complex business requirements into clear, interactive dashboards and reports

Develop and maintain technical documentation, training guides and FAQs. Integrate organizational change management practices into training and adoption strategies to ensure smooth transitions and high user engagement

Maintain documentation of system architecture, changes and user acceptance testing. Ensure quality and consistency of corporate data and produce ad-hoc reports or data extracts as necessary

Your ideal profile:

3 to 5 years of experience as a Business Systems Analyst, Functional Consultant, Power Platform Consultant, or similar role

Undergraduate degree in Information Systems, Business, Finance, Computer Science or a related discipline, or equivalent practical experience

Strong hands-on experience with:

Dynamics 365 / Dynamics 365 Sales

Power Apps, particularly model-driven applications

Microsoft Dataverse

Power Automate

Dataverse security roles and record-level security

Solution management and application lifecycle management

Certification in Microsoft Dynamics 365 and/or Power Platform (e.g., PL-200, MB-210, PL-400), an asset

Understanding of integrations, APIs, connectors, relational databases, and data mapping

Experience gathering and translating business requirements into system and solution designs

Experience with business-process analysis, functional specifications, testing and UAT

Experience with Business Central, an asset

Experience with Charles River Private Markets or similar Investment Management systems, an asset

Experience with Microsoft Copilot Studio, including designing or supporting agents and integrating them with Power Platform business processes, an asset

Working knowledge of Power BI and reporting concepts

Experience in agile methodologies and project management

Able to work independently with excellent organizational and priority management skills

Excellent analytical and problem-solving skills

Strong written and verbal communication skills

Sense of initiative, entrepreneurial spirit

Bilingual English/French, an asset
