Job description

At the Canada Infrastructure Bank (CIB), we make it better for Canada!

Canada Infrastructure Bank employees are building a better Canada, together. We partner with First Nations, Métis and Inuit communities on project investments to enable much-needed community infrastructure and meaningful economic participation opportunities. Our active projects include Indigenous partners from coast to coast to coast.

Our work is shaped by our values: to make it better, for Canada; break new ground; be the best of public and private, and to celebrate our differences. Want to know more about what life is like at the CIB?

Job Summary:

As an Investments Internship, you will broaden your financial acumen and gain exposure of project finance and infrastructure. Working with the CIB’s Investments team, you will be involved in all aspects of screening, due diligence and execution of investment opportunities. Daily responsibilities will vary with time and will include screening potential investment opportunities, financial modelling, completing sector analysis and transaction due diligence, drafting of investment memoranda, and participating in investment decision-making processes.

Your responsibilities include:

Assist with building financial models, project finance/infrastructure models, valuation analyses, corporate finance solutions and any additional necessary financial analyses

Undertake due diligence on selected aspects of investment opportunities

Assist with project development (coordinating data with clients, assisting in presentation and creation of pitchbooks to clients) and origination of transactions

Prepare and present investment analyses and investment memos

Collect and analyze industry research and trends

Your ideal profile:

This internship has been created to support Indigenous student talent and career development in the investments industry. We invite First Nations, Inuit, and Métis students to apply

Currently completing a bachelor’s or master’s degree in business, Finance, Economics or equivalent

Strong cultural knowledge, community connections, and firsthand understanding of the systemic challenges facing Indigenous peoples

Demonstrated interest and knowledge in infrastructure finance and impact investing

Excellent verbal and written communication skills

Strong interpersonal skills and ability to work in a collaborative environment

Excellent organizational and time management skills

Proficiency in the Microsoft Office suite, particularly Excel, Word and PowerPoint

Fluency in French is an asset but not required
