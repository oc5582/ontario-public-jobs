Job description

Headquartered in Toronto, the Canada Infrastructure Bank (CIB) is responsible for investing in new infrastructure projects. The CIB’s mission is to work with provincial, territorial, municipal, federal, Indigenous and private sector investor partners to transform the way infrastructure is planned, financed and delivered in Canada.

The

Advisor, Corporate Communications

, is responsible for using communications tools and platforms, notably the corporate website, to support campaigns to advance the corporate narrative of the CIB.

Reporting to the Director, Corporate Communications, this position provides communications advice and planning support for a variety of communications initiatives and projects. The role develops and delivers communications strategies, content and campaigns that drive awareness of the CIB and strengthens its reputation. Working closely with stakeholders across the organization, this individual identifies communications opportunities, provides trusted counsel and manages integrated communications activities across multiple internal and external channels to support organizational priorities.

Your responsibilities include:

Proactively support the development and implementation of the annual corporate communications action plan, delivering initiatives and messages that engage internal and external audiences across multiple channels

Develop communications and multimedia materials, including video and other digital content, to support integrated campaigns across internal, external and digital channels including the CIB website and intranet, corporate events, publications and social media

Support communications for corporate programs and priorities including accessibility, diversity, equity and inclusion, as well as official languages initiatives

Ensure communications messages and programs are captured in departmental planning and results and progress is tracked

Apply private sector communication best practices to corporate, digital, web and social media communications

Take on additional responsibilities and special projects consistent with the needs of a growing and evolving communications function

Your ideal profile:

A strong executor who will deliver outcomes that drive business objectives through effective corporate communications and multimedia content

Post-secondary education in communications, public relations, marketing, journalism, digital media or a related field

Three to five years of corporate communications experience with experience supporting a corporate communications program across internal, external and digital channels

Strong writing skills (research, writing, editing and proofreading) across a range of communications vehicles and platforms

Experience creating multimedia content, including video capture and editing using Adobe Premiere Pro and/or Final Cut Pro

Demonstrated interest in digital innovation and the evolving role of AI in communications, content creation and multimedia production

Exceptional organization and project management and a strong commitment to delivering excellence individually and at the team level

Effective ability to interact with others in ways that respect diverse backgrounds, experience and style

Experience in either the financial services, government or Crown corporations, an asset

Bilingualism in English and French, a strong asset
