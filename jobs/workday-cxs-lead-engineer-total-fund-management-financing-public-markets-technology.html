Job description

Purpose. Performance. People.

Joining CPP Investments means joining one of the world’s most admired and respected institutional investors to drive a single mandate: to deliver strong, sustainable returns for generations of Canadians.

With a long-term horizon and global reach, we deploy capital at scale across public and private markets. Our size, stability, and disciplined investment philosophy allow us to pursue complex opportunities and build enduring partnerships worldwide.

For our people, this means meaningful work with tangible impact, real opportunity, and collaboration with exceptional colleagues who value partnership and performance. Here, you’ll contribute

to outcomes that matter alongside team members committed to excellence and shared success.

Role Summary:

As a Lead Software Engineer, Total Fund Management, Financing, you will provide hands-on technical leadership for the design, development and delivery of modern, cloud-native applications supporting critical investment and treasury operations.

You will lead a team of software engineers, establish modern engineering practices and partner with business and technology stakeholders to deliver secure, resilient and scalable solutions. You will also advance the responsible use of artificial intelligence across the software development lifecycle to improve engineering productivity, software quality and delivery.

Accountabilities & Qualifications:

This role is part of the technology organization supporting CPP Investments’ Capital Markets and Treasury platforms. The team develops and supports enterprise applications across areas such as trading, post-trade processing, collateral management and cash management, enabling reliable and efficient investment operations.

Accountabilities

Lead the architecture, design, development and deployment of scalable, resilient and secure applications using Java and AWS

Lead, mentor and develop a team of 7–8 software engineers while fostering collaboration, accountability and continuous learning

Establish engineering standards and advance cloud-native development, Infrastructure as Code, microservices and event-driven architecture practices

Champion the responsible adoption of AI-assisted development, coding agents and intelligent automation throughout the software development lifecycle

Drive engineering excellence through code reviews, automated testing, CI/CD, DevSecOps, observability and continuous improvement

Partner with business stakeholders, product owners, architects and technology leaders to translate business objectives into scalable technical solutions

Lead the delivery of applications that meet performance, availability, security, compliance and operational support requirements

What You Bring

A bachelor’s degree in computer science, engineering, information technology or a related discipline

Extensive hands-on experience developing enterprise applications with Java and building cloud-native solutions on AWS

Demonstrated experience leading and developing engineering teams, including technical coaching, work planning and performance feedback

Hands-on experience with Terraform, Infrastructure as Code, CI/CD pipelines, DevSecOps and cloud automation

Experience applying generative AI and AI-assisted development tools to software engineering, including coding agents or intelligent workflow automation

Strong knowledge of software architecture, distributed systems, microservices, event-driven architecture, Python, containers and application observability

Experience supporting Capital Markets or Treasury technology, with knowledge of trading, post-trade processing, collateral management or cash management

You are motivated to contribute to something larger than yourself, approach complex challenges with rigor, and hold yourself to

high standards in a collaborative, performance-driven environment.

We provide colleagues with cutting-edge AI tools, dedicated learning time, and practical support to help them deliver with greater impact.
