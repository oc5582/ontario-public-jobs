About the Supervision Department

The payments landscape in Canada and globally is evolving rapidly, driven by technological advancements that are introducing innovative ways for consumers and businesses to make payments. Under the Retail Payment Activities Act (RPAA), the Bank of Canada is responsible for registering retail payment service providers (PSPs) that perform retail payment activities, maintaining a public registry of PSPs, and supervising PSPs’ compliance with operational risk management, fund safeguarding and reporting requirements.

The Bank assesses whether PSPs are managing risks effectively, identifies areas for improvement, and promotes compliance through a range of supervisory, compliance and enforcement tools. These positions will support the ongoing implementation and continuous improvement of the Retail Payments Supervisory Framework.

What you will do

As a Supervisor within Retail Payments Supervision, you will contribute to fulfilling the Bank’s regulatory mandate by supporting work across one or more areas of Registration, Risk Supervision and Enforcement. You will apply supervisory judgment, strong analysis and sound recommendations to help implement the Bank’s legislative and regulatory authorities under the RPAA.

Depending on the area of focus, you may assess registration applications and scope questions, evaluate PSPs’ operational risk and fund safeguarding practices, review PSP reporting and incidents, investigate potential non-compliance, or support the use of appropriate supervisory, compliance and enforcement tools. You will also contribute to policies, procedures, guidance, training materials and process improvements that strengthen the Bank’s approach to supervising PSPs.

Apply technical expertise and knowledge of supervisory concepts, principles, practices and methodologies aligned to the Bank’s Supervisory Framework.

Analyze complex information, identify issues and trends, and develop recommendations for Senior Supervisors, Principal Supervisors, Directors and Executives.

Collaborate with colleagues and internal or external partners to support registration, supervision, compliance and enforcement activities.

Contribute to business documentation, operational policies, procedures, guidance, training materials and continuous improvement initiatives.
