Job Summary

TRANSIT ENFORCEMENT OFFICER

or

SECURITY OFFICER:

Reporting to the Supervisor, the successful candidate will be responsible for the safety and security of all City of Mississauga transit system properties or all City of Mississauga facilities/properties while maintaining confidentiality, exercising discretion, and ensuring a high level of quality and integrity.

PHYSICAL DEMANDS:

Must be able to meet physical demands of the job such as: sitting and/or standing for prolonged periods of time, walking, climbing, lifting, foot action, push/pull, gripping, fine finger movements, using extremity motion such as reaching (vertical/horizontal), bending/twisting of back, twisting/turning of neck on patrol; sensory/perceptual demands along with good concentration, attention to detail and ability to deal with frequent exposure to emotionally stressful situations.

Duties and Responsibilities

Provide exceptional Customer Service to patrons and staff

Assist in emergency response, respond to urgent and potentially volatile situations, investigate suspicious activity, provide access to security sensitive areas to authorized individuals; responding to all access and environment alarms, removing unauthorized persons from City property and issue fines and or Trespass Cautions to violators in accordance with the Trespass to Property Act and relevant City of Mississauga By-Laws

Exercising the powers of arrest and detention

Identifying hazardous situations which could jeopardize the public and staff safety: conducting visual checks, investigating personal and property related incidents; assisting the public and staff in resolving situations by offering advice and guidance

Perform emergency first aid services to public and staff as necessary

Investigate and attend court on by-law matters when required

Assist team members in dealing with volatile situations, assist and participate in training needs as assigned

Communicate effectively by generating security reports and emails for all incidents in accordance with policies and procedures

Security Officers:

Recommend and initiate the removal of unauthorized vehicles when circumstance warrant such actions; directing traffic on City property as required

Conducting property patrols of all City facilities in accordance with defined procedures

Operating in control room; entering/maintaining/collecting information, conducting radio checks frequently

Transit Enforcement Officers :

Removing unauthorized persons from Transit property and issues fines and or Trespass cautions to violators in accordance with the Trespass to Property Act and City of Mississauga Transit By-Law 425-03

Skills and Qualifications

Post-secondary education in Law and Security, Police Sciences/Management or related discipline with a minimum two years of security related experience, or an equivalent combination of education and experience preferably in a municipal environment

Demonstrated experience dealing with the public in a position which requires excellence in customer service, problem-solving and conflict-resolution

Able to complete First Aid/CPR certification

Experience monitoring and responding to alarms in a dispatch environment, control room, mobile and foot patrol is preferred

Ability to identify and deal with potentially volatile situations independently

Proven ability to understand, speak and write in English in order to communicate effectively with the public and to report on incidents involving safety and/or service

Previous experience with CCTV systems, security access systems, security and fire alarm systems for large buildings is an asset

Must have the ability to interpret and apply the Criminal Code of Canada and Trespass To Property Act; familiarity with City of Mississauga's by-laws is considered an asset

Experience with Microsoft Office is preferred

Must possess a valid, unrestricted Class "G" Driver's License or equivalent, with a Driver's Abstract that has no more than 5 demerit points

Must successfully complete all required training for the position including physical demands

Flexible to work a variety of shifts up to 12 hours including days, afternoons, evenings and weekends, holidays, and may involve working alone

Officers are expected to perform their duties in all types of inclement weather
