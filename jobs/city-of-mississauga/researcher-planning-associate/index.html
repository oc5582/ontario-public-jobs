Job Summary

This is your opportunity to join a dynamic and exciting team that supports the development and implementation of municipal priorities such housing, economic development and complete communities. Collectively, our staff plays an important role in influencing the City’s cultural vibrancy, economic prosperity, social well-being, environmental resiliency, built form, and overall financial sustainability of the City. If you enjoy working collaboratively to create innovative city building strategies, we would love to meet you!

This position is a hybrid work arrangement (work in the office and some work from home). Our offices are located at Mississauga City Hall, 300 City Centre Drive, Mississauga.

Duties and Responsibilities

The Successful candidate will:

Generate timely and accurate research and analysis on planning and related projects to inform staff recommendations

Collaborate effectively with Planners and consultants to assist with the delivery of project work or provide research or administrative support at the Ontario Land Tribunal

Respond to project as well as ad-hoc inquiries from internal stakeholders and the public

Prepare compelling and easy to understand briefing notes, staff reports as well as presentations (live and virtual) effectively incorporating graphic and statistical information

Create, post and update content (e.g. website copy, media items, feature articles, newsletter copy, social media posts etc.) on the City's internal and external platforms (website, intranet site, SharePoint, event calendars etc.) to support project websites

Support the process of creation, revision and delivery of policies that implement the City’s vision and priorities

Research and keep abreast of new planning legislation and planning trends on various information and media platforms

Monitor demographic, housing, real estate indicators and other city planning trends and maintain related datasets

Assist with the design and delivery of community engagement initiatives including website format and content and face-to-face meetings, logistic and production support for stakeholders and public engagement including conducting surveys and preparing meeting notes/summaries and presentations

Skills and Qualifications

Post-secondary degree in urban or regional planning, or related degree or relevant combination of experience and degree is an asset

Demonstrated understanding of the planning framework and municipal working processes

Minimum of 2 years of experience in a research and analytical role

Demonstrated experience producing succinct research reports, discussion papers and conducting detailed research, benchmarking and trend analysis

Effective research, writing, oral communications and planning skills required

Demonstrated experience analyzing statistical, demographic and housing data analysis

Ability to collect and maintain sensitive information with confidentiality

Ability to be prioritize, multitask and produce accurate work to meet deadlines in a fast-paced environment

Ability to display tact and diplomacy when working with dignitaries, Senior Leaders, Mayor and Council

Working knowledge of MS Office, Excel, SharePoint, presentation software, Adobe Acrobat and basic desk top design publishing experience is required

Membership or eligibility for membership with the Ontario Professional Planners Institute and/or Canadian Institute of Planners is considered an asset

NOTE: Must be able to perform on-site checks and attend virtual and in-person meetings, events and activities outside of regular business hours
