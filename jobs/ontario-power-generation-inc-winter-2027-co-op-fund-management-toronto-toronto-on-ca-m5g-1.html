Ontario Power Generation (OPG) is looking for a dynamic, strategic and results-driven professional to join our team in the role of Co-Op/Developmental Student College/Uni.

Job Overview

As a Co-op Student at OPG, you’ll gain hands-on experience and develop valuable skills for your future career. You’ll work alongside colleagues from diverse backgrounds and contribute to projects that support a sustainable future.

Key Accountabilities

Assist with the daily administration and monitoring of OPG's Pension Fund and Nuclear Funds investment portfolios.

Support operational activities related to public and private market investments, including capital calls, distributions, cash funding, and portfolio administration.

Assist in maintaining accurate records and supporting investment operations controls.

Update and maintain daily cash flow forecasts and assist in identifying upcoming funding requirements.

Assist in preparing recurring reports, dashboards, and analysis for investment, operations, and management teams.

Assist in maintaining the accuracy, integrity, and governance of investment data across internal systems and external service providers.

Support audit-related activities to help ensure adherence to investment policies and procedures

Identify opportunities to improve efficiency and reduce manual processes.

Assist in developing Excel-based tools, trackers, and reporting enhancements.

Participate in process documentation and workflow standardization initiatives.

Assist with investment manager information requests, document tracking, and operational follow-ups.

Conduct research on investment managers, market data, industry trends, and operational best practices.

Assist with special projects related to portfolio operations, compliance monitoring, and reporting initiatives.

Collaborate closely with Investment deal teams, Total Portfolio Management team, Custodian, and external service providers.

Participate in team meetings and contribute to continuous improvement initiatives.

What makes a career at OPG different?

With operations across Ontario, OPG is one of the most diverse power producers in North America. As the largest generator in Ontario, we meet approximately 50% of the province’s electricity needs, largely from low-carbon sources like nuclear and hydro.

As we work to achieve our vision of Electrifying life in one generation, OPG and our family of companies are also helping advance the development of new low-carbon technologies such as Small Modular Reactors (SMRs), refurbishment projects, and electrification initiatives to help power the growing demands of a growing economy. Join OPG and make history.

The Darlington New Nuclear Project is leading the way in the advancement of

Small Modular Reactor

(SMR) technology in Canada – the future of nuclear power generation! Using new and innovative technologies such as small modular reactors, hydrogen and battery storage, while expanding our hydroelectric and nuclear portfolios, we are changing the electricity landscape in Ontario.
