‎

We are looking for a highly motivated Manager to join our Client Relationship Management team!

Reporting to the Managing Director, Head of Client Experience and Relationship Management, the Manager, Client Relationship Management is accountable for assigned client relationships and client interactions, delivering thoughtful, value-added engagement that strengthens IMCO’s client experience. The role works closely with institutional clients and internal teams to understand client priorities, apply sound judgment, and coordinate high-quality materials, insights, and deliverables that support informed decision-making.

The Manager also develops client-facing materials, insights, and engagement approaches that reinforce IMCO’s value proposition and enable clear, effective client conversations. Working through influence in a matrixed organization, the role coordinates across functions, exercises independent judgment, and ensures assigned initiatives are delivered with quality, consistency, and a client-first focus.

As a Member of Our Team You Will be Responsible For:

Client Strategy & Relationship Management

Manage assigned client relationships, building credibility and maintaining regular engagement on investment, asset allocation, and service matters.

Lead assigned client interactions, both virtual and in-person, identifying needs, coordinating follow-up, and delivering tailored, value-added responses within established relationship strategies.

Identify and advance improvements to CRM practices for assigned clients, helping deepen engagement and strengthen long-term partnerships.

Client Experience & Strategic Initiatives

Lead assigned client-focused initiatives aligned with IMCO’s client success priorities, bringing a practical client perspective to cross-functional projects.

Manage assigned client experience initiatives, including strategic asset allocation advisory work, investment strategy updates, and policy-related engagement.

Coordinate implementation and refinement of the client segmentation framework to enhance service delivery and prioritization.

Investment Communication & Advisory Support

Partner with internal partners to develop high-quality materials and insights for client discussions.

Translate investment concepts, strategies, and policy considerations into clear, accessible messaging for a range of audiences.

Maintain current knowledge of IMCO’s investment capabilities, asset classes, and market trends and apply that knowledge to client discussions and deliverables.

Client Insights, Research & Education

Support the annual client survey process, synthesizing insights to inform continuous improvement in client experience and service delivery.

Develop and enhance client-facing content that advances IMCO’s client education efforts and highlights organizational capabilities.

Conduct research and prepare materials that support client engagement, assigned initiatives, and broader relationship management priorities.

Internal Collaboration & Enterprise Contribution

Coordinate client-related initiatives across functional teams, using influence and sound judgment to align stakeholders, manage execution, and deliver high-quality outcomes.

Develop materials that improve internal visibility of client initiatives, including town halls, offsites, and related leadership updates.

Bring a client perspective to broader organizational priorities and growth initiatives within the scope of assigned relationships and projects.

Provide coaching, guidance, and quality review to team members and project teams, contributing to a collaborative, high-performing team environment without formal people-management accountability.

Reinforce strong execution standards, accountability, and quality across assigned deliverables and stakeholder engagement.

What do you need to succeed?

Undergraduate degree in Finance, Economics, Business, or a related discipline; advanced degree considered an asset.

6–8 years of relevant experience within the investment industry, investment consulting, or a related client-facing environment.

Demonstrated experience engaging institutional clients or stakeholders on investment-related matters.

Strong understanding of institutional investment strategies, asset allocation, and portfolio construction concepts.

Broad knowledge of public and private market asset classes and evolving industry trends.

Professional designations (e.g., CFA, CAIA, CIPM, FRM) are considered an asset.

Strong ability to build credibility and establish effective working relationships with senior stakeholders.

Strong communication skills, with the ability to translate complex investment concepts into clear, compelling messaging for diverse audiences.

Highly effective at navigating and influencing within a multi-stakeholder, matrixed environment.

Experience contributing to initiatives that improve client experience and support business priorities.

Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with sound judgment and attention to detail.

Ability to manage multiple priorities, operate under pressure, and deliver high-quality outputs within tight timelines.

Strong team orientation, with the ability to collaborate across functions and build alignment toward shared objectives.

Experience supporting team members and contributing to a high-performance, accountable culture.

Strong writing and presentation skills, with experience developing executive-level materials.

High level of professionalism, discretion, and adaptability in a dynamic environment.
