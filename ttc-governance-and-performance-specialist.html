What You Will Do Reporting to the Senior Manager, Governance & Performance, the incumbent is responsible for the day-to-day execution, coordination, and maintenance of procurement governance artefacts, training materials, and continuous improvement activities, including implementation of new technologies and programs that support PCM’s objectives and reporting functions. This role supports governance over TTC’s standard public-facing tendering documents and internal procedural tools; develops and maintains dashboards and user guides, assists in the development and maintenance of KPI/statistical reporting (e.g. PowerBI); and coordinates staff and client training to support operational excellence. The incumbent applies continuous improvement methodologies, conducts detailed process reviews, engages stakeholders, captures business processes and complies data for analysis, prepares documentation, including process maps, and delivers change management and communication materials for approved programs/projects (e.g., transition to SAP/SAP Ariba, ESG initiatives, and other modernization efforts). The role partners closely with PCM staff, client groups, and counterparts in the Commercial Management Department to ensure policy, procedure, reporting, and training alignment, enabling efficient client–PCM business partnerships and improving service levels, compliance, and value Promotes a respectful work and service environment that supports diversity, inclusion, and is free from harassment and discrimination. Provides leadership in the development and implementation of inclusive and accessible policies, programs and/or services for employees and customers in accordance with TTC's commitments and obligations under the Ontario Human Rights Code (OHRC) and Related Orders, the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA), and TTC's policies.
