Position Purpose: The Painter Grade 1 is responsible for preparing and painting all Zoo assets, and maintenance of the physical plant. This position will assist and provide support in implementing the Strategic Plan. Core Role Responsibilities: Prepares various surfaces such as, wood, metal, masonry, concrete, drywall and compositions for finishing or painting. Required to mix colours or tints for paint or stains. May be required to apply wall coverings. Some elevated work requiring use of ladders, scaffolding, zoom boom and elevated platforms. Operates power tools and specialized painting equipment. Special event set-up including animal shades, tents, banners etc. In the absence of the Painter Gr.2, this position may be required to perform lead hand duties. Performs all duties of this trade in compliance with municipal, provincial, or federal requirements and within standards set by the Toronto Zoo. Assist with and communicate details regarding contractor work on site as it pertains to the area including, but not limited to, projects put forth through the Project Management Office. Respond to and communicate details regarding emergency situations while on site as it pertains to the area and Toronto Zoo policies. Follows all policies and branch instructions. Attend appropriate training courses as required. Technical Knowledge and Skills: Must have completed high school, OSSD, or equivalent. Must have a minimum of 5 years’ proven experience in the same or similar position. Must possess an active 404C (Commercial Painter) Journeyperson trade license valid in the province of Ontario. Knowledge of modern methods and technology of the painting trade and possess a thorough knowledge of current paint products and related materials. Ability to mix and match paints and stains. Knowledge of the operation and maintenance of airless spray equipment an asset. Familiar with the use and maintenance of pressure washer, ladders, scaffolds and various safety and personal protection equipment. Ability to read architectural, mechanical and schematic drawings would be an asset. Working knowledge of the Ontario Building Code is an asset. Must have Working at Heights, Fall Arrest and Elevated Work Platforms Certificates. Must be capable of meeting the physical requirements of the position, including the ability to perform the following functions: Standing, gripping, reaching (both above and below the shoulder), standing, walking, sitting, climbing, stooping, crouching, kneeling, and twisting. Must be able to work in changing climatic conditions. Must ensure all applicable licenses and certificates remain valid and in good standing. Copies of licenses and certificates to be provided at time of hire and upon renewal. Well-developed interpersonal skills, along with strong communications skills. Working knowledge of the Ontario Occupational Health & Safety Act and WHMIS legislation. Must possess an unrestricted Class G driver’s license valid in the Province of Ontario along with a good driving record. Must be knowledgeable in the use of computerized programs and systems. Must be able to work weekends, shifts and holidays as scheduled. Must be able to deal with staff and public in a courteous and efficient manner. Capable of meeting the physical requirements of the position. While we appreciate the interest of all applicants, only those selected for an interview will be contacted.
