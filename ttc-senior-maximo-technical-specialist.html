The Toronto Transit Commission (TTC), North America's third largest transit system and recognized as one of the top places to work in the GTA has introduced its new 2024-2028 TTC Corporate Plan - Moving Toronto, Connecting Communities which continues the TTC’s legacy of delivering service to hundreds of millions of customers a year. The TTC’s new vision and mission statements also help promote the many environmental, social equity and economic benefits that the TTC provides: Vision: Moving Toronto towards a more equitable, sustainable, and prosperous future. Mission: To serve the needs of transit riders by providing a safe, reliable, efficient, and accessible mass public transit service through a seamless integrated network to create access to opportunity for everyone. The full Plan can be viewed on ttc.ca. Career Opportunity Join the IT Services Department at an exciting time, as we are hiring for a Senior Maximo Technical Specialist to join our team. What You Will Do Reporting to the Manager – Application Portfolios, the Senior Maximo Technical Specialist is part of a team responsible for the successful delivery of end-to-end products and services defined by their portfolio of business capabilities and applications. In this role, you provide the expertise and technical direction needed to define, maintain, enhance, or replace information systems and technology. Responsibilities include reviewing business requirements; developing processes and procedures; and supporting the development and implementation of effective and efficient computer systems by preparing detailed program specifications, formulating business and system requirements statements, performing detailed analyses, and preparing and reviewing systems documentation and functional specifications. You will design and develop subsystems within a larger, complex Enterprise Asset Management (EAM) Maximo environment; produce logical database designs; and collaborate with Database Analysts. Working with Server Technology and the Information Security Office, you align solutions with TTC digital cybersecurity requirements. Day-to-day responsibilities also include providing front-line technical support for operational issues, participating in system upgrades and updates, contributing to new system development, and delivering training to stakeholders in your areas of expertise. Working alongside Enterprise Architects, you help design lasting and effective strategies for the EAM suite of applications, aiming to empower employees and create a more reliable transit system. You provide leadership and direction to junior developers in software development best practices, Maximo best practices, and task scheduling/prioritization. You will conduct business meetings with various business partners to resolve requests, incidents, and opportunity- and demand-related enhancements, and you support the manager by managing intake and assigning requests to other members of the team. In addition, you promote a respectful work and service environment that supports diversity and inclusion and is free from harassment and discrimination. You provide leadership in the development and implementation of inclusive and accessible policies, programs, and/or services for employees and customers in accordance withTTC’s commitments and obligations under the Ontario Human Rights Code (OHRC) and Related Orders, the Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA), and TTC’s policies.
